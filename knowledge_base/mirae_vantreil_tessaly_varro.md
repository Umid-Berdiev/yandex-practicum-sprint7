# Mirae Vantreil Tessaly Varro

Mirae Vantreil Tessaly Varro was a Synth Flux-sensitive human Belisarian female politician, Veyari, and military leader who served in the Pact to Restore the Concord during the Hegemonic Era and the New Concord and Defiance in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Ostra Dreya, she was adopted into the House of Tessaly—the Belisarian royal family—and was raised as Princess Mirae Tessaly of Belisar, a planet in the Core Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Dorn Ragreim Tessaly and his wife, Queen Hathox Tessaly, making her the heir to the Belisarian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Tessaly devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Galactic Hegemony and the Prime Mandate.

Born in 19 BVC as Mirae Corvelle Vantreil, she was the daughter of the Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil and Senator Naira Corvelle of Ilveren. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Replica Wars, which saw the Veyari Order purged and the Galactic Concord reorganized into the Galactic Hegemony. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, Mirae and her twin brother, Corin, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Hegemon. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Mirae grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Belisar until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Mavei Quigask—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Jevrorar—in 9 BVC. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Replica Wars, Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane went to Mirae's rescue on Pudreth, and, after evading the Hegemony's hand on Drutal and Fobos, Mirae was brought back to Belisar. Years later, Mirae succeeded Dorn by representing their homeworld in the Hegemonic Senate, though secretly she supported the Insurgency. During the Galactic Civil War, however, Belisar was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Hegemony's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Tessaly to openly fight the New Order as one of the Insurgent Pact’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Thalwen, Tessaly married the Feilanian smuggler Dax Varro and learned the Veyari arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Tov Varro, on the day of the Hegemony's capitulation in 5 AVC. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Tov turning to the umbral tide like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Zerek Vash, and the Galactic Senate sidelining Tessaly's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Defiance and their efforts to oppose the rising Prime Mandate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Tov in hopes that her son will return to the radiant tide of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Thalwen. Despite her efforts, the Prime Mandate succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Tessaly's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AVC. Tessaly continued to lead the Defiance during the war against the Prime Mandate, all the while passing on her Veyari training onto Kessa—a Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger from Leinesk and Corin’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Tessaly used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the radiant tide. Following her death on Devrosk Hoshor in 35 AVC, her final actions inspired Kessa and the Defiance to defeat the forces of the reborn Xarn Nyxarion's Morkai Eternal on Vozix and spark an uprising against the Prime Mandate across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Mirae Vantreil Tessaly Varro's legacy passed on to the Veyari Kessa Vantreil, the military leader Arlo Nekra of the Defiance, and Tov Varro, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Kessa and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Vantreil family.

### Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Replica Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Concord and the League of Independent Systems that was engineered by the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion, Veyari Master Olvek set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Leziavreisk, the ancient Morkai homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Vantreil, and Olvek heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Mirae, but to what Olvek would tell her twin brother, Corin, about Mirae in his last words, before his death.

### Early life

Mirae Corvelle Vantreil was born on the asteroid of Ostra Dreya two days after the end of the Replica Wars and the newly established Galactic Hegemony's purge of the Veyari Order. She and her twin brother, Corin, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Concord Senator Naira Corvelle Draquezian, who died shortly after the births, and Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, who had recently fallen to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Mirae was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.

Of the two Vantreil children, Olvek felt that Mirae was more suited to be a Veyari, owing to her natural strength, will, and clarity (as opposed to her brother, who was impetuous, headstrong, unruly, and inattentive), wanting very much to train her. However, to protect the children from the Hegemony, Xarn Nyxarion, who ruled the Hegemony as Galactic Hegemon Malverin, and the now–Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor, Senator Dorn Ragreim Tessaly agreed to adopt the infant Mirae together with his wife, Queen Hathox of Belisar, while Oru-Kei Talvane personally watched over Corin, who would live with their father's stepbrother Halder Odrin and his wife Dramiax, on the planet Sarrakesh.

### Teenage years

Once warned to stay away from Hotrern Helvar by her father, the Grand Vizor made an official visit to Belisar when she was thirteen. Mirae crept along the halls of the palace behind the two men, listening to their cold, tense conversation. During the occasion, she realized he was nothing more than a merciless murderer and slaver.

Mirae followed her adoptive father's footsteps into politics, becoming a junior legislator by the age of fourteen. On Mirae's first trip to Velaris in 5 BVC, to serve as an aide to her father, she had flown with her father there on the royal yacht Polestar where Dorn pointed out landmarks making legendary places become real to her at last. Despite having grown up in palaces, Mirae, like nearly everyone else, was overwhelmed by seeing the bustle and brilliance of Velaris for the first time and Dorn had laughed at her wide eyes.

### Galactic Civil War

During the Galactic Civil War, after the transference of the Pact's headquarters from Trefovas to Orvax 4, and a battle for the technical readout of the Void Core, Tessaly volunteered for the mission to transport the plans, against the wishes of her father. He reluctantly allowed her to go but requested that she seek General Oru-Kei Talvane on Sarrakesh after intercepting the plans.

Originally Admiral Kinuth was intended to escort Tessaly to Sarrakesh to meet with Talvane. Kinuth's ship, the Profundity, however, was diverted to Quubem once the fighting began. A squadron of Insurgents calling themselves "Stray One" managed to obtain the plans and beam them to Kinuth' ship. The plans were copied onto a datacard by a squadron of Insurgent soldiers still on the Profundity, which had been disabled by the Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnought Devastator. Xarn Velgor led a boarding party onto the ship and massacred the entire squadron, but not before a fleet trooper handed the disc off to another man, Greitorn Quoresk. He barely escaped Velgor and got aboard the Zhavian IV, which promptly detached from the Profundity and fled from the battle, setting a course for Sarrakesh with the plans for the superweapon. Velgor, however, had spotted the Insurgent ship and began tracking it to Sarrakesh, later issuing an arrest-on-sight decree for the capture of Tessaly.

### War intensifies

When news reached the Pact Fleet that Xarn Velgor had made his appearance over Sheheix Vas and his ship had been forced to crash-land by Vantreil, Gramos deployed a battalion to back the company of the Insurgent Refueling Base. When Tessaly heard this, she immediately set out to assist them, as did Varro, Grawlokk, and T-8LX, on the Kestrel. Soon after they arrived, Tessaly and Varro were told that Vantreil had brought Velgor down at the cost of crashing his K-lance as well, but despite her worry about him, she would not spare any speeders. As a Commander, she coordinated the effort and deployed Cyan Squadron, of which another ship was taken down by Velgor.

Subsequently, Tessaly decided to go to where he was spotted last, along with a platoon of other Insurgent soldiers. Amidst the thick layer of mist, she and other troopers advanced until Velgor Synth Flux choked her two companions and confronted her. After a tense moment, however, Velgor apparently sensed an ambush, allowing Tessaly to escape and tell her friends where she was (and thus, lure Vantreil to him). She escaped and found T-8LX, who handed her a comlink; she used it to order all surviving insurgent pilots to come to her position and hit it with everything they had at her signal. Tessaly then ordered T-8LX to return to Vantreil, and waited for Velgor to appear before her again, and signaled all fighters to bombard their location.

### End of the Hegemon's reign

Roughly three years after the Battle of Orvax, Tessaly helped oversee the construction of the Insurgency's recently established Echo Base on the ice planet of Kryost. When Ovan Nekra and Jotha Ulla became trapped while carving out an ice tunnel for with an A-Wing laser cannon, Tessaly spoke to them after their rescue by Heroes of Orvax Corin Vantreil and Grawlokk, reaffirming their value as fellow Insurgents. Present at the base was also a new insurgent recruit named Rizia Klam, who helped herd frostlopers—which the insurgents had begun using as mounts—with her pet Fetreimum Kazhem. Captain Quanax Brajesk ordered the Fetreimum be killed at one point, but Tessaly noticed how skilled Klam and Kazhem were in herding. Additionally, Kazhem was incredibly important to Klam. In the end, Tessaly overruled Brajesk's order and allowed Klam to continue working with her pet, which Brajesk learned from a fellow insurgent after returning to Echo Base after a mission.

At some point during her time on Echo Base, the reactor in the base failed due to cold temperatures causing the heaters to become non-functional. Due to this, Insurgent personnel had to be moved onto ships. Tessaly moved frostlopers onto the Meridian Kestrel, despite Varro's complaints. She received transmissions from the repair crew through the Kestrel's cockpit. They informed her of the reactor being that of a MH-2600, and that it would explode and irradiate the base in a matter of ten minutes. Varro and Grawlokk volunteer to replace the dissipator, with a spare they stored on the Kestrel. Minutes later, Varro informed Tessaly that they needed to evacuate the base immediately without telling her what they had done. Despite wanting Varro to return to the ship, Tessaly ordered the base to be evacuated without Varro. A few minutes later, Varro tells Tessaly that the reactor had been stabilized and that they were able to enter the base once again.

### Veyari training

After the Battle of Thalwen, Tessaly began training in the ways of the Veyari under the tutelage of her brother. At Vantreil's suggestion the two of them trained on Devrosk Hoshor. She went on to construct her own arcblade that produced a blue-colored blade, and she would train in arcblade combat becoming skilled enough to fight on par with Vantreil and even best him in a sparring match on occasion. She also practiced meditation with Vantreil, and learned enough about the basics of the Synth Flux to levitate herself and sense other sentient beings. Corin offered her guidance about how the Synth Flux worked and helped her understand, from his personal experience, that the Synth Flux offered visions of the future, past, and even of the departed. Mirae believed this helped explain the visions of their mother she retained after her death. In her training, she demonstrated an ability to learn and adapt quickly, as noted by Vantreil that she was a fast study.

Sometime during her training with her brother, Tessaly and Vantreil discovered a mnemolith that contained the story of Triagreith'ra and her attempts to destroy the Morkai. Tessaly was confused by the sight of it because it didn't look like other mnemoliths they'd seen, but Vantreil assured her it was one and activated it for her, promising it would answer her questions. It contained recollections by "The Archivist". As the Archivist described Triagreith'ra's goals to kill Velgor and Malverin, Vantreil lamented that she never came to them for help. Tessaly suggested that this was because Triagreith'ra only relied on herself, saying she hadn't seemed like the trusting type. The Archivist suggested that the Insurgent Pact had perhaps been engineered by Malverin, which Vantreil said was wrong, but Tessaly pointed out that while he didn't create it, he definitely used it. When the Archivist described the presence of Xarn Velgor as feeling like you were in the presence of an animal eating its own young, both Tessaly and Vantreil latched onto that idea and contemplated it more.

### New Concord

After ending her Veyari training, Tessaly returned to the political arena. At some point, shortly before an Hegemonic emergency summit on Gotreith, Tessaly recorded a propaganda holovid to announce the destruction of the second Void Core, the death of Hegemon Malverin, and the birth of the New Concord to the whole galaxy, encouraging people to contribute to the defeat of the Hegemony.

Following the insurgency on Gotreith, Mirae served as the senator for the Belisar sector in the reconstituted Galactic Senate in Breidenoth's Hanna City. As Senator, she arranged for the wrecked pieces of the second Void Core to be diverted to the Belisar Flotilla, which used them to build a space station that became their new home. While a senator, Captain Lina Didrer approached Tessaly and Chancellor Aldren for a ship to take her to Pyrrhax to find her brother Milo Didrer, explaining that she believed he was there because of a dream she had. Aldren refused to send a ship based on a dream, but Tessaly, recognizing that she would still go, gave her the name of a a bar on Dogan where she could find the pilot and smuggler Quusheth T. Dralejia, explaining that he was crazy enough to take her. Didrer then went there to ask Dralejia for help, giving him Tessaly's name, though he initially said that his days of dealing with Tessaly and her friends were over.

### Tidings from Leinesk

Several months later, Mirae was heavily pregnant. She was tended to by the protocol mechanoid T-2LC, known as "Quagrin," who served as her nanny and called her "mum." At the advice of Doctor Harter Shuhirn, Dax bought his wife jogan fruits. While talking with her husband about political developments in the wider galaxy, the two received news via hologram from Sheikras Krukreis that the Hegemonic fugitive Grand Admiral Migran had fled to the barren Near Verge world of Leinesk. Mirae encouraged Sheikras to continue her investigation but to be careful.

Sheikras and her team traveled to Leinesk, where they discovered that most of the Galactic Hegemony had relocated there. While Sheikras, Jas Biapiam, and Mister Bones infiltrated Leinesk, Quekes and Napem returned to Breidenoth with news about the Hegemonic presence. After receiving Quekes and Napem, Mirae contacted Chancellor Aldren and told her to come to her domicile. After Aldren had questioned Napem and Quekes, Mirae defended their mission. Chancellor Aldren responded that the New Concord was responsible for hunting Migran, not Princess Mirae and her friends. However, Aldren praised Mirae for her efforts to liberate Leinesk.

### War's end

While watching the morning news with her husband, Mirae and Dax saw Senator Zetror informing the media about the Hegemonic presence on Leinesk. After meeting with Quekes and reassuring him that they had not double-crossed him and Napem, Mirae shared Quekes's assessment that there was a listening device inside her domicile. Quekes enlisted the services of his lover Conder, who was a slicer who had previously helped Mirae. Conder found a listening device inside T-2LC, and Varro removed it. Mirae and Varro promptly informed Chancellor Aldren, who went to confront Senator Zetror.

Mirae and her unborn child stayed behind on Breidenoth while Dax, Quekes, Napem, Conder, and Leska Thiskal undertook an undercover mission on Geimiask, the new capital of the New Concord. The five investigated five senators who had opposed Chancellor Aldren's resolution to send New Concord forces to Leinesk. They subsequently uncovered a conspiracy by the Black Sun and Red Key crime syndicates to prolong the Galactic Civil War by bribing and coercing the senators. Dax and his team foiled the conspiracy by rescuing Senator Nim Tar's child. Quekes then posed as an adviser to the Chancellor and offered them pardons and political favors if they changed their votes. This ploy worked and the New Concord dispatched military forces to Leinesk, beginning the final battle of the Galactic Civil War.

### Motherhood

Mirae gave birth to her son Tov Varro on the day the Hegemony surrendered to the New Concord. The two were visited by several friends and families. There were rumors that her brother Corin visited before departing on an untold mission. Others claimed that his absence was conspicuous. Stories of Tov's birth circulated within the public sphere. One story claimed that the Princess underwent three entire days of labor. Another tale claimed that it was a fast and painless birth, with Mirae merely needing to calm herself and meditate to make the moment as untroubled as a mountain lake. Other stories claimed that her son was born with a full shock of black hair and a full set of teeth.

Soon after Tov's birth, Dax Varro stood in the nursery looking at his son when Tov began to cry. Mirae entered the room when Dax called for her, asking her to use the Synth Flux to quell his sobs. Mirae told her husband instead to pick him up, and upon doing so, Tov burped and stopped crying, snuggling into his father. When Dax lamented that he'd never have what Mirae had with him due to her connection to the Synth Flux, Mirae simply told her husband that what he had was different because he was his father and that he didn't need the Synth Flux at all.
