# Edrik Vantreil

Edrik Vantreil was a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive human male who was a Veyari Knight of the Galactic Concord and the prophesied Chosen One of the Veyari Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Vantreil earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Replica Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Morkai, was created when Vantreil turned to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Synth Flux, Edrik Vantreil was born on the desert planet of Sarrakesh in the Far Verge Territories in 41 BVC. He was the son of Revex Vantreil, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand flux-motes, surpassing Grand Master Olvek and all other Veyari in the galaxy. Sael-Rhal Ormis, the Veyari Master who discovered Vantreil on Sarrakesh in 32 BVC, theorized that Vantreil was conceived by the flux-motes. Following the Battle of Ilveren, the Veyari High Council admitted Edrik Vantreil into the Order as the Tirren of Veyari Knight Oru-Kei Talvane despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Veyari Code. Vantreil remained a Tirren through the beginning of the Replica Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Varnoth. Although Veyari doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Edrik Vantreil had a secret wife, Senator Naira Corvelle of Ilveren. During the early days of the Replica Wars, Vantreil served as a Veyari General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the replica troopers of the elite 501st Legion against the League of Independent Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Vantreil oversaw the Veyari training of his own apprentice, Ilyra Seshu. By 19 BVC, when Seshu walked away from the Veyari Order, Vantreil began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Varnoth during the Battle of Velaris, Edrik Vantreil's faith in the Veyari was further shaken when he was put on the Veyari Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Deito Malverin was Xarn Nyxarion, the elusive Dark Lord of the Morkai who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Veyari had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Corvelle in childbirth, Vantreil betrayed and turned against the Veyari Order, believing Nyxarion had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Veyari Knight marched his legion on the Veyari Temple of Velaris where they executed its inhabitants, including Veyari younglings, as part of Directive 91 and the Great Veyari Purge. Velgor was then sent to Pyrrhax to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Morkai Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Hegemon. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Talvane, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Corin Vantreil and Princess Mirae Tessaly who were hidden from their father and the Hegemon as the Galactic Hegemony rose to power.

Being the Hegemon's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Hegemonic fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Hegemon's Morkai apprentice throughout most of the Hegemonic Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Hegemonic Military. In the aftermath of killing Talvane in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Orvax, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Corin to the umbral tide. Corin sought to become a Veyari, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the radiant tide of the Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Corin during the Battle of Thalwen in 4 AVC, but the young Veyari refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Hegemon to torture Corin with Synth Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Edrik, resulting in a redeemed Vantreil killing Nyxarion at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Morkai and fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One, Vantreil made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.

Edrik Vantreil was one of the most powerful Veyari and Morkai in galactic history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Vantreil bloodline, which culminated in Edrik's grandson, Tov Varro, Tessaly's son who was born by the end of the Galactic Civil War in 5 AVC. Varro would turn to the umbral tide as well, becoming the dark warrior Zerek Vash during the rise of the Prime Mandate. Like his grandfather, however, Varro found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Veyari apprentice Kessa, who heard Vantreil's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Vozix in 35 AVC. Varro's death ended the Vantreil bloodline, although Kessa, the granddaughter of Nyxarion, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

### Early life

Believed to have been conceived by the flux-motes, Edrik Vantreil was born to the slave Revex Vantreil. Although Vantreil was listed as being born on the desert planet of Sarrakesh in some sources, confusion existed as to where Vantreil was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Sarrakesh, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Sarrakesh was Vantreil's homeworld. While living on Sarrakesh, Vantreil and his mother were originally owned by Hesska the Zogg until she lost them to the Sheidequox Queipa in a skimracing bet when Vantreil was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Ani," he worked in his master's shop, located in Kar Zhezes.

Even at a young age, Vantreil exhibited exceptional piloting skills, and built the protocol mechanoid T-8LX, specially modified to withstand Sarrakesh's sand and heat for his mother. Once, while he was working in Queipa's scrapyard, he found a broken servomotor which he thought he could repair and use on the mechanoid that he was building and asked his master for it. With a glance, Queipa saw that it was worthless and grunted that he could, but as Vantreil walked away, Queipa told him nothing was free and had him work harder the next day. On one occasion, Vantreil and his mother were lost in a sandstorm, but the boy refused to listen to her when she demanded he return home if he could see it. Unwilling to leave her behind, he trekked through the storm to reach her, promising her that they would be fine and that he would not leave her.

### Discovery

One day, at the age of 9, Queipa shouted for him to come inside the junk shop, to find a tall man, a young woman, a Heihis, and an navimech mechanoid, conversing with Queipa. Vantreil had first seen the young woman, Naira Draquezian, when he looked up from his work, mistaking her for an angel, a species he had overheard spacers talk about, from the moons of Skemoth. Unknown to Vantreil, Draquezian was in fact Naira Corvelle, the Monarch of Ilveren, who had switched places with one of her Royal Handmaidens, to visit Sarrakesh.

At Queipa's instructions, Vantreil manned the shop while Queipa took the man, Sael-Rhal Ormis, into the junkyard. Vantreil was fascinated by the beauty of Naira, so much that he asked her if she was an angel. Shortly after Queipa and Ormis returned to the shop, Ormis informed his companions that they were leaving.

### Joining the Veyari Order

During the Master's funeral, Talvane told him that the Council granted permission for him to train Vantreil to become a Veyari. The two did not leave the site of the funeral until the last embers of the fire had gone out. The only others to remain as long as them were Corvelle and her court. Later, Vantreil and his fellow Veyari attended a parade, held by the Ilveren and the Heihiss, in Quillan to celebrate their victory and mark their newfound friendship. Afterward, Vantreil and the other Veyari left Ilveren, marking the last time Vantreil would see Corvelle for a decade.

### Talvane's Tirren

As he grew, Vantreil's exceptional skills made him cocky, but he nevertheless idolized Talvane, thinking there was "no one better" than his master. Having given up his life with his mother and lost Ormis, Vantreil clinged to his master, not wanting to lose him; throughout his first year as a Tirren, he had a habit of sleeping on the floor next to Talvane's bed, wanting to ensure his mentor would not vanish in the night. Talvane sometimes awoke at night and saw this. During one of their many adventures, he also rescued Talvane after he fell into a nest of gundarks.

Shortly after becoming Talvane's Tirren, a surge in overwhelming emotions shut Vantreil off from the Synth Flux entirely. This was able to give Vantreil temporary relief from his worries and fears for a time, but he soon realized that avoiding the Synth Flux was not the answer, and he reconnected to the Synth Flux. During his time as Talvane's apprentice, Vantreil was distrustful of teachers who corrected him, wondering if they were trying to help him or simply put him in his place, knowing that many had not wanted him trained in the first place. Even so, there were many Veyari who had faith in him, seeing his potential.

### Protecting Naira Corvelle

Ten years after the Battle of Ilveren in 22 BVC, as the Secessionist Crisis threatened to tear the Galactic Concord apart, the 19 year old Vantreil and Talvane were involved in an important mission to settle a border dispute on Fosei. Upon their return to Velaris, an assassination attempt on Naira Corvelle, now Senator of Ilveren, prompted the Veyari Council—at the behest of Chancellor Malverin—to send Vantreil and Talvane to protect Corvelle from future attacks. Edrik was delighted to see her after so many years; he had thought about her every day since they parted. In at least her first year in the Senate, Corvelle had at times thought about the boy she had met on Sarrakesh, such as when she passed the Veyari Temple. She also dispatched Kubrian to free slaves on Sarrakesh with the particular mission to free Revex, feeling as though she had made an unofficial promise to set her free, but Kubrian was unable to locate Vantreil's mother. When Naira said she wanted to know who wanted her dead, and Oru-Kei started to explain that they were only there to protect her life, not to investigate, Edrik promised Naira to find who was responsible, annoying Oru-Kei. When Naira retreated to her chambers, Edrik was disappointed, claiming that Naira hardly even recognized him.

However, Nib Nib, who was a representative in the Senate during this time, said Naira was very happy to see him too. During that night, Edrik and Naira agreed on a plan of using her as bait, in order to capture the assassin, much to Oru-Kei's dismay. Edrik told him that although Naira had covered the cameras in her bedchamber, he was certain he would sense if anything were to happen in the room. Talvane had doubts about his Tirren's power. Shortly after, when his master noticed that Vantreil was tired, he admitted that he had constant nightmares about his mother; he also revealed his powerful affection for Corvelle. Oru-Kei warned him to control his emotions, saying that Naira was a politician after all, and could not be trusted. This led the two into a debate about whether every politician, including Corvelle and Malverin, was corrupt. As Vantreil expressed his faith in Malverin's benevolence, he sensed something in Naira's room. He arrived just in time to cut two venomous kouhuns in half, just a few centimeters from the senator's face. Oru-Kei noticed the assassin's mechanoid at the window and threw himself out, grabbing it.

### The Replica Wars begin

Following the engagement on Kharrab, Vantreil built a new arcblade to replace the one he lost. Early in the war, Vantreil quickly came to have encounters with Varnoth's personal agents. Later, Vantreil served with his Veyari Master as an informal Veyari Commander, participating in battles on a rocky planet alongside Talvane's 7th Sky Corps and Replica Marshal Commander Jajis. Afterwards, Vantreil encountered the replica trooper known as Sister, who was arranging the helmets of her fallen brothers beneath a tree as a memorial.

Vantreil spoke with Sister, and she explained that she was saddened by her brothers' deaths, as well as the origins of her name and the camaraderie her replica brothers had shown for her and her gender identity. Vantreil shared that the Veyari supported such identity and the belief of rising above things, such as Sister's transcendence of the concepts of gender. Sister agreed, and both moved to rendezvous with Talvane at his location. A short time later, Vantreil was promoted to the rank of Veyari Knight. This was initially done informally as a result of the increased need for Veyari in the battlefield. Though the Veyari were not yet formally integrated within the Grand Army of the Concord, his appointment to Knighthood made Vantreil an acting Veyari General.

### No end in sight

Even as the Replica Wars continued with no end in sight, Vantreil was allowed a leave after a long tour of duty. After returning home to his wife's apartment for a romantic evening, Vantreil was recalled to the Temple by the Veyari Council. Believing Senator Rush Rakeil to be a Secessionist supporter, they decided they needed a spy—in this case, Senator Corvelle, of whom Rakeil was an old friend. The next day, Vantreil met Corvelle in the Senate and asked her about Rakeil, who initially refused to do it until she heard that Rakeil could be a Secessionist. Vantreil accompanied her to the Veyari Council, where the Masters encouraged her to find out about his possible treachery. After learning that he was planning to go to Fetre Senereix, Vantreil dressed as the pilot of Naira's starship and took them there, all the while frustrating Rakeil's attempts to get closer to his wife.

During the mission, Vantreil and K7-M4 waited for Corvelle to give them a signal; when she did, Vantreil sneaked into her quarters, where he saw Corvelle and Rakeil embracing. Though it prompted a surge of anger and jealousy in Vantreil, she discretely showed him the disk she had stolen from Rakeil and gave it to Vantreil, after which the Veyari retreated. It was soon after that the poison that Corvelle had been given by Jidern Dod began to set in, and she passed out. After Dod revealed to Rakeil she was a spy, he helped Vantreil take her back to their ship, back to Velaris, and forced Dod to give him the antidote, only for Vantreil to leave Rakeil behind, stranded on the planet.

### Personal and wartime conflicts

When the Veyari Council received a 2,000-year-old Veyari distress signal, Vantreil, along with Talvane and Seshu, were sent by the High Council to the Bidrithen system, where they were supposed to meet up with Captain Tarn and an armed cruiser. Though they both made it to the rendezvous point, neither Tarn's nor the Veyari's ship could see each other. In fact, the Veyari's ship was guided to a mysterious planet, Skikia, where they met by a mysterious figure called the Daughter, who asked Vantreil if he was "the one" and who wanted to lead them to the Father, when Vantreil was separated from Talvane and Seshu, and the Daughter.

Vantreil tried to follow her, only to reach the monastery, where he met the Father and was given shelter for the night. As he tried to sleep, the figure of his mother, Revex, appeared before him and encouraged him to tell her of his pain and guilt—that he was too late to save her, and that he had failed as a Veyari and that he had failed her, having tasted only vengeance when he slaughtered so many to avenge her death. But, as he mentioned his wife, who he claimed was "everything" to him, the figure finally revealed itself to be the Son, briefly assuming a monstrous appearance before disappearing.

### Nearing the end

During the defense of Fetre Senereix, Vantreil and Seshu engaged Secessionist forces on their Eta-2 Sobro-class interceptors, until they received an urgent mission recalling them to the Veyari Temple. Its hangar had been bombed and, as the two had been off-world during the attack, Vantreil and Seshu were to lead the investigation to uncover the criminal—likely to be a Veyari. As they entered the Temple's hangar, they discussed the possibilities a Veyari was behind the attack. Seshu doubted a Veyari could bomb such a sacred location to the Veyari, and in the process, kill their own colleagues and employees. However, Vantreil told Seshu to keep an open mind, and he reminded her there were Veyari that did not agree with their methods, and went rogue, such as Varnoth and Votram. As a result, everyone who was at the Temple during the bombing were potential suspects, including Veyari.

As Vantreil and Seshu walked across the burning hangar, they met Brohian-NFT, whom Vantreil sent alongside Seshu to interview the witnesses, whereas he meditated at the hangar. Unable to discover anything, Vantreil met up with Seshu and Brohian, who told him about their missing suspect, Puborn Jiadre. With this clue, Vantreil went to speak with Chief of Security Skafith Drilern. As Vantreil approached Drilern on the Veyari Temple's steps, he witnessed a crowd of angry protestors, rallying against the Veyari for their violence during the war. After Vantreil showed Drilern the hologram of Jiadre on an imagecaster, Kagril Fepeth identified Jiadre in the hologram and informed the Veyari she was Jiadre's wife. Later Vantreil and Seshu cornered Fepeth near her home, and she under pressure admitted to feeding Jiadre nano-mechanoids but would not reveal her motives.

### Birth of Xarn Velgor

At some point before the Battle of Velaris, Vantreil was on Halus and had another encounter with Varnoth, yet he failed to defeat the Count. Along with the 501st and 212th, Vantreil and Talvane were sent to Doskosk to take down the Secessionist forces on the planet. The fight caused Vantreil and Talvane to split their respective battalions into two fronts.

Vantreil, Tarn, and the 501st Legion defeated the Secessionist Mechanoid Army on their front, so they left to assist Talvane and his 212th Attack Battalion, which was suffering heavy casualties from the ongoing conflict. While Talvane and Commander Jajis were taking cover from the blasts, Vantreil ordered his men to hide under the bridge, while K7-M4 would tell them when to launch. Next, Vantreil stepped into the fight and convinced his Master to follow his plan. Vantreil faked a surrender to draw out the mechanoid commander, who saw through the lies of the Veyari Knight; however, it was too late, as Vantreil used his Synth Flux abilities to destroy the mechanoid commander, allowing his 501st Legion led by Captain Tarn to make a surprising ambush on the rest of the mechanoid army, ending the conflict.

### Becoming Xarn Velgor

Soon thereafter, Velgor saw Corvelle's star skiff land and ran to meet her. She told Velgor that Talvane had discovered their secret and had also told her that Velgor had joined the umbral tide, hoping that he'd prove Talvane wrong. However, Velgor only confirmed it to be true. Driven power-hungry by the Umbral Tide and declaring the galaxy was at peace, Velgor tried to convince her that they could be happy together as rulers of the new Hegemony, promising he could overthrow Nyxarion. In Velgor's ideal world, Corvelle would have taken his hand, believing the man before her was still her husband. In reality, however, Velgor's propositions and actions horrified his wife, who begged him to leave the umbral tide behind.
