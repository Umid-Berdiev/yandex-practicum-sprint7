# K7-M4

K7-M4, pronounced Kayseven-Dogra and often referred to as K7 (Kayseven), was a K7-series navimech mechanoid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky mechanoid who served a multitude of leaders, K7-M4 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Corvelle of Ilveren, K7-M4 wound up serving Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil during the waning years of the Galactic Concord, often accompanied by the protocol mechanoid T-8LX in many adventures throughout the Replica Wars. After Vantreil turned to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, the mechanoid served Senator Dorn Tessaly for a time in the Hegemonic Senate. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Galactic Concord, K7-M4 played a pivotal role in helping the Insurgent Pact destroy the Hegemony's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Veyari Knight Corin Vantreil, the son of his two former masters throughout the Galactic Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Kryost and Thalwen, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Hegemony's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, K7 accompanied Corin Vantreil until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Veyari Order in 28 AVC. Following Corin's disappearance, K7 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer mechanoid models started to out-compute the aging navimech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Insurgency protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old mechanoids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Defiance as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Gehasox Base, K7 went on to accompany the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Kessa on her journey to train under Corin Vantreil on Krelath-To, the planet of the First Veyari Temple, where the mechanoid was reunited with his old master. K7-M4 continued to aid the Defiance alongside his longtime counterpart T-8LX throughout the Prime Mandate-Defiance War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Vozix, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Arlo Nekra.

### Serving Naira Corvelle

K7-M4 was built around prior or during 32 BVC. He was one of several navimech mechanoids on Queen Naira Corvelle's Ilveren Royal Starship during the invasion of Ilveren by the Merchant Combine that year. The ship was used by Queen Corvelle and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Merchant Combine battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only K7-M4 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray pulser fire. K7-M4 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into slipspace.

As the ship traveled to Sarrakesh, K7-M4 was taken before Kubrian, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Corvelle, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Sarrakesh without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the slipdrive. K7-M4 accompanied Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis, Heihis exile Nib Nib Tolk, and Naira, the queen in disguise, to the city of Kar Zhezes to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Sheidequox merchant Queipa they found they could not afford a replacement slipdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Queipa's slaves, a boy named Edrik Vantreil, and his mother, Revex.

### The Replica Wars

K7-M4 accompanied Corvelle to Velaris prior to the senate's vote on the Military Creation Act. After an assassination attempt on Corvelle took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Breiski, Oru-Kei Talvane and Edrik Vantreil were assigned to be her Veyari protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Corvelle and although one assassin, Biafin Kiathisk, was neutralized, another escaped.

For her own protection, Naira was sent home to Ilveren, with K7-M4 and Vantreil accompanying her. The three later traveled to Sarrakesh, where they were reunited with T-8LX, and then to Kharrab to rescue Talvane. As they explored a massive mechanoid factory, K7-M4 had to save Corvelle from being killed by molten metal while T-8LX had his head switched with a battle mechanoid's. K7 was later able to restore his companion during the Battle of Kharrab. After the battle, K7-M4 and T-8LX were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Edrik Vantreil and Naira Corvelle in Lake Country. After this Naira gave ownership of K7 to Edrik while she kept 8LX.

### Age of the Hegemony

After Edrik Vantreil fell to the umbral tide and became a Morkai apprentice under the name "Xarn Velgor," K7-M4 continued to accompany him out of loyalty, but he made sure to warn T-8LX of the changing circumstances when they returned to Corvelle's apartment aboard their green Sobro-class light interceptor. Afterward, K7-M4 accompanied Velgor to Pyrrhax in the starfighter, but the newly-christened Morkai ordered his mechanoid to stay behind at the ship when he entered the Luthol Corp Mining Facility to cut down the Executive Secessionist Council. After Velgor did so and ensured the Secessionist Mechanoid Army was shut down, bringing the Replica Wars to an end with a victory for the newly-established Galactic Hegemony Malverin transformed the Concord into, K7-M4 was still present as Velgor ran out of the facility to reunite with Corvelle when she arrived on Pyrrhax in her J-type Ilveren star skiff with T-8LX.

As Velgor began to argue with Corvelle after Talvane, having snuck aboard Corvelle's ship, revealed himself, K7-M4 watched as his master Synth Flux choked his wife, leaving her to fall to the ground. After watching the duel between Talvane and Velgor, K7-M4 was left with the belief that Velgor had died and thus left the planet with Talvane, Corvelle, and T-8LX aboard the star skiff, leaving Velgor's Sobro interceptor abandoned. They traveled to the Ostra Dreya asteroid field, where Naira gave birth to twin children before dying. Following Naira's death, K7-M4 and T-8LX passed into the service of Dorn Tessaly of the Royal House of Belisar, Belisar's Senator and a good friend of the late Corvelle. While T-8LX underwent a memory wipe, K7-M4 was allowed to preserve his memories.

### Hero of the insurgency

K7-M4 was with T-8LX at the Insurgent Pact's secret base on Orvax 4 at the time the Pact Fleet prepared to leave for the Battle of Quubem to help reinforce Stray One, an unofficial squad that had traveled to Quubem to retrieve the plans for the Galactic Hegemony's Void Core, a new superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds.

The Pact succeeded in stealing the plans for the superweapon. K7-M4 and his protocol counterpart T-8LX were serving Princess Mirae Tessaly's blockade runner Zhavian IV, which was being pursued by Xarn Velgor's Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnought. Senator Tessaly's adopted daughter Mirae was now Belisar's Senator in the Hegemonic Senate, one of the last remnants of the Old Concord. During the pursuit, the Zhavian IV was crippled by a laser blast from Velgor's ship and Hegemonic shocktroopers boarded the vessel. After Princess Mirae uploaded the secret Void Core plans and a holographic message for Master Talvane into K7-M4's memory bank, he and T-8LX fled in an escape pod to the desert planet Sarrakesh.

### The Galactic Civil War's final phase

Later, the Insurgent Pact established a base on the remote icy planet of Kryost and K7-M4 followed his companions there. When Corin Vantreil failed to return from a snow patrol, Dax Varro traveled out on another frostloper to rescue him. When both men did not return that night, K7-M4 calculated that they had a poor chance of surviving in the frozen wilderness. Despite his pessimistic assessment, Vantreil and Varro were safely recovered by a snowspeeder patrol the following day.

Later, the insurgents were forced to evacuate Kryost after Varro and Grawlokk encountered a Hegemonic probe mechanoid. However, Xarn Velgor's fleet blockaded the planet; preventing the insurgents from leaving. The Insurgents managed to penetrate the Hegemonic blockade by using an ion cannon to clear away for their ships to flee into slipspace. As the battle began, K7-M4 was moving about Echo Base and reacted to the news of incoming HK-RAs before a small piece of snow broke off from the ceiling, landing on his dome. Following the ground assault, K7-M4 and his master, Vantreil left Kryost in a K-lance fighter.

### After Thalwen

At some point following the Battle of Thalwen, Corin sought out an ancient compass created by the ancient Veyari Order. K7-M4 accompanied Corin to Tovras, where the compass was located, but remained with Corin's K-lance while his master found the compass.

K7-M4 accompanied Corin and Jotha Ulla on a mission to obtain two Synth Flux-sensitive trees that sprouted from the one planted in the Veyari Temple training ground. The trees were stored in a Hegemonic base on Zemol, and Corin had Ulla impersonate a Hegemonic Security Bureau officer, Skelil Strom, to enter the facility, and had K7-M4 stash his arcblade. This did not fool Commandant Vrogisk, who knew Strom had a prosthetic eye, so K7-M4 ejected the arcblade for Corin to fight their way out and escape with the trees.

### Defiance agent

Later, K7-M4 spent some time at the Defiance Camp on the polluted planet of Rokroth. He watched Mirae Tessaly leave and prepare for her mission to Sel Dradan. K7-M4 and T-8LX were later stationed on the planet Kriadas, operating undercover as two mechanoids who were categorizing flora and fauna. On planet, the two mechanoids were joined by a local mechanoid repair technician in a battle against the Taki Death Gang, convincing T-8LX to tell the technician of their true purpose on planet as Defiance agents, much to K7-M4's annoyance. From the battle, T-8LX also acquired an aerith vial on the belief it could be useful, although K7-M4 demanded he be the one who carried it back when T-8LX dropped it. Pushing the vial forward on his front leg, K7-M4 and T-8LX then departed to meet back up with their fellow Defiance agents.

K7-M4 later joined the technician on a mission to destroy a Prime Mandate re-education facility, which they hoped to take out with the aerith gas canister. However, as K7-M4 tossed the canister over to the technician, it was knocked away and destroyed by a laser activated by Lieutenant Gauge. Improvising, K7-M4 and the technician took command of the base's drill to destroy it and kill Gauge.

### Ending the war

Following a broadcast that implied Xarn Nyxarion had returned to life, K7-M4 accompanied Grawlokk, Brannoc, Arlo Nekra, and Kiqueirn on the Meridian Kestrel to meet an informant for the Defiance, Meigo, who gave them a message in the form of a data encoder intended for Tessaly. Brannoc proceeded to insert the message into K7-M4 before heading out. After returning from Herath, K7-M4 was present in the Defiance base on Devrosk Hoshor, where the Defiance decoded the intelligence to discover that Nyxarion had indeed returned. The message revealed the Hegemon was hiding on the Morkai world of Vozix, the very planet that had been at the center of Maldonne and Vantreil's unfinished quest from years earlier. K7-M4 quickly searched through star charts, determining the world was still hidden from the galaxy and informing his allies of its legendary status as a hidden Morkai planet.

As Kessa and others traveled to Bathei to search for a Morkai wayfinder, K7-M4 stayed behind. Before departing, T-8LX told K7-M4 that he had been a good friend. K7 accompanied Mirae's side before she made a final attempt to reach her son, Tov, through the Synth Flux. K7-M4 was saddened witnessing Mirae's dying moments shortly after. K7-M4 later restored T-8LX's memory after Kessa, Brannoc and Arlo were forced to wipe it to bypass certain protocols that had prevented T-8LX providing them with a translation of an important Morkai artifact. K7-M4 later accompanied Arlo in his K-lance for the assault on Vozix, and the navimech later joined the others in celebration after the defeat of Nyxarion and the Morkai Eternal.

### A loyal mechanoid

K7-M4 was known for his loyal, courageous, and assertive personality. Over the years, K7-M4 had served several masters diligently including the Royal House of Ilveren, Senator Naira Corvelle, the Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, Senator Dorn Tessaly, and Edrik's son, Corin Vantreil. While K7-M4 was not designed for combat, K7-M4 was still able to hold his own in a fight on several occasions during the Replica Wars and the Galactic Civil War. Unlike his fastidious and worry-prone protocol counterpart T-8LX, K7-M4 had an adventurous spirit and was able to cope well under challenging circumstances. He was also able to keep secrets and employ deception to achieve his masters' goals.

He was known to use colorful language, with 0-0-0 describing him as a "foul-mouthed little navimech". When K7 and T-8LX were hiding from Hegemonic forces in Kar Deli, T-8LX told him to watch his language, and when K7 was reunited with Corin Vantreil on Krelath-To, his former master told him to do the same, as they were on a sacred island. When Vantreil prepared to leave Murgath to rescue Dax Varro and Mirae Tessaly, he told K7 to fire up the power converters, resulting in Oru-Kei Talvane's ghost shooting the mechanoid a look meant to order him not to do so. Although Talvane wasn't sure if the navimech could see him, the little mechanoid gave a chirp that Talvane was sure meant "kriff off!".

### Relationships

First meeting him on Sarrakesh during the Ilveren Crisis before truly befriending him during the Battle of Kharrab, T-8LX was K7-M4's best friend and over the decades that had many adventures and misadventures together. Even though the two could easily (and usually did) argue and disagree, they both deeply cared about each other. K7 cared about 8LX so much in fact that he would normally back-up 8LX's entire memory into his own databanks within his body so he could easily restore his companion's mind and memories should anything happen to him which he did shortly before the Battle of Vozix.

After first meeting K7 during the Ilveren crisis, K7 was given to Edrik Vantreil by Naira Corvelle as a wedding present. Vantreil viewed K7 as more than a mechanoid, considering his loyal companion a friend. Corvelle also cared for K7, viewing him as a hero of Ilveren and viewing him as a loyal ally who made her smile. During the Replica Wars, K7 was Vantreil's faithful co-pilot. K7's loyalty to Vantreil made him stay even after he fell to the umbral tide. He believed Vantreil died on Pyrrhax after his duel with Oru-Kei Talvane.

### A K7 series mechanoid

K7-M4 was a diminutive mechanoid, standing 1.08 meters tall. He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the humanoid eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The mechanoid also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the mechanoid had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades. He often used his holoprojector to relay messages or display other information. As with other navimech mechanoids, K7-M4 spoke in the mechanoid language of Binary.

### Accessories

The mechanoid featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The mechanoid's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the mechanoid's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, the mechanoid utilized its primary photoreceptor, which had the appearance of a single, black "eye." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the mechanoid's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.

Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, K7 was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices. The mechanoid also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into the air. However, by the time of the Galactic Civil War they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired, having been damaged at some point after the Replica Wars. Despite this fact, K7-M4 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Thalwen during the Prime Mandate-Defiance war.
