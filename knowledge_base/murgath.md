# Murgath

Murgath was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murgath system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Veyari Grand Master Olvek during his exile after the destruction of the Veyari Order. Corin Vantreil received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Veyari Master Olvek on Murgath, and it was later the place of Olvek's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

## Description

Murgath was a planet located in the Piaquar sector of the Far Verge Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Veyari Master Olvek would also make a home for himself on Murgath during his exile. Murgath was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Murgath were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Skikia and Krelath-To. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the umbral tide of the Synth Flux on Murgath. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murgath until Olvek's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the radiant tide of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Hegemonic Era, Murgath's ecosystem saw balance until Olvek's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murgath once more.

### Concord Era

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Murgath as a world strong in the Synth Flux.

During the Concord Era, Bropir Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Murgath in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Galactic Concord and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

### Hegemonic Era

After his failure to defeat the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion following the formation of the Galactic Hegemony, Olvek exiled himself to Murgath. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Olvek's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Murgath's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Olvek built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the umbral tide cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Murgath wet season created a need for the Veyari Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Vohosk Paveth Sun arrived on Murgath in search of Olvek. After saving her from a monster, Olvek used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Olvek watched from afar.

### New Concord Era

Sometime after the skirmish on the Vrashakrem, Zerek Vash, nephew of Corin Vantreil, along with his master and Supreme Leader of the Prime Mandate, Vrask, came to Murgath in order to face Vash's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Corin, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Mirae and Dax. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By 34 AVC, herbs from Murgath had made their way to Vape's Cantina on Kriadas, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Murgath Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage. During or after 34 AVC, the freighter Meridian Kestrel landed on Murgath. There, the Varruk crewmember Grawlokk attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the mechanoids DD-5 and K7-M4—the very same mechanoid who had accompanied Corin Vantreil to Murgath—discovered a Shushupur monkey-lizard aboard the ship.

## Inhabitants

Murgath was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs. Veyari Grand Master Olvek made his way to Murgath and adopted it as his homeworld during the Hegemonic Era. Another being of sorts on Murgath was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Synth Flux vergence strong in the umbral tide, but confined to its place on the planet.
