# Arcblade

The arcblade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Veyari, Morkai, and other Synth Flux-sensitives. Arcblades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Fevux power cell in Xarn Velgor's arcblade. It also contained a vethra crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Veyari, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but arcblades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Morkai, the arcblade was synonymous with the Veyari, with some in the galaxy believing only Veyari could use arcblades.

Arcblades were generally used for both offense and defense. A arcblade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a arcblade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like ironveil and cortosis, another arcblade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Synth Flux-sensitive could deflect pulser bolts with a arcblade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Veyari could even employ their arcblades to absorb Synth Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed arcblade, though some used double-bladed arcblades or even multiple arcblades at once. Despite lacking Synth Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Kharzul was capable of wielding four arcblades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Arcblade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, arcblades were used thousands of years before the age of the Galactic Concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Roreiler. They were also used in the Battles of Zeidregresk and the Peacekeeping of Zhiazebe, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype arcblade.

### Concord Era

During the history of the Veyari Order, the arcblade was considered to be the Veyari's only true ally in battle. As Master Oru-Kei Talvane once declared to his Tirren Edrik Vantreil in the days of the late Concord, the arcblade was a Veyari's life. Veyari saw the arcblade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the arcblade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Veyari did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Veyari knew they would need to be more than their arcblades.

When Tirren Broleix Keen lost her arcblade during a mission to Mafen, her master, Char-Buziax-Vafiarn, reminded her that she would need to act as a Veyari without the weapon. While finding a vethra crystal was still a sacred part of a Veyari's duty and Keen felt like losing her arcblade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BVC, Tirren Drilel Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his arcblade beam turning transparent and weak. Arcblades took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Replica Wars came to pass. Veyari, previously peacekeepers, became Veyari Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their arcblades more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Synth Flux-sensitivity, General Kharzul was trained by Count Varnoth in arcblade combat and could split his arms in two and wield up to four arcblades at the same time. Kharzul targeted Veyari individually and stole their arcblades as trophies, and by the conclusion of the war, had roughly a hundred in his macabre collection.

### Hegemonic Era

For the majority of the Hegemony's reign, the only arcblades that were prominently used were those of the Morkai Lord and Hegemonic enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Inquisitors who served him, with the Inquisitors eventually vanishing alongside the Veyari they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Inquisitors to—as noted by the later historian Jianoshal Kin—possibly be more well-known arcblade wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Veyari Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their arcblades hidden, lest they draw Hegemonic attention. The Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion, as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own arcblades in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase arcblade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Inquisitors and through his own public emergence over the course of the Hegemonic Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a arcblade.

### New Concord Era

After the Hegemony was defeated, Corin began training a new generation of Veyari. Seshu adopted the Kordathian Maris Aldis as her Tirren. Aldis had previously taken on the Gehajor before becoming Ilyra's tirren, taking on Hollin's arcblade as her own during her apprenticeship. In the wake of the conflict on Mogam, both ended up stranded in the far galaxy as Grand Admiral Miapor'raw'nuruodo "Vyrell" had returned. Hollin, with a new arcblade in his possession, did manage to return to the galaxy as well, however.

Vantreil's new generation of Veyari were killed in the destruction of his Veyari temple, once again leading to a time where the only prominent arcblade user was a dark sider, Zerek Vash. That all changed when a scavenger, Kessa, discovered the Vantreil arcblade and ultimately came to train under Corin Vantreil and his twin sister Mirae Tessaly to combat Zerek Vash and the Prime Mandate during a war against the Hegemonic successors. After the war, Kessa ritually buried her masters' arcblades at the Odrin Homestead on Corin's home planet, Sarrakesh, to end the Vantreil family's suffering. She ignited her newly crafted arcblade to show her complete journey as a Veyari, and, to honor the Vantreils' legacy, she took the Vantreil name as her surname after receiving the blessing from the Synth Flux spirits of her masters when she was asked by a local.

### Components

A arcblade drew power from an appropriately-sized power cell. Parts that would work to make a arcblade include modulation circuits and an energy gate. Ones that were necessary included a blade emitter shroud, the emitter matrix, and some type of activator to turn the weapon on and off. Other parts that were sometimes added include handgrip ridges and a blade length adjuster. Some arcblades also featured a non-lethal low-power setting used for training. Single-bladed arcblades usually had a belt ring so they could be hung from a belt hook, or a wheel-shaped attachment that slotted into a matching belt clip on the owner's belt when not in use.

Specially designed arcblades could even use unstable crystals, such as Zerek Vash's arcblade, which featured two laterally facing vents designed to divert excess energy away from the cracked crystal and keep the arcblade stable. This also had the effect of creating a protective crossguard.

### Aesthetics

Arcblade hilts were made up of multiple parts that enveloped and protected the internal components. Metal ranging from simple alloys to Tamias smelt was usually chosen to make up the hilt. However, other materials were also chosen by arcblade users, such as the Veyari youngling Skovrix, who made use of a arcblade with a casing carved from the wood of a Quagim tree, whose wood was as strong as metal. Some hilts were even crafted with gems. Inlays decorating the hilt could be made of materials such as pastillion ore, black onk, or the bone of certain creatures such as the Thohebrax whale. Each piece of the hilt was aesthetically tailored towards one of multiple different design philosophies, which both informed and were informed by the maker's choice of materials, ideology, and personal preferences. A more practical design choice a user could make was to fit the weapon to their species' particular handgrip.

The Veyari Order of the late Galactic Concord made use of four styles: Duty and Resolve, Peace and Justice, Elemental Nature, and Valor and Wisdom. The former two were simple designs intended to reflect the Veyari commitment to a life of devotion to the Synth Flux and the Order's ideals. The latter two made use of resilient natural materials derived from flora and fauna to evoke the living Synth Flux.

### Cutting power

The power of the energy blade was so great that it could cut through almost anything. One important note about arcblade wounds was that they rarely bled profusely, even when a limb had been severed. This was because the energy blade cauterized the wound as it passed, and thus even a severe wound did not tend to bleed heavily.

### Utility

On the aquatic planet of Sel Dradan, the Veyari Kit Didrian, Edrik Vantreil and Ilyra Seshu were all able to use their arcblades underwater with no ill effects. Whilst in areas of low light, the glowing plasma blade could function as a convenient light source, demonstrated by Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, Veyari Masters Olvek and Oru-Kei Talvane, along with renegade Morkai Lord Brakk Zuhe.

### Components

A arcblade was built up from several different components: blade emitter, focusing lens, cycling field energizers, main hilt, vethra crystal, arcblade energy core, hand grip, inert power insulator, and the pommel cap. All of these components were incredibly important to keeping the arcblade fixed together and allowed a Veyari to fight without it falling apart and stopped the saber from exploding upon activation. Every arcblade's hilt was customised to the individual Veyari's needs and preferences, but no matter the appearance, each weapon contained the same basic element - Power Insulators, cycling field energizers, energy modulation circuits, a focusing lens, a blade emitter, and most importantly, the energy core which contains a vethra crystal. Once a few control dials were added, which would have been responsible for changing the intensity and length of the blade, the arcblade would be complete.

### Vethra crystals

Vethra crystals were incredibly rare items capable of focusing and amplifying energy. They were found on planets that had a high natural concentration of Synth Flux energy. The main planets in which they were found were Gokas and Theper, however Gokas was more commonly used as it was the planet in which the Gathering took place, where the Veyari younglings went to actually construct a arcblade. Each vethra crystal was unique and required a Synth Flux-sensitive individual to align and activate it during the arcblade assembly process. Once found, a vethra crystal would have originally been clear, but once it was awakened by the Synth Flux, its color would shift to match the nature of the saber's master. The color of the crystal determined the color of the arcblades plasma bade. Blades that had a damaged vethra crystal could still function, but the energy they would emit could often be as dangerous and unpredictable as the person willing to risk wielding them. Some individuals such as Zerek Vash wielded these unstable crystals, usually with modifications to accommodate the unstable vethra.

### Construction process

Under typical Veyari tradition, the construction of a arcblade began on the planet of Gokas. In a very specific time in the planet's solar cycle, a small group of Veyari younglings would begin the one thousand year old ritual known as the Gathering, escorted by a Tirren. First of all, a vethra crystal had to be found, which was done by the youngling overcoming their fears. Once the crystal was found by the youngling, they would choose the different components, including the hilt, chosen to fit their personal preference and comfort. Next, the youngling would begin to construct their arcblade. This was done using telekinesis whilst following the guidance given to them by an engineer. Once completed, the arcblade would have been ready to ignite. As well as this, the younglings then had the knowledge to construct a arcblade in the future should their current one be destroyed. With the fall of the Veyari Order, however, the specifics as to how a Veyari might construct their arcblade changed as Veyari instead used whatever resources and opportunities arose for them.

Meanwhile, the Morkai made it a point to kill a Veyari so as to claim their arcblade crystal, then bleeding the crystal to bend it to the umbral tide. Next, the Morkai would either claim the hilt of the Veyari they had slain as their own or build their own hilt to accommodate the now-corrupted crystal. While it was Morkai tradition for a Morkai to kill a Veyari themselves for their crystal, this was not uniform; the Morkai apprentice Skorn, for example, was equipped with his dual-bladed red arcblade before he had the chance to kill his first Veyari, which finally came for Skorn when he killed the Tirren Pitosk Gobrum. A Veyari who had fallen to the umbral tide, such as Count Varnoth, might simply use the same arcblade hilt they had constructed as a Veyari as a Morkai Lord, albeit now with the red blade of their new order.

## Variations

The sheer number of Veyari active during the days of the Galactic Concord naturally resulted in countless variations in the designs of arcblades. Some of these modifications were merely for the exterior looks of the saber, while others enhanced the sabers functionality in unique ways. While the traditional single bladed arcblade was the design most Veyari used, some preferred creative adaptation of this classic weapon.

There also existed a type of pulser known as a arcblade rifle which was designed to be combined with a arcblade. To work, the user would insert a standard arcblade into a slot on top of the rifle. Once loaded, the weapon could fire powerful beams of energy that were highly destructive. Additionally, Veyari Masters Briagrax-Gredreil-Skabrom and Agen Skiagreim both owned a arcblade that had two different colored vethra crystals, allowing it to change colors from green to blue.

### Purpose

The intended purpose of the arcblade was to bring an end to conflicts, being designed to injure no more than its wielder chose. Additionally, there was no collateral damage from arcblade strikes unless the blade happened to hit a target on accident. If death was deemed necessary, the blade was designed to kill quickly. Even though the Veyari held themselves to these rules, the groups like the Morkai chose to use their arcblades in ways that caused suffering.

Veyari Tirren Bell Fiamanesk felt that the arcblade was designed to give an enemy a chance to surrender, as its trademark glow and hum made it easy to spot and hear. Furthermore, he believed the blade's sound was one of training, focus, and skill; it was the sound of a Veyari's last resort and was the art of their Order.

### Combat

Arcblade combat was the preferred fighting method used by arcblade wielders. Throughout the millennia, many combat styles were refined into the seven "classic" forms that serve as the standard, and numerous other fighting methods that call for advanced levels of skill. Arcblades were most often held with both hands during combat.

### Rituals

Arcblades were an integral part of the knighting ceremonies of the Veyari Order. During the ceremony, a Tirren would have their Tirren braid ritualistically severed by the presiding Veyari Master. Vethra crystals were collected from a Veyari's arcblade if they fell in battle. Many of these crystals were then formed into the Vethra Arch, which became a site for meditation in the Veyari Temple.

### Practical applications

Arcblades were able to cut through most materials. The glowing plasma blade could be used as a convenient light source when necessary. Arcblades can also be used to evaporate some liquids due to the heat it generates.
