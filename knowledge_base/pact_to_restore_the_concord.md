# Pact to Restore the Concord

The Pact to Restore the Concord (2 BVC–4 AVC), commonly known as the Insurgent Pact, Pact, and the Insurgency, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Galactic Hegemony and to restore the Galactic Concord and liberate the galaxy. The Pact was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Insurgent Pact by Senator Sel Aldren, which brought numerous insurgent cells together as a common force opposed to the Hegemony during the Galactic Civil War.

Founded and united by Aldren, Dorn Tessaly and Ilyra Seshu, the early Pact were assisted by Replica Wars veterans, such as Captain Tarn or Dorrak Dreihix as well as operatives, including Teodric Kriasi and Lovi Zhunal. The Pact was secretly overseen by Aldren and Dorn Ragreim Tessaly, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Jan Gramos, Ryder Tretrern, and Tobin Thoneskias. Indeed, the concept of a "Insurgent Pact" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Aldren. Such early groups and the eventual Pact came together from a number of unrelated insurgent cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Riapem Defiance Group was one of the first cells to join what Aldren began calling "the Pact" before the formal formation of the Pact to Restore the Concord.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Pact established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Skuquem Jogros and Stray One stole the plans for the Hegemony's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Orvax with the help of Corin Vantreil, the tide of the war began to turn, and the insurgent threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Hegemony's side. Vantreil, Mirae Tessaly, Dax Varro, Grawlokk, K7-M4 and T-8LX would have a significant role in the Pact efforts against the Hegemony following Orvax. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Hegemonic forces succeeded in defeating the insurgents on Kryost, forcing the Pact to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Pact survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Hegemony by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Thalwen. Not only did the Pact manage to destroy the battle station, but the Hegemonic Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Nova Dreadnoughts, and Vantreil redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Hegemon Deito Malverin, before dying himself in the battle.

The Pact to Restore the Concord was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Galactic Concord. The newly-established galactic government continued the Pact efforts against the fragmented Hegemony, lasting one year after Thalwen until the Battle of Leinesk, when the Galactic Hegemony finally fell.

### Early foundations

While the Pact to Restore the Concord was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Galactic Hegemony and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Hegemony, when the Galactic Concord was in its last years of existence. During the Replica Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the League of Independent Systems, the Veyari Order helped create resistance units on Secessionist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, one example being the Bretheis insurgents. However, it was not for many more years that such insurgent cells would coalesce into a single Insurgency with a centralized leadership.

Also during the Replica Wars, Supreme Chancellor Deito Malverin had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Galactic Senate, which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Loyalist Committee, including Dorn Ragreim Tessaly of Belisar, Naira Corvelle of Ilveren, and Sel Aldren of Breidenoth, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Malverin's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator Devrei Tills of Sel Dradan was among the petitioners.

### Rise of the Insurgency

The Insurgent Pact formally came into existence after Aldren resigned from the Hegemonic Senate and denounced Hegemon Malverin for his role in the Zegreis Massacre. With the help of Phoenix Leader Briron Bafogra and her Spectres, Aldren escaped Vyrell's forces by traveling through the dangerous Brafi pass to reach Trefovas, the site of a temporary base that was increasingly built up as the Pact grew. While Pact had already established a base on the moon Orvax 4 that Aldren and Dorn Tessaly intended to become Pact headquarters, they recognized it was not yet ready as of 2 BVC. Nonetheless, the makings of the Pact Army could be found at Orvax. Over Trefovas, Sel Aldren gave a Nitiarn speech calling for the galaxy to rise up in insurgency against the Hegemony. Aldren's message was heard by the insurgent cells throughout the galaxy including Heifu and Grobe. The Insurgent Fleet converged above Trefovas, and the cells were unified into the Pact to Restore the Concord, which officially began the Galactic Civil War.

Around the same time as the Pact's formal formation, Garven Dekriask and Dreivern Quovrei joined the insurgency. As the Pact grew in publicity and around that same time, a grouping of Sel Bunimo cruisers joined the Pact fleet. While Aldren admitted in Short Notes on the History of the Insurgent Pact Navy that the Pact perhaps did not deserve the Sel Bunimo city-ships that joined its ranks, as it failed to carry out significant action against the occupation of Sel Dradan, she believed the Pact's ability to secure these ships and the boon to the fleet they represented was proof of how important it was to win the hearts of the galaxy.

### Post-Orvax advancements

In the shadow of Orvax, the Pact had won its first major tactical victory, but at an enormous cost in lives. In addition to the Pact pilots who died during the assault, at least millions of Belisarians had perished with their planet, Dorn Tessaly among them. After an award ceremony during which Princess Mirae presented the heroes of the day with medals of Bravery, General Gramos made it his priority to have Orvax 4 evacuated. Now that the Hegemony knew their location, they had to find a new base of operations as soon as possible. Xarn Velgor became obsessed with finding the young insurgent who destroyed the Void Core and killed Governor Helvar and most of the Hegemony’s best forces right under his nose. Unbeknownst to him, until learning Corin's last name through Teko Drayk, and the insurgents at the time, he was in fact the biological father of both Corin and Mirae.

Not long after the Battle of Orvax, the Insurgent Fleet relocated to the Teishas system. The Insurgency also spread footage of their victory over the Void Core via the Thiavri, and the destruction of the battle station was a boon to the insurgency's efforts: with such a major victory secured, the battle proved that victory over the Hegemony was actually possible. Shortly after Orvax, the Hegemony targeted and destroyed the Pact's fuel refinery within the Queiza Belt, only for the insurgents to respond by capturing the Hegemonic fuel tanker Black Ice. The Black Ice was lost in a collision at some point later in the war. The reinforcement of Trutrex was a harrowing event experienced by insurgent personnel at one point.

### Rebounding from Kryost

In 3 AVC, one of the many Hegemonic probe mechanoids searching the galaxy ended up discovering the Insurgents' presence. Death Squadron, Xarn Velgor's personal armada, headed for Kryost with a contingent of ground troops, ready to crush the insurgency once and for all. However, due to a mistake by Admiral Rufiarn Vigriarn, the Pact forces were warned of the Hegemony's arrival. The Insurgents had time to activate the deflector shield protecting Echo Base, rendering orbital bombardment impossible. As the Pact prepared for evacuation, Hegemonic troops disembarked on the surface of Kryost, beyond the range of the energy shields. Once there, several legions of snowtroopers advanced on Echo Base. A squadron of All Terrain Armored Transports under the command of General Pequiaskath Drask was tasked with destroying the massive generators powering the Insurgent base's shield. Perimeter Outpost Delta fell first with Sergeant Drakrar Vovu, Zhedron, and Beak being forced to retreat back to the main base to find Captain Shator Evon and Dreisask Quedo.

Corin, who was promoted to Commander by Aldren, led Rogue Squadron of snowspeeders against General Drask' armored transports. By using a harpoon gun and tow-cable, the Insurgents managed to bring down one of the transports, but Vantreil's snowspeeder was shot down. Unlike his gunner, Dak Trilath, the Rogue commander survived the accident. With the help of his arcblade and a grenade, he still managed to destroy another walker. Despite the Insurgents' best efforts to delay Drask' armored transports, the Hegemonic general successfully blew up the protective shield generator, allowing Xarn Velgor and his men to enter Echo Base. While the Insurgent command personnel and material was loaded into transports and whisked away just in time, the unprepared Pact soldiers engaged the Hegemonics in conventional warfare. Vovu and his two men got back to the main base to find Quedo with and Captain Evon, who was unconscious. They then encountered Xarn Velgor who killed Captain Evon, Zhedron, and Beak. Vovu and Quedo escaped on a shuttle with Quedo' clearance codes.

### Founding a New Concord

In the days following the Battle of Thalwen, news of the Hegemon's death spread to several worlds across the galaxy including Sarrakesh, Ilveren, Velmora, and Velaris despite many attempts by the Hegemony to conceal the fact and declare this information to be false. While there was much celebration, a riot broke out at Velaris's Monument Plaza. With the Hegemony in turmoil, the Insurgent Pact launched an assault on an Hegemonic outpost on the far side of Thalwen. This attack was led by General Varro and uncovered a lot of data and communications on Hegemonic plans.

The Insurgent Pact was eventually reorganized into the New Concord. The new government, however, still had to contend with Hegemonic holdouts. But luckily, the Hegemon's contingency plan had the Hegemony thrown into chaos with Operation: Cinder turning them on their loyal planets.

### Legacy

One year after the destruction of the second Void Core, Concord and Hegemonic forces fought a major battle on the desert world of Leinesk, which saw a Concord victory and the end of the Galactic Civil War. The Pact also dispatched shock troopers to eliminate former Hegemonics that became warlords. The shock troopers were later assigned peacekeeping roles after the formation of the New Concord.

The symbol of the Pact to Restore the Concord was used in the insignia of the New Concord and later became the emblem of the Defiance, a military force founded by Insurgent veteran Mirae and made up of several Pact officers, such as Sihen and Shabiath Jakox, to combat the Prime Mandate, the successor of the Hegemony. During the Dorn Tessaly statue-dedication ceremony in 28 AVC, speeches were delivered by Senators Tai-Lin Zeikrorn and Tessaly that reminded the gathered audience of the sacrifices made by the Insurgent Pact, which were further reinforced by an air show performed by Concord K-lances.

### Government

The Pact to Restore the Concord was a military resistance movement, which was headed by the Pact High Command and dedicated to upholding the ideals of the Old Concord, but also contained the Pact Civil Government. The Pact Cabinet served as political High Command, existing separate from the Pact Military High Command, and represented both civilian and military interests. It was led by a Chief of State and had six cabinet posts: Minister of Education, Minister of Finance, Minister of Industry, Minister of State, Minister of Supply, and Minister of War.

The Pact frequently changed its base of operations, ranging from numerous worlds across the galaxy in order to avoid detection or regroup from an attack by its nemesis, the massive Galactic Hegemony. A diplomatic corps who sought to maintain friendly relations and procure support from various worlds in the galaxy was maintained. Despite this, the Pact had few worlds openly declare their support for the movement, as those few that did found their worlds cordoned behind Hegemonic blockades.

### Military

Prior to the Pact's official founding, the various insurgent cells operated their own military forces. As the cells began coordinating, joint military endeavors were often overseen by cell leaders such as Troku Thihin of Phoenix Group, Cham Bafogra of the Free Nejes movement, and Ryder Tretrern of the Heifu resistance. Insurgent cells received information from "Fulcrum" intelligence operatives

The formal Pact Military was served by capable military leaders such as Admiral Shevrask Sihen, and received intelligence updates through its own intelligence branch known as Insurgent Pact Intelligence Service. It could also receive information through the Ziaskix spynet, and maintained the Pact Fleet and Pact Army, along with the Insurgent Pact Starfighter Corps. Insurgent troopers across the galaxy fought for an end to galactic Hegemonic rule in units, such as the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry and Thirty-Second Infantry, while the Pact Special Forces was composed of elite commandos of the trooper ranks. Owing to the larger size of the Hegemonic war machine, the Pact relied on guerrilla warfare, hit-and-run tactics, and surgical strikes instead of outright engaging in a more typical war for territory.
