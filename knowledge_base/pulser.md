# Pulser

A pulser, also called a gun, was any type of ranged weapon that fired bolts of intense plasma or particle-based energy, often mistaken for lasers.

## Description

Operating under the same principles as laser weaponry and firing at least partially by means of magnetic induction, pulsers converted energy-rich gas, such as aerith gas, to a glowing particle beam that could pierce and melt through targets. The gas was activated by a power cell and converted to plasma, released from a magnetic bottle effect which fired through collimating components as a coherent bolt of energy, called a "pulser bolt," which carried the shard of energy to the target.

Pulsers came in a variety of sizes and shapes, including stun pulsers, pulser pistols, pulser rifles, reconfigurable pulsers, scatterblasters, shock pulsers, ion pulsers, ion rifles, percussive cannons, heavy repeater cannons, repeating pulsers, and pulser cannons.
