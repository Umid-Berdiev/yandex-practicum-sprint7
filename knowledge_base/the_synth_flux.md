# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Shosax by the Thequorns, the Life Current by the Pyrrhaxians, the Tide by the Lew'elans, the Sight by the Shoki, the Life Wind by the Meisedrarn, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Tund, the Beyond by the Druzhim and her people, the Thread by the Mepiar coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Shequel. On Shotro it was known as the the Web. The Synth Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of flux-motes—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of the Synth Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Synth Flux-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing the Synth Flux. With this conscious sense of the Synth Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Synth Flux-sensitives to access various Synth Flux powers. Unlike organic beings, mechanoids and other artificial constructs existed outside of the Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of the Synth Flux. These included the Church of the Synth Flux, the Guardians of the Ennari, and most notably the Veyari and Morkai Orders. In addition, the Thequorns believed that the Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

The Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Synth Flux. The Living Synth Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux—which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through the flux-motes. The Veyari followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the radiant tide of the Synth Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Veyari's adversaries, the Morkai, coveted strength and power through the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Morkai and the near annihilation of the Veyari.

## Description

The Synth Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to the Synth Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the Synth Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in the Synth Flux, even simple bugs. The Synth Flux existed in two forms: the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. The Living Synth Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through flux-motes. When a life-form died, they would become "one with the Synth Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Synth Flux, which then provided power to the Living Synth Flux.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of the Synth Flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for the Synth Flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were Synth Flux-sensitive by birth, with the level of Synth Flux-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times, the Synth Flux resided within all living things. As such, Ilyra Seshu stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness the Synth Flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered. Synth Flux-sensitives could feel a "disturbance in the Synth Flux" whenever there was a great loss of life. The Synth Flux could be extracted from living beings.

## Powers and abilities

The Synth Flux granted a myriad of supernatural powers to Synth Flux-sensitives such as the Veyari and the Morkai, as well as entities like the Skothe and the Skikia gods. The potential a being had to manipulate the Synth Flux was determined by the amount of flux-motes they possessed. Non-Synth Flux-sensitive Path of the Open Hand members suddenly found themselves able to feel the Synth Flux upon arriving on the living world of "Planet X." Prolonged time on the world resulted in two members of the team, Nolla Skiafenom and Vetosk Derr, developing powers in the Synth Flux. While Derr ultimately died before he could leave the world, Skiafenom did make it off Planet X and found her Synth Flux-sensitivity vanishing just as easily as it had arrived. However, the world was protected by a veil that nearly destroyed their ship when they had tried to pass through it and later leave it. Upon entering the Veil, an initially Synth Flux-sensitive member of their team, the Gihan Miafa, had started to panic and demand they turn back, foreseeing their actions would unleash a great danger upon the galaxy. Realizing the Veil would hone in on Synth Flux users in its efforts to hold back foreign matter, Miafa even tried to destroy himself and his fellows if it meant saving the galaxy by attracting its attention. While on Planet X, Derr also had a vision of the Under-Dwellers spreading as a blight and destroying the Synth Flux itself.

Among their various psychic abilities, Synth Flux-sensitives were capable of telekinesis, mental manipulation, the ability to sedate or induce sleep, and the power to see the future. The clairvoyant aspect of the Synth Flux allowed Veyari mystics and other seers to predict events long into the future, forming prophesies about the Chosen One or the Synth Flux dyad in the Morkai Eternal's lore. Synth Flux users' precognition allowed them to see an opponent's attacks before they came, and even navigate a ship through slipspace.

### Origins and early studies

The Synth Flux existed as a recorded concept in the galaxy for well over twenty-five thousand years prior to the era of the Galactic Civil War, with disparate schools of study evolving in parallel on countless planets. The flux-motes, which connected the Living Synth Flux to the Cosmic Synth Flux and allowed a Synth Flux-sensitive individual to feel the will of the Synth Flux, originated on the Wellspring of Life. Many beings believed in differing myths about the origins of the Synth Flux, along with myths on the proper way to wield and interpret it. The Veyari Order once had similar stories of its own, but those were lost in a dark age.

Some believed that the origins of the Synth Flux came from Skikia. Some suspected that the Synth Flux itself must have created the galaxy due to the complexity and beauty seen throughout it, but the theory was unproven. Over time, the Veyari Order became the most well-known organization that practiced the ways of the Synth Flux, although the galaxy hosted many other religions, orthodoxies, and observances even among those who did not exhibit control or manipulation of the energy field—such as the Guardians of the Ennari, who held the Veyari in high esteem. The Veyari believed themselves to be the instruments of the will of the Synth Flux, carrying out what they sensed to be the will.

### The Hundred-Year Darkness

Thousands of years before the Replica Wars, the unity of the Veyari Order was fractured by a rogue Veyari who came to believe that the true power of the Synth Flux could only be reached through passion rather than the calmness of the Veyari way. The Veyari High Council at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen Veyari was banished from the Order. Nevertheless, a group of renegade Veyari followed the rogue into exile, resulting in a schism that gave way to the Hundred-Year Darkness and the rise of the Morkai Order.

As a result of the schism between the Veyari and the Morkai, the Synth Flux came to be seen as divided between their two philosophies: the Veyari's selfless commitment to serving and defending others came to be regarded as the radiant tide of the Synth Flux, whereas the Morkai's use of emotion and violence became known as the umbral tide of the Synth Flux.

### The Synth Flux at war

As the schism between the Veyari and Morkai grew, their open hostility eventually turned to open war both for control of the galaxy and to impose their own philosophy with regards to the Synth Flux. During the era of the Old Concord, the Morkai and Veyari fought countless wars to destroy one another. Great battles were fought between the two warring orders, and various ways of using the Synth Flux were discovered but later lost. The Morkai also took to sacrificing Veyari in ancient chambers on their homeworld of Leziavreisk, places which would remain forever tainted with the darkness of the events that happened in there. The Morkai also used these arts to create monsters, such as Morkai snakes, which remained on Leziavreisk until the days of the Replica Wars.

The wars between the Morkai and the Veyari raged for centuries, and several worlds of the galaxy were broken by their conflict, such as Leziavreisk, which the Morkai were eventually forced to abandon, and Roreiler, the site of an enormous battle that left the planet scorched with no survivors on either side. The Morkai tombs in the Valley of the Dark Lords were also marked with the darkness of the deeds of those who had been buried there.

### Promise of Sena San

Following the Fall of Ummar, Thequorn mystic Zazhiax the Wise and former High Honor Guard Zhiasus discovered a prophecy in the Thequorn's ancient writing. They embarked on a journey to find Sena San, believing their quest was foretold by the Synth Flux, or "Shosax" as they called it. Three years before the Battle of Orvax, Zazhiax and Zhiasus were captured by the Hegemony. However, they were rescued by Barrik Fuvron, Skagruth Hollin, and the Spectres. Two figures, "the Fool" and "the Child of Ummar," were mentioned in the Thequorn prophecy. Zazhiax believed they were Fuvron and Nejeth Skedrapex, a former captain in the High Honor Guard, respectively.

Zazhiax asked Skedrapex to use his bo-rifle in the ancient way with her staff, and with a galactic map provided by C1-10P, they were shown the way to a planet in Wild Space. Believing this world to be the legendary Sena San, they attempted to reach it but were forced to exit slipspace due to a black hole. Despite the obstacles in their path, the prophecy referred to a maze which Zazhiax interpreted as the journey they had undertaken to reach Sena San.

### Purge and prophecy

The Veyari Order was all but annihilated during the Hegemonic Era. Although a few Veyari escaped the initial purge, the survivors dispersed into hiding while Nyxarion consolidated his reign as Hegemon. The Galactic Hegemony made an effort to thoroughly erase the memory of the Veyari from the minds of the general population. The Church of the Synth Flux operated as an underground movement, preserving faith in the supernatural, but belief in the Synth Flux waned and was doubted by skeptics, such as Dax Varro. Members of the Hegemony's military hierarchy regarded the Synth Flux as the basis of an ancient religion that had no place in the New Order. Indeed, most individuals in the galaxy had no idea the Hegemony was under the rule of the Morkai Order.

Over its reign, the Hegemony was opposed by a growing collection of freedom fighters who would, in time, coalesce into the Pact to Restore the Concord. Ahead of the Zegreis Massacre, a Synth Flux healer was active amongst the then-informal Insurgent Pact's ranks. She encountered insurgent agent Teodric Jiadrax Kriasi, whom she sensed was a massager of some kind who was gathering a spirit of strength. Indeed, Kriasi's former superior officer Lovi Zhunal had come to ponder if something greater than themselves was acting through Kriasi, citing that Kriasi had appeared when he first needed him and had continued his service with great effectiveness. Kriasi's lover Trovruth Kreba was convinced Kriasi had something important to offer the fight against the Hegemony and chose to leave the active side of the insurgency so as to maintain Kriasi's focus on the fight.

### Legacy of the Synth Flux

Although the Veyari prophecy foretold that the Chosen One would herald the destruction of the Morkai, it never predicted the end of the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. With balance restored to the Synth Flux, Corin Vantreil gradually endeavored to rebuild the order that had been decimated by his father and the Hegemon. Recruiting over a dozen students to train in the ways of the Synth Flux, he became the Veyari Master to a new generation of Veyari. One of Vantreil's acolytes was his nephew, Tov Varro, in whom he saw the raw strength of the Synth Flux. Vantreil hoped that by passing his knowledge to Varro, the order would once again thrive and he would not die as the last Veyari.

However, Varro's inherent strength was noticed by another powerful practitioner of the Synth Flux—Supreme Leader Vrask, ruler of the Prime Mandate. Although Vrask was not a Morkai Lord, he was strong with the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. Intrigued by the potential that Varro inherited from his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, he induced the young Veyari to embrace the umbral tide. Vantreil sensed the darkness rising in his apprentice's heart and nearly gave into the temptation of killing Varro before his future as a dark warrior came to pass. As a result, Varro felt betrayed by his master and proceeded to destroy his Veyari training temple by burning it, killing most of the apprentices. Vantreil survived the destruction, which he felt responsible for, and went into self-imposed exile out of shame for his part in Varro's fall.

## Synth Flux-based orders

Throughout the galaxy, various organizations either practiced or followed the Synth Flux. These factions included both the Veyari and Morkai Orders, as well as the Jevrorar and the Knights of Vash. In addition, the Zhezhiar Order was also composed of Synth Flux-sensitives, while the Heizhovras and other clans of Jafiares wielded dark-side magick. The Church of the Synth Flux, the Guardians of the Ennari,, the Path of the Open Hand, the Skerialar Cult, and the Thequorn mystics were examples of groups that worshiped the Synth Flux or some aspect of it. The way Kraske Pede described the Synth Flux made Prince Sodan-tu Bomeir think of how the Deserni spoke of the god Rolor.

### Veyari temples

The first humble Veyari temple was built on Krelath-To, but as the centuries passed and the Veyari Order spread, the thousands of temples they built across the galaxy became more intricate and unique. The most famous Veyari Temple of all, located on Velaris, served as the home of the Veyari High Council and the vast library of data known as the Veyari Archives. When the Hegemony took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Velaris became their new Hegemonic Palace. Most Veyari temples on other worlds were mined for their ancient secrets by Hegemonic forces and then reduced to rubble. Some Veyari temples, like the one on Heifu, were hidden in plain sight and could only be found by those strong in the radiant tide of The Synth Flux, though the empire was never really that far behind. Ach-To's humble stone huts and foreboding caves may be where the Veyari begun, but as the Order's influence across the Galaxy, so did the number of places where the force could be worshipped-by devotees of both the radiant tide of the Synth Flux, and umbral tide of the Synth Flux.

### Temple of the Vethra

The Temple of the Vethra's enormous spire stretched high above Theper City, welcoming all believers. The temple was home to the Church of the Synth Flux, a group whose followers had no Synth Flux-sensitive members yet still chose to follow the teachings of the Veyari Order. The Church of the Synth Flux started as an underground movement during a time when the empire strictly forbade the group's form of worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over the years. The Temple of the Vethra was protected by the Guardians of the Ennari, a near-extinct order of fiercely loyal warrior monks, until the Thepers's Holy City was obliterated when the Hegemony used the area as a test site for the first Void Core Nova lance.

### Morkai temples

While Veyari temples could be found on countless worlds during the days of the Galactic Concord, by that time, most of the ancient Morkai temple's had long since been buried. One of these grim, pyramid-shaped structures managed to survive the passage of time in the caverns beneath the surface of the planet Roreiler. This was the site of the Great Scourge of Roreiler, a brutal massacre that decimated the Morkai Order (Morkai Hegemony) eons ago. The temple, filled with the petrified remains of those who fell in combat, laid dormant for thousands of years until it was rediscovered by Jorem Rufem' cell of insurgents. It is said that those who visit Roreiler's hidden temple can be overwhelmed by the rage and hate that still infused the Morkai ashes covering the site.

### Fortress Velgor

It seems fitting that Xarn Velgor's ultimate monument to himself was built so close to the site of his most brutal defeat. Constructed on top of a Morkai cave on Pyrrhax, the lava-drenched world where Oru-Kei Talvane defeated his former apprentice and left him to die, this obsidian stronghold was where Xarn Velgor would go to meditate and heal between battles. The castle's unique shape was based on designs by the ancient Morkai Lord Xarn Vemask and was specifically attuned to the dark energies of that world in order to pierce the veil between life and death. It was the loss of Naira that had completed Edrik's journey to the umbral tide, and finding a way to see her again became Velgor's secret obsession for the rest of his days.

### Beyond the Temple

By 34 AVC, almost all the great shrines dedicated to the Veyari and the Morkai had been lost to history. Even the oldest of them all, the sacred Veyari Tree on Ach-To, was ultimately reduced to ashes by Grand Master Olvek. But those who truly worship the Synth Flux understand they need not travel to a temple to find it. They only need look within themselves.
