# Ilyra Seshu

Ilyra Seshu was a female Hajo who was a Synth Flux-sensitive outcast from the Veyari Order. After the Replica Wars, she helped establish a network of various insurgent cells against the Galactic Hegemony. Seshu was discovered on her homeworld of Zhurem by Veyari Master Skeigam Jethus, who brought her to the Veyari Temple on Velaris to receive Veyari training. Following the outbreak of the Replica Wars, Veyari Grand Master Olvek assigned the young Seshu to be the Tirren learner of Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, who nicknamed her "Snips" when she joined him at the Battle of Griahasian. Whereas Seshu was eager to prove herself, Vantreil had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Geiskar, the son of crime lord Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk, and returned Geiskar to his father, thus facilitating a crucial alliance between the Zogg Clan and the Galactic Concord.

As a commander in the Grand Army of the Concord, Seshu found a mentor in Replica Captain Tarn of the 501st Legion, with whom she and Vantreil collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the League of Independent Systems. In her first command position at the Battle of Nejes, Seshu disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots, but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Nejes. Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from General Kharzul and Morkai apprentice Mebril Hokrijoth to bounty hunters such as Zev Skar and Sati Lyth. Over time, she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key Concord victories like the Second Battle of Kharrab and the Battle of Sel Dradan. Seshu even died during a series of events on Skikia, but the Daughter, a Synth Flux wielder who personified the radiant tide, sacrificed herself to resurrect Seshu and bring her back to life.

Seshu gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Veyari younglings to revolt against their Skarrim captors, helping rescue a colony of Hajo from enslavement by the Bokrazhann Slave Hegemony, and serving as an advisor to the Bretheis insurgents—with her close friend, Davin Quiameim, among them—as they liberated their world from the League. In a stark turn of events, Seshu was framed for the bombing of the Veyari Temple hangar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Velaris Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely alliance with Mebril Hokrijoth, she was detained by Concord forces and was consequently barred from the Veyari Order. Ultimately, Vantreil uncovered the true culprit, Seshu's friend, and fellow Tirren Ziasin Lebisk, and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Seshu's faith in the Veyari. She refused the Veyari High Council's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Seshu briefly returned to lead Concord forces during the Siege of Kordath and faced Skorn and his allies. Directive 91 was enacted shortly after the Morkai Lord's capture and Seshu alongside Tarn, after freeing Tarn from the control of Directive 91, escaped the rest of her 501st division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Galactic Hegemony came to power, Seshu went into hiding on Thekrutorn and Vrekrex disguised as "Shosax." Following the evacuation of Vrekrex, Seshu joined Senator Dorn Tessaly's growing insurgent movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename "Fulcrum." As Fulcrum, Seshu provided intelligence to various insurgent factions including Briron Bafogra's Spectres insurgent cell. Seshu took a particular interest in Bafogra's insurgents because one of their members, a Veyari named Jorem Rufem, had begun to train his own Tirren, Skagruth Hollin. In addition, Seshu's efforts against the Hegemony brought her into contact with the Dark Lord of the Morkai Xarn Velgor—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Edrik Vantreil—as well as the Hegemonic Inquisitors known as the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Tarn. Following the mission to Roreiler, Seshu became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Morkai temple and was believed by many, including Rufem and Hollin, to have perished during a duel with Xarn Velgor.

However, Seshu was unexpectedly saved from Velgor's wrath by a future Skagruth Hollin, who pulled her through a portal into the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane of the Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Xarn Nyxarion, who desired access to the dimension, Seshu returned to her time, promising Hollin that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Galactic Civil War, Seshu joined Maris Aldis in her quest to find the missing Hollin, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Heifu, and trained Aldis in the ways of the Veyari until parting ways. Seshu later journeyed to the city of Givrurn on the forest planet Drororn looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Vyrell, who had disappeared alongside Hollin, from Magistrate Morgan Ridam. Needing to rescue the city from Ridam's rule, she allied herself with the Kordathian Kol Lodia, who had been on a quest to bring the Synth Flux-sensitive youngling Breipem to the Veyari. After freeing Givrurn, Seshu refused to train Breipem and directed Lodia to the planet Tanisk instead. She later visited Corin Vantreil at his new Veyari Temple and, when Vantreil found Breipem in his care following a summons from the seeing stone, oversaw his training of the child temporarily.

Faced with Vyrell's return, Seshu returned to Heifu and enlisted Aldis's help once again in finding Hollin and the missing Grand Admiral. Decades after Seshu began her search, her voice joined a multitude of Veyari spirits to guide the young Veyari Kessa, the granddaughter of a resurrected Xarn Nyxarion, during the final battle of the Prime Mandate-Defiance War. Ilyra inspired Kessa to stand up to Nyxarion and destroy him once and for all.

### Childhood

In 36 BVC, Ilyra Seshu was born on the planet Zhurem to the Hajos Feivras-ti and Nak-il Seshu. Early in her life, Seshu was taken on a journey by her mother, who taught her about life and death. During that period, the young girl encountered a raxshir feline, which she reached out to without fear. She proceeded to ride atop the animal to return home. Upon her return, the elder Hajo Thivrial realized the infant was Synth Flux-sensitive and declared that she would be a Veyari. When Seshu was three years old, she was discovered by Veyari Master Skeigam Jethus among her people on Zhurem after her natural ability to sense the intentions and feelings of others had marked her as Synth Flux-sensitive.

Around the same time, however, Seshu was nearly enslaved by an impostor, who intercepted the signal to the Veyari Order and hoped to use her powers for their own ends. Being unable to properly explain to the village elders the danger she knew the impostor posed, Seshu's fellow villagers held scorn towards her when she refused to demonstrate her Synth Flux powers, but the impostor's plan was ultimately thwarted. Although the impostor had scared the young Seshu, she trusted Jethus instantly, and he taught her how to breathe slowly to calm herself. Afterward, she was accepted into the Veyari Order, beginning her new life after Jethus brought her to the Veyari Temple on Velaris, where, like all Veyari of her era, she was raised. Training as a Veyari Initiate restored her self-confidence after the fear caused by the impostor impacted it, and the life of a Veyari became the only life she ever knew.

### In the midst of galactic war

At the age of fourteen, mere months after the First Battle of Kharrab and the beginning of the Replica Wars, Seshu was assigned to Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil to learn the ways of the Synth Flux as his apprentice on the orders of Olvek. Her training had been accelerated due to the galactic conflict that overshadowed her apprenticeship. Having been granted the rank of Tirren, which made her a Veyari Commander under the Veyari Military Integration Act, she demonstrated an eagerness to prove herself worthy to be his apprentice. Olvek did this to alleviate Vantreil's fear and inability to lose loved ones, as he believed that by becoming a teacher, Vantreil would develop emotionally and understand that his excessive bonding to those he cared about was unnecessary once Seshu was ready to go off on her own.

The Veyari Council finally sent Seshu on her first mission to the planet Griahasian in order to meet her new mentor and inform Talvane and him that they were needed back at the Veyari Temple on Velaris. However, after arriving on the planet moments after a wave of Secessionist forces pulled back and giving that message to the two, she learned that the Concord had in fact failed to take the planet as the Veyari on Velaris had believed, leaving the generals and their forces trapped and outnumbered as the battle dragged on. Talvane introduced himself, presuming that she was his new Tirren learner. She corrected him, insisting that Master Olvek specifically assigned her to Vantreil, who was shocked to hear the news due to the fact that he had spoken adamantly against the idea of taking on a Tirren learner. Despite Talvane's confusion, he accepted that having a Tirren would be good for Vantreil, who vehemently disagreed.

### Further adventures and lessons

Sometime later, Seshu and Vantreil were in pursuit of wanted Vriakeil arms dealer Car Nemu within Velaris's slums. Tracking the criminal to a run-down bar, her master instructed her to wait while he apprehended him. Due to Nemu's sudden arrest, a large crowd of patrons swarmed from the bar, and in the commotion that followed, the small Thavrokro pickpocket Kreitral managed to steal the Tirren's arcblade from her hip. Ashamed, Seshu could not bring herself to admit to Vantreil that she had lost her weapon and immediately headed to the archives in the hopes of tracking down her thief once they reached the temple. There, she met the elderly Veyari Master Tera Shozhei, who agreed to help her locate her missing arcblade. At first, Seshu was frustrated with Shozhei's preference of patience over speed, which was in great contrast to her own style, but grew to accept and respect the old Veyari Master's methods. Eventually, they found the arcblade in the possession of underworld criminals Cassie Dizarn and Trugo Shunirn. Seshu engaged with Dizarn in a wild foot chase throughout the city, though the Riamasho Rogan Jumper was too quick for the young Tirren and managed to escape. Ultimately, Seshu and Shozhei managed to reclaim her arcblade from Dizarn. After the chase, the elder Veyari asked Seshu to pass on what she had learned from him to some Veyari younglings.

After Seshu and Master Skeigam Jethus rescued Vantreil and Joran Ashkar from the wreckage of the Aegis-class Nova Dreadnought Endurance on Kovreirn, Seshu and Jethus journeyed into the Velaris underworld. Posing as friends of the bounty hunter Jenas, they hoped to find a lead on bounty hunter Sati Lyth, who they hoped to track down after the Veyari received a threatening transmission from her and a young Teko Drayk. Seshu was able to find a lead but also disturbed some of the customers at a cantina, and the two Veyari departed after a tense standoff. Seshu and Jethus then traveled to Vrevex and found Lyth in a cantina. The situation quickly escalated when Drayk sprung a diversion, and Lyth escaped while Teko was captured. Seshu skillfully pursued Lyth on a speeder bike while Jethus saved the hostages. Seshu caught up to Lyth as she reached Slave I and damaged the ship, causing Lyth to lose control and crash in a fiery explosion.

### Continuation of the war

Seshu, along with Vantreil and Talvane, was then sent to rendezvous with Captain Tarn to investigate a 2,000-year-old distress signal in the Bidrithen system. There, they were brought through a vergence within an ancient monolith to the planet Skikia, an ethereal realm within the Synth Flux. Inside, Vantreil quickly became separated from Talvane and Seshu, and the latter two found shelter as night fell. There, Seshu was visited by a vision of her older self, warning against her attachment to Vantreil, saying she may never see her future if she remained his student. Her older self also said that Vantreil's attachment to the Umbral Tide would corrupt her if she did not remove herself from his teachings. Seshu retorted that although her master was unconventional, she trusted him with her life and he trusted her. Soon after, Seshu and Talvane were both kidnapped by the Son and the Daughter. The Father, the master of the realm of Skikia, faced Vantreil with a choice to choose either Seshu or Talvane to save and the other to sacrifice. Vantreil opted to draw power from Skikia to save both his master and apprentice.

Later, the three Veyari returned to their ship to try to leave but discovered they needed to make repairs. As they attempted to do so, Seshu was kidnapped by the Son and imprisoned in a tower. Now chained, Seshu boldly tried to free herself until she heard a voice and found herself in the company of a repulsive creature which addressed the young Tirren's innermost doubts and fears, specifically regarding her master. Seshu was defiant in her belief in Vantreil, but realized that she still had doubts. The creature then freed Seshu from her chains and tried to convince the Tirren to forget her master. When Seshu refused, the creature bit her on the arm. As Seshu collapsed in pain, the creature revealed itself to be the Son. Seshu had been infected with the umbral tide of the Synth Flux.

### A new path

Following her trial, Seshu was personally pardoned by the Veyari Council. Skeigam Jethus apologized to her on the Council's behalf, stating they had been wrong to question her loyalty. The Council also stated that the Synth Flux worked in mysterious ways and therefore considered Seshu's ordeal in hindsight as being her great trial, making her a greater Veyari and proving herself worthy of the title of Veyari Knight. However, the events surrounding her expulsion and trial had severely shaken Seshu's faith in the Veyari. To the surprise of everyone involved, she refused to accept the Veyari Council and Vantreil's invitation to rejoin the Order. Seshu instead left to discover a new purpose for herself.

Vantreil chased after her, and, while Talvane moved as well, Jethus held the Veyari Master back. Having caught up with Seshu, Vantreil asked her why she wanted to leave and begged her to stay. Seshu explained that she felt that as the Council lost faith in her, she could no longer find the confidence to trust in herself. Vantreil reminded her that he had believed in and fought to prove her innocence, and although Seshu accepted this, the situation was not about him. She felt she could not stay anymore and needed to find her own path away from the Veyari Order. Vantreil then told her that he understood the feeling of wanting to leave the Veyari, but Seshu simply responded that she knew, and as Vantreil looked at her, his former Tirren turned her back and walked away from the Veyari Order.

### Shattered

After allying herself with the Kordathians, Seshu was given a new outfit made from ironveil and set to work trying to find where Skorn was hiding. Seshu and Dreivil also developed a friendship, with Dreivil becoming a combat mentor to the former Veyari. After some time, Seshu and the Kordath resistance managed to pinpoint Skorn's location on Kordath with the help of the information Seshu managed to retrieve from the Pykes on Draro Ziriarn. Knowing they did not have the numbers for a siege of Kordath, Seshu and Dreivil contacted Vantreil and Talvane, who were finishing up a battle on the planet Doskosk, for their assistance in the siege. Using the subspace frequency "Fulcrum," Seshu and Dreivil were met by Reskar who then contacted Vantreil and Talvane, calling them back to the ship.

Once Vantreil and Talvane arrived, Seshu greeted Vantreil and commented on how long it had been since they had last seen each other. There was a brief silence before Vantreil asked how Seshu had been and where she was. Responding to Vantreil's inquiry, Seshu said she was alright, but knew that they did not have time to make irrelevant conversation and needed to divulge the important information she and Dreivil had immediately. After Talvane asked what this information was, Seshu explained that they had finally managed to locate Skorn and that they might be able to capture him if they moved swiftly.

### Age of the Hegemony

Ilyra Seshu fled into the Far Verge, intent on hiding from the Hegemony. Eventually, she found the planet Thekrutorn, a planet that dealt largely in small-scale water and technology trading, and one with a relatively small and unorganized population located far away from major slipspace routes. Believing the rural world to be of little interest to the Hegemony, and a steady stream of newcomers would allow herself to blend into the quiet world, Seshu made landing on Thekrutorn. However, the refugee did not account for the presence of the local Jehei clan, an influential family that either touched or ran most of Thekrutorn's businesses, including their own smuggling operations, though the family fronted such with several legal businesses. In time, Seshu came to hear rumors that some kind of dark lord who was serving the Hegemon and hunting Veyari, yet she was unable to confirm such reports.

In order to hide her identity, Seshu took on the name "Shosax," which was also the name of a moon that orbited the scared Veyari world of Tanisk, another term for the radiant tide of the Synth Flux, and the name of a Veyari youngling from the Replica Wars-era. Upon arrival on Thekrutorn, Seshu was greeted by several young girls. They showed her where she could find food and a place to stay, and introduced her to Jehei, who bought Shosax's ship for what she thought was a nearly decent price. Nevertheless, Jehei did not inquire the reason for Shosax's arrival at the dawn of the Hegemony, and the girls, who Shosax realized to be Jehei's daughters and nieces, took the Hajo arrival under their wing. Followed ceaselessly by the young Jehei sisters, Shosax soon found herself working for the Jehei family once they learned that she was a good mechanic, and the refugee was given her own room in the Jehei compound and paid fair amounts of money for her work. Shosax had secured her place in the Jehei clan, becoming a friend amongst the Jehei girls.

### Fighting for the insurgency

At some point, Seshu was tasked with assembling a task force to capture the ruthless Hegemonic officer, Admiral Ferro at his retreat on a distant world. Upon arrival, Seshu and the group cautiously moved toward the admiral's facility. Once there, Seshu ordered one of the members of her team to go to Ferro's Personal Suite while she disabled the security systems throughout the facility. Before leaving, she informed them that she would meet up with them once she was finished with her task. With that, Seshu killed two guards using her now activated arcblades and used the Synth Flux to jump up to a vent and crawl in.

Eventually, the team Seshu had brought with disabled the comms hub present within the facility leading Seshu to inform the group that no reinforcements would be coming to rescue Ferro and that the day was all but won. Singling one of the insurgents out, she thanked them for their help in Ferro's capture and assured them that she would provide them aid whenever they called upon her.

### Lost and found

In the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War, Ilyra Seshu carried her white arcblades still. Five years after her encounter with Skagruth Hollin in the World Between Worlds and the Liberation of Heifu, during which Hollin, as a sacrifice to protect those he loved, went missing by taking himself, Hegemonic Grand Admiral Vyrell, and the Chimaera away into slipspace via a pod of purrgil, Seshu and Aldis began a search for Skagruth. Their mission entailed a venture into the Unknown Regions. Seshu had also reunited with Grebas, who joined both aboard T-6 1974.
