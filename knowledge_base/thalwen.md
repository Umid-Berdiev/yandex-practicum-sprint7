# Thalwen

Thalwen, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Thalwen or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Far Verge planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Zhoska, Tumli, and Zutrask species, as well as the Tezho and semi-sentient Kriaqua races.

## Description

A remote moon in the Thalwen system of the Far Verge Territories, Thalwen was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Tumli, Zhoska, and Zutrask species. The semi-sentient Kriaqua also dwelled on the moon as well as the Tezho. Some of Thalwen's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Thalwen I and Thalwen II.

## History

Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Thalwen to hunt the native Tumli population to make Tumli Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Far Verge.

During the reign of the Galactic Hegemony, the Hegemony, deeming Thalwen to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Hegemony occupied Thalwen. In order to make way for the complex, a Tumli village was razed by Hegemonic troops, leading to strained Hegemonic—Tumli relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Thalwen was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Hegemony also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Helvar Initiative.
