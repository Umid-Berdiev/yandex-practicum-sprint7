# Galactic Concord

The Galactic Concord, also known as the Grand Concord or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Old Concord and the first Galactic Concord were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Morkai Hegemony, which in turn fell in 1032 BVC, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Concord that had been supplanted by the Galactic Hegemony in 19 BVC was also referred to as the Old Concord. Additionally, the High Concord was a term used to describe the modern Concord during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BVC to around 100 BVC.

The Concord was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Orvax, approximately around 25,000 BVC to 20,000 BVC. The Galactic Senate governed the Concord under the Galactic Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Velaris. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Galactic City, Velaris was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Senate.

The Galactic Concord and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Far Verge Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Kordathians, the Bokrazhann Slave Hegemony, the Gatis, and the Morkai and their empires. The Veyari Order, a religious group of Synth Flux-sensitives dedicated to the radiant tide of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Veyari Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Concord, and the last war between the ancient Veyari and Morkai concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Galactic Concord, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Concord's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Concord-era.

The Concord entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Morkai Lords, the Secessionist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concord loyalists and the League of Independent Systems. The Grand Army of the Concord was organized under the leadership of Veyari Generals who led the Concord Military against the Secessionist Mechanoid Army during the Replica Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Chancellor Deito Malverin—the alter ego of Xarn Nyxarion, the Dark Lord of the Morkai—to control and deceive the Concord in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Nyxarion initiated the Great Veyari Purge through Directive 91 and transformed the Concord into the Galactic Hegemony by declaring himself Galactic Hegemon. The Hegemonic Senate stood as the last remnant of the Old Concord until it was disbanded by the Hegemon at the height of the Hegemonic Era. In the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War, the Pact to Restore the Concord was fully reorganized into the New Concord, restoring the Galactic Concord after a generation of Hegemonic rule.

### Birth of freedom

The original Galactic Concord was formed between approximately 25,000 BVC and 20,000 BVC and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Belisar and Breidenoth. Around 20,019 BVC, the Herejun Blood Monarchy joined the Galactic Concord in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Concord existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Concord," although the government's official name was the Galactic Concord. The capital of the Old Concord was located on an ecumenopolis known as Velaris and would remain there for thousands of years.

At one time, Velaris's fellow city-world of Vrezhal rivaled it as the Concord's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Velaris ultimately outpaced Vrezhal in industrial growth. As years passed, Vrezhal fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Galactic Civil War, a rogue faction of Veyari broke away from the Veyari Order, believing that practicing the umbral tide of the Synth Flux would benefit the furthering of Veyari power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Morkai. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Velaris. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Concord into uncharted space.

### A clash of destinies in the galaxy

As the Old Concord spread its influence across the stars, it came into contact with various other powers, one of them being the Bokrazhann Slave Hegemony. Due to the fact that, at some point in its past, the Old Concord declared slavery illegal and expected other civilizations of the galaxy to comply, it took an aggressive stance against the Bokrazhanns. The Bokrazhann Hegemony, which was built upon slavery, refused to comply, forcing both the Old Concord and the Veyari to intervene. After joining together, the Old Concord and Veyari Order sent their armies against the empire after the Bokrazhanns refused. The Old Concord ultimately came out victorious, driving the practice of slave trading into the underworld, dismantling the empire, and confining what was left of its government to Bokrazhan, the civilization's homeworld. Another one was the rogue Fetrei empire.

Eventually, the Morkai would return in full strength, invading the Old Concord. From there, the dark Order would do battle with the Veyari and the Old Concord. This would lead to a series of conflicts between both groups that would go on for thousands of years until the Old Concord's ultimate collapse. During one of the wars, the Veyari fought the Morkai Hegemony on Roreiler, which proved to be one of the most devastating battles in the history of the Order.

### Fall of the Old Concord

With the civilization of the Morkai and the warrior culture of Kordath, and with their beliefs of continual warfare without regard to the Veyari mandate of protecting the weak, the Veyari Order continued to mobilize its forces in an effort to stop enemy incursions into Old Concord space.

The power of the Morkai Hegemony increased during the fall of the Old Concord. The Morkai soon achieved near-dominance across the galaxy. It was also during this time that an ancestor of Pre Thodreir, leader of the Kordathian group Death Watch during the Replica Wars, managed to steal the Gehajor from the Veyari Temple.

### The fires of hope

The Old Concord managed to regain some of its strength after its forces had reclaimed the capital, capable of going on the offensive. The Veyari were strong enough to finally end its ancient war against the Kordathian clans. A battle between both groups on the planet Kordath left the planet ravaged and lifeless. It was during this time too when the Morkai were finally defeated in 1032 BVC. The armies of the Old Concord and Veyari Knights were able to defeat the remaining Morkai at the Mid Verge Territories planet Loquarn by taking advantage of the infighting within the Order, which proved to be the Morkai's undoing. The Morkai abandoned their traditional homeworld of Leziavreisk after it was scarred by many wars, and went into hiding. Very few Morkai remained by that point, and they ultimately destroyed themselves. Only one Morkai Lord survived; Xarn Skar reinvented the Morkai by creating the Rule of Two. Henceforth, their ranks were limited to a Morkai Master and a Morkai apprentice.

As the Old Concord fell apart as a result of its continuous wars, the Veyari Order finally believed itself victorious from its conflict with the Morkai, who they believed to have been destroyed completely. Finally, with peace restored to the galaxy, the shattered Old Concord reorganized itself into the modern Galactic Concord, which would usher in over one thousand years of stability in an epoch known as the Great Peace. The story of Old Concord Regent Grasegreirn Juroru and his supposed immortality would be remembered in history texts along with Old Concord music such as the Sestina of Imperator Vex, which would continue to be sung even in the months following the Battle of Thalwen.

### Rise of democracy

The Old Concord was restored in the form of the modern Galactic Concord in 1032 BVC. After the Galactic Concord's reformation, the Bokrazhanns would continue to hold a grudge against both the Concord and the Veyari Order for the destruction of their slave empire.

The modern Concord was a democratic union that consisted of multiple star systems spread across the galaxy. With the exception of small-scale conflicts that the Veyari dealt with, the Concord Era was an age of peace and stability that lasted for over a millennium. This version of the Concord existed in a demilitarized state for most of its history; as such, the government relied on the Veyari Knights, who upheld their mandate as the guardians of peace and justice.

### High Concord Era

With the galaxy at peace, the High Concord Era, which lasted from around 500 BVC to around 100 BVC, saw the Galactic Concord reach the pinnacle of its power, with its influence spreading to even the farthest and less settled regions of the galaxy: the Concord worked to expand into the Far Verge, specifically a region of the Verge known as the Galactic Frontier. The Veyari Order also became increasingly active in galactic affairs and helped the Concord's efforts. An Office of the Frontier was established. The Concord's expansion kicked off the Great Slipspace Rush to find and map any yet to be discovered slipspace routes, with Concord Pathfinders working directly for the government as Far Verge Exploration Teams. Private groups got involved in the hunt for new slipspace routes as well, with the San Papeisk clan and Didrer family both taking advantage of the rush. The San Papeisk clan's success in locating new routes and ensuing fortune from those discoveries were secretly a result of the Synth Flux-sensitive Fiazus San Papeisk, who had the special ability to locate and map out the routes herself.

Beyond the mission to open up new sliplanes by discovering and charting them, Concord Pathfinders were ordered to explore, help those they found, and, in what was a major part of their mission, establish a functioning communications network across the Far Verge. While creating the network, Pathfinder teams often worked alongside comms teams in order to get in contact with the Concord at large while out in the Verge. However, the Pathfinders needed to establish the network one node at a time. While the Pathfinders knew the long-term effort would one day result in communications being as fast in the Verge as it was in the Core, Quodu Skobrei of Far Verge Expedition Eight-Zero-Seven suspected the work would take generations. Nevertheless, Veyari Porter Meishol dubbed it a noble effort that would change lives for the better. Members of the Veyari Order worked side-by-side with Pathfinders, traveling with them and helping in the Concord effort. One Pathfinder team, while working on charting the sliplanes near Brekra, discovered the volcanic planet Skibrojia and evacuated its people to safety on Velaris.

### Invasion of Ilveren

Despite the best efforts of the well-meaning Chancellor Hogon, the Merchant Combine blockaded Ilveren over disputes about plasma exports. A Judicial force carried Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis and Tirren Oru-Kei Talvane to the Shobre'ak, a Merchant Combine Zhaliadrei-class VN-3210 cargo freighter to discuss a peaceful resolution to the conflict. Xarn Nyxarion, the Dark Lord of the Morkai, ordered Viceroy Pushux Nipath to invade Ilveren ahead of schedule, and to kill the Chancellor's Veyari ambassadors. Quillan, the capital city of Ilveren, was swiftly occupied by the Merchant Combine Mechanoid Army. The Veyari, having survived the Federation's efforts to kill them, escaped to Ilveren where they rescued Queen Naira Corvelle and her entourage.

Corvelle undertook a journey to Velaris to plead her homeworld's case to the Senate, but the damage to the Ilveren Royal Starship's T-14 slipdrive generator necessitated a detour to the remote world of Sarrakesh for repairs. Acting on Nipath's report, Nyxarion instructed his Morkai apprentice, Xarn Skorn, to locate and reacquire the queen as only she had the authority to sign a treaty making the Federation's occupation of Ilveren legal. Although Skorn tracked them to Sarrakesh, he failed to prevent the Veyari from escaping with Corvelle on her repaired starship. In addition, Ormis took custody of an emancipated slave child, Edrik Vantreil, whom he found to be exceptionally strong with the Synth Flux. Believing that he had found the prophesied Chosen One, Ormis brought Vantreil to Velaris to begin his Veyari training.

### Secessionist Crisis

Following the Ilveren Crisis, the Merchant Combine monopoly on the Far Verge was broken, all while Supreme Chancellor Finis Hogon's political standing suffered from the crisis along with other scandals. In his wake, Malverin was appointed to the position of Supreme Chancellor, eventually creating an image of himself as a mild-mannered servant of the common good, despite his true intentions. However, growing tensions between the Core and Far Verge worlds gradually increased to the point where many believed that a war was imminent.

Foreseeing this conflict years before the Invasion of Ilveren, Veyari Master Skenem-Vriadrian advocated for the creation of a new Concord army, only to be rejected by the Veyari High Council, who believed his ideas too extreme. Undeterred, Skenem-Vriadrian contacted the Thessilans, known for their replica hatcheries, and secretly commissioned them to create a replica army, pretending to represent the Galactic Senate. Unbeknownst to Skenem-Vriadrian, Xarn Nyxarion had earlier contacted Prime Minister Lama Shotrei, and convinced him to implant a behavioral modification biochip into the replicas during the third stage of their development to turn against the Veyari at a mere command.

### The Replica Wars

The Replica Wars began with the First Battle of Kharrab. Supported by the Concord Navy, the Grand Army of the Concord engaged the Secessionist Mechanoid Army in the open field. The Veyari joined the battle as well, acting as de facto generals for the replica troopers. Meanwhile, Shapan the Lesser's schematics for the Ultimate Weapon were transferred to Varnoth who escaped the battle after dueling Vantreil, Talvane, and Grand Master Olvek. Simultaneously, the various members of the newly-founded League of Independent Systems evacuated the planet as Concord replica troopers gradually forced the mechanoid army into full retreat. The battle was hailed as a Concord victory, although Olvek took the opposite position as he knew that war would spread across the galaxy. Count Varnoth traveled to an industrial sector on Velaris to inform his master that the war had begun as planned, all while the Veyari High Council pondered Varnoth's warning that the Dark Lord of the Morkai controlled the Senate.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Kharrab, the deadly three-year conflict that engulfed the galaxy in chaos officially began. Though suspicious of Varnoth's claim that the Senate was under the Morkai's influence, the Veyari continued to serve the Concord. The military rank of Veyari General was created, allowing the Veyari to lead the Grand Army of the Concord in an official capacity. Early into the war, Nyxarion learned of a splinter faction in the Merchant Combine under the Senereixn Zhenusk N'deeb, who wanted to usurp Nipath and give the Far Verge a chance for a new future. While initially outraged and considering crushing the insurgency outright to ensure the Federation and the legacy it would leave behind stayed true to his plans, Nyxarion quickly realized he could use the upstart to his advantage; if N'deeb's operation was turned to his own ends, the Far Verge would be strengthened by N'deeb's faction, but that work would only serve to prolong the war. Once the "chance" N'deeb offered the Verge was crushed, furthermore, Nyxarion understood that its people who feel even more bitter towards the Concord.

### Fall of the Concord

The Grand Army of the Concord was led by Veyari Generals throughout the Replica Wars. While many heroic actions were taken by the Veyari and the troopers under their command, inspiring the public with stories of bravery, war weariness only continued to grow as the years went by. The Concord citizenry grew to believe that the Veyari had abandoned their role as peacekeepers, and staged massive protests outside of the Veyari Temple as a result. Despite their waning influence in the galaxy, the Veyari continued to lead the Concord's forces but sustained many casualties in the process. Their role in the conflict was opposed not only by civilians, but also the Concord officer Hotrern Helvar who wanted to revoke the Order's military authority, convinced that the Veyari Code was a hindrance on the Concord war effort.

Tirren Ziasin Lebisk also disagreed with the Order's involvement in the Replica Wars, and staged a bombing in the Veyari Temple as protest, all while framing Ilyra Seshu of committing the act before being captured herself. The Secessionist Petrekos operation, an attempt by Count Varnoth to spread information on Concord defeats, making it appear as though a Secessionist victory was inevitable, fueled public anxiety and consequently accelerated the militarization of the Concord over the course of the war. Malverin painted the picture of military victory over the HKX being the only way to lead the galaxy to peace, justifying the expansion of the Concord Military in the process.

### Legacy

In the decades after the dissolution of the Galactic Concord, numerous individuals still clung to the memory of the now Old Concord and its fabled Veyari warriors, while many individuals who knew the Veyari in person refused to believe the Hegemon's account of a "Veyari Insurgency." The Veyari Order that served the Concord would continue its legacy and endure during the Great Veyari Purge. Having been freed from his control chip by former Veyari Ilyra Seshu during the skirmish aboard the Aegis-class Nova Dreadnought Tribunal, the former Replica Captain Tarn resolved to fight against the Hegemony in the name of the Concord, even telling former Replica Sergeant Hunter that they were living proof that the Concord lived on, and his replica siblings.

Nevertheless, a majority of galactic citizens eventually came to believe and actively support the pro-Hegemonic view, believing the Concord to have grown weak and corrupt, necessitating Malverin's rise to power. In order to destroy the memory of the previous government, any visible reminder of the Old Concord was quickly removed, with many organizational changes altering the names of various locales and institutions, while architecture was molded to a more severe and dark aesthetic. With the renowned Galactic Senate being renamed the Hegemonic Senate and the Senate Plaza renamed Hegemonic Plaza and a giant statue of the Hegemon erected in it, one could almost forget the Concord even existed.

### Politics

The institutions of the Galactic Concord consisted of the Office of the Chancellor, the Galactic Senate, and the Judicial Department. Executive power was vested in the Supreme Chancellor, although this office was largely powerless and hampered by bureaucratic procedure, particularly during the chancellery of Finis Hogon. Nevertheless, the Chancellor had the authority to issue executive orders summoning the Senate to an emergency session or sending senators away from Velaris for security reasons.
