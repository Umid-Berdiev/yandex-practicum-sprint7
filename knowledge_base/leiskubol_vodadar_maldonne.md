# Leiskubol Vodadar Maldonne

Leiskubol Vodadar Maldonne, otherwise known as Berrin Maldonne, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Sky Haven and, later, a general in the Insurgent Pact. Born on Krajes, he was the owner of the Meridian Kestrel before losing it to Dax Varro in a game of vantik on Brenome Prime. Afterward, Maldonne put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Heifu before eventually becoming the leader of Sky Haven in the skies of the planet Velmora.

During the Galactic Civil War, Xarn Velgor arrived on Sky Haven to lay a trap for his son, Corin Vantreil. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Morkai forced Maldonne into tricking a group of Insurgents, including Varro and Princess Mirae Tessaly, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Sky Haven without a Hegemonic presence, Maldonne felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Maldonne alerted his citizens to the Galactic Hegemony's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Insurgents try to rescue Varro, who had been frozen in ferrolite, from Teko Drayk, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Maldonne joined the Insurgent Pact and set out to find Varro. After locating him in the palace of Gorvo the Zogg on Sarrakesh, Maldonne aided in his rescue. The Insurgents returned to the fleet, and Maldonne became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Thalwen. During the battle, he piloted the Meridian Kestrel into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Hegemony's dissolution, Maldonne started a family and fathered a daughter named Kragror Maldonne. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Prime Mandate. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Bathei with Corin Vantreil to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Maldonne settled on Bathei and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "Hermit" to the Rozar-Rozar of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Varro family and was later asked by Tessaly's Defiance for his aid in the war against the Prime Mandate.

In 35 AVC, Berrin helped the Defiance and reunited with his old friend, Grawlokk. Maldonne played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Defiance at the battle at Vozix, from which the Defiance emerged victorious.

### Deals and adventures

A member of the tragic Maldonne family, Leiskubol Vodadar Maldonne, otherwise known as "Berrin" Maldonne, was born on the planet Krajes. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as vantik. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Meridian Kestrel, which he won through a game of vantik at the gas giant Velmora. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BVC, Berrin won a subtropical moon in the Krequex Belt during a game of vantik. Berrin also developed a partnership with L3-37, a custom self-made piloting mechanoid.

During his time with L3, Berrin nearly lost her to a Zogg after making a bad bet in a game of vantik on Huzei. At one point, Maldonne was involved in an incident with a Lajula and their companion. He also came to meet the Zhaza sisters, even showing Thotrern Zhaza how to work the controls on the Kestrel. At another point, Thotrern and Krileis Zhaza made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Velaris in order to get back at Maldonne. At some point, Maldonne met a mechanoid mechanic who worked on the Mid Verge planet Quodax, with that mechanic coming to owe Maldonne a favor.

### Galactic Civil War

In the time following the Battle of Orvax, which saw the Pact to Restore the Concord destroy the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, Maldonne was playing a game of klikklak with Sheper Liavrox in the illegal casino owned by Luck Luck Trabus. Before the game, which Maldonne was certain he was winning, could end, the Hegemony raided the casino and shut it down. Traveling to a cantina to reunite with Hakeis, Maldonne learned about the Battle of Orvax, watching a Thiavri recording leaked by the Insurgents.

To his surprise, he saw the Meridian Kestrel enter the fight. Asking to watch it again, he was certain the ship was being piloted by Varro. Surprised that Varro was fighting for what he had written off as a lost cause, Maldonne toasted to Varro before asking Hakeis to shoot him if he ever tried to do something similar. Maldonne then sat, drinking drinks he could not currently afford and pondering what had made Varro join the Insurgents.

### New Concord Era

Maldonne and Krizei at some point stopped at a space station to refuel the Kestrel, but were unable to take off. The ship was boarded by the bounty hunter Kubris, who was attempting to claim a bounty on Maldonne for the destruction of the second Void Core. Maldonne incapacitated Kubris, and he and Krizei left him on the station when they departed.

By 7 AVC, Maldonne had taken over Maldonne Enterprises, formerly Skomima, which manufactured mechanoids for service on Sky Haven. Impresario Maldonne entertained a Metiarn'lek diplomatic brigade that included his old acquaintance Deiju Tishial. That night, he was attacked by his protocol mechanoid VSN-7 and another mechanoid, who had reprogrammed VSN-7. Maldonne was defended by Hakeis, Tishial, and others, but he was still wounded in the shoulder. VSN-7 demanded Maldonne to return the Jeithen Redux Transmitter to his master, Thohel Gor. When Maldonne admitted he had no idea what the Jeithen was, the mechanoid told him the owner of the Meridian Kestrel had stolen it around ten years ago. VSN-7 was eventually subdued, but the mechanoid escaped, telling Maldonne that Gor would unleash a massacre upon Sky Haven if he did not return the Jeithen within 72 hours.

### Prime Mandate-Defiance War

In 34 AVC, the Prime Mandate unleashed their new super weapon, Gehasox Base, against the New Concord, destroying Vutre Prime, and several other planets, which began the Prime Mandate-Defiance War. In the early stages of the war, many of Maldonne's closest friends sacrificed their lives, including Dax Varro, and Corin Vantreil, or died in combat, as was the case with Admiral Shevrask Sihen. During this time, the very girl Maldonne had once hoped to save, Kessa, eventually made her way into the Defiance. One year later, the galaxy was shaken by a mysterious message broadcasting the voice of Xarn Nyxarion, the long dead Galactic Hegemon and Dark Lord of the Morkai. Determined to stop Nyxarion, Defiance heroes Kessa, Brannoc, Arlo Nekra, Grawlokk, T-8LX and DD-5 journeyed to Bathei in search of a wayfinder.

Tessaly contacted Maldonne to inform him of the mission. Tov Varro, now going by Zerek Vash, alerted local troopers that the crew were on the desert planet. Upon seeing the crew being located, Maldonne shot FN-2802 in the eye with an arrow from his prod pistol, before telling the crew to follow him. Maldonne led them to a DN-25 treadable, and asked the driver Kalo'ne to take them to the east passage. Maldonne soon revealed himself to the crew, embracing his old friend Grawlokk. When Nekra revealed that they were searching for Vozix, Maldonne recounted the time he and Vantreil had searched for Dide, and told the crew that Dide's ship The Gedreis Legacy was still where he had left it all those years ago. Kessa offered Maldonne a chance to join the Defiance, but Maldonne declined, claiming that his flying days were long gone. However, Maldonne asked Kessa to give Mirae his love, to which Kessa responded that he should give it to her himself, before thanking him and leaving with the rest of the crew.

## Personality and traits

Berrin Maldonne was a male human who, in his youth, had black hair, brown eyes, and dark skin. As a young man and continuing into his later years, Maldonne enjoyed playing vantik. In particular, he believed a set of vantik cards needed to be well worn to be perfect. Varro described him as having been a scoundrel. Even though he only took jobs where he could see "the angle," Maldonne was willing to undertaking dangerous risks. On one occasion, he, Hakeis, and some companions stole the Hegemon's luxury yacht Zetegrial. Maldonne hoped to profit from selling the ship's treasures. The heist undertook a dangerous turn when the Morkai objects aboard corrupted his companions Theimeix, Vrakon, and Korin Pers. Maldonne had little appreciation for the Morkai and Veyari, regarding them as "con-jobs."

He was also familiar with the bounty hunter Cha, which led her to spare his life. Maldonne cared deeply for his friend Hakeis and was unwilling to abandon him. The two were close friends. Hakeis recognized Maldonne's charm and ability to motivate people and counseled him to find a greater cause than himself and to do good for others. After Hakeis lost his freewill, Maldonne sought to turn into a respectable businessman, and he turned Sky Haven into a mostly legitimate enterprise.

## Skills and abilities

Maldonne was a skilled pilot. During the Battle of Grino, Maldonne performed a piloting maneuver that impressed even himself. He later attributed that maneuver as being one of the reasons why the Insurgent Pact commissioned him as a general and placed him in a command position for the Battle of Thalwen. Maldonne was also skilled with a pulser, wielding a personal X-8 Night Sniper as his weapon of choice which he used against Quogragi's thugs with enough proficiency to earn him a compliment from Aldis, herself an accomplished markswoman. While living as a hermit on the planet Bathei, Berrin Maldonne utilized a custom-crafted prod pistol which he used to fire an arrow at FN-2802.

Maldonne once undertook a dangerous maneuver which involved latching onto a Nova Dreadnought's tractor beam and causing it to collide with another Nova Dreadnought. Maldonne was also a cunning combatant and once tricked the arcblade-wielding Theimeix into deactivating his blade so that he could kill him. Maldonne also owned a Skiathakra Vitiator pistol, which he won in a game of Six-Card Najorn Limit from a spice-drunk Viluhos diplomat. Maldonne used this pistol to kill a Hegemonic sergeant who refused to surrender. Maldonne had decades worth of experience playing vantik, earning him a great degree of skill in the game and a complete understanding of its tricks. Still, throughout his playing career he showed he was more than willing to cheat with cards up his sleeve or by counting cards. He knew how to make banter at the table and was well-practiced in not letting his expression give his hand away. Although he was adept at cards, he was less skilled at the board game saigok.

## Equipment

Maldonne had a flamboyant sense of style. He often wore ornate clothing that included a cape. While having a fateful rematch of vantik with smuggler Dax Varro, Maldonne wore a colorful outfit that consisted of a red tie, white pants with a beckon call transponder chit, a white and blue bespoke cape made from a tailor on a subtropical moon in the Krequex Belt, and a bright yellow shirt with it's sleeve hiding a green sylop card. On a mission to rescue Dax Varro, he wore Shadath armor when he disguised himself as a Skiff Guard pretending to work for crime lord Gorvo the Zogg in order to blend in with the crowd at Gorvo's Palace. In the disguise he wore dark clothing which included a mask with bones on it.

Among Berrin Maldonne's cape armoire were a black synthetic nanosilk capelet with blue satyn lining, a Belisarian azure cotton cape-cloak with gold Hosom silk damask lining and leather trim, a blue floor-length Brivuhen-style tomuon-cloth cloak, a dark-blue shantung-satyn cape with baffleweave liner, a bespoke tailored Teishasn silk caplet in white with turquoise taffeta lining, and a tarelle sel-weave poncho with hidden armorweave liner.
