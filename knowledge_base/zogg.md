# Zogg

Zoggs were a large slug-like sentient species who were native to the planet Zhalias, but considered their homeworld to be Skushu Trenim after abandoning Zhalias due to the Shalovoth Kapa. While strongest in the Far Verge Territories, they dominated organized criminal activities across the galaxy, controlling the Zogg Clan crime syndicate. Speaking Zoggese, their social structure was divided into clans known as kajidics.

## Biology and appearance

Zoggs were a massive slug-like reptiloid species who had large mouths and stubby arms. They had three lungs. They were tough and muscular with thick leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. Zoggs often had watery eyes and slack facial expressions. Their tails were supported by a skeletal spine and allowed Zoggs to propel themselves through muck which legs would sink into. Despite their legendary adult size, Zoggs started out as tiny Mahiasks less than half a meter in height.

Zoggs possessed skeletons, and were not known for being healthy. Some Zoggs suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Whilst the Zoggs were originally native to Zhalias, Skushu Trenim—a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Zoggs were most comfortable—later became the planet that was considered their homeworld. Zoggs were immune to mind tricks.

### Organized crime

The Zoggs originally hailed from the planet Zhalias, where they were hunted by the Shalovoth Kapa. In order to escape the Shalovoth Kapa, the Zoggs ultimately abandoned Zhalias and settled on the planet Skushu Trenim. For centuries, the Zoggs dominated much of the organized criminal activities in the galaxy. They dominated the Metiarn'leks of Nejes and the Viquu species for several centuries.

The Zoggs approached Lord Pekrox of the Directorate, striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Desern and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Pekrox instigated the Desern–E'ronoh crisis, but in actuality the Zoggs intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

### A galactic war

During the Replica Wars, the Galactic Concord forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk after the Veyari Edrik Vantreil and his Tirren Ilyra Seshu rescued his son Geiskar. The Concord Senator Naira Corvelle also foiled a plot by Gorvo's uncle Rokra to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the League of Independent Systems.

Later in the war, the renegade Morkai Lord Skorn approached the Grand Zogg Council on the planet Skushu Trenim to offer them a place in his Shadow Collective. After denying him, a force of bounty hunters attacked Shadow Collective's forces, but after being defeated, saw one of their members, Jemi, killed by Brakk Zuhe. Eventually, Skorn's forces would visit Gorvo and Miaqual on Sarrakesh and, after a short battle, convinced the two Zoggs to join them.

### Age of the Hegemony

During the Age of the Hegemony, the Zoggs and other organized criminal groups exercised a powerful grip on the galaxy. The Zoggs also had extensive dealings with the Galactic Hegemony in return for the protection of Zogg Space. They performed certain important, but off-the-books, services, including helping to run several black-site prisons.

When an auction intended to sell notorious smuggler and Insurgent Pact member Dax Varro, who had been frozen in ferrolite and stolen from the bounty hunter Teko Drayk, on Jequal was announced by Crimson Dawn leader Triagreith'ra, the Grand Zogg Council, along with the Hegemony and numerous other criminal factions from across of the galaxy, arrived to join in on the auction. Varro was eventually bought by Gorvo, but before Gorvo could take his prize, the auction was invaded by Xarn Velgor, who after defeating Triagreith'ra, ordered his forces to take Varro away as he left to face the insurgent Corin Vantreil. Shortly thereafter, a battle in Jequal's orbit between the forces of the Zoggs and the Hegemonic forces, provoked by Trokrei, began. During the battle, the Grand Zogg Council was killed by Xarn Velgor, who withdrew from his confrontation with Vantreil after being informed that the Hegemon had ordered him to end the Zogg insurrection personally.

### New Concord Era

Following Gorvo's death, the Zoggs were left to determine who would fill Gorvo's seat as leader of his empire. Several months after Gorvo's demise, a decision had yet to be made.

Following the liberation of Gotreith, the New Concord took steps to curtail the influence of the Zoggs and other organized groups by introducing financial regulations and undertaking comprehensive patrols of the galactic shipping lanes. Still, the Zoggs were able to exercise some influence over the Viquu species and their government into the New Concord era.

## Zoggs in the galaxy

One Zogg was based out of Vatreth Quedroskeis space during the Replica Wars. The Zogg hired the smuggler Has Zhahe in 19 BVC for a menial job. At some point, the Zoggs established but ultimately abandoned settlements on the planet Kreidrith, where they left behind security mechanoids.

## Culture

Zogg society was divided into families known as kajidics, Gorvo's family was the Kruskoske kajidic, whose mark was tattooed on his arm. Zoggs bandaged and buried their dead.

Classical architectural Zogg style could be seen on the planet Kreidrith. It was characterized by terraced buildings with sloping gates, and white and red colors. Clothing was optional for Zoggs, and some would use live Sha'rellian toops as hairpieces. Zoggs liked to eat gorgs, slime pods, and Zhaskadreth paddy frogs, and were also known to smoke hookah pipes. The Zoggs believed that at least one of their own, Keikol Grashalern Shad'ruu, had ascended to godhood, and held the Keikol Eve holiday in their honor.
