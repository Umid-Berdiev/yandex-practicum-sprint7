# Oru-Kei Talvane

Oru-Kei Talvane was a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive human male Veyari Master who served on the Veyari High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Veyari General, Talvane served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Secessionist Mechanoid Army during the Replica Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Talvane, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Veyari Purge initiated by the Concord's own replica troopers when Concord Supreme Chancellor, secretly Xarn Nyxarion, implemented Directive 91. As a mentor, Talvane was responsible for training two members of the Vantreil family, Edrik and Corin Vantreil, both of whom served in turn as his Tirren in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Talvane was born on the planet Kreveth in 57 BVC. He was Synth Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Veyari Order as a child. Raised in the Veyari Grand Temple on the planet Velaris, Talvane was assigned to Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis. In 32 BVC, Talvane attained the rank of Veyari Knight by defeating the Morkai Lord Xarn Skorn during the Invasion of Ilveren. He also chose to train Edrik Vantreil—the prophesied Chosen One who was destined to destroy the Morkai and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Ormis who died in the arcblade duel against Skorn.

With the advent of the Replica Wars in 22 BVC, Talvane adopted the military rank of High Veyari General in the Grand Army of the Galactic Concord, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Replica Marshal Commander Jajis served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Independent Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Skorn re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Talvane for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Skorn failed to end Talvane's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Dreivil, Duchess of Kordath.

In 19 BVC, Talvane killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Kharzul, during the Battle of Netho, effectively ending the Replica Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Veyari Purge against the Veyari Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion. After discovering that Vantreil had betrayed the Veyari and turned to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, becoming Nyxarion' Morkai apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Talvane defeated his former Tirren on Pyrrhax and exiled himself to the Far Verge planet Sarrakesh as the Galactic Hegemony rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Tov Talvane," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Naira Corvelle's son, Corin.

Ten years later, Talvane left exile to rescue Mirae Tessaly, Corin's twin sister, and was pursued by the Hegemony's Jevrorar and Velgor himself. Later, Talvane was hunted down once again by Skorn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Corin. Talvane began the boy's Veyari training in 1 BVC, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Corin and his allies escaped from the Morkai Lord. In death, Talvane became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Corin throughout the Galactic Civil War. In 4 AVC, Talvane reunited with his former student, Edrik Vantreil, who died destroying his Morkai Master in order to save his son. Talvane was remembered as a legendary Veyari Master during the rise of the New Concord; Tov Varro, the son of Corin's twin sister, Princess Mirae Tessaly, was named after Talvane. Varro's turn to the umbral tide left Corin disillusioned with the Veyari Order's legacy, and he held Talvane responsible for Edrik's transformation into Velgor. However, Corin's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Xarn Nyxarion's granddaughter Kessa, to whom Talvane would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Vozix in 35 AVC.

### Early life

Oru-Kei Talvane was born on the planet Kreveth in 57 BVC. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

### Joining the Veyari Order

When Talvane was discovered to be Synth Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Veyari Temple on the Core Worlds planet Velaris to be trained as a Veyari within the first six months of his life. In the Veyari crèche he was placed with other Veyari younglings in the Zhorask Clan during his early years, including his friends Kunus and Deitu. Talvane received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Veyari Order's Temple and began training in several simple Synth Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BVC.

Talvane studied in the ways of the Synth Flux and Veyari traditions under Grand Master Olvek, eventually coming to respect him and the Veyari Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of arcblade combat from Veyari Master Skafith Drilern in the basic arcblade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Tirren, Talvane attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Talvane, during his days as a youngling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Tirren. To curb Talvane's behavior, Olvek searched for a teacher for Talvane that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.

### Sael-Rhal Ormis's Tirren

Around 44 BVC, Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis chose Talvane as his Tirren learner when he was thirteen years old. During his apprenticeship he became friends with Jape, a fellow Tirren who he kept correspondence with, and also maintained contact with crèche-mate Deitu and the Sel Bunimo Bant Eerin. In their first months together, Ormis attempted to adapt his teaching methods to suit Talvane's strengths, but felt he failed to do so adequately. At some point during his training, Talvane constructed his first arcblade, modeled after Ormis's arcblade hilt style and his own personality. As the pair struggled to establish a workable affinity, the Master continued to train his Tirren in the basic cadences of arcblade combat, preventing him from picking a specialty. Ormis was proud of his apprentice's skills, and sparred with him in training sessions while at the Temple between missions. Talvane, in turn, spent a significant amount of time in the training dojos to hone the techniques taught to him.

In their lessons, Ormis taught Talvane that there was a strength and nobility in restraint. As time passed, Talvane's demeanor shifted to become more serious and respectful of the Council, in contrast to the maverick Ormis's usual way of approaching situations based on his instincts in the present, even if that meant going against the Council's wishes. Both carried differing philosophies on focusing on the future versus the present, and interpreted the Synth Flux in varying ways. Despite their differences, Talvane strived to be a good student, and supported his Master's hobbies regarding ancient Veyari prophecy research. To Talvane's chagrin, the majority of his free time in the Temple was consumed by gathering information from the Veyari Archives and the mnemolith of prophecy under the watchful eye of the Veyari Master librarian Trafia Nu. Over time, he learned the language Old Belisarian, which many of the mnemolith's prophecies were written in. As an escape from the workload, Talvane played rounds of the game holochess in the lower levels of Velaris in the evenings after finishing his Master's research requests.

### Invasion of Ilveren

In 32 BVC, by the time Ormis considered Talvane to be ready for his trials, they were sent by Galactic Concord Supreme Chancellor Corvan Hogon to negotiate with the Merchant Combine, who had set up a blockade around the planet of Ilveren. Both Veyari extensively studied political documents and trade relations between the parties prior to the endeavor, sensing its necessity. The two Veyari were tasked with negotiating a settlement with Merchant Combine representatives.

The pair traveled to the system on the Consular-class space cruiser Radiant VII and landed on the modified Zhaliadrei-class VN-3210 cargo freighter Shobre'ak, the flagship of the blockade. There, Talvane and Ormis were brought to a conference room, where protocol mechanoid FQ-14 informed them that a Merchant Combine representative would be with them shortly, and invited the pair to make themselves comfortable. However, upon learning that the pair were Veyari from FQ-14, Viceroy Pushux Nipath of the Merchant Combine instead sent the protocol mechanoid back into the room to distract them, with the mechanoid offering Talvane and Ormis drinks. Nipath then ordered his security to get rid of the Veyari and their assets, with an overhead turret destroying the Radiant VII and its crew.

### Master of the Chosen One

Following the end of the Battle of Ilveren, Talvane was promoted to the rank of Veyari Knight by the Council, with Olvek reluctantly accepting his decision to train Vantreil. Talvane was later present during Ormis's funeral, where he informed the boy that he would become a Veyari. Along with Corvelle and her court, Talvane and Vantreil remained at the funeral long after the Veyari Council had left, waiting until every last ember had gone out. Though he deeply grieved the loss of his teacher, Talvane also felt that Ormis had abruptly cast him aside in favor of prophecies and unbending belief in Vantreil as the Chosen One. Nonetheless, as he watched his mentor's body in its final moments, he promised that he would chose to believe the prophecies. Later on, Talvane, alongside his new Tirren and several members of the Veyari Council, took part in a victory celebration in Quillan.

During his first year as Vantreil's Master, he sometimes woke up at night to see his Tirren was sleeping on the floor next to his bed; he realized Vantreil, having lost both his mother and Ormis, was clinging onto him, desperate to ensure his new master did not vanish one night. During one of their many adventures, Talvane fell into a nest of gundarks, prompting Vantreil to rescue him. At the Veyari Temple, Talvane trained Vantreil in meditation and levitating rocks. Though Vantreil wished to know more about his mentor's early life, he did not remember much of it by that point. Talvane had several misgivings toward taking on Vantreil as his Tirren, but stuck to his firm commitment of honoring his late master's dying wish. But Talvane was still mourning Ormis, and he began to have the perspective that Vantreil got a very raw deal being forced to be with a master who was hardly fit to be a teacher and had just barely got beyond the Veyari trials himself.

### Secessionist Crisis

In 22 BVC, Talvane and Vantreil were involved in a border dispute on Fosei that involved Presidente Pajeth Vrofix of the Commerce Guild. Following their success on Fosei, the two Veyari were sent by the Veyari Council—at the behest of Supreme Chancellor Malverin—to protect the now Senator Corvelle from future attacks following an assassination attempt on her life. Talvane and Vantreil made their way to Naira's Apartment and whilst they traveling up a turbolift, Talvane sensed Vantreil's anxiety and warned him of over-stepping their duties, only to be told later of Corvelle's plan to use herself as bait.

That night, Biafin Kiathisk attempted to kill the Senator with venomous kouhun deployed from a probe mechanoid, but her plot was foiled when Talvane and Vantreil sensed a disturbance in the Senator's room. While Vantreil eliminated the creatures, Talvane leaped out of the window and held onto the mechanoid as it attempted to flee into the skylanes of Velaris. The probe mechanoid moved rapidly between the express ways in an attempt to dislodge the Veyari, unsuccessfully. As the mechanoid moved closer to Kiathisk's position, the assassin shot the mechanoid with her rifle, destroying it and causing Talvane to fall rapidly towards the surface of the planet. Vantreil was quickly approaching Talvane's position in a speeder, and moved beneath him in order to catch him. The pair then proceeded to pursue the bounty hunter through the skyways of Velaris through traffic lanes and industrial areas, until Vantreil leaped to her speeder and disabled it, causing it to crash outside the Outlander Club.

### The Replica Wars

Sentenced to execution in Kharrab' Quizeha Arena, Talvane was joined in the arena by Vantreil and Corvelle who were also captured in their attempt to rescue him. Though reassured that his message had been re-transmitted, he expressed disappointment that Vantreil had come and failed to free them from the situation. The trio worked to control the three creatures deployed to kill them—a reek, an acklay, and a nexu. After they had freed themselves from their restraints, Talvane fought the most formidable of the three creatures—the acklay. Talvane, Corvelle, and Vantreil were saved by the arrival of a Veyari task force led by Joran Ashkar who refused to surrender to Varnoth, intending to complete their rescue. The Veyari fought back against the overwhelming mechanoid armies, but soon only a few survivors were left in the center of the arena surrounded by battle mechanoids. When Ashkar declined Varnoth's offer to let the Veyari surrender, Talvane stood in solidarity with the decision, intending to go down fighting.

Moments prior to their execution, replica army forces arrived in transports led by Master Olvek. The replicas fought back against the mechanoids and rescued the Veyari and Corvelle, forming a perimeter around the survivors. Talvane then boarded the same Concord MNFT/i transport with Vantreil and Corvelle while the rest of the Veyari task force also escaped. As they flew, Vantreil instructed their replica pilots on how best to shoot down enemy forces, which Talvane complimented. Soon, both caught sight of Varnoth fleeing on his speeder with a mechanoid escort. Talvane felt that there was not enough time to pursue reinforcements, assuring Corvelle that he and his Tirren could handle the situation alone. When Varnoth's escort rattled their MNFT/i, the Senator fell out of their vehicle, causing Vantreil to panic.

### The war drags on

Talvane later participated in a meeting in which he and the council assigned Corvelle to spy on Rush Rakeil, whom they believed to be a Secessionist spy. He was also present alongside Master Olvek and Master Ashkar to assign Zhunan and her battalion to engage the enemy on Sketim.

Assigned to capture Kharrab, alongside Briagrax-Gredreil-Skabrom, Fakosha Sasho, Vantreil, and Seshu, Talvane participated in a conference to delineate the invasion plans to destroy Shapan the Lesser's primary mechanoid factory, which was being protected by a massive ray shield. With their primary target being the shield generator at the Ray Shield Fortress the Veyari mounted a three-pronged attack on the Shoquosu defenses. Talvane and Jajis commenced the central push while Skabrom and Vantreil led the northern and southern assaults, respectively. Though Talvane was able to reach the landing zone, which was known as Point Rain, the Veyari General was injured and forced to wait for Vantreil and Skabrom's arrival who, despite their crashing, managed to fight their way up and join Talvane. In a combined effort, they managed to destroy the shield generator, and Talvane and Skabrom returned to the fleet with the rest of the wounded for treatment.

### Nearing the end of the war

When the two brothers attacked Koquex Transfer Station in the Meridian sector, the space station sent out a distress signal, leading Talvane and Tudre there to investigate about the two brothers, who were now operating under Skorn's singular vision. The Veyari learned they were headed to Vrevex. There, Talvane contacted Barrik Fuvron to inquire about the situation, who said that "two horned men" had appeared and threatened to attack his stronghold with pirates who had betrayed him. Talvane told him he was unable to help him with his men, but that he could help stop the Morkai.

Talvane and Tudre managed to engage Skorn and Zuhe just as they began their attack on Vrevex. As the fight progressed, Tudre was slain by Zuhe and, after taking up his fallen comrade's arcblade to help defend himself, Talvane was forced to retreat and reunite with Fuvron. Inside a cave, Fuvron decided to ambush the men while Talvane decided to confront Skorn and Zuhe. With great skill and Tudre's arcblade, Talvane violently fought the pair, changing to Trubrern and demonstrating mastery of his former fighting style. Eventually, he injures Zuhe' knee and cuts his left arm off. However, before Talvane could continue the fight, Skorn Synth Flux-pushed him into another part of the cave, bringing the fight to an end. Realizing their plan had failed, the Jafiaresians left and headed off-planet to try again another time. Their ship, however, was shot down and the two were forced to head for an escape pod. As the ship fell, Talvane went with Barrik to see "what riches [had] fallen from the sky," but there were no signs of Skorn nor Zuhe, leaving Talvane to doubt their demise.

### Age of the Hegemony

Intending to infiltrate the building to shut off the Veyari recall beacon, Talvane and Olvek made their way to the still-smoking Veyari Temple, where they found replicas were wearing Veyari robes in an attempt to deceive Veyari survivors into a false sense of security. Both Veyari fought the 501st patrols outside and entered, finding the aftermath of a slaughter that distressed Talvane even more than the smoke and robe-wearing replicas. When he made it to the central security station of the Veyari Temple, Talvane remembered Zheivras Sheneisk's suggestion that the signal could warn Veyari away; instead of disabling the Veyari recall signal, he changed it into a heartfelt holographic message of himself, warning any of the surviving Veyari to stay away from the Temple and the planet, to persevere for the future, and to trust in the Synth Flux. Copies of the message would persist and be seen by Veyari in hiding in the following years.

Desperate for answers and despite Olvek's warning that he would only find pain, Talvane checked the security recordings to find that Vantreil had led his battalion of replicas to attack the Temple, where he fought the Veyari & their Younglings and knelt to the newly-proclaimed Hegemon, who was proud of his apprentice, now going by a new name—"Velgor." Though Talvane was horrified, Olvek determined that they had to destroy the Morkai; Talvane, however, refused to fight Velgor and requested that he be sent to kill the Hegemon instead, but Olvek vetoed the idea, claiming that Talvane wasn't strong enough to go head-to-head with Nyxarion on his own. Talvane expressed that Vantreil was like a brother to him and that he did not have the heart to kill him, but Olvek explained that the boy he had trained no longer existed and that he needed to be put out of his misery, and told Talvane to use his instincts to find Velgor.

### Continued exile on Sarrakesh

In preparation for Corin's future training, Talvane wrote a set of journals and placed them in a box labeled "For Corin." In one of his journals, Talvane wrote about how he stopped Gorvo the Zogg's men from collecting their "water tax" on the local farmers during the Great Drought. He kept Gorvo's thugs from extorting the Odrin family into paying their water tax; following an action which involved subtle use of the Synth Flux to cause the thugs' firearms to misfire with the intention of preventing harm from coming to the townspeople, he decided not to go into town anymore.
