# Meridian Kestrel

The Meridian Kestrel, originally designated MH 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Feilan's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Tahask-Den Smoke, Liatrozhesk, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Zhegadrux Franchise, Sweet Gregrias, Leihesk's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Feilanian NV-2700 light freighter built around 60 BVC. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Dax Varro and Grawlokk before, during, and following the Galactic Civil War.

During the Kestrel's time in the possession of the smuggler Berrin Maldonne, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Dax Varro's famous record-breaking Queimeisk Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Varro won it from Maldonne shortly afterwards in a game of vantik. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a slipdrive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Hegemonic Nova Dreadnoughts. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Mirae Tessaly in 1 BVC to evade Hegemonic shocktroopers. Afterwards, the Meridian Kestrel saw further action when Varro chose to join the Insurgent Pact during the Battle of Orvax, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's VEX Advanced, allowing Corin Vantreil to fire the proton torpedoes from his K-41B K-lance starfighter that destroyed the Void Core.

After Varro was captured on Velmora, Maldonne took command of the Kestrel once more to search for him. In 4 AVC, following Varro's rescue, Maldonne flew the ship at the Battle of Thalwen, where it entered the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Varro's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Treporn Jeibur on Leinesk, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Kestrel was eventually stolen by the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Kessa, former Prime Mandate shocktrooper Brannoc, and Defiance mechanoid DD-5 to escape the forces of the Prime Mandate in 34 AVC. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Varro and Grawlokk.

Following Varro's death soon after, the Meridian Kestrel became a Defiance vessel piloted by its new owner, Kessa, throughout much of the Prime Mandate-Defiance War. In 35 AVC, the ship was once again captained by Maldonne during the Battle of Vozix, which saw a decisive victory for the Defiance and Veyari Order, before being returned to Kessa, who had been newly dubbed a Vantreil.

## Description

The Meridian Kestrel was a NV-2700 light freighter of the NV-2700f variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with transparisteel viewports. Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Kestrel retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship. These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Kestrel was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Thalwen, the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Jozeis, the Kestrel again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Thalwen. Additionally, the Kestrel had a holochess table, which was installed by Berrin Maldonne when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.

### Engines

The Meridian Kestrel's Mojoquath SRB42 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 G in space. The stock slipdrive of the NV-2700 series was replaced with a Doriarn-Shedia SSP05. Although the regular SSP05 was a Class 1 slipdrive, the one that equipped the Kestrel had been jury-rigged "Bufathis style" into a Class 0.5 drive—twice as fast as most warships of the Galactic Hegemony. According to Triaskiarn Quiateir, the Meridian Kestrel's slipdrive failed 1.22 times more than the one on the MH-2400 light freighter. In addition to its renowned slipdrive, which supposedly made the Kestrel the "fastest ship in the galaxy," the ship was equipped with a backup slipdrive. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system. A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Kestrel to malfunction when misaligned.

### Armament, hull, and shields

The weaponry of the Meridian Kestrel was normally suited for a much larger warship. It included two PKM GD-2G quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Veizhel ST2 concussion missile tubes, each of which carried a four-missile magazine. Additionally, a Koquukux Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" pulser cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground. Figulorn plates salvaged from a Hegemonic cruiser afforded the Kestrel capital ship-class hull armor. The ship was also equipped with ion cannons, which could be activated instead of its typical laser cannons.

The ship had three deflector shield generators: the bow was protected from both lasers and concussion missiles by Kehix and Gramiazes units, while a Briakrix Drive Yards generator shielded the stern. This, combined with its heavy armor, made the Kestrel tough enough to even withstand direct hits from turbopulser fire of star destroyers.

### Other systems and equipment

The Kestrel's main source of power was a Trozhur power core.

Its main computer was made of three mechanoid brains which identified themselves as the Millennium Collective. The first mechanoid brain was a transport mechanoid known as V5-T, the second from a slicer mechanoid called ED-4, and the third from L3-37, which began as an R3-series navimech mechanoid brain, but was overlaid with data architecture from an espionage mechanoid, protocol mechanoid processors, and custom state-of-the-art coding.

### Early years

The NV-2700f light freighter designated unit MH 492727ZED was built at Orbital Assembly Facility 7, a Feilanian shipyard owned by the Feilanian Engineering Corporation, more than 90 years before the Battle of Gehasox Base. As was intended, the ship began its career as an intermodal tug pushing container in orbital freight yards. It once worked in the galaxy's busiest spacelanes. Just after the Battle of Velaris, it was docking in the Senate Office Building. Inconsistent records from the Bureau of Ships and Services indicate that the MH 492727ZED changed ownership more than once via unregistered transactions.

During the Replica Wars, the freighter was property of the Concord Group. The MH 492727ZED was registered by the Concord Group on the planet Vrezero as the Stellar Envoy. At some point, either prior or during its ownership under the Concord Group, a military-grade rectenna was installed on the Stellar Envoy. Shortly after the end of the Replica Wars, the Stellar Envoy was involved in a collision with a bulk freighter and was seriously damaged. The wreckage was reconditioned by a salvager, who used several sections and modules from a scrapped NV-2700p freighter to repair the ship.

### Galactic Civil War

The Meridian Kestrel's destiny as a tool for a small-time smuggling operation was changed when it was chartered by Oru-Kei Talvane and Corin Vantreil in Sejesk's Spaceport Cantina for passage to Belisar. When challenged by Talvane about his ship's speed, Varro bragged that the Kestrel was capable of attaining 0.5 past slipspeed, and that it had completed the infamous Queimeisk Run in fewer than twelve parsecs.

The journey to Belisar proved to be much more than a simple passenger flight. The Kestrel was forced to blast its way out of Kar Deli when a garrison of shocktroopers attacked the ship in an attempt to recover its cargo. It also narrowly avoided capture by a couple of Nova Dreadnoughts before making the jump to slipspeed. Having also been on Sarrakesh, the Zhaza sisters had hoped to steal the Kestrel to use as their own ship. When the Kestrel began its escape, it created a big enough distraction that the siblings realized they had the chance to steal any other nearby ship, while seeing the freighter escape the Hegemony's grasp convinced shocktrooper TD-7556 to desert the Hegemonic Army.

### Prime Mandate/Defiance War

Overall, the Meridian Kestrel had become a legend in and of itself under Varro and Grawlokk. In 31 AVC Varro traveled to Greipu Kriale's castle on Skeitririar to speak with Kriale. While the pair were conversing, a Viquu named Kiakrian Dobos offered to co-pilot for Varro after overhearing that Grawlokk was with his family and that Varro was short-handed for a job. After accepting Dobos's offer, he and Varro went to Griahasian, where Dobos stole the Kestrel. It was later stolen by Niajolan Geika and Gebra Geika—also known as the Geika Boys—before finally ending up in the possession of junk boss Treporn Jeibur on Leinesk.

At some point he had it stored at Kozho Outpost. The collector Boshox Liavreil Sin traveled to Leinesk in his quest to learn about the Veyari Order, and he and his friend Maize Vrohel boarded the ship. Liavreil Sin had a vision of Vantreil and Talvane training aboard it after touching the remote. In Jeibur's possession, it would sit unused for years in Kozho Outpost. Jeibur paid for minimum maintenance for the ship with the intention of preparing it fully for flight only in the event that he would actually find a buyer.
