# T-8LX

T-8LX (See-Eightel) was a 8LX-series protocol mechanoid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Eightel, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the navimech mechanoid K7-M4, T-8LX constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

T-8LX was rebuilt from spare parts by Edrik Vantreil, an enslaved human who lived in Kar Zhezes, a city on the Far Verge world of Sarrakesh. During the Replica Wars, he would serve the Ilveren Senator Naira Corvelle. Shortly after the Replica Wars, T-8LX's memory was erased, though K7-M4's memory was not. T-8LX and K7-M4 were assigned to the Belisar cruiser Zhavian IV, where they served senator Dorn Tessaly for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Galactic Hegemony. At some point during this time, T-8LX's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

T-8LX remained constant companions with K7, and in 1 BVC, the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Mirae Tessaly of the Insurgent Pact entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane on Sarrakesh. After meeting Vantreil's son, Corin and the smuggler Dax Varro, the mechanoids helped rescue Princess Mirae from the Hegemony's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their insurgency defeat the Hegemony and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Tumlis to aid the Insurgents at the Battle of Thalwen.

Following the Battle of Thalwen, T-8LX continued serving the Vantreils and Varro during the time of the New Concord. About thirty years after the events on Thalwen, T-8LX was still an aide to Tessaly, who had become a general in the Defiance, a private military group opposed to the Prime Mandate, a military junta that emulated the Old Hegemony. By that time, T-8LX was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, T-8LX's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Morkai dialects. He would assist Kessa in translating Morkai writings in order to locate Xarn Nyxarion and the Morkai Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Vozix, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend K7 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Defiance defeated Nyxarion and the Morkai Eternal in the Battle of Vozix, his memory was restored.

### Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, T-8LX served as a protocol mechanoid to the chief negotiator for the Quisapir system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

### Serving the Maker

At some point, however, T-8LX was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Sarrakesh. Edrik Vantreil, a slave boy from the Sarrakeshian city of Kar Zhezes, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding T-8LX in 34 BVC so the mechanoid would help his mother. As Edrik longed for a life far from Sarrakesh and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have T-8LX as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given T-8LX's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol mechanoids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Vantreil specially modified T-8LX so he could withstand Sarrakesh's sand and heat. T-8LX served Edrik and his mother Revex by performing household chores. During his time with Vantreil and Revex, T-8LX's parts and wiring were left exposed since Vantreil was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to mechanoid plating being too expensive.

Following the Merchant Combine's invasion of the Mid Verge planet of Ilveren, T-8LX's life entered a new chapter when his owner Edrik met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Queipa, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis, the Queen of Ilveren Naira Corvelle, the Heihis Nib Nib Tolk, and the navimech mechanoid K7-M4 were brought to the slave quarters that Edrik shared with his mother Revex. As a result of this invitation, Corvelle and K7-M4 were introduced to T-8LX. Edrik plugged in one of T-8LX's photoreceptors, which became the first time T-8LX had experienced sight. Upon meeting K7-M4, T-8LX was shocked when the candid navimech mechanoid commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two mechanoids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Vantreil make final adjustments to his skimracer in time to race it in the Keikol Eve Classic race. While working on the skimracer, the two mechanoids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Tolk.

### Beyond Sarrakesh

Later, his owner Revex was sold to the moisture farmer Vilos Odrin, who freed and married her. Revex brought T-8LX along with her, and the protocol mechanoid found himself in the service of the Odrin family, who owned a moisture farm in the Kehiaho Wastes. Revex covered him in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Sarrakesh's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Edrik Vantreil, Revex was kidnapped by a group of Dhural Raiders, fearsome nomads native to Sarrakesh's desert wastes. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through the Synth Flux, Edrik returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Naira Corvelle to find his mother. Vantreil had advanced in his Veyari training as a Tirren under the tutelage of Oru-Kei Talvane, while Corvelle had become a Senator in the Galactic Senate.

When Vantreil and Corvelle visited the Odrin' moisture farm, they found T-8LX working on a moisture vaporator. He initially didn't recognize Vantreil until the latter finished his name for him when introducing himself. Greeting his Maker, T-8LX discovered that Vantreil had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Vilos Odrin, as well as his son, Halder Odrin and his girlfriend, Dramiax Dreimani. Learning of his mother's peril, Vantreil took Halder's speeder bike to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Dhural village. T-8LX was present when he arrived, and later attended a funeral for Revex on the farm's property. When K7-M4, who had been waiting aboard Corvelle's nearby yacht, interrupted the funeral with an urgent message for the Tirren, T-8LX translated the navimech's binary beeps and whistles. After viewing the holocomm message, Vantreil and Corvelle departed Sarrakesh for Kharrab to rescue Vantreil's Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane, taking Eightel with them.

### The Replica Wars

During the Replica Wars, T-8LX would serve Senator Naira Corvelle, the spouse of Edrik Vantreil, and was given a shiny gold-colored plating to better suit his new role. On one occasion, Corvelle was captured by Gorvo's uncle Rokra during a mission to reopen communications with the Zoggs after Gorvo's son Geiskar was kidnapped by the Secessionist leader Count Varnoth. Corvelle contacted T-8LX, who in turn contacted the Velaris Guard, which promptly raided Rokra's Palace and freed Corvelle. Meanwhile, Vantreil and his Tirren Ilyra Seshu managed to rescue Geiskar and return him safely to his father. T-8LX and his comrades' actions help the Concord win over Gorvo, who later signed a favorable treaty with them.

Following the Geiskar affair, T-8LX accompanied Senator Corvelle on a mission to contact the Gonojei Banking Clan's executive, who purportedly wanted to sue for peace. Their planned rendezvous was a point of space near the Dretron Shoals Medical Center. Unknown to the two of them, this was a trap concocted by Xarn Nyxarion and his apprentice Count Varnoth. The Secessionist military commander General Kharzul's flagship Malevolence had been damaged during a recent space battle and was unable to enter slipspace. The Morkai and Secessionists hoped to use Senator Corvelle as a hostage to extract leverage from the Concord commander Vantreil, who was pursuing the Malevolence.

### Rise of the Hegemony

Following the Battle of Velaris, T-8LX joined the Veyari Master Joran Ashkar and the Galactic Senate's Loyalist Committee in greeting K7-M4 and his Veyari companions Edrik Vantreil and Oru-Kei Talvane following their successful mission to rescue Supreme Chancellor Malverin, who had been kidnapped by the Secessionist General Kharzul. T-8LX would continue serving Senator Corvelle during the last days of the Galactic Concord and the Great Veyari Purge, which ushered in the Galactic Hegemony. He also accompanied his master to Pyrrhax and piloted the skiff, allowing some reprieve for Corvelle, who was distraught at her husband's turn to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux and his role in the murder of Veyari younglings at the Veyari Temple on Velaris.

Unknown to him and Corvelle, Vantreil's former Master Oru-Kei Talvane had stowed aboard the skiff. On Pyrrhax, Talvane's presence enraged Vantreil, who Synth Flux choked his wife. While Talvane dueled his former Tirren, T-8LX and K7-M4 attended to the ailing Senator and brought her back aboard the skiff. After Talvane defeated Vantreil, he and T-8LX piloted the skiff to Ostra Dreya, a remote Far Verge world, where she gave birth to twins, a boy named Corin Vantreil and a daughter named Mirae Tessaly, before passing away. While the Vantreil twins were spirited away to different locations to hide them from the Hegemony, T-8LX and K7-M4 passed into the ownership of Naira's friend Dorn Tessaly, the Senator for Belisar. Due to Eightel's talkative nature, Senator Tessaly made arrangements for the protocol mechanoid to receive a memory wipe in order to keep Mirae's true parentage a secret.

### Age of the Hegemony

Around 19 BVC, T-8LX stayed with Tessaly as the Hegemonic Senate voted on the Hegemonic Rebirth Act. Asked to stay by his side by Tessaly, who saw it as a way to honor Corvelle, T-8LX believed watching the votes unfold with Tessaly as an enormous privilege and thanked the Senator for the honor, although Tessaly by that point was too focused on the countdown to listen. In the end, the act would pass, but it saw major vetoes from the Hegemon shortly thereafter, curtailing its effectiveness and forcing Tessaly and Sel Aldren to look for other ways to oppose Malverin.

A year after the Replica Wars, T-8LX served aboard Senator Tessaly's CR90 corvette Zhavian IV, which was captained by Captain Tuzei Thoneskias. When K7-M4 returned from a solo mission to rendezvous with the former Veyari Tirren Ilyra Seshu and bring her to Senator Tessaly, T-8LX berated his navimech companion. Following the evacuation of Vrekrex, Ilyra Seshu subsequently joined Senator Tessaly's insurgent movement and became the spymaster known as "Fulcrum." Nine years later, during Hathox Tessaly's party for her sister's visit, T-8LX translated for the other guests.

### Galactic Civil War

During the Galactic Civil War, T-8LX was with K7-M4 at the Insurgent Pact's secret base on Orvax 4 at the time the Pact Fleet prepared to leave for the Battle of Quubem to help reinforce Stray One who had traveled to Quubem to retrieve the plans for the Galactic Hegemony's Void Core, a new superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. T-8LX remarked how nobody told him anything.

The Pact succeeded in stealing the plans for the superweapon. T-8LX and K7-M4 were later aboard Senator Mirae Tessaly's blockade runner, the Zhavian IV, which was being pursued by Xarn Velgor's Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnought, Devastator. Unbeknownst to T-8LX, Xarn Velgor was actually his former master, Edrik Vantreil. The Zhavian IV was crippled by a laser blast from Velgor's ship, and Hegemonic shocktroopers boarded the vessel. After Princess Mirae managed to insert these secret plans and a holographic message for Master Talvane into K7-M4, the two mechanoids fled in an escape pod to the desert planet of Sarrakesh.

### New Concord

In the morning after the Battle of Thalwen, T-8LX was present at Bright Tree Village when Dax Varro and the Pathfinders traveled to the far side of Thalwen to attack a Hegemonic outpost. Later, Eightel accompanied Dax and Grawlokk on a raid on a Hegemonic Security Bureau black site at the Wretch of Dumor. After the Pathfinders secured the base, T-8LX accessed the Hegemonic data files and uncovered Operation: Cinder, a posthumous order by the Hegemon to devastate various worlds following his demise.

T-8LX continued serving Mirae Tessaly following the Battle of Thalwen, and at some point, had his silver lower right leg replaced with a more gold one, becoming fully golden once more. The Insurgent Pact was transformed into the New Concord, which became the dominant galactic government following the Battle of Leinesk and the Galactic Concordance. The Galactic Hegemony disintegrated with the remnants of the Hegemony retreating into the Unknown Regions, where they established the Prime Mandate. During the first years of the New Concord's rule, 8LX continued serving Mirae, who was appointed head of the Senate Defense Council.

### Defiance

T-8LX found himself in the service of the Defiance, a military force founded by Mirae Tessaly to combat the Prime Mandate. At some point, Defiance leader Admiral Sihen was captured by the Prime Mandate. In an attempt to rescue Sihen, the Defiance sent a team led by Captain Driaskia. The team captured a Prime Mandate protocol mechanoid named O-MR1, referred to as Vezur, who was believed to know where Sihen was. However, the ship carrying the team crashed on Kriadron before it could return to the Defiance base. The crash killed all organic beings on board, leaving only Eightel, Vezur, the construction mechanoid CO-34, the security mechanoids QD-99 and VZ-44, and the medical mechanoid 2MED2.

Shortly after the crash, the mechanoids picked up a homing beacon signal from what they presumed was a crashed Prime Mandate vessel. Despite crashing several cycles ago and that it was 87 kilometers away, the group of mechanoids decided their best chance of survival was to follow it. As they began their trek, Vezur remarked that it was interesting how their programming made them think so humanly, and also willing to serve the side they were on. He went on to observe that any one of them could have been serving the opposite side due to a mechanoid's frequent memory wipes. While on their way to the vessel, they were attacked by spice spiders. Following his primary programming, QD-99 fought off the spiders and in order to give the others time to escape, he drew the spiders toward him, destroying himself in the process, but allowing the others to escape and continue their mission.

### Aboard the Kestrel

Following the Battle of Jozeis and having escaped from the Prime Mandate aboard the Kestrel, T-8LX found K7-M4 and DD-5 talking in a hallway. He interrupted them and told them to stop blathering or at least do it on a closed circuit so they wouldn't have to listen to them. He commented about the heavy losses of the Battle of Gehasox Base and told the two mechanoids he was involved in a very important project and they were making it extremely difficult for him to concentrate. As the two mechanoids departed, T-8LX told Kessa, who was reading one of the Veyari Order's sacred texts, that he had dealt with the mechanoids and they wouldn't bother them any further before asking her where they had gone.

Kessa asked T-8LX to translate a paragraph in one of the books. Finding the translation initially a bit difficult due to the fact the language used was unusual, Eightel took the book and translated the text, leading Kessa to comment that she liked the passage and that it was a lovely place to begin. Later, upon receiving a transmission from Black Squadron from one of their relay stations, T-8LX went to report the transmission to Nekra and Brannoc, to which Nekra explained to Brannoc that they just needed to have a little faith. However, due to Nekra's excitement over the news, T-8LX reported that Nekra needed to listen to the message as he wouldn't call it good news.

### Time on Rokroth and mission to Sel Dradan

Eightel was present at the Defiance Camp on the garbage planet of Rokroth when Mirae Tessaly decided to go and seek aid from the inhabitants of Sel Dradan. T-8LX later travelled to Sel Dradan along with Rose, Kessa, and Grawlokk to convince its people to support aid for the Defiance. When they arrived, however, they were confronted by Nivrox Gorix and others. They were soon greeted by Sihen's son Natha. While Mirae went to speak to Sel Dradan's king, T-8LX remained behind at the Kestrel when he and the others were attacked by representives. He was later captured and placed in a pod and was brought before the king.

### Defeating the Prime Mandate

T-8LX and the Defiance eventually established themselves on the jungle world of Devrosk Hoshor. After learning of the return of Xarn Nyxarion and that he was based on the planet Vozix, which K7 and him stated was not on any star map, T-8LX travelled with Kessa, Brannoc, Grawlokk, and Arlo Nekra to Bathei on their mission to find a Morkai wayfinder, where they encountered T-8LX and Grawlokk's old friend Berrin Maldonne. During his time on Giazeru the group recovered the Blade of Dide of Gedreis, a Morkai dagger. While T-8LX was able to translate the writings on the Morkai dagger, his programming prevented him from being able to reveal the translation to anyone.

The group travelled to Quodax, where the droidsmith Babu Rubru was able to determine how to bypass T-8LX's programming restrictions, but said doing so would cause T-8LX to undergo a complete memory wipe. T-8LX willingly allowed Rubru to perform the procedure, believing that this would result in the permanent loss of his memories as he didn't think K7-M4 had a backup of his memory. T-8LX's photoreceptors turned red while he translated the Morkai writings on the dagger. After completing the translation T-8LX rebooted, and his memory was wiped in the process, leaving him to restart with his basic factory programming.

### Reflecting on the past

At some point, T-8LX was able to recover at least some of the memories he lost in 19 BVC, including how he had been built by Edrik Vantreil, the story around the Invasion of Ilveren, and how he met K7-M4 in 32 BVC. One day, T-8LX introduced himself, K7-M4, and DD-5 to another party, explaining in the process they had gone through many adventures and remarking that their missions had impacted the fate of the galaxy on several occasions. Agreeing with K7-M4 that their adventures were rarely easy, T-8LX was asked by DD-5 how he had met K7-M4 in the first place, to which T-8LX began to explain the story around the Ilveren Crisis, which he promised would be fascinating.

Beginning the story by explaining the Concord Era and how the greedy Merchant Combine had launched a blockade against the world of Ilveren, T-8LX introduced DD-5 to Sael-Rhal Ormis and Oru-Kei Talvane, explaining how they represented the peace and just-upholding Veyari Order, whereas he had much less kind things to say about the Federation. Continuing the story and revealing the role Xarn Nyxarion had played in the blockade and how he ordered the deaths of both Veyari, something that scared DD-5, T-8LX questioned how a mechanoid could be programmed for murder as he relayed the deployment of Federation battle mechanoids against the Veyari, then assured DD-5 that the protocol mechanoid FQ-14 was unaffected by the dioxis gas Krushias Nipath pumped into the room where Ormis and Talvane were kept waiting. As he relayed the Veyari duo's fight towards the vessel's control bridge, K7-M4 spoke up, expressing how much he wanted to own an arcblade, something T-8LX admitted he was happy had not come to pass.
