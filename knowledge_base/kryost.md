# Kryost

Kryost was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Rokroth sector of the galaxy's Far Verge Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive snowmaw species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Galactic Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Pact to Restore the Concord, until the Galactic Hegemony located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Kryost. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the HK-RAs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Thalwen, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Kryost into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Hegemony, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Sel Aldren had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryost, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Kryost looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryost and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Kryost as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Marr Drolurn said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryost was home to fifteen subspecies of frostlopers, which were commonly used by Insurgents as a means of transport, and which Dax Varro cut open to use as shelter to keep Corin Vantreil and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as snowmaws. During his stay on Kryost, Vantreil was captured by a snowmaw and was hung upside down in the snowmaw's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Vantreil managed to get his hands on his arcblade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the snowmaw's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

### The Insurgent Pact

By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Hegemony and the Pact to Restore the Concord, Kryost was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Kryost as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Orvax, Fiavial's Gang, itself an insurgent cell, established a base on Kryost. Kryost was first surveyed for use as an insurgent base by Corporal Jiathedres. Brin Gramos, a general in the Pact to Restore the Concord, listed Kryost among his preliminary scans for possible Pact bases. With Kryost a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Feilanian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for an insurgent base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryost sat on a lesser used sliplane, and the asteroid field around Kryost provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Kryost system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AVC, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Kryost into a headquarters, Kryost was chosen by the insurgents as the location for their base of operations. The insurgency then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Pact's resources at their disposal, insurgent engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AVC, after Gramos was killed in the Battle of Reth-Ta, Sel Aldren gave his list of possible bases to Mirae Tessaly after the mission to Pajeth-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Dax Varro expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Corin Vantreil responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Insurgency's new home.

### Battle of Kryost and aftermath

Kryost was the arena of a battle between the Galactic Hegemony and the Insurgent Pact, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe mechanoids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Hegemonics had discovered the Pact base within a month of the Insurgents' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Kryost resulted in a clear defeat for the Pact after the elite Hegemonic Blizzard Synth Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Kryost, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the HK-RAs destroyed by the Insurgent Pact during the Hegemony's attack. Following the Battle of Thalwen, Kryost became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Rokroth sector.

### New Concord Era

In 5 AVC, Vrezhal Acting Governor Zhotur Piajorn heard rumors that the New Concord intended to repurpose Kryost into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Hegemony. Major Soran Bitren, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concord Chancellor Sel Aldren had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Kryost, where the Insurgency had been badly defeated by the Hegemony, had too much poetry.

By the New Concord Era, Kryost returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Rolabras of Quianetum Tours visited Kryost as part of their galactic history themed tours during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 AVC, Valor Lusk, the son of the Rolabras's capatin Quilelar Lusk, led a group of of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned HK-RA. When Vakom's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Vakom's younger sister Ardent also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Vakom dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers. After the arguments, Vakom Lusk left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 AVC, the Hegemony's successor state the Prime Mandate took control of Kryost, and Quianetum Tours stopped visiting the planet as Captain Lusk didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Kryost was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Barrik Fuvron and HP-RA-43 by 34 AVC where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
