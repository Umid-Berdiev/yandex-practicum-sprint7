# Grawlokk

Grawlokk, known affectionately to his friends as Grawl, was a Varruk male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Replica Wars, the Galactic Civil War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Prime Mandate-Defiance War. He hailed from the planet Ruwokka where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Varruk military leader. During the Replica Wars, he was captured by Skarrim slavers and held captive on Krebom, but he worked with a fellow captive, Veyari Commander Ilyra Seshu, to escape. He later commanded Varruk forces of the Jiazesheith Varruk Militia during the Battle of Ruwokka alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Veyari General Olvek. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Olvek's replica troopers received Directive 91 from the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion and, with the help of Grawlokk and his fellow Varruk Chieftain and General Hokir, Olvek escaped Ruwokka and the destruction of the Veyari Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Galactic Hegemony, Grawlokk was enslaved by the Hegemony until he was rescued by Corporal Dax Varro, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Meridian Kestrel during a raid on Queimeisk for Crimson Dawn with Kriaze Shiler and Triagreith'ra. Winning the Kestrel from Leiskubol Vodadar Maldonne, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Gorvo the Zogg until Varro was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Hegemony, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Grawlokk and Varro were hired by Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane to ferry him, Corin Vantreil, T-8LX, and K7-M4 to Belisar on a mission vital to the survival of the Pact to Restore the Concord. After finding Belisar was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Mirae Tessaly escape from captivity and return to the Insurgent base on Orvax 4. Though Grawlokk and Varro initially chose to leave Orvax 4 and not join the Insurgency, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Orvax and were instrumental in Vantreil's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Insurgency.

Throughout the war, Grawlokk and Varro fought the Hegemony in a number of engagements, including an assault on Fiazhal 1 and the battle on Sheheix Vas. Along with Princess Tessaly and T-8LX, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Kryost after the Hegemony tracked the Insurgent Pact there. The group sought refuge with Maldonne on Sky Haven, but Maldonne betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Hegemon's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Varro was frozen in ferrolite and Maldonne helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Varro from being taken by Teko Drayk to Gorvo the Zogg. Grawlokk and Maldonne set off to find Varro and located him in Gorvo's Palace on Sarrakesh, where Grawlokk and his friends saved Varro and destroyed Gorvo. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Thalwen, where Grawlokk helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Pact Fleet to destroy the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Nyxarion was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Hegemony.

After Thalwen and the rise of the New Concord, Grawlokk and Varro fought to liberate Ruwokka from the Hegemony. When the war finally came to a close, Grawlokk returned home to Ruwokka with his family. After Varro's son Tov, turned to the umbral tide and destroyed Vantreil's new generation of Veyari, Grawlokk reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Meridian Kestrel and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Zulel, but they eventually found the Kestrel and, with it, stowaways named Kessa, Brannoc, and the mechanoid DD-5. The mechanoid had part of a map that led to the exiled Corin Vantreil, so the group brought the map to the Defiance that opposed the Prime Mandate, which had risen from the ashes of the Galactic Hegemony. Grawlokk soon fought in the assault to destroy Gehasox Base, a devastating Prime Mandate superweapon, a conflict in which Varro lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Grawlokk joined Kessa in traveling to the planet Krelath-To on the Kestrel, where they found Corin Vantreil. After Varro's death, Grawlokk often served as the sole pilot of the Kestrel, including piloting the ship to aid the Defiance during the Battle of Jozeis.

Roughly a year after the battle of Jozeis, Grawlokk was one of the last of the Defiance. Having learned of the mysterious return of Xarn Nyxarion, Grawlokk and the crew of the Kestrel, now including Arlo Nekra, traveled to the planets Bathei and Quodax to find a clue to how to find where the Hegemon was hiding, being reunited with his friend Maldonne in the process. The quest nearly ended in Grawlokk's apparent death, as Kessa, having been revealed to be the Hegemon's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Zerek Vash and destroyed a Prime Mandate transport that she had thought Grawlokk had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Grawlokk survived and was rescued by the Defiance as they continued the quest. Kessa eventually found Vozix during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Morkai, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Kessa finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Tov Varro in the climax of the battle, while the Defiance, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Morkai Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, that Nyxarion planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Morkai once and for all, the Defiance reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Mirae, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

### Early life

A Varruk male, Grawlokk, nicknamed "Grawl," hailed from Ruwokka, a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Vreiquoburn, one hundred and eighty years before the Replica Wars to Kokraquask and a female Varruk. during the High Concord Era. He was married to a female named Miapedar, and had a family to support, including a son named Mabrobrul. During his life, he explored Ruwokka, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

### The Replica Wars

At some point during the Replica Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Galactic Concord and the Secessionists, Grawlokk famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Ruwokka and the Concord, he served as a combat engineer while other Varruk soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BVC, he was captured by a Skarrim hunting guild led by Fapu; taken to Island Four on Krebom to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Veyari apprentices—the Tirren Ilyra Seshu and the younglings Jinx and O-Mer, also prey of the Skarrims.

Grawlokk escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Seshu's understanding of Pahodisk, to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Seshu, Grawlokk joined in their endeavor and raided the Skarrim base, only to be joined by a Varruk relief force led by his old friend General Hokir—who had received Grawlokk's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Sugi and Bibro. Once all the Skarrims had been killed, Grawlokk and the Varruks accompanied the young Veyari to the Veyari Temple on Velaris, where they parted ways.

### Rise of the Hegemony

Following the reorganization of the Concord into the Galactic Hegemony, Grawlokk saw his people sent to work as slaves, or as miners in places such as Queimeisk, or made to serve as science experiments. A year into the Hegemony's reign, former Tirren Ilyra Seshu, the very same Synth Flux-sensitive Grawlokk had met on Krebom, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Ruwokka, starting to lose her hope that he remained free.

At some point, while on the run from Hegemonic forces, Grawlokk and several other Varruks were captured and enslaved by several Skarrim slavers. Like them, he was forced into slavery, working to cut the wroshyr trees in order to feed the Hegemonic Army. Grawlokk temporarily escaped but was turned over to the Hegemony by a back-stabbing and greedy Shushupur monkey-lizard bounty hunter. Whilst aboard a prison train on Nikrorex, he had the opportunity to escape captivity, but sacrificed his own freedom to save a fellow prisoner. He bounced around at other Hegemonic facilities before eventually being sent to Skushiarn. Whilst on Skushiarn, he came to be referred to as "the Beast." Grawlokk also learned that the Hegemonics were trying to sell him off as a pit fighter.

### Galactic Civil War

During one of their smuggling attempts at the time of the Galactic Civil War, the Meridian Kestrel was boarded and the two lost the cargo that they were hauling for their usual employer, Gorvo the Zogg. Arriving at Kar Deli on Sarrakesh to speak with Gorvo about the lost cargo, they sat down in Sejesk's Spaceport Cantina. At the bar, Grawlokk was approached by Tov Talvane, who was searching for passage for himself, Corin Vantreil and the mechanoids K7-M4 and T-8LX to Belisar. Promised 2,000 credits in advance and an additional 15,000 upon arrival to Belisar, Varro and Grawlokk agreed to the commission.

In Docking Bay 94, Varro and Grawlokk found Gorvo waiting for them with a crew of hired help. One of them, the famous bounty hunter Teko Drayk, kept an eye on Grawlokk, noting the Varruk getting ready for a fight as Varro spoke with Gorvo. Thus, he moved his arm enough to ensure the Varruk braids he wore were visible to Grawlokk, which the Varruk decided to show no reaction to. Ultimately, Varro managed to convince Gorvo to give him more time. After a brief firefight with shocktroopers, the Kestrel left Sarrakesh with its new passengers, escaping from Hegemonic vessels before entering slipspace.

### New Concord

Now agents of the New Concord, just as Tobin Thoneskias set out on a reconnaissance mission that took him to Gotreith, Grawlokk and Varro went on their own mission to find the Hegemony's supply lines, during which they visited several planets, including Ord Pobirn, Leisho Prime, Vriazhei-bin and finally Redriask. However, Grawlokk was forced to remain on the Kestrel, while Varro investigated on his own; having grown bored, he got to messing around with the ship's navigation system, trying to chase down a glitch that had been screwing up the slipspace drive. Though he fixed it, the guns stopped working, which they only discovered when they were ambushed by a trio of Jokrux marauder-ships.

However, as they reached Redriask, Drasax contacted the duo and informed them that they had a window of opportunity—Ruwokka, still in the Hegemony's thrall, would have its blockade temporarily lifted for a few days, before its ships were replaced. Seeing Grawlokk's anxiety, Varro told Drasax to gather any underworld scoundrel who owed him a favor, anyone who hated the Hegemony, and others in the false promise that they would obtain full pardons from the Concord, whereas Grawlokk was tasked to contact the Varruk mercenaries, among them Thitarn, Hebre, and Zhovosho.

### Cold war

In 31 AVC the Meridian Kestrel was stolen from Dax and Grawlokk and they were using a Baleen-class heavy freighter, the Zulel, for their smuggling operations. They eventually recovered the Kestrel using their freighter's tractor beam after it was found floating in space not far from the planet Leinesk. Upon boarding their old vessel, Dax and Grawlokk went about inspecting their ship and found the Leinesk scavenger Kessa and the defecting shocktrooper Brannoc hiding, whom Dax assumed were the thieves that had stolen the Kestrel. When Kessa and Brannoc explained that they were going to take a mechanoid named DD-5 to the Defiance as he had a map that would reveal Corin's location, Dax and Grawlokk agreed to help them.

Unfortunately, their freighter was boarded by two notorious criminal factions, the Taki Death Gang and Kabitho, who had hired Varro on previous jobs, which he had failed to complete. Not wanting Kessa and Brannoc to be involved, Dax placed both of them in the lower corridors of the freighter while he and Grawlokk went to meet with the criminals, with DD-5 accompanying them. The smuggler tried but failed to convince both parties that he would fix things eventually, with the Taki Death Gang negotiator, Zobrask-Trifein, recognizing the ZV-series navimech mechanoid from a Prime Mandate bounty placed on it. They demanded that Dax hand over the mechanoid and any stowaways he might have on board. As the scuffle continued, Kessa accidentally released three rathtars. Dax, Grawlokk, Brannoc, Kessa, and DD-5 managed to escape the gangs and jumped into slipspace in the Kestrel, though Grawlokk was injured from a pulser shot during the escape.

### Adventures after the Battle of Jozeis

During the Defiance's time on Rokroth, Grawlokk piloted the Meridian Kestrel to save Kessa after she provoked an attack by a Rokrothian pit beast. He then piloted the Kestrel to go to Sel Dradan and get help from its inhabitants.

### Prime Mandate-Defiance War

Traveling aboard the Meridian Kestrel to the Herath Glacier Colony, Brannoc and Arlo Nekra engaged Grawlokk in an intense game of holochess. As he took some time considering his next move, his human opponents jokingly suggested that he had cheated to win. The implication infuriated Grawlokk, forcing Brannoc and Arlo to calm him. Their game was interrupted by an alarm from the cockpit, and Grawlokk ordered his opponents not to turn off the game as he left to check it, but Brannoc and Arlo agreed that he was cheating and shut off the holochess board.

The Kestrel arrived at the Herath Glacier Colony, and Brannoc opened the docking hatch to meet a Defiance informant, Meigo, who informed them about a spy in the Prime Mandate and gave them a message in the form of a data encoder, ordering them to give it to General Tessaly. As Prime Mandate VEX fighters jumped into the system, the Defiance members downloaded the message into K7-M4 and made their escape. Arlo damaged the Kestrel in the escape, angering Grawlokk, and further infuriated him performing several slipspeed skips to shake off their pursuers.

## Personality and traits

Known for his great strength, bravery, and loyalty, Grawlokk often served as Varro's conscience in their smuggling endeavors, which made people trust him more than they did Varro. However, he was also known for his stubbornness and dislike of losing. He would also show no qualms about using violence as a means to achieve his goals, but he certainly never killed without a good reason.

As a Varruk, Grawlokk knew passion and thus, the Insurgency, and would have stood with them from the beginning, if not for Varro and the Kestrel; he would not abandon them as he was bound to both, as they were to him. Few people knew that he had a mushy heart under his warrior exterior. He was a kind individual and was good at reading a room. Grawlokk enjoyed tinkering with mechanics. Sometimes, when he was bored, he would "mess around" with the Kestrel's navigation system. Like Dax Varro, he regarded the ship as the epitome of classical design, ignoring the vast majority of observers who called it "junk."

## Skills and abilities

Besides the characteristic strength of his species, Grawlokk possessed exceptional skills in piloting as well as mechanic repairs, from his experience of piloting the Meridian Kestrel, of which he had a great understanding of the temperamental nature of its systems. Grawlokk was notably faster at operating the Kestrel's controls, a fact he kept hidden from Dax to prevent hurting the smuggler's ego. Although not particularly meticulous, Grawlokk's technical skill allowed him to understand the inner workings of a great number of devices, including mechanoids and transmitters, being able to build one out of a wrecked starship.

Like many of his people, Grawlokk preferred their native quarrelgun as his personal weapon. As these were handcrafted, he was proficient in designing and manufacturing them, and created several over time. The latest known quarrelgun that he crafted was during the Hegemonic era. It was an unconventional design, as he had used the frame and power pack of a shocktrooper pulser. He is also skilled in using other pulser range weapons like a QR-21 pump-action scatter pulser and a sniper rifle.
