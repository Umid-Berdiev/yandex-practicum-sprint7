# VC-1 Orbital Battle Station

The VC-1 Orbital Battle Station, also designated as the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Shaneti, was a moon-sized, deep-space mobile battle station constructed by the Galactic Hegemony. It had been designed to fire a single planet-destroying nova lance focused by massive vethra crystals which would destroy any planet targeted. It was the pet project of Hegemon Malverin, Director Neven Rograth Kafe, and its eventual commander Grand Vizor Hotrern Helvar to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Helvar Doctrine.

Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Morkai. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Replica Wars, the Shoquosus, aligned with Count Varnoth and the League of Independent Systems, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the League, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Hegemony. It was built in orbit of Kharrab and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Kharrab to Quubem in its final stages of construction.

The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Hegemonic Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the Pact to Restore the Concord at their secret base on Orvax 4. Though the Pact was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Insurgent spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Insurgents to begin an investigation. Division emerged within Pact leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Skuquem Jogros and Teodric Kriasi led a mission, which the Insurgent Pact soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Jogros had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.

Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Hegemonic Center of Military Research on Quubem, Jogros was able to transmit them to the Insurgent fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Mirae Tessaly of Belisar aboard the Zhavian IV. Mirae then fled through slipspace toward Sarrakesh, where she was apprehended by Xarn Velgor, but not before having hidden the plans in her navimech K7-M4. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her mechanoid was able to escape and bring the plans to Oru-Kei Talvane, a former Veyari Knight living in hiding on Sarrakesh.

In an attempt to coerce Mirae, now held captive on the battle station itself, into revealing the location of the Insurgent Pact's secret base, Governor Helvar ordered the first full test of the Void Core, destroying the Princess' home planet of Belisar. Shortly thereafter Mirae managed to escape captivity on the Void Core with the help of Oru-Kei Talvane and Corin Vantreil, who had been brought to Belisar by pilots Dax Varro and Grawlokk in an effort to reunite the recovered Void Core plans to Mirae's father and Insurgent Pact leader, Dorn Tessaly.

Mirae brought the plans to the Pact leadership on Orvax 4, who quickly analyzed the data and discovered the design flaw deliberately left in the battle station by Hegemonic engineer Aldric Jogros, which would allow a single proton torpedo, fired into the thermal exhaust port, to destroy the entire station. The Pact launched a small assault against the Void Core, which had followed the escaping insurgents to the Orvax system. The original Void Core was destroyed moments before opening fire on the moon by a shot fired by Insurgent pilot Corin Vantreil with the aid of the Synth Flux. While Xarn Velgor escaped, due to having joined the dogfight above the station in his VEX fighter, many high-ranking Hegemonic officers perished aboard the space station, including Hotrern Helvar, Greiniar Reskar, and Noka Gezarn.

The destruction of the Void Core weakened the Hegemonic Military, and Insurgent assaults on Hegemonic installations prompted the construction of a second Void Core, both as a sign of defiance and technological terror. Despite this, it, like its predecessor, was destroyed with all hands lost. Decades later, the Hegemony's successor state, the Prime Mandate, constructed Gehasox Base, a much larger planet-destroying superweapon considered to be an evolution of the Void Core project of the Old Hegemony.

### Overall design

The station was spheroid, and measured 160 km (100 miles) in diameter with 357 internal levels and a surface area of over 45,000 square kilometers. A large, concave dish in the northern hemisphere made up the nova lance emitter. The station's command bridge, the Overbridge, was located above the dish of the station's nova lance, within the Northern Polar Command Sector. The war room featured designated posts for Helvar and other officers, a Nitiarn booth for communicating with the Hegemon, and large banks of viewscreens. Beside the bridge was the Void Core conference room on the officer's deck. The room was inbuilt with a circular table and twelve seats for high-ranking Hegemonic officers. The Hegemon's tower and its throne room also fell within the Command Sector.

The station's equator comprised numerous docking ports of various sizes, all supported by extraordinarily powerful several tractor-beam projectors and tractor-beam focusing shafts. The station's equator housed docking and hangar bays, tractor-beam generators, projectors, and emitter towers, turbopulser emplacements, and mooring platforms for ships of the fleet, including Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnoughts. "Tulip" style Hegemonic work stations were located throughout the station in different rooms.

### Armaments and defensive systems

The most prominent weapon emplacement on the Void Core was its nova lance—a weapon powered by a hypermatter annihilation reactor and focused through giant vethra crystals with sufficient firepower to destroy an entire planet. The hypermatter reactor at the center of the station fed energy into the various components of the nova lance, including the primary power amplifier, the nova lance power cell, the firing field amplifier, and the induction hyperphase generators. When firing, the energy was redirected into eight tributary beam shafts located around the perimeter of the nova lance, producing lasers that were converged using focusing coils, forming one large beam powerful enough to destroy a planet. If the firing-chamber arrays were not precisely aligned, the crystals would burn out and overload, sending dangerous levels of waste heat back into the Void Core's main reactor.

In addition to the Void Core's nova lance, its surface was peppered with 15,000 turbopulser batteries along with 768 tractor beam emplacements. As the station's defenses, these were installed in order to repel a large-scale fleet attack, rather than fend off individual starfighters, which the Hegemony viewed as no real threat. Shield projectors and communications arrays were distributed across the armored surface, analogous to colonies. A network of security stations were situated around the entire circumference and were equipped with holographic maps of the local region of space.

### Complement

For space-based offense and defense, the battle station contained numerous VEX fighters, which frequently patrolled the space surrounding the station. Due to the Void Core's enormous size, it had a heavy gravitational pull, which forced VEX fighter pilots to adjust their ships' thrusters for the kind of takeoff more common within a planetary atmosphere than on a space station. VEX staging areas had twenty VEX craft ready at any time for immediate takeoff.

The battle station housed 342,953 members of the Hegemonic Army and Navy, 25,984 shocktroopers, and nearly 2 million personnel of varying combat eligibility. Furthermore, although there were communal barracks, there also were enough private bunks that most people could expect to receive one within three to six months after arrival. While enlisted personnel used walkways or turbolifts that could move both vertically and horizontally, officers had access to a high-speed, officer-only shuttle system that orbited the station. Massive housing blocks for enlisted personnel featured a large atrium for off-duty personnel to walk in. Officers could expect their own exclusive accommodations. Life-support modules inhabited by workers during the original construction of the Void Core could still be used in an emergency. The station featured several hospital wings. Several color-coded life-support modules existed in the station's lower levels: gray for workers, red for overseers.

### Power

The station was powered by a cavernous hypermatter reactor encased in radiation insulator plating located at the center of the station, the destruction of which had the potential to be catastrophic to the station. Sukusk and dolovite were essential for shielding the hypermatter reactor core, focusing dish, and nova lance, while kalkite was used to coat the reactor lenses. The reactor was connected to various components in the station, such as the nova lance, the power diversion solenoid, and the station's enormous power cells. The Void Core's tractor beam generator was also coupled to the main reactor through seven power generator terminals located throughout the station. The power of the Void Core's nova lance could be controlled depending on how many reactor ignitions were used to power it as it shot fuel. For example, a single reactor ignition was capable of destroying a large area of a world's surface instead of destroying the body entirely.

### True purpose

For all the power the Void Core held, Xarn Nyxarion, the Hegemon of the Galactic Hegemony, had a different view of the battle station, as he planned for it to serve as a constant reminder and tangible symbol of the umbral tide of the Synth Flux's power. With the Hegemon freed from needing to portray this, he and his Morkai apprentice, Xarn Velgor, would have devoted themselves to what Nyxarion dubbed "more pressing matters" which had little to do with his Hegemony and that only the two of them could investigate. Ultimately, Nyxarion wanted to have the power to reshape the universe to his design, a desire beyond mere immortality.

### Origins

The idea of a massive planet-killing battle station dated back millennia, including to the old Morkai Hegemony, when tyrants were enjoying the idea of such a superweapon. The project that would be the VC-1 originated on Kharrab, and plans for what the Shoquosus dubbed as the Ultimate Weapon project were given to Count Varnoth, publicly the leader of the League of Independent Systems and secretly the Morkai Lord Xarn Relel, by Archduke Shapan the Lesser for safekeeping during the First Battle of Kharrab, in the waning decades of the Galactic Concord. Shortly after the battle, Varnoth presented his Morkai Master, Dark Lord of the Morkai Xarn Nyxarion, and publicly Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Concord Deito Malverin, with the plans. During the war, Veyari General Shalax Treimer and her navimech mechanoid, HR-KV, came close to discovering the plans to the Void Core when the two infiltrated the Secret Research Facility on an unmapped moon. However, the assassin Mebril Hokrijoth engaged in an arcblade duel with Treimer and stopped her.

The massive project was funded by a conglomerate of factions, including the Merchant Combine, Kashakreith Banking Clan, the Thobrein Union, and covertly, the Concord itself. During later Concord interrogation, Shoquosu leader Shapan the Lesser claimed that the schematics were merely presented to them by Varnoth and that the Shoquosus had simply refined them. Furthermore, he admitted that the Dudakre hive hadn't had time to design the station's main weapon before the Battle of Kharrab interrupted development. Its design, centered on a nova lance array using massive vethra crystals, was inspired by technology developed by the ancient Morkai.

### Phase One completed

Funding for the project would have been viewed as spurious before the Replica Wars. However, as the war dragged on, more and more research firms were dragged into government contracts focusing on a different aspect of the space station, such as in shielding or power supply, and all unaware of what their research was ultimately for. Researchers involved in defense projects were required to sign the Official Secrets Oath. Initial construction on the Void Core had been carried out by newly designed machines, some of which were controlled by sentient operators stationed in orbital command habitats. A vast array of ships provided the station with metals, organic materials, and supplies of water. Viable asteroids were towed and tractor-beamed to the station from both around the planet and from fields surrounding the Kharrab system. Once mined, ores were sent to foundries in synchronous orbit for the production of durasteel and other metals.

Cannibalized mechanoid factories built by Husesk Armor on Kharrab's surface allowed additional foundries to go online soon after the start of asteroid mining. In 21 BVC, after the Second Battle of Kharrab and before the first anniversary of the First Battle of Kharrab, the Strategic Advisory Cell announced the completion of phase one of the Void Core's construction. The supports for the station's superstructure at one hundred and sixty kilometers from pole to pole had been completed. The next phase of construction revolved around the fabrication of a temporary equator, along with a series of longitudinal bands to roughly form the sphere. As the bands were secured, construction of the hull commenced, along with the partitioning of individual interior sections. The cabin spaces were to be clad, sealed, and pressurized in order to permit the use of sentient laborers in addition to mechanoids.

### Shoquosu labor

In the wake of Shapan's announcement of the project to the Shoquosus, tens of thousands of drones had been transferred to Orbital Foundry 7, the second-largest structure in view from the command habitat. The drones oversaw the construction of enormous pie-slice-shaped concavities that, when assembled, would form the battle station's focusing dish and power well. By this time, three slices had been completed, and another six were in various stages of completion. The drone laborers were overseen by winged soldiers, loyal to castes overseen by Shapan, who had a lavish suite linked by a series of tubular connectors to the foundry.

The plan for assembling the station's dish called for assembling it in space and maneuvering it by tug and tractor beam into a gargantuan well that had been framed into the sphere's upper hemisphere—the dimple, as some called it. The parabolic dish was engineered to telescope away from the hull to facilitate aiming the early composite-beam proton nova lance design some of the Special Weapons Group scientists had proposed. Because design on the weapon had been incomplete by the time construction had started, most individuals involved in the project held the notion that function would have to follow form. Neven Kafe was placed in charge of the construction, assembly, and installation of the dish.

### Hegemonic Era

The nascent Hegemony immediately appropriated the Ultimate Weapon project. Access to Kharrab was restricted to an inner circle of Hegemonic scientists and engineers, and very few members of the Hegemonic hierarchy even knew about the project. A highly secure supply line with various false routes and outposts was developed to ensure further secrecy, and it was during that time that the battle station was given the official name of Void Core and given the hull designation of VC-1, though the early codename, Shaneti was still used in design files. Deinorn Kregofu, an architect and starship designer from Brudro IV, was enlisted to help build the megaweapon. As construction continued under the Hegemony, Malverin gave responsibility for the project to the Hegemonic Navy and charged Hotrern Helvar and Neven Kafe responsible for the battle station.

Despite construction moving ahead, not all Hegemonics believed in the viability of the project, with some voicing protest over what would later become known as "Helvar's folly," and others not knowing of the project at all. General Shiamiath Shale was one of many who protested its development, only to result in her input being marginalized over the coming years. Grand Admiral Fibali Drialex opined that the Void Core might be able to serve for five, ten or even fifty years. However, he believed that eventually someone would figure out a way to disable or destroy it. Likewise, General Queigol Vranu believed that having the Void Core as the Hegemony's sole weapon was a mistake.

### Test firings, losing Jogros

Hotrern Helvar believed that Kafe's appropriation of planets without the Hegemon's permission was a sign of his overreaching ambition, impulsiveness, and disdain for authority and the chain of command. Helvar, like Kafe, believed that he should ultimately be made responsible for the Void Core project. Consequently, Helvar planned to use Kafe as a convenient scapegoat for every construction delay and issue that was bound to plague the project, well aware that the Hegemon was eyeing them both for a promotion. To avoid accepting the privilege prematurely, Helvar continued to defer to Kafe until the proper time arose to oust him. Kafe also planned to keep Helvar occupied. In 18 BVC, Kafe orchestrated an insurgency within the Salient system, aware that Helvar, unable to retreat, would be caught in a weeks-long battle, lowering his favor with the Hegemon.

Early attempts at test-firing the Void Core's nova lance led to the destruction of an entire city on Paquirn. The first successful test firing occurred much later at an isolated black-hole binary known as the Hero Twins. Preliminary scans indicated that the energy released during the test-fire had the destructive fire of the combined batteries of a qaz-class Nova Dreadnought. The nova lance was fired by a vethra crystal-assisted twin laser array carefully assembled and calibrated on Kalax, and installed on a Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnought appropriated by the Special Weapons Group. Grand Vizier Mas Traga wished the test firing to occur closer to the Core, but Kafe urged caution, knowing that a misfire would most likely result in the destruction of the ship. As a result of the successful test firing, Kafe was promoted to full commander.

### Continued construction

The construction of the first Void Core continued under Kafe for several years above Kharrab in utmost secrecy, under Admiral Hotrern Helvar's supervision. The Hegemony utilized construction modules operated by Varruk slave laborers and various other species to complete the project. Several manufacturing facilities, such as those on Quujeisk, were commandeered by the Hegemony, with their workers pushed to the brink to manufacture components for the battle station. Most of the construction work on the station was done in micro-g, while omni-directional boosters supplied standard gravity to a large cabinspace near the surface of the station that would eventually become the Overbridge. The epicenter of a throng of construction mechanoids, the station was guarded by four Nova Dreadnoughts and twice as many frigates and hovered in a fixed orbit above the planet. The northern hemisphere focus lens frame for the nova lance was nothing more than a metallic crater five years after the station's appropriation by the Hegemony, and while some of the slipdrive components had been installed, the station was far from being jump-ready.
