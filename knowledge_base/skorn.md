# Skorn

Skorn was a Synth Flux-sensitive Jafiaresian Grojusk male who was apprenticed to the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion, and after his defeat by the Veyari Oru-Kei Talvane, he established himself as a crime lord during the Replica Wars and the Hegemonic Era. The renegade Morkai Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Veyari and his former Morkai Master.

Born in 54 BVC during the time of the Galactic Concord, Skorn was a son of Mother Traquan, who led the Heizhovras of Jafiares. Taken as a child by Xarn Nyxarion, Xarn Skorn was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Morkai, growing in hatred of the Veyari Knights who served the Concord and the radiant tide of the Synth Flux—while the Veyari Order had deemed the Morkai extinct for a thousand years, Skorn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Merchant Combine's Invasion of Ilveren in 32 BVC. Xarn Skorn announced the return of the Morkai on Sarrakesh, where he tried to kill the Veyari emissaries of the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. However, Skorn's failure to do so allowed Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis and his pupil, Tirren Oru-Kei Talvane, to bring Queen Corvelle of Ilveren and a boy strong in the Synth Flux—exceptionally so, to the extent that Ormis believed this "Edrik Vantreil" to be the prophesied Chosen One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Velaris, the heart of the Concord.

Nevertheless, Xarn Nyxarion used the resultant crisis in the Senate to elevate his public persona, Senator Malverin of Ilveren, to the position of Supreme Chancellor. The Queen and Veyari, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Heihiss on Ilveren to throw off the yoke of the Merchant Combine. Xarn Skorn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Federation leadership, was redirected to handle the Veyari. Skorn dealt a fatal blow to Sael-Rhal Ormis, but Talvane in turn defeated Skorn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Hezhorn Minor. Around the same time, Nyxarion took on Ormis's Veyari Master, Varnoth, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Varnoth set the second part of the Morkai plan into motion with the start of the Replica Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Traquan plotted against Varnoth and had one of her sons, Brakk Zuhe, seek out Skorn. Indeed, the defeated Grojusk was brought back to Jafiares and had his sanity and mobility restored by Traquan's magick. Skorn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Morkai, initially wanting to take revenge against Oru-Kei Talvane. After the brothers were defeated by the Veyari, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Kordath, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Concord and the Secessionists. Skorn seized control of the self-styled Death Watch and used them terrorise Kordath while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective—the Vosk Syndicate, Black Sun, and the Zogg Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Nyxarion, who killed Skorn's brother and captured Skorn.

Skorn was rescued by his Kordathian loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Xarn Nyxarion was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Jafiares with the use of the Secessionists led by Varnoth and General Kharzul. Mother Traquan herself was killed while protecting Skorn, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Kordath, Skorn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Edrik Vantreil—the Veyari Knight trained by Oru-Kei Talvane whom he correctly believed was central to Nyxarion' schemes—alongside Talvane himself to Kordath. Skorn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Concord forces intervening on the side of the Kordath resistance were instead led by Vantreil's old apprentice, Ilyra Seshu.

While Skorn and Seshu fought at the Siege of Kordath, Nyxarion successfully executed his plan to destroy the Veyari Order and anoint himself Galactic Hegemon. With the support of some Kordathian and Grojusk loyalists, Skorn continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Hegemonic regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Hegemon's Inquisitors, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Hegemony's later years, Skorn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Crimson Dawn syndicate through Kebria Drel and Triagreith'ra.

Skorn also sought a great weapon on Roreiler purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Veyari Jorem Rufem and Skagruth Hollin as well as Ilyra Seshu. However, they were all stopped by Xarn Velgor, the fallen Vantreil. Skorn escaped and continued to tempt Hollin towards the umbral tide in service of his own ends. Hollin rejected Skorn's overtures, but not before the former Morkai Lord used their connection to track his old nemesis Talvane back to Sarrakesh. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Skorn. Learning that Talvane had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Chosen One who would destroy the Morkai, Skorn died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.

### Early life

In 54 BVC, the boy who would be dubbed Xarn Skorn was born the son of Mother Traquan on Jafiares with mysterious origins. As a Jafiaresian Grojusk male, he was a Beidadrom and had two blood brothers, Feral and Brakk Zuhe. Later in his life, Skorn claimed that he once had a real name but that he had long since forgotten it. Before his first birthday, Traquan gave the Grojusk infants their first tattoos, although Skorn, in his unreliable memories, personally remembered living much of his early life without his tatoos. As a child, since his days living in deep caves, Skorn wrestled with a deep inner anger.

As immortalized in legend, Skorn grew up with one of his brothers in a house in the Beidadrom Village, often needing to take care of his younger, weaker brother due to Traquan always being away with the Heizhovras. Growing up, he always felt a connection to nature and the forest outside their house, a by-product of his Synth Flux-sensitivity. While he was still a child, Mother Traquan became an ally of Xarn Nyxarion, the Dark Lord of the Morkai, the two beings exchanging knowledge of Umbral Tide arcana and Heizhovra magicks. When Nyxarion visited Traquan, Skorn always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Traquan's increasing bond with him.

### Taken by Xarn Nyxarion

Skorn's struggle with anger eventually caught the attention of Nyxarion. Around 40 BVC, Nyxarion would take Skorn away from Jafiares to become his Morkai apprentice. According to Traquan, Nyxarion betrayed her and stole Skorn away. According to Skorn's unreliable memories, as Nyxarion prepared to take the young Skorn away with him, Zuhe attempted to intervene and convince him not to go with the "monster," although Skorn believed he had no choice and departed, much to Zuhe' upset as Zuhe accused Nyxarion as having done something to Skorn.

Alternatively, according to a legend, Nyxarion ordered Traquan to give up Skorn to a keeper, threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Skorn ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Beidadrom took him and threw him into a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother. Over the course of months, Skorn was put through relentless domestic abuse, all orchestrated by Nyxarion as a test. He lived with his Beidadrom keeper's two sons, who beat him to the point of being unable to stand up every few days and kept starved by only being given the scraps his "brothers" didn't eat. This eventually started to warp Skorn's perceptions and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him.

### Serving the Morkai

Under Nyxarion's tutelage, Skorn trained as a Morkai apprentice, Morkai warrior, and Morkai assassin, eventually being given the title "Xarn Skorn." For years, Xarn Skorn was trained to become a weapon of the Morkai. He became an acrobatic warrior, one trained to be relentless against his enemies, and learned to use a dual-bladed arcblade, eventually crafting his own, using plans given to him by his master. The plans had been hidden by Nyxarion in a forgotten entrance in the ancient Morkai archives. For his arcblade, Skorn used pieces that he had stolen or otherwise acquired. As he noted to himself while sitting in darkness in the time before the Shiatrotre sector summit, a large amount of pain and blood that had gone into the construction of his weapon. However, the majority of the blood that had been spilt in the creation of the weapon was not his. During arcblade training, Skorn trained with Marksman-H training remotes. He also learned martial arts through mechanoid training.

As recounted in an unreliable memory, one training session saw Nyxarion insult Skorn's form and strike him with Synth Flux lightning. During his training, Nyxarion took Skorn to Roreiler to bear witness to a great battle that occurred there between the Veyari and the Morkai. Nyxarion forced Skorn to breathe in the ashes of Morkai warriors, slain when an ancient weapon was activated in the Morkai temple on the planet. While breathing in this ash, Skorn had visions of members of the Morkai order being struck down by the Veyari. He felt the slash of saber after saber as he felt the death of his Morkai brethren. His Morkai training made Skorn long for revenge against the Veyari Order, who had defeated the Morkai a millennium earlier. With his master, Xarn Skorn hoped to destroy the Veyari and restore the Morkai to galactic power. Ultimately, Skorn mastered his anger, letting it burn in him, and, under his master's supervision, began to turn his anger into hate.

### Exile in the Far Verge

However, through his hate and rage, Skorn managed to survive being cut in half as his top half fell down the reactor shaft and into a trash container. A combination of his Heizhovra magicks, Jafiaresian physiology and Morkai tenacity served to keep him tethered to life even as his sanity began to leave him. Skorn's shattered body was dumped on the junkyard world of Hezhorn Minor, where he lived in the bowels of the planet. There he utilized discarded metal to fashion for himself a six-limbed apparatus that allowed him to walk again.

During Skorn's exile, the mechanoid Captain Zhazhaga forced several people, including Lomox, Krebrex, Mozosk, and Seles to spend a night on Hezhorn Minor in order to join her crew. When they first got on the planet, a confrontation broke out between Lomox and Mozosk, and the ground gave way into a labyrinth.

### The Replica Wars

Mother Traquan knew that Skorn had survived. During the Replica Wars, she told his brother, Brakk Zuhe, that the former Morkai Lord could be found in the Far Verge. Traquan gave Zuhe a talisman that would help him find Skorn, and Zuhe set out to locate his brother. Thanks to a Bishia cargo hauler, Zuhe found Hezhorn Minor and discovered his crazed brother living there, but Skorn did not know who Zuhe was, and barely had a hold on his own identity, frequently breaking out into fits of laughter and sobbing. All that remained of the one-time Morkai Lord was his lust for revenge against the Veyari. Believing that Zomem had severed his brother and driven him crazy, Zuhe killed the Anacondan and, to restore Skorn's memories, took him to Jafiares, where Mother Traquan repaired Skorn's mind and gave him a pair of cybernetic legs through an incantation, using parts of the Secessionist B2-series super battle mechanoids destroyed during the Battle of Jafiares.

With his mind restored, Skorn set out to seek revenge against Talvane for defeating him on Ilveren, and led the now-Veyari High Council member to Jutadrer by slaughtering civilians to draw Talvane out. Skorn and Zuhe captured Talvane and brought him to their Turtle Tanker ship, but the Veyari was rescued by Mebril Hokrijoth, a former acolyte of Xarn Relel and the Jafiares Witch who once commanded Zuhe. Talvane and Skorn engaged in a arcblade duel for the first time in over a decade, and Skorn goaded Talvane into anger by talking about how he killed Sael-Rhal Ormis. Talvane was overwhelmed as a result, and decided it would be best for he and Hokrijoth to escape the DG-TL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker. Although Talvane had escaped, Skorn knew that the Veyari would begin pursuing him, which would play into Skorn's hand in his quest for revenge.

### Ruler of Kordath

Shortly after the coup, Skorn learned that the Duchess had tried to escape from prison and transmitted a distress call to the Veyari. Having learned of Talvane's old friendship and relationship with Dreivil, Skorn realized she could serve as bait for his archenemy and the perfect tool for his revenge against the Veyari Master. As Kordath was technically neutral in the Replica Wars, Skorn knew that Talvane could not receive an official sanction from the Veyari Council for a rescue mission. As such, he predicted that Talvane would mount his rescue alone.

After Talvane infiltrated the palace and liberated his friend, Skorn and the Kordathian forces chased the two of them all the way to Talvane's ship, Twilight. The Kordathians fired missiles at the ship and crippled it, forcing Talvane and Dreivil to abandon ship before it was destroyed. Although dazed from the crash, Talvane recognized Skorn and Zuhe and attacked them, but Skorn quickly disarmed him and ordered his forces to bring Talvane and the Duchess to the palace.

### Age of the Hegemony

As Skorn had foreseen, Edrik Vantreil did indeed fall to the umbral tide, becoming Nyxarion' next apprentice and taking on the name Xarn Velgor. With his new student at his side, Nyxarion established the Galactic Hegemony and enacted the Great Veyari Purge, the next step in his grand plan. Velgor and the Veyari-hunting Jevrorar were set loose to eradicate the Order that Skorn had once wished to topple. Nearly losing his life to the final machinations of his former master's grand scheme, Directive 91, and having been deprived of his planet of operations on Kordath, Skorn found himself at a critical juncture in his plans for power and vengeance. Now, having failed his race against the clock to carve out a corner of the galaxy to build a stable influence, the former Xarn Skorn now saw the end results of the Grand Plan he had once been a tool of. Far from what he had envisioned, the burgeoning Hegemony that had evolved from the bones of the decaying Concord did manage to rid the galaxy's prime source of Skorn's hatred, the Veyari Order. Thousands of years of peace, civilisation, and stability under the guidance and protection of the institution representing the Radiant Tide had been uprooted by the very system that it was serving. In spite of this, Skorn was perturbed by the replacement of a cold, sterile, bureaucratic entity which demanded order through threat of force, and a galaxy filled with corruption and unpredictable chaos that even Skorn found difficulty navigating.

For all his resentment and immense hatred for the Veyari, Skorn ironically found himself wishing for their continued presence rather than near annihilation at this time. The Replica Wars, which had weakened much of the galaxy and distracted the attention of the Veyari to allow Skorn the opportunity for building his own power base, one which he hoped would grow enough in the shadows of the underworld to rival Nyxarion' might by the end of the war. Unfortunately, despite abandoning his former destiny as the enforcer of Nyxarion' will, the ex-Morkai still used the methods of his brutal teachings in the Umbral Tide: puppetry, fear, hatred and domination. His greed and ambition exposed Skorn too early, and with the Siege of Kordath, much of the Shadow Collective's unity was severed once its head was forced into hiding. Seeking to access the allegiance of his most powerful syndicates, Skorn travelled to Draro Ziriarn to ask for assistance from the Pykes. However, now that the feared Grojusk had been defeated twice on Kordath and found himself weakened and alone, the Vosk leader, Vagren Theitrarn, desired to take control back and tried instead to have Skorn killed, betraying his own boss. The assassination attempt would fail and Skorn escaped and was left with a desire for revenge against the treasonous Theitrarn..

### Legacy

Talvane honored his old adversary by building him a funeral pyre, something he recalled shortly after becoming one with the Synth Flux. The Spectres' witness accounts and information on Skorn were placed in the Insurgent Pact Archives. Skorn's ship, the Beidadrom, was taken by Skagruth and Rivet back to Rivet Base as a new vehicle for the Pact. It was key in the Battle of Grobe, in which Skagruth and Maris used it to transport members of Clan Aldis to take down an Hegemonic Interdictor and allow the Insurgents to escape Grand Admiral Vyrell's blockade.

Nyxarion considered Xarn Skorn to be a loss, whereas Count Varnoth was labeled as both a proton torpedo and a mere placeholder. While having a Synth Flux vision surrounding Corin Vantreil, Nyxarion appreciated that he was a simple boy not covered in demonic tattoos like those of Skorn.

## Personality and traits

A former Morkai Lord and a skilled warrior in his own right, Skorn for most of his life came to embody the obsessive and frenzied aspects of Morkai revenge. As a Morkai apprentice, Skorn sought revenge against the Veyari for their triumph over the Morkai and wanted to return the Morkai to power over the galaxy. After his exile and the restoration of his mind, Skorn turned his thoughts of revenge towards the galaxy itself, declaring himself and his brother the true Morkai Lords; and especially towards Oru-Kei Talvane, after the Veyari defeated him and forced him into exile after their duel on Ilveren. He felt the vacuum from losing power, having known about the Replica Wars and intending to be part of it, only to learn that the Morkai plans moved on without him. Skorn was, therefore, willing to go to great lengths to achieve his vengeance, including slaughtering civilians and thrusting Kordath into civil war to gain power and draw Talvane out into the open. He knew the Veyari would not ignore a call for help. Skorn, as a follower of the Morkai religion, ignored the existence of the duality of good and evil, putting ambition and survival above it.

Like all Morkai, Skorn's anger was his fuel. However, Skorn still worked on his hatred. Skorn felt there was so much to hate–from the fog of Jafiares to the way he had been cast aside as a child.

### Arcblade abilities

As the apprentice of Xarn Nyxarion, Skorn was trained to be a living weapon of the Morkai, one intended to help Nyxarion bring about the destruction of the Veyari and take over the galaxy. Under Nyxarion' tutelage, Skorn became one of the deadliest, most efficiently trained Morkai in the Order's history. He became an acrobatic and highly trained Morkai warrior, one who could relentlessly pursue his enemies while utilizing his double-bladed arcblade. Indeed, Skorn had been trained in several forms of arcblade combat. He put these skills to the test for the first time in his duel against the Veyari apprentice Pitosk Gobrum; although the young Metiarn'lek managed to hold her own against the Morkai for a time, Skorn came out of the encounter victorious. Later, during his duel on Ilveren against Ormis and Talvane, he demonstrated the ability to fight both the Veyari Master and his prodigious apprentice simultaneously on equal grounds as well as overpower Talvane after he killed Ormis.

Although Talvane defeated Skorn, this was in part due to Skorn's own hubris and neglect for the fallen Ormis's arcblade. He later outmatched Talvane in a number of encounters after emerging from exile, including the fight over Jutadrer, though their duel on Vrevex ended in stalemate. Skorn was also extremely skilled in unarmed combat, which he often integrated into his fighting style. This was most notable when he defeated an armed Pre Thodreir with little to no assistance from the Synth Flux.
