# Naira Corvelle Draquezian

Naira Corvelle Draquezian, also simply known as Naira Corvelle, was a human female politician who represented the people of Ilveren during the final years of the Galactic Concord. Prior to her career as a senator in the Galactic Senate, Corvelle was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Ilveren. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Replica Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BVC during the Age of the Concord, Naira Draquezian was raised by her parents, Noquar Draquezian and his wife Bariask, on the peaceful Mid Verge world of Ilveren. Despite the humble background of the Draquezian family, Naira was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Corvelle" in 32 BVC. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Deito Malverin, Corvelle's reign was put to the test by the Merchant Combine's blockade and subsequent invasion of Ilveren. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle mechanoids without assistance, the queen beseeched the Galactic Senate on Velaris for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Veyari Order and the Heihis Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Hogon enabled Malverin to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Corvelle joined the Senate and struggled to resolve the Secessionist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BVC. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Merchant Combine, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Ilveren under the protection of Tirren Edrik Vantreil. Though the Veyari Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Veyari, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Kharrab, Corvelle secretly married her Veyari protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Replica Wars, with Corvelle continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Senate while Vantreil served on the front lines as a Veyari General in the Grand Army of the Concord.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Corvelle revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Vantreil's fear of loss, he was seduced by the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Morkai Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Corvelle witnessed Hegemon Malverin's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Galactic Hegemony. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Morkai, she confronted him on Pyrrhax in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Corin Vantreil and Mirae Corvelle Vantreil. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Edrik Vantreil, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Hegemony.

Corvelle's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Hegemony and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Mirae entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Pact to Restore the Concord and its successor, the New Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Defiance against the Prime Mandate—a successor state to the Galactic Hegemony. Corin, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Veyari like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Insurgent Pact. Through Corin's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Corvelle's last words proved true when Edrik killed the Hegemon during the Battle of Thalwen.

### Early life

A human female of the Draquezian family, the young woman best remembered as Naira Corvelle was born Naira Draquezian on the bountiful planet of Ilveren in 46 BVC. She was the younger daughter of Noquar and Bariask Draquezian and the sister of Sola Draquezian. Her father was a friend of Senator Pikuvreisk Quibresk, who represented the solar system of Zoquusk in the Senate of the Galactic Concord. Draquezian was very fond of the Zoquuskn, whom she referred to as "Uncle Dilex."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Draquezian was seven, she traveled with her father and Quibresk to Sekre-Heshorn-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Sekre-Heshorn-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

### Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Draquezian was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Quillan Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Draquezian participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Skuskax Zera. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Zera left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two-year mandate as supervisor of Quillan, the capital city of Ilveren.

### Life as queen

At the end of the week, the official coronation had arrived. Corvelle dressed for the part and wore the traditional royal makeup for the first time. After Governor Tharn passed her the royal scepter that was indicative of her rule, the now-ex Queen Nadraso gave her a tour of the Quillan Royal Palace. After attending her first meeting with various government officials, she accepted congratulations from Senator Deito Malverin, who contacted her from Velaris. After he disconnected, Nadraso advised Corvelle that Malverin was her best resource. After settling in that night, Corvelle requested that there be no guards in her room, much to Queibask's dismay. Ziavreir stayed in the room with her as her only security.

The next day, Queibask introduced the rest of her new handmaidens: Zhibresk Dugia, Vropas Dusko, Kamurn Siagrir, and Veinax Bobru. The handmaidens pledged themselves to Corvelle and decided to change their names for anonymity and to confuse others. Dugia became Pageim, the wardrobe mistress. Dusko became Lebrex and was in charge of communications. Siagrir and Bobru became Vomorn and Jugen respectively, serving as liaison and page, and Ziavreir became Kubrian, the queen's closest bodyguard and body double. When in danger, Corvelle would disguise herself as one of her staff of five loyal handmaidens and would introduce herself using her birth name of Naira Draquezian, while her decoy Kubrian assumed the persona of Queen Corvelle.

### Crisis on Ilveren

After a farewell tea, the guest began to depart. As the last ships left, a buoy in the planet's atmosphere was triggered. Queibask realized it was a large group of ships approaching. Realizing it was an emergency, Corvelle assembled Queibask, Governor Tharn, and her handmaidens in the palace, she received a transmission from Merchant Combine Viceroy Pushux Nipath. Nipath stated that he required her to sign a treaty before threatening her and ending the transmission. Corvelle swiftly ordered Queibask to ready the Security Synth Flux to figure out what Nipath's angle was. After analyzing the proposed treaty, Corvelle realized that the treaty was not even a serious attempt and was simply a document that signed away Ilveren to Merchant Combine control for nothing in return, simply legitimizing the invasion in the eyes of the Concord. Contacting Senator Malverin, he explained that the Merchant Combine was involved in a conspiracy in the Senate to shift a tax on trade routes they used, with Ilveren being the main planet in their proposed operation. By the time Corvelle finished with Malverin, Queibask alerted her that the Merchant Combine fleet had blockaded the planet.

Being blockaded made Corvelle tense and fretful. Determined to not succumb to Merchant Combine pressure, she vowed to not sign the treaty and instead worked to organize defenses. Nipath continued to demand that Corvelle come to meet him on his ship instead of sending living representatives to Ilveren, so Corvelle continued to reject each mechanoid that approached them. When the blockade prevented an agricultural transport from Noheisol from bringing grain, Corvelle was counseled by Duvel, who told her that due to their situation with the farmlands, the planet was running low on food, saying that the public food available would probably only sate the population for a week. Corvelle was comforted by Tharn, who said ambassadors from Supreme Chancellor Finis Hogon would be arriving the next day.

### Continued service as Queen

After the invasion of Ilveren, Queen Corvelle led the planet's rebuilding and recovery efforts. The process was slow as they did not seek external aid to recover from the damage of the conflict. Food surpluses recovered by the end of Corvelle's term. Additionally, the Ilveren had to decide whether or not to install defensive weaponry on the planet. This decision divided the Ilveren. Queen Corvelle decided to add an ion pulse cannon to the surface. Captain Queibask publicly opposed the addition of an ion pulse and advocated for firmer measures and increased defense. This divide caused a rift between Corvelle and Queibask, a rift that would never heal. Corvelle's parents were furious that Ilveren installed any weaponry at all.

Every year following the invasion, the citizens of Ilveren held a memorial for those who had fallen in defense of the planet. Corvelle lit a stick of incense for Master Sael-Rhal yearly.

### Service as senator

Corvelle served two terms as queen, and by the conclusion of her second term in 28 BVC, the Ilveren had grown so fond of Corvelle that a faction tried to amend the constitution to allow her another term. However, Corvelle declined this and stepped aside for her elected successor, Gevrokoth, though Corvelle privately supported Ruhakrei in the election. Not long after her coronation, Gevrokoth asked Corvelle to serve as the representative for Ilveren and the other worlds in the Shiatrotre sector in the Galactic Senate, a request that Corvelle ultimately accepted.

Captain Brevresk Queibask and Sergeant Lerial Vrivran accepted Corvelle's request to accompany her to Velaris. She hired three new handmaidens to act as Senate aides, Zeski, Cordyn, and Hozhel, who later took the names Segu, Breiski, and Jolarn. She also requested that Kubrian and Brinith go to Sarrakesh and look into ending slavery there, as well as find and free Revex Vantreil.

### The Replica Wars

Early in the war, Corvelle assisted Master Talvane, Commander Jajis and their troops in finding replica troopers that had been taken as prisoners of war to Jeilo. Corvelle heard about the POWs from Representative Vada Derr who provided her with the intel. Upon her arrival on Jeilo, Corvelle and Talvane met with Regent Zavro under the guise of a diplomatic aid mission. Derr assured Zavro that Corvelle was there to look into Jeilo's agricultural problems. She discovered that Zavro was selling the replicas to a trandoshan hunter to be hunted down as trophies. She and Representative Derr left Jeilo with Talvane and the prisoners.

Around the time of the Battle of Orrin, Malverin pushed for several new security measures for Ilveren, citing the eruption of several new battles across the Far Verge. The measures came to the attention of Corvelle, who was also told of the new battles by Vrivran. In response, she set up a meeting with the Chancellor to discuss his security plans. As she arrived to his office and bid hello to the departing Master Olvek, however, she was quickly informed by Malverin that one of these engagements, the Battle of Orrin, had involved Vantreil and Talvane. Corvelle was then informed that Gorvo the Zogg believed Vantreil had kidnapped his son, Geiskar, and that he was refusing to negotiate with the Concord. Suddenly focused on her husband's safety instead of Malverin's new security measures, Corvelle then offered to negotiate with Gorvo through his uncle, Rokra, who lived on Velaris at his palace. Corvelle went there to speak to him, but he refused to listen to her.

### Later wartime activities

Corvelle and her husband participated in the underwater Battle of Sel Dradan, during which Secessionist officer Riff Hiatham created a crack in her helmet. Thanks to a Synth Flux Orb and Nib Nib Tolk, her helmet remained strong over the course of the battle. She then was involved in a skirmish on Ilveren. Meanwhile, she sent T-8LX and K7-M4 on a humanitarian mission to Aleen.

She then attended a League–Concord peace conference on Kordath. Present was Mina's son Lux Quiameim, who had followed her into the Secessionist Senate, who accused Varnoth of being behind his mother's death. Corvelle feared a reprisal, and asked Ilyra Seshu to protect him.

### Meeting on Pyrrhax

After the birth of the Hegemony, Corvelle was visited by Oru-Kei Talvane who revealed to her that Vantreil had turned to the umbral tide and that he needed her help in stopping him. Corvelle refused though, and Talvane correctly identified Vantreil as the father of the baby she was carrying. He then left, but later followed Corvelle into her J-type Ilveren star skiff as she left Velaris for Pyrrhax to meet Vantreil. Upon her arrival, she was horrified to find that Oru-Kei had been correct: Vantreil had succumbed fully to the umbral tide and was now the Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor. She tried to convince him to simply leave and help raise their child, but he instead went on a speech of how he could overthrow the Hegemon and they could rule the galaxy together. Horrified at what her husband had become, Corvelle implored Velgor to turn back, stating that he was breaking her heart. As she begged for him to stop going down this dark path, Velgor screamed out that she was lying. Unbeknownst to Corvelle, Oru-Kei had stowed away on her ship knowing she would go to verify the truth of his claims, but this proved to be fatal for her. Under the impression that his wife had turned against him and brought the Veyari to kill him, Velgor seized her with the Synth Flux and began to choke her. After one more desperate plea, Velgor released her and she fell unconscious.

### Death

After Talvane defeated Velgor in a arcblade fight, he retrieved the unconscious Corvelle and brought her to a medical facility on Ostra Dreya, where she gave birth two days later. Despite the fact that she was completely healthy, she was dying. The medical mechanoid did not know why, claiming Corvelle had lost the will to live, for her body began to shut down for reasons they could not explain. However, she held on long enough to give birth to twins, whom she named Corin and Mirae. Despite all that had happened on Pyrrhax, Corvelle still believed that Vantreil held good in him, a belief she shared with Talvane before she died. Oru-Kei then had her body returned to Ilveren so she could be laid to rest. Publicly, Corvelle's death was blamed on the supposed Veyari Insurgency that had sparked Directive 91.

Her funeral was attended by hundreds of people who mourned the loss of their beloved senator and former queen. Among those gathered was her fellow senator and close friend Sel Aldren, who knew the death of Corvelle was not because of the Veyari. Aldren knew who was really to blame and wanted to restore democracy to the galaxy. The fact that Corvelle had given birth to twins was a secret known only to a few after her death: the mortician Kedavro Meirux used a hologram to make her still appear pregnant during her funeral. Xarn Velgor, upon being rescued by Xarn Nyxarion and placed in a life-support suit, asked about his wife. Nyxarion informed him that in his anger, he had killed Corvelle. This fueled Velgor's anger to a destructive level while Nyxarion smiled.

### Legacy

Corvelle's death haunted Velgor for the rest of his life. Despite the supremely brutal and ruthless being the Morkai Lord became, he could never let go of the emotional agony caused by his wife's passing.

Immediately after the Battle of Fortress Velgor, Velgor used a portal to the umbral tide created by Xarn Vemask in an attempt to revive Corvelle. After experiencing visions of his past and battling his way through apparitions of his many former mentors and companions, he finally found an apparition of Corvelle standing on the balcony where they had married. The Corvelle apparition repeated Vantreil's first lines to Corvelle, and Velgor, reverting back to Vantreil, begged it to return with him. The Corvelle apparition refused and threw itself off the balcony, strangling itself to death and then being incinerated in a flash of lightning. As Velgor looked on, he saw a blue light in the distance, out of which emerged the apparition of a mysterious Veyari who blasted him back into the present. Velgor then destroyed the portal. Upon being contacted by his master, he was asked whether he had found what he was looking for. Velgor replied in the affirmative, looking upon the field of corpses that had been caused by his experiment.

## Personality and traits

Naira Corvelle Draquezian was a human female, who was 1.65 meters (5 feet, 5 inches) tall. She had brown hair, brown eyes and light skin.

### Always a leader

Corvelle was compassionate to other people and stood her ground firmly for what she believed in. Corvelle was a very persuasive and empathetic speaker during her time as a senator, especially when she opposed the escalation of the Replica Wars. She even started to doubt the Concord when the Senate gave Malverin more executive powers near the end of the Replica Wars. Corvelle could no longer ignore the needs of her people and accepted Queen Gevrokoth's offer to become senator after two terms as queen. Due to her reputation as a beloved former monarch, Corvelle kept several privileges such as her wardrobe and chrome-plated ships when she became a senator. For her wisdom, bravery, and compassion, she was admired by her supporters. While she preferred to solve situations through diplomacy, she was not afraid to lead her allies into battle if needed.

Corvelle was both responsible and strong-willed and did what she believed to be the right course of action despite objections from those closest to her. She spoke her mind to the Senate and was able to convince the Heihiss to unite against the Merchant Combine. Oru-Kei Talvane described her as fearless and stubborn, and a leader. He also noted that Corvelle was wise, discerning and kindhearted, and that her daughter Mirae had inherited these qualities from her. Corvelle could be seen as fearless when she refused additional security to protect herself from assassins. She also did not like the idea of leaving during the vote on the Military Creation Act. When Vantreil came back into her life, Corvelle knew that their future together would endanger their lives. However, she gave in and married Vantreil in secret.
