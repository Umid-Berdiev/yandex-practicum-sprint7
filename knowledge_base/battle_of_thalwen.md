# Battle of Thalwen

The Battle of Thalwen, also known as the Mission to Thalwen, the assault on the second Void Core, or the attack on the second Void Core, was a major and decisive battle of the Galactic Civil War, one in which the Pact to Restore the Concord shattered the rule of the Galactic Hegemony and saw the destruction of the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Xarn Nyxarion and Xarn Velgor. The triumph of the Pact over the Hegemony, and the void left in the wake of the Hegemon's demise, gave the Pact the opportunity to give rise to the New Concord, which ultimately prevailed over the Hegemony in the Battle of Leinesk one year later. The legacy of the Battle of Thalwen reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Hegemonic-inspired Prime Mandate and the Defiance that arose to combat it.

The origins of the Battle of Thalwen emerged from the discovery that the Hegemony was using the Forest Moon of Thalwen as a staging ground to construct a second Void Core, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Orvax. Ziaskix spies employed by the Pact gained the intelligence needed to determine the Void Core's location and learn that the Hegemon planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the Pact was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Corin Vantreil, a Veyari and the son of Xarn Velgor, and the Pact itself once and for all. The battle began after the Pact Fleet assembled near Brekra, and special operations ground forces were deployed to Thalwen to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station.

The Pact Fleet soon arrived to discover the Hegemonic Navy waiting for them, along with a fully operational Void Core. Allied with the native Tumlis, ground forces under the command of General Dax Varro and Princess Mirae Tessaly destroyed the shield, allowing the Pact Fleet to attack the superweapon. Meanwhile, Vantreil engaged Velgor in a final arcblade duel aboard the Void Core. After defeating his father, the Hegemon betrayed Velgor by attempting to convince the Veyari Knight to kill Velgor and turn to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, only for Corin to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Hegemon, Velgor surprised and betrayed Nyxarion, killing the Dark Lord of the Morkai, and sacrificing his own life in the process—an act that fulfilled the Veyari prophecy of the Chosen One and brought balance to the Synth Flux. Shortly thereafter, the Insurgent Pact forces destroyed the Void Core. Hegemonic forces were left in tatters, enabling the Pact to form the New Concord and forge a new future free of the Morkai and the rule of the Hegemony, thus allowing the Veyari Order to be restored.

### Background

Unlike the Battle of Orvax, the last major battle between the Pact to Restore the Concord and the Galactic Hegemony, which had taken place on the ice world of Kryost, had resulted in a disastrous defeat for the Pact. The loss of the recently established Echo Base forced the Pact High Command to break the Pact Fleet into small task forces that would constantly jump from one star system to another to prevent another defeat for Hegemonic propagandists to celebrate. Still, the Battle of Kryost showed the insurgency would escape and survive, instead of stand and fight, if discovered. As such, the key to destroying the insurgency laid not with finding its gathering area but luring it to a choke point. However, the Battle of Orvax showed that, in order to trick the insurgents into staying in one place to fight, the Hegemony would need a great threat the insurgents had no choice but to face.

During Crimson Dawn's ill-fated war against the Hegemony, Dawn agents Kreirask and Jon Melton revealed the existence of the second Void Core to the Insurgent Pact. The Ziaskix Spynet, in time, intercepted information that revealed the Hegemony was building the station in orbit over the forest moon of Thalwen. The Hegemony had occupied the moon for the Void Core project, thereby bringing Hegemonic forces into conflict with the native Tumlis. With the Hegemony largely paying the Tumlis no mind, only seeing them as annoyances instead of a threat, they failed to recognize the dangers the native lifeforms could cause and did not notice the species had a strong warrior culture.

### Baiting the trap

To bait the trap, Hegemonic Intelligence leaked the information that fell into the hands of the Ziaskix spies, ensuring the Pact obtained incomplete information about the Void Core: the Pact learned that Malverin would be present but was tricked into thinking that the battle station's nova lance was inoperative, when in fact the battle station would be fully operational. In addition to maintaining a skilled legion on Thalwen's surface to counter an insurgent ground assault, which the Pact would need to launch to disable the Void Core shield generator on Thalwen's surface, the Hegemon would also hide a large fleet of Nova Dreadnoughts on the far side of Thalwen so as to entrap the fleet. However, Vizor Quukrar Reiqua had sent a message containing orders for Death Squadron to join the fleet at Thalwen. To keep the information secretive, commander Sarra Jebrum from the special forces Inferno Squad, was sent to the Invincible Faith and deleted it.

Ultimately, the Hegemony prepared itself to face the insurgency on a spacefront and battlefront under its complete control, with the looming threat of the Tumlis not something the regime realized would be a danger—unbeknownst to the Hegemonic personnel, the Tumlis actively prepared to attack the bunker and, over the course of several weeks, established defensive positions around the facility which would prove vital in the battle to come. So as to capture the inevitable insurgent ground team, Malverin selected Tempest Synth Flux, an elite formation composed of some of his finest troops, to be garrisoned on Thalwen, where Tempest Synth Flux reinforced the shield generator personnel. As the deployment continued, whole squads were lost to the dark forests of Thalwen.

### Insurgent stratagem

A week after the intelligence report reached Insurgent Command, its leader, Chancellor Sel Aldren, organized a top-clearance meeting on Mishu to relay this information to the other Insurgent leaders. Alongside Admiral Shevrask Sihen, commander of the Pact Fleet, and General Airen Bigeil, the chief of Insurgent Pact Intelligence Service, Aldren invited a number of top leaders to the meeting: Admirals Dreya and Fagask, Generals Niakax and Vefia, and the rest of the Pact High Command—General Hushu Butrim, General Brivre Viren, and Princess Mirae Tessaly. After swearing all the Insurgent leaders to secrecy, Aldren adjourned the meeting for the next day.

The group of leaders devised a plan whereby Pact warships would engage the battle station's defenses, buying time for starfighters to enter the unfinished Void Core's superstructure to destroy its exposed reactor core. Prior to the arrival of the Pact Fleet, a strike team of commandos from Viren's Special Forces would be sent to the forest moon to destroy the deflector shield generator protecting the superweapon—though they had yet to secure a Hegemonic transport to do so. They also decided to use the Sanctuary Pipeline, a slipspace route that would allow them to go from Brekra directly into the Thalwen system. Tessaly and a crew of insurgents placed hyper-transceivers in the Greibrir sector as a part of Operation Yellow Moon. The hyper-transceivers would attract Hegemonic attention to Tessaly, deflecting the Hegemony's attention away from the fleet's gathering at Brekra. The operation was a success. In addition to placing the hyper-transceivers, Tessaly and her team destroyed the Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnought Drezashix and stole the Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Leimeiher, which would prove essential to the infiltration mission to the Thalwen moon.

### Gathering of strength

A Hegemonic fleet consisting of Nova Dreadnoughts, attack cruisers, light cruisers, and more gathered in the Hotreim system as a display of the Hegemon's power. Their mission was to attract the Insurgents' attention and confirm the Hegemon's arrival to Thalwen. In response, Sihen sent Corona Squadron to observe the Hegemonic ships and obtain as much information as possible. Corona Squadron engaged in a dogfight against VEX fighters, during which Corona Three (Zokex) was shot down. Hegemonic forces allowed Corona Squadron to escape, hoping that the resulting counterintelligence would lead the Insurgents to believe that the Hegemonic Mameibol was dispersed.

With the Pact Fleet nearly assembled, wing leaders and other major players were briefed on plans for the upcoming battle by Aldren, Sihen, and Viren. Sihen would command the Pact Fleet, providing cover for the starfighter attack. Berrin Maldonne, who was promoted to the rank of general for his actions in the recent Battle of Grino, would lead Gold Squadron and the rest of the starfighter groups in the attack against the Void Core's main reactor. General Dax Varro, having recently been rescued from Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk on Sarrakesh, would take over command of the ground assault team from the recently deceased Major Vradara. Tessaly, Grawlokk, and Vantreil along with his mechanoids, K7-M4 and T-8LX, volunteered during the briefing as the command crew to pilot the Leimeiher and assist Varro and his strike team.

### Arrival in the Thalwen system

Departing for the Thalwen system, Varro's strike team consisted of Pathfinders, including Tuck Zeru and Sergeant Ovan Nekra. As they entered Thalwen's orbit, they were faced by Executor and two Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnoughts, and Varro requested that the deflector shield be deactivated and transmitted the clearance code for shield passage as ordered by a flight controller, under the watchful eye of Admiral Tetrun Memal. As the Insurgents awaited for the codes to be worth the price they had paid, Velgor sensed the presence of his son, Vantreil, aboard the shuttle and allowed them to land on the moon.

Agreeing to use comlinks only as a last resort to avoid alerting the Hegemony to their presence, the Insurgent landing party made their way through the forest, but they were soon spotted by four Hegemonic scout troopers from Theta Squad, who made use of 74-Z speeder bikes. Although Varro and Grawlokk took out two of them, Vantreil and Tessaly were forced to pursue the remaining two and two other troopers—QK-290 and QK-151—with the speeder bikes. During the pursuit, however, Tessaly was separated from the group and, after taking out another two scouts, brought to Bright Tree Village by the Tumli Pimm W. Vrabom, prompting Varro to split the strike team. While the Pathfinder squad made their way to the shield generator ahead of them, Varro, Vantreil, Grawlokk, and the two mechanoids searched for Tessaly before going to the rendezvous point.

### Pact with the Tumlis

During their search for the princess, Varro's group fell into a Tumli verkle-baited trap and was captured to become a banquet in T-8LX's honor back at Bright Tree Village, as the primitive species believed the protocol mechanoid to be a god. Tessaly, already a guest of the Tumlis, was unable to talk them out of this plan. However, skillful use of the Synth Flux allowed Vantreil to trick the Tumlis into believing T-8LX would unleash his "magic" over them if they did not release them at once. The Tumlis quickly complied.

Now considering the Insurgent team their guests, the village in full listened to T-8LX, who used his translator skills to speak Tumliese and tell the tribe of all that had led to their presence in their forest—his and K7-M4's service to the Galactic Concord, K7-M4's work during the Replica Wars, the Veyari Order's demise at the betrayal of the replica army, the birth of Xarn Nyxarion's Hegemony and the end of freedom, the gradual birth of the Pact and the message of a boy across the worlds that ignited a spark of insurgency, Tessaly's mission to Sarrakesh, the mechanoids' purchase by Vantreil, their encounter with Oru-Kei Talvane and meeting with Varro and Grawlokk, the destruction of Belisar by the first Void Core, the princess' rescue and the Battle of Orvax, the Insurgent defeat at Kryost, the Meridian Kestrel's escape to Sky Haven and Vantreil's fateful duel against Velgor. Finally, T-8LX told the Tumlis that the Hegemon had constructed another planet-killing "metal moon" above them.

### Capture of Corin Vantreil

At the same time, Vantreil left the village, as he had sensed during T-8LX's narration that Velgor had landed on the moon. Aware that he could not hide from his father, he revealed to Tessaly that he was her brother, and Velgor was their father. Vantreil then left her to face the Morkai Lord in combat alone. Subsequently, Vantreil ventured into the woods and turned himself over to a Hegemonic patrol, Commander Zhetian Sovar's Gamma Squad, which was searching for the missing Theta Squad. After claiming Vantreil's arcblade, Sovar was ultimately able to identify Vantreil as a Veyari due to his own experiences in the Replica Wars. Before taking Vantreil to Velgor as he requested, Vantreil guided Gamma Squad to the remains of Theta Squad, whom he had clashed with after landing on Thalwen.

During his capture, Vantreil claimed to be alone, something Sovar doubted. After Sovar prevented shocktrooper Skuhorn from killing Vantreil after Vantreil admitted to killing the scout trooper Lazhosk, they transported their prisoner on an All Terrain Armored Transport to the shield generator base, where Sovar was greeted by Velgor, who had arrived aboard shuttle ST 321. There, he gave Vantreil's arcblade over to the Dark Lord of the Morkai and convinced himself to see Vantreil as nothing more than another enemy. As Sovar departed, Vantreil began a short conversation in which he attempted to turn Velgor to the radiant tide, but the Morkai Lord refused and had Vantreil sent to the Void Core above to meet the Hegemon. Meanwhile, Sovar went on to conduct a further search of the area for other insurgents, although he had overheard from the conversation that Velgor and Vantreil were none-other-than father and son.

### The Tumlis ready for war

Unbeknownst to both the Insurgent team and the Hegemony, in the middle of the night, the Tumlis held a council of war in Bright Tree Village. Recalling the tribe members killed and the villages that were destroyed by the Hegemony, Zuje convinced the village to take arms against the Hegemony, as did Jitrurn, whereas Jeke said it involved all Tumli tribes. In the end, Zuje sent messengers through the forest to explain it all to the leaders of other tribes, and ask for their help.

The morning after, when the Insurgents awoke, they found the village nearly deserted, as those who were able to fight had left to prepare the battlefield under the leadership of Zuje, Niame, and Jitrurn. A few Tumlis—Jeke, Pimm, and Brisun—remained there to help them find the easiest way to the rendezvous point, where Varro presented the Tumlis to the Pathfinders before going to a bit of high ground to get a first look at the military base and shield generator that he had promised to destroy, just as ST 321 departed, transporting Vantreil and Velgor to the Void Core. Upon finding how heavily guarded the main entrance was, the Tumlis showed Varro an alternate entrance, guarded only by a few Hegemonic soldiers. There, Brisun stole a speeder bike to distract most of the guards, which enabled Varro and his strike team to easy sneak into the facility.

### The ground battle begins

Led by Varro, members of the Insurgent strike team entered the bunker with intentions of planting the explosive charges to destroy the shield generator. However, as planned by the Hegemon, a legion of his best troops awaited them; even though Varro and his team forced Major Neigrax and his men to surrender and quickly took out Colonel Dyer, shocktroopers under the command of Commander Posam flushed the strike team out of the bunker at blasterpoint. With the generator still intact, the arriving Pact Fleet above was forced to engage the Hegemonic forces for longer than expected, while the whole Insurgent strike force was surrounded by the Hegemonic ground legion. Soon, however, the Tumli forces entered the fray; as they provided a distraction for the Insurgent strike team, they also inflicted many casualties among the Hegemonic ground forces.

During the confrontation, the Hegemonic forces scattered to fight the Tumlis, who engaged in guerrilla warfare with their primitive weapons and traps designed to combat Kriaqua. With fewer troopers to deal with, the strike team was left free to take up positions on each side of the bunker. As the door code was changed, Varro and Tessaly had K7-M4 attempt to open it, but a shocktrooper shot the mechanoid, temporarily disabling him and leaving Varro to manually try and open the doors. Unbeknownst to them, Grojusk bounty hunter Jas Biapiam was present and targeted Tessaly; however, she decided against it, even though the Hegemonic bounty on her head was very high.

### The space battle

Ready to lead the planned attack on the second Void Core, Sihen, from the bridge of the MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser Home One, initiated the countdown to enter the Sanctuary Pipeline, and ordered all groups to assume attack coordinates. On his mark, the entire fleet jumped into slipspace to arrive at the Thalwen system—except for Aldren's ship, which after a moment, she ordered to take her to a safe hiding place to await news of the impending battle.

High above the moon, the fleet reverted to realspace with Maldonne leading the charge aboard the Meridian Kestrel, alongside his co-pilot, Natro Krizei, and their gunners, Lieutenant Fogias and Bigeil. Using the comlink, Maldonne had all wing commanders of his strike force report in: Red Leader (Tobin Thoneskias), Gray Leader (Metru Drosax), and Green Leader (Arvel Miadi). Upon their leaders' orders, all starfighter squadrons locked their S-foils in attack position to initiate the attack on the Void Core. However, as Krizei realized there were no readings about the deflector shield, and their transmissions likely were being jammed, Maldonne broke off the attack as he correctly surmised the shield was still up, and the leading Kestrel and K-41B K-lance fighters peeled away just before hitting the shield, followed by the other starfighters.

### Demise of the Morkai

Escorted by Velgor, Vantreil was brought to the Hegemon's throne room in an attempt to turn him to the umbral tide, shortly before the Pact Fleet arrived. After telekinetically releasing Vantreil from his binders, the Hegemon dismissed his protectors, the Royal Guard, and revealed the extent of his plan to destroy the Pact. Aware of Vantreil's anger and hate, the Morkai Master tempted him to give in to his emotions and strike him down with his arcblade.

In spite of his initial refusal to succumb to the Hegemon's temptations, when faced with the imminent destruction of the Insurgent fleet and the loss of his friends, Vantreil allowed himself to be coerced into a confrontation. Within the darkened confines of the Hegemon's throne room, Corin engaged his father in a fierce arcblade duel. Although Corin first fought Velgor in anger, the Hegemon's taunts made him realize that he was following the path Olvek had warned him against. Instead, he once again tried to reach his father, sensing conflict in him about his allegiance. However, Velgor dismissed him and threatened to turn Corin's sister, Tessaly, to the umbral tide in his stead, something that enraged Vantreil, allowing him to draw upon his anger to overwhelm the Dark Lord. With one final strike, Corin severed Velgor's cybernetic right hand, taking the Morkai's arcblade with it as it plummeted into the depths below. Pleased with Vantreil's display of power, Nyxarion ordered Corin to kill Velgor and then take his father's place at the Hegemon's side, thus betraying Velgor in a way similar to how he betrayed his former apprentice Count Varnoth 23 years earlier. Seeing at once what he would become if he failed to pull himself back, Vantreil tossed his arcblade aside and declared himself a Veyari, as his father had been before him.
