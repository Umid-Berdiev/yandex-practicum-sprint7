# Sarrakesh

Sarrakesh was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy's Far Verge Territories. Part of a binary star system, the planet orbited two scorching suns, resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via moisture farms. The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Skrit and Dhural Raider species and of Edrik and Corin Vantreil, who would go on to shape galactic history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large sandcrawlers, which were later used by the Skrit population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Zogg Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Sarrakesh gained the notice of the Galactic Hegemony during the Galactic Civil War when an escape pod carrying two mechanoids in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Far Verge world. The Hegemony went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk of the Zogg Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Hegemony and an Insurgent Pact cell on Sarrakesh that culminated in a battle that left the Hegemony's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Gorvo and the withdrawal of Hegemonic forces after the Battle of Thalwen, an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime syndicates like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Teko Drayk returned and took over Gorvo's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Zogg Clan and the Vosk Syndicate, the new Dodreim was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Sarrakesh. With help from the Kordathian Kol Lodia, Drayk rallied the denizens of the settlements Kar Zhezes and Breifeibur to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.

## Description

Sarrakesh was a hot desert planet located in the Far Verge, a region of the galaxy far removed from its core. It had three moons, Jupozern, Gikrahax, and Legi. Sarrakesh orbited twin suns, Netra I and Netra II. According to Skrit folklore, the desert known as the Dune Sea was once a true ocean. The whole planet was covered in harsh deserts, and only a small part of its northern hemisphere could sustain intelligent life. The landscape was covered with sand dunes, mountains, and canyons, which made traversing the planet very difficult. Due to the absence of surface water, those who lived on Sarrakesh had to draw moisture through the dry air.

Rain was incredibly rare on Sarrakesh, coming once a year, decade, or century. When the rain came, some plants were able to grow in a brief yet glorious period. Inhabiting the planet were many native species, the largest of which were the carnivorous sarn dragons. The common sarn dragon, while more plentiful, was shadowed by the greater sarn dragon, which submerged itself under the shifting sands and used its powerful limbs to traverse through the dunes. In addition, the common dunebeast and eopie species also found a home on the world, along with dewbacks, rontos, womp rats, massiffs, and scurriers.

### Early history

Sarrakesh was the homeworld of the Skrit and Dhural Raider species. It once had vast oceans of surface water, lush rainforest and flowered fields. According to the Legend of Tike, Sarrakesh had also been inhabited by a group called the Bapiagex during this time, but there was little evidence to support the story. Sometime in Sarrakesh's history, the whole planet became a desert by unknown means when the oceans dried away. Much later, it would be colonized by early settlers and explorers, who mistook the planet for a third sun owing to its highly reflective silicate surface.

As a result of sporadic and unsuccessful colonization ventures, the planet became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants attempted to earn a living in the wastes. A long-ago mining boom saw the widespread use of sandcrawlers on the desert plains in an effort to mine valuable minerals. However, after it was discovered that most valuable minerals could be found on the surface, along with most minerals having unwanted metallurgic properties, the sandcrawlers were abandoned and eventually picked up by the native Skrits for use as shelter and mobile bases, from which they collected scrap from thousands of years of crashed spacecraft and failed mining projects.

### Scum and villainy

In the last decades of the Galactic Concord, Sarrakesh was known as a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, including slavery. The sand people regularly attacked Fort Dhural, earning them their name of "Dhural Raiders." It was home to a human slave named Revex Vantreil and her son Edrik.

During this period, Queen Naira Corvelle of Ilveren escaped the capture of her homeworld by the Merchant Combine, and she took refuge on Sarrakesh with her companions and protectors, including the Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis and his apprentice Oru-Kei Talvane. The Vantreils befriended Corvelle's party, and the young slave ended up participating in a skimrace to help his new friends obtain a new T-14 slipdrive generator for their damaged starship. After discovering that Edrik Vantreil would make an exceptional recruit for the Veyari Order, Master Sael-Rhal Ormis managed to obtain his freedom from the junk dealer Queipa in the city of Kar Zhezes, and the Queen's party left Sarrakesh with the boy. After participating in numerous adventures, including the Invasion of Ilveren, the former Sarrakeshian slave joined the Veyari Order under Talvane's tutelage.

### Time of great hardship

In 9 BVC, The Grand Inquisitor, The Fifth Brother, and the Third Sister had landed on Sarrakesh looking for a Veyari on the run from the empire. The Veyari, "Skihith," had escaped the Inquisitors originally, but was eventually found and killed. The inhabitants of the planet were surprised by the arrival of the Inquisitors, since Sarrakesh was not part of the Galactic Hegemony, and for this reason the Jevrorar did not have jurisdiction on the planet, but this by no means prevents them from operating. Later, Dorn Tessaly shows up on Sarrakesh looking for Oru-Kei Talvane's help in finding his daughter, Mirae Tessaly. The Third Sister eventually returns to Sarrakesh looking to murder Corin Vantreil. The Third Sister redeems herself and decided not to hurt Corin, and brings him back to his uncles.

In the years following Hegemon Malverin's formation of his New Order, the Great Drought of Sarrakesh scorched the world's surface, impoverishing the many moisture farmers of a reliable source of income to procure food and other basic supplies. The situation was not helped by Gorvo's thugs collecting "water taxes" from the many towns and isolated settlements across the planet, often resorting to threats and brute force to acquire enough water for the crime lord. With Oru-Kei Talvane witnessing the suffering of the population, he disabled the weapons of the thugs, allowing the populace to chase them off. Later, a young Corin Vantreil attempted to confront the thugs, but was ultimately rescued by Oru-Kei after falling unconscious in a brief scuffle. With the legacy of the Veyari safe, Oru-Kei vowed never to return to town, while a week later the drought finally ended, and Gorvo vowed revenge on the man who had stolen his water, to no avail.

### War comes to Sarrakesh

Shortly after the Battle of Quubem in the Galactic Civil War, the mechanoids K7-M4 and T-8LX landed on Sarrakesh's surface in an escape pod after their CR90 corvette the Zhavian IV was captured by the Hegemonic I-class Nova Dreadnought Devastator. With the mechanoids carrying the technical readouts to the Void Core, a task force was sent to the planet in order to capture the two mechanoids, marking the moment the Hegemony began to truly pay attention to Sarrakesh. Upon coming in contact with a Skrit sandcrawler, the two were abducted and later sold to Halder Odrin of the Odrin moisture farm, with K7 being chosen after another navimech mechanoid, R5-D4 broke down. Later that day, Corin Vantreil, unknowingly Edrik Vantreil's son came across a recorded message of Princess Mirae pleading for the help of Oru-Kei Talvane against the Galactic Hegemony. Intrigued, if only because K7 later ran away, forcing Corin and T-8LX to retrieve him, the two were attacked by native Dhural Raiders.

Being saved by Talvane, Vantreil learned that he was a Veyari while Talvane offered to train him. Refusing and heading home, Vantreil came across the remains of the sandcrawler that originally sold the two mechanoids to his family. Realizing that it was the work of Hegemonic forces and that they may have traced the mechanoids back to his house, he rushed back only to find the moisture farm burned to the ground, with the charred remains of his aunt and uncle laying outside in the sun. Agreeing to go with Talvane to Kar Deli, they discovered the city under Hegemonic control and they hired the services of smuggler Dax Varro and his partner, Grawlokk to transport them offworld to Belisar. Barely escaping an intense firefight with multiple sandtroopers, the two escaped Sarrakesh and several Nova Dreadnoughts into slipspace.

### New days ahead

Following the creasing of Hegemonic rule, numerous crime syndicates attempted to build legitimacy as mining companies, with one such company, the Red Key Raiders, afraid that the New Concord would eventually stamp out their operations on the planet. These companies tried to take advantage of the power vacuum caused by the death of Gorvo. However Drayk lived on, having only been knocked unconscious for an unknown length of time, and dug himself out of the great pit. Fuvrufen and suffering wounds from the enounter, collapsed, where a Skrit sandcrawler scavenged the wreckage from Gorvo's sail barge, including his set of Kordathian armor. The enigmatic lawman Kroliarn Brozhask then obtained Teko Drayk's armor from the Skrit sandcrawler and went on to defeat the Red Key gangster Shokirn Peiskeisk.

By 5 AVC, Brozhask had become the mayor and Sheriff of Kar Mehirn and dedicated his life to protecting the town from the Red Key Raiders. He also enlisted Gorvo's former Vreiquobria Shanosian in helping to tame two unruly rontos and to raise the Mahiask named Ladrex. With Shanosian's help, Kroliarn struck a deal with the local Dhural Raiders to protect the town in exchange for water and receiving a pearl from the stomach of a sarn dragon. This alliance proved important in repelling an attack by the Red Key syndicate led by Greni Briabiajex.

### Rule with respect

Eventually, Drayk returned to Sarrakesh accompanied by Morrow and took over Gorvo's Palace and the criminal empire that once belonged to the Zogg after killing Ose Sajeim. Some time later, Drayk was attacked and was injured by the Order of the Night Wind, which was later revealed to have been sent by Gorvo's cousins, the Twins, who wanted to acquire the territory that formerly belonged to their deceased cousin.

The Twins sent Zaskinon to kill Lord Drayk at his palace. After Zaskinon's failure and capture, they arrived to apologize to Drayk and offer him a rancor as tribute. They also announced their departure from Sarrakesh, as they've been informed that Gorvo's former holdings have actually been promised to the Vosk Syndicate by Mayor Brebiam Thobrusk, and they had no interest in starting a war over a "worthless rock". With a war with the Vosk Syndicate on the horizon, Drayk began assembling an army, recruiting even Zaskinon despite his attempt on his life and Lodia. Although Zaskinon and Lodia joined the fight against the Pykes, Drayk still needed a infantry as the Pykes outnumbered them. Lodia offered to help with this. He went to Breifeibur, the town formerly known as Kar Mehirn, to enlist the help of his acquaintance, Sheriff Kroliarn Brozhask. Lodia asked that he and the townspeople join Drayk, saying that the Pykes' influence would inevitably reach the town as well. Though Lodia didn't know it, Brozhask had already faced the Pykes when he saw them trading spices in the city's territory.

### Native peoples

The Raiders, native to the planet, were a nomadic tribal society with strict social and moral codes, along with defined territorial boundaries. Frequent colonial trespasses on their sovereign territory, along with the collusion between old values and galactic modernization, often forced the Dhurals to attack colonists whom they both vehemently feared and despised. Dhural Raiders often attacked isolated settlements on the fringes of their territory such as Grekatu, and often took potshots at skimracers during the Keikol Eve Classic, celebrated once every year as part of the Keikol Eve holiday.

Skrits were also a native species to Sarrakesh, and as such deeply entrenched into a nomadic lifestyle. Unlike the Dhurals, whom they attempted to avoid, Skrits frequently sold collected scrap metal and mechanoids to the scattered settlers on the planet, earning a reputation as merchants and vagabonds. Myth and folklore also played an important part in Skrit society, with the Skrit's belief that the great Dune Sea was once a true ocean. Later research into ancient fossil-bearing rock and eroded canyons seemed to support this notion. Nonetheless, most of Sarrakesh's inhabitants refused to believe the story, finding it far-fetched and ludicrous. The Skrits made use of sandcrawlers, former mining vehicles claimed by the Skrits as mobile bases.

### Other inhabitants

Lawful inhabitants on the planet often utilized moisture vaporators owing to their energy efficiency, resulting in their status as one of the most crucial pieces of equipment for survival. While single vaporators often cost 500 credits each, some individuals bought and owned multiple units, creating moisture farms. Often utilizing the extra water for small hydroponic gardens, few gardens yielded enough to make substantial profits. In addition, vaporators required frequent maintenance and security systems to deter attacks from Dhural Raiders, who believed all water was sacred and belonged to them.

Sarrakesh had only a few port cities, including Kar Zhezes and Kar Deli. Beside those cities, which attracted many criminals, most settlers of Sarrakesh operated moisture farms in the expanse of the desert. Due to the planet's dangerously high daytime temperature and whipping sandstorms, most residents of Sarrakesh kept their skin covered with practical materials that wick away sweat and limit exposure to the harmful ultraviolet radiation of the twin suns.

### Economy

The economy of Sarrakesh largely ran on water and crime. With the Zogg Clan taking near-full control over the trafficking and smuggling trades, new citizens had a large time breaking into the criminal market even though it was more lucrative than the water market. Slavery, while banned through Concord laws, was readily and blatantly practiced under the twin suns. An entire section of Kar Zhezes was purposed as a Slave Quarter. On Sarrakesh, at least, the institution of slavery was seen as a socioeconomic symbol of wealth and prestige, resulting in highly protective owners who reluctantly parted with their possessions. Used as capital in business transactions, nearly all slavery operations were overseen by the Zogg gangsters, who regarded slavery as a useful institution.

### Cultural traits

An annual holiday celebrated on the planet was Keikol Eve. The holiday was celebrated in honor of Keikol Grashalern Shad'ruu, a Zogg who rose to be a god. The Keikol Eve Classic was a big skimrace held on Keikol Eve. The Grenern Jejian Band's song "Drezha Sogask" ranked number two in the top five of Sarrakesh's greatest hits, beaten by Zegreisk D'an and the Modal Nodes' number-one single "Mad About Me." The Modal Nodes followed up their success with the number-three hit "The Sequential Passage of Chronological Intervals" and ranked-five hit "Vrofavrei, But Not Goodbye (Mad About Me Remix)." The Grenern Jejian Band was left with only two of Sarrakesh's top five songs, with their number-four hit "(That Joyous Night) I Ate My Mate."
