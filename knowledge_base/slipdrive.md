# Slipdrive

The slipdrive, also known as warp drive, was a propulsion system that allowed a starship to reach slipspeed and traverse the void between stars in the alternate dimension of slipspace. As a consequence, the slipdrive was a key instrument in shaping galactic society, trade, politics, and war.

The slipdrive required a functional slipdrive motivator to operate. The vessel then traveled along a programmed course until it dropped back into normal space—realspace—at its destination.

A slipdrive only functioned to keep a vessel in slipspace, and should a slipdrive be forcibly deactivated or destroyed during transit, the ship was violently and instantly pulled back into realspace.

Being short-range craft, most starfighters lacked a slipdrive. This was notably the case with the Galactic Hegemony's VEX fighters, which had to be ferried to combat zones aboard larger ships. However, the K-lance and E-lance starfighters used by the Insurgent Pact were fitted with slipdrives, allowing them to make long-range jumps.

Upon entering slipspace, a ship emitted cronau radiation, which was possible to detect with specialized sensor suites.

Large objects in normal space cast "mass shadows" in slipspace, thus slipspace jumps required accurate plotting to avoid collisions, which were often fatal.

Later technologies could pull vessels out of slipspace. For instance, interdiction fields created gravitational shadows, simulating mass, in the path of an oncoming vessel, yanking the vessel out of slipspace. Hegemonic technologists developed the widely used Interdictor vessels and their various sub-models, some of the most effective interdiction technologies.

### Pre-slipdrive technologies

In the ancient times, due to the lack of slipdrive technologies the earliest spacers used generation ships to travel through the galaxy and traverse the vast distances from one planet to another. Most notable for being used by a patriarch of the Velarisi to blaze the Vriasho Trunk Line, generation ships were soon replaced by sleeper ships following the invention of ferrolite by the scientists on the planet Vriasho Major. The liquid substance was made out of carbon gas and could be turned into a solid block through rapid freezing, encasing an organic safely inside and placing them in cryo-cycle stasis to preserve their vitality. The process, known as carbon-freezing ushered in a new era of space exploration and inadvertently helped the formation of the Galactic Concord as the people of the galaxy came closer together. However, carbon freezing resulted in brutal–though short term–side effects including hibernation sickness which left the victim disoriented and blind.

### Early developments

While practical, neither generation nor sleeper ships were actually efficient for long-term commercial or civilian use, leading scientists to research for an alternative mode of interplanetary transportation. Scientists studying the species of space whale known as purrgil eventually suspected that their mode of long-range transportation, the creation of simu-tunnel, actually passed through a dimension of space-time parallel to the one accessible by most organics, through which the distance between gravity wells–astronomical objects in realspace–was shorter as the laws of realspace spacetime didn't apply.

By studying the behavior of purrgil, scientists discovered that slipspace could be accessed by molecular displacement, which could only be achieved by breaking the speed of light. As slipspace was coterminous with realspace–meaning that every point in realspace had its equivalent in slipspace in the form of mass shadows–it was possible to use the dimension to travel from one point to the other in a shorter period of time without needing to actually cover the distance between them. According to legend, this technology was in fact developed by the mythical species of Jukrodrol, native to the planet Tike. Developing slipdrive technology on the planet Meilom independently from extraterrestrial assistance, the Meilomians had explored and interacted with the galaxy before the Galactic Concord ever made contact with their region of space. To this end, Meilomian galactic explorers were a common sight across the galaxy as far back as 30,000 years ago. As early as 27,000 BVC, the Briakron Treivreigri reached the planet Tadern with the use of a tumbledrive propulsion engine. Eventually, Feilanian engineers independently from their Duros counterparts developed the technology necessary to access the dimension, and dubbed it the slipdrive–in contrast with standard engines, which were named sublight engines.

### Galactic exploration using the slipdrive

Once slipdrives made faster-than-light travel possible, galactic civilization flourished throughout ancient history. The Old Concord sent pioneering scout ships to explore the galaxy as increasing numbers of civilians from the Core Worlds sought a better life. The Meilomians were among the early explorers along what became the Feilanian Trade Spine slipspace route. Early slipdrive routes were blazed by scouts such as the San Papeisk clan by using slipspace sextants or simply following purrgil in their travels. The latter practice demonstrated the species' tendency to feed on the gas Kabrias-36, which formed in places subject to intense interstellar energy, like Queimeisk or the Luzago system. Knowledge of this practice led humans to discover the mineral quorite, which also formed in the same places and, like Kabrias, fuels faster and better slipdrive jumps. Unfortunately, the faster-than-light travel also created new possibilites for war, as the now-mythological Great Slipspace War between the Concord and Morkai Hegemony proved just over 5,000 years ago.

Prospectors and explorers started using the primitive slipspace paths and the advanced slipdrives of the time to explore the vastness of space, ushering in the Great Slipspace Rush. The Concord continued financing the scouting of new territories with its Pathfinder Teams, while also hiring private scouts such as the Didrer family. The development of major sliplanes such as the Kavaveim Trade Route and Dritheil Way further accelerated galactic expansion.

## Operation

Slipdrives consisted of two basic parts: a navigation computer and the actual reactor assembly–also known as the slipdrive generator. The navicomputer calculated a safe path through slipspace by comparing the pilot's desired path with known sliplanes and dangers already present along the way such as supernovas and asteroid fields to ensure a safe journey, while the reactor handled the work of generating enough power to perform a jump without straining the ship's main reactor. Species in the Unknown Regions such as the Shoki did not use a slipdrive, and instead used Synth Flux-sensitive beings who were capable of seeing the safest path through the normally difficult,to impossible, to traverse through (with a standard navicomputer) unknown regions. In the past other force sensitives have been known to use this method, such as Fiazus San Papeisk, who was used by the marauders known as the Gatis to create paths for their unique path drive, a slipdrive unhindered by the normal operating rules of a slipdrive. Xarn Velgor used this method.

Most modern slipdrive reactors had a main chamber coated in quorite, which significanty improved their efficiency. Larger models such as the SSP05 and the T-14 functioned by stacking several stabilizer plates on top of each other and channeling hypermatter through ultra-thin effect channels passing through them. Hypermatter, whose energy output was controlled by the navicomputer, reached the ship's main engines and charged the ions already present there, powering them for the jump to slipspeed; a slipdrive motivator controlled the jump based on sensor output and ensured a risk-free trip and return to realspace. Smaller ships such as starfighters or bombers ditched the slipdrive reactor altogether and instead relied only on the motivator, with power provided by the ship's standard reactor. Examples of standalone motivators were the R300-H slipdrive motivator of the ZRG-A4 J-lance starfighter and the Krupath-585 slipdrive motivator of the K-41B K-lance starfighter.

## Advanced operation

During the Replica Wars, a microjump was used to rescue Veyari Master Kumos Shutein from the forces of the League of Independent Systems in orbit of Trivrukis. Using the exact coordinates of General Kharzul' command ship, Edrik Vantreil precisely jumped through slipspace, arriving meters away from his target, undetected. The same precise jumping technique was used by Dax Varro to infiltrate the Prime Mandate superweapon Gehasox Base, since the planet's shields were using a fractional refresh rate that would keep anything below light speed from passing through. Without careful planning, using the slipdrive in such a manner could result in the loss of a ship due to the nature of mass shadows, but the most gifted pilots like Varro and Vantreil could use those characteristics of space to their advantage.

In 34 AVC, Defiance Vice Admiral Krako Toquux executed a slipspace jump with the cruiser Kinuth that was directly aimed at the Prime Mandate flagship Supremacy, causing the enemy ship to be sliced apart in the resulting collision.

## Classes

A numerical "class" rating denoted the speed of a slipdrive: the lower the class, the faster the engine. Most civilian ships used slipdrives rated Class Three or higher, while government, diplomatic, and military ships were equipped with Class Two or Class One slipdrives. With its Class One slipdrive, the Carrion Spike was the fastest ship in the Hegemonic Navy, while top-of-the-line capital ships such as the Hegemonic I-class Nova Dreadnought featured Class 2 slipdrives. The First Void Core, meanwhile, had a mere Class 4 slipdrive. The T-14 slipdrive generator equipped on J-type 327 Nubian royal starships was rated Class 1.8. Some ships, such as Quunas Kelen's Desert Jewel or Dax Varro's Meridian Kestrel, had exceptionally fast slipdrives—Class 0.8 and 0.5 respectively. The Supremacy, meanwhile, had a staggering slipdrive Class of 0.3.

By 3 AVC, a contract broker for the Mobon Trade League named Reess Piabrer had acquired a class-one slipdrive that been stolen from the Galactic Hegemony. As a result the Hegemony dispatched a number of ships and shocktroopers to Zhabrirn Station in the Gotreith system where Piabrer was storing the slipdrive. Shortly after, Piabrer hired Kay Kiago, ND-5, and Barrik Fuvron to smuggle the slipdrive past the Hegemonic patrols and to him at Hauler's Keep in the Skakrol system of Wild Space. As the group left the space station Hegemonic ships immediately locked on to their ship, the Jemajo, and directed them to proceed to an inspection point. The group however managed to make it past the checkpoint and jump to the Skakrol system where they were targeted by members of the Vosk Syndicate who were looking to steal the valuable slipdrive. The group eventually managed to make it to the station and transfer the slipdrive to Piabrer.
