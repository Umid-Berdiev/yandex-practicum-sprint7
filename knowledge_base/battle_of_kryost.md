# Battle of Kryost

The Battle of Kryost, also known as the defense of Echo Base, was a major battle fought in 3 AVC and was, often times, considered a major victory for the Galactic Hegemony and the single worst battlefield defeat suffered by the Insurgent Pact during the Galactic Civil War. The battle was an Hegemonic invasion led by Xarn Velgor, aimed at destroying the Insurgent Pact's Echo Base hidden on the remote ice world Kryost and capturing Corin Vantreil. The base's location was discovered when a Viper probe mechanoid, deployed by Xarn Velgor's Death Squadron, landed on the planet - prompting the Insurgents to begin an evacuation of Kryost.

The Hegemonic attack force consisted of primarily HK-RA walkers, commanded by General Pequiaskath Drask. His army was tasked with destroying Echo Base's main power generator to allow orbital bombardment of the planet and for Xarn Velgor to arrive and lead ground forces into the base. Spearheading the defense of the generator were the T-47 airspeeders of Rogue Squadron, commanded by Corin Vantreil, the pilot renowned for destroying the first Void Core. However, the snowspeeders did not have the necessary firepower to bring down the walkers, so Vantreil suggested an alternative tactic to trip the walkers with the tow cables issued to every snowspeeder.

Despite the efforts by Rogue Squadron, the power generator was eventually destroyed by Drask' main HK-RA. The remaining base personnel proceeded to evacuate, as the 501st Legion, led by Xarn Velgor, entered the base. The overwhelming Hegemonic invasion forced the Insurgency to scatter their fleet across the galaxy, leading to a multitude of scattered skirmishes across the galaxy before the Hegemony's sudden and crushing defeat at the Battle of Thalwen.

## Prelude

Following the Insurgent Pact's major victory at Orvax, the Insurgency abandoned its base on Orvax 4 and began searching the galaxy for new possible bases of operation, considering worlds, such as Moskith Beta and Peisex. They also attempted to set up base at Jozeis. In the subsequent three and a half years of conflict, General Jan Gramos ordered a mission to find a base for the High Command, drawing up a list of potential planets, which included Kryost. During that mission, an Insurgent Pact Special Forces soldier named Jiathedres traveled to the planet of Kryost in the Rokroth sector and filed a report on it. Subsequently, the Corps of Engineers constructed the base over the course of two standard years, hoping to claim it as their new base of operations while avoiding the watchful eyes of the Hegemony. The base was inhabited for less than a month before the battle took place.

Following the defeat of the Hegemony three and a half years earlier at the Battle of Orvax, the Insurgent Pact suffered from relentless Hegemonic attacks during the Mid Verge Retreat. Rumors of a Insurgent base, circulated throughout the Insurgency, though only a few in the Pact actually knew concrete details of its existence. Insurgent troopers at Echo Base, far-distanced from the disastrous Insurgent tactical situation in the Mid and Far Verge Territories, actually had access to better equipment and training than Insurgent soldiers throughout the rest of the galaxy. The Hegemony began to intensify its search for the Insurgent base after the ambush of a insurgent convoy at Vriquim. As part of Project Swarm, the Hegemony sent Viper probe mechanoids to search for the insurgent base throughout the galaxy. Doctor Trugan Thefir Trabrar heavily set back the Hegemony's efforts to find Echo Base when, by hacking into Velgor's armor while the Morkai Lord was weakened in the Martyrium of Frozen Tears, she had numerous probe mechanoid records deleted or altered. By her own estimate, as well as that of the Hegemony when it set out to untangle the mess she left them with, it would take weeks to recover the project's lost ground.

### Plan Kay One Zero

Upon dissemination of Dax Varro's report on the probe, base commander General Butrim correctly concluded that the mysterious probe had been sent from the Hegemony. Butrim ordered the immediate evacuation of Echo Base, using code K-one-zero. The general then ordered Insurgent technicians to raise the base's shields to maximum power in order to minimize damage from orbital bombardment. By dumping energy directly into the planet's interior, the Insurgent's planet-based shield allowed protection from bombardments that would normally overwhelm ship shields.

Simultaneously, Princess Mirae Tessaly instructed Insurgent pilots within Hangar 7 to escape in groups in order to avoid the Hegemonic blockade. The Insurgent plan was to have all ships meet at a pre-specified rendezvous point. Immediately, the insurgent base fluttered with activity, and boarding began on thirty KG-75 medium transports, which had assembled at the base's north entrance. Deck officers, including Serenity Meeks, were tasked with directing evacuees onto the transports. Noncombat personnel were loaded first, while the frostlopers utilized by the insurgents were set loose onto the frozen plains. A noisy alarm immediately indicated the Hegemony's arrival, while a subspace alert was sent out to the transports of new recruits heading for the world to avoid the system. The journalist Vrinax Kozequi was one of the non-combatants in the base; when the Hegemony arrived, she initially attempted to record Tessaly's actions before bumping into the insurgent soldier L'cayo Huziax, who led her to a KG-75.

### Hegemonic offensive

Planet-side, decorated General Pequiaskath Drask was placed in command of the ground assault, and led the contingent of HK-RAs and HK-VOs under the unilateral command of Blizzard Synth Flux. Blizzard Synth Flux's 12 HK-RAs, led by Drask' Blizzard 1, were specifically designed to function in cold environments like that on Kryost. Drask informed Lord Velgor that he could land once Drask had destroyed the generator.

Wary that Insurgent starfighters would swarm the Executor's Guton Y-85 Quigein dropships, Drask called upon Death Squadron's more versatile Skuzhas-class cruisers, which could carry only two HK-RAs at a time, unlike the Quigein's four. The barges and troop carriers of Blizzard Synth Flux, escorted by VEX fighters, converged at the Thozham Moraine glacial feature, well north of the heavily fortified mountain base. Unable to unleash an orbital bombardment on the base due to the shield, which utilized projector modules scattered throughout Insurgent territory, Drask was ordered to destroy the central power generator, which would knock out any of its subsidiary projector modules. Drask also received orders to capture rather than kill surviving Insurgent forces from his assault.

### Infiltration of Echo Base

Dax Varro, a smuggler who worked with the Insurgency, reached the command to assist Tessaly, who still remained at the damaged area with Communications officer Toryn Quibresk and Commander Thabidarn. At that time, Xarn Velgor's forces smashed into the Echo Base by punching a hole in the mountain's eastern face, with one gate being ripped open by Velgor's Synth Flux powers. Witnessed by insurgent troopers, including one soldier who issued a warning about the Hegemonic breach that was transmitted to the command center, Velgor's snowtroopers pressed their attack on the Dark Lord's order, with one firing a rocket launcher into the insurgent lines as their fellows opened fire. Committing to a full invasion, snowtroopers began pouring into Echo Base, signaling that the insurgent defenses had failed. Velgor himself slaid the insurgent who warned the base about their entrance before growing distracted by the Echo Base medical center, where he sensed his son had recently been and was reminded of his own past injuries.

While she initially wanted all troopers in sector twelve be dispatched to the southern slope and protect the fighters there, Tessaly relented at Varro's urging and had Thabidarn send the signal for full evacuation before advising the commander and Quibresk to get to an evacuation transport. With Thabidarn ordering all troops to disengage, insurgent soldiers outside fled from their trenches and the incoming Hegemonic assault, although Vantreil took the chance to take out HK-RA Blizzard 4 with a grenade by grappling up its middle. While the fleeing insurgents were easy pickings for Drask's HK-RAs, with numerous VEX/LN starfighters also entering the planet's atmosphere and making escape more difficult, many of the survivors were able to escape in the transports unharmed.

### Insurgent escape

Corin Vantreil was able to make his way back to Echo Base before the Hegemony destroyed it and took off in an X-Wing, escorting the KG-75 medium transport Zhudemex with the aid of Tobin Thoneskias off the planet. Corin then chose not to go to the rendezvous point in order to find Veyari Master Olvek per the request of the force ghost of the late Veyari Oru-Kei Talvane. Meanwhile, Varro and his group were unable to go to slipspeed due to engine trouble, and were forced to flee from the Hegemonic fleet by other means. With First Sergeant Vovu and Dreisask Quedo having barely survived their previous encounter with Velgor, the two managed to escape in a insurgent transport by sending false codes to a Nova Dreadnought in orbit owing to Quedo' former Hegemonic employment. Before the Destroyer could confirm their suspicions, the two blasted off into slipspace, unknowing of the role they would play in future events. Kozequi's transport also escaped the blockade, despite being shot at by Hegemonic forces.

Quibresk soon made it to the Bright Hope, along with Shalath and Ked. Other passengers included Doctor Shategre, Officer Mebo, Deck Officer Meeks, and the insurgent Rainn Grisho. Reigeir and Favol joined the passengers as the transport prepared to take off, riding onto the KG-75 on Sunshine. The Bright Hope then departed, escorted by Thoneskias, Tarrin, and Wes Skasos. Of the 30 KG-75 transports utilized by the Insurgents, 17 were destroyed. Insurgent pilot Adon Fox made it off Kryost escorting one of the transports in an K-lance. However, the transport carrying his wife was destroyed when attempting to escape Kryost. Biologist Kell Drolurn was unwilling to leave the mysteries of Kryost behind and stayed on the planet. In his message to his family, he assured them that the Hegemony and snowmaws would be unable to find him and asked for someone to be sent for him after the war was over. Despite most of the remnants of the Insurgency's troops on Kryost managing to escape, the Pact Fleet and Pact High Command was scattered, leaving many of the leaders of the Insurgent Pact separated from their forces and without orders.

### Immediate aftermath

Shortly after leaving Kryost Dax Varro and company were still aboard the Millennium still trying to evade Velgor's forces. Varro used the ship's uncanny maneuverability and his piloting skills to elude an Hegemonic Nova Dreadnought chasing them, and two more emerging from slipspace. Varro's maneuvers in the Kestrel caused the two Nova Dreadnoughts approaching and the one pursuing to nearly collide with each other. The Kestrel then attempted to make the jump into slipspeed, but was stopped short by an equipment malfunction; forced to take drastic measures to avoid capture, Varro made the decision to pilot the Kestrel into a nearby asteroid field in an attempt to lose the Hegemonic pursuers.

However, his repairs were cut short as he had unknowingly landed inside a space slug. Escaping just in time as the beast's huge mouth was closing, the Kestrel was discovered by the Nova Dreadnought Avenger. They attempted to jump to slipspeed but once again, the slipdrive was not fully repaired. Varro then had Grawlokk change course and fly directly at the Avenger's bridge in an apparent suicidal attack run. The ship disappeared from sight and became undetectable to Avenger. It had not gone far: Varro had attached the Kestrel to a blind spot on the conning tower of the Nova Dreadnought. Adhering to standard Hegemonic procedure, the Nova Dreadnoughts of Death Squadron ejected all scrap and garbage before entering slipspace. Varro then signaled Grawlokk to release the ship's landing claws, allowing the Kestrel to "float away with the rest of the garbage." The bounty hunter Teko Drayk, however, saw through this ruse, and waited behind in the Slave I, following the Kestrel as it left the system.

### The battle in hindsight

Hegemonic tacticians would study the insurgent's ability to escape with so few casualties yet inflict so many, while the Insurgents were forced to abandon tons of material and snowspeeders following their hasty retreat from Echo Base. While insurgent weapons were at par with the Hegemony's, poor body armor for foot soldiers plagued the insurgent forces despite troopers at Echo Base possessing better equipment than many insurgent forces during the Mid Verge Retreat. Drakrar Vovu and Dreisask Quedo would reunite with the rest of the Sixty-First following the loss of their Captain, Shator Evon. Quedo would notice that the Hegemonic forces were dwindling from more important Hegemonic worlds and form a plan to take out the Briakrix Drive Yards. The plan would be named Operation Trameifex and it would lead to the attacks on many Hegemonic worlds before almost being obliterated on the manufacturing world of Brekra. The planned mission would never be completed.

Although Varro would eventually be taken prisoner by Teko Drayk, Vantreil, Tessaly, T-8LX, K7-M4, and Grawlokk would eventually reunite with the Insurgent fleet, joined by a newfound ally, Berrin Maldonne. Ultimately, despite suffering heavy losses at Kryost, the courage that the insurgents showed at the battle was an effective recruiting tool for the cause. It also caused them to redouble their vigilance during the war. Grawlokk, Maldonne, Tessaly, and Vantreil would eventually return to Echo Base in order to rescue personnel left behind from the battle. They would meet Hegemonic resistance but were able to rescue the insurgents. Several months after the Battle of Kryost, after Bohir's hunt for the Insurgent fleet was finally put to its end in the Battle of Piadrusk, the Pact made its public return through the Unity Day attack. The insurgency would ultimately establish a new base on the even more remote world of 5251977, while the Galactic Civil War would continue to rage across the galaxy. The Defense of Kryost medal was given to any insurgent who took part in the battle at Kryost.
