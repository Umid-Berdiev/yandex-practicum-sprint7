# Varruk

Varruks, known in their own language of Pahodisk as "the People of the Trees," were a species of tall, hairy humanoids that were native to the planet Ruwokka. A notable member of this species was the warrior Grawlokk, Dax Varro's best friend and co-pilot, who played a vital role in the Replica Wars during the Battle of Ruwokka, the Galactic Civil War by aiding the Insurgent Pact in their fight against the Galactic Hegemony, and the war against the Prime Mandate. They were quite strong, and were known to rip people's arms out of their sockets when provoked. Though being from a temperate planet known for its swamps and forests, Varruks were perfectly comfortable on icy worlds like Gokas and Kryost, not requiring any additional protective clothing, i.e. gloves or boots.

## Biology and appearance

Varruks were a tall species of furry humanoids from the planet Ruwokka, who could grow to a height of nearly three meters. They were covered from head to toe in a thick, shaggy coat of hair with water-shedding properties that notably came in shades of brown, black, gray, and white. The species had two sexes, female and male. The latter grew long beards in adult life. The average male weighed 150 kilograms while the average female weighed 100 kilograms. The Varruks' eye color ranged from blue to brown. Varruks were big eaters, with the average adult requiring 3,500-6,000 calories a day of food. Their diet consisted of wild plants, berries, meat, and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot.

While Varruks did not like eating blosphi extract, it provided them with enough nutrition to survive. The "Varruk flu" was a sickness that affected Varruks. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Varruks was a grove of Varruks. Many of their kind were known to understand Galactic Basic but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

### The Replica Wars

Varruks originated on the Mid Verge forest planet of Ruwokka. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the Replica Wars and the reign of King Skikefu, the Varruks remained fiercely loyal to the Galactic Concord and fought alongside them in several star systems. Towards the end of the conflict, Varruk forces also defended their homeworld from a Secessionist invasion with the help of Galactic Concord forces led by Veyari Master Olvek, who had good relations with them.

### Age of the Hegemony

At the end of the Replica Wars, numerous Varruks were slaughtered by the replica troopers they allied with in the ensuing chaos of the anti-Veyari Directive 91, and soon after the establishment of the Galactic Hegemony, their homeworld of Ruwokka was blockaded by the Hegemony. The Age of the Hegemony saw the softening and repeal of anti-slavery laws, which ultimately led to the Hegemony classifying the Varruks as non-sentient. The Hegemony enslaved the Varruks not because they were a meaningful threat to the Hegemony but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Varruks were enslaved and made to build much of the Hegemonic war machine, sent to be worked to death in the dangerous spice mines of the planet Queimeisk, or on construction sites such as the Void Core, though a number escaped this fate. Numerous Varruks were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for Grand Vizor Tragem Krabrei, Hegemonic governor of Ruwokka, who occasionally hunted live Varruks for sport.

### Joining the Insurgency

During the Galactic Civil War, several Varruks, including Grawlokk and Foseshiam, fought for the Pact to Restore the Concord and its successor government, the New Concord. One year following the Battle of Thalwen, the Varruk homeworld had become a Hegemonic remnant known as Hegemonic territory G5-623 led by Grand Vizor Krabrei. Ruwokka was later liberated by the New Concord forces. Working with a group of insurgents led by Dax Varro and Grawlokk, the former Hegemonic loyalty officer Quekes Rath Quokreth, used a slipspace transceiver to neutralize Krabrei's inhibitor chips. This enabled the Varruks to rise up en-masse against the Hegemonic occupiers and take their well-earned revenge, with Grand Vizor Krabrei himself being killed by his personal slave, Reidreikruth. The Hegemonic forces attempted to orbital bombard Ruwokka but were defeated by a New Concord fleet led by Mirae Tessaly and Admiral Sihen.

Varruk and New Concord forces then conducted mopping-up operations against the remaining Hegemonic holdouts on Ruwokka. During one of these skirmishes, Grawlokk reunited with his son, Mabrobrul, who had escaped from a slave labor camp run by Commandant Bokre.

### New Concord era

During the New Concord era, Ruwokka's forests including the Black Forest grew back. Many Varruks including Grawlokk and Chief Skeskesox lived in villages. About two years after the Battle of Leinesk, the rogue Pau'an surgeon Thohel Gor began kidnapping and killing Varruk younglings to use as spare parts for his mechanoid followers, the Original Dozen. Due to his shadowy appearance, he earned the nickname the "Long Man." Grawlokk agreed to help Dax Varro and Berrin Maldonne retrieve the Jeithen Redux Transmitter in return recovering the limbs of the murdered Varruks for funeral rites.

## Society and culture

The name for their species was known to translate from Pahodisk into Galactic Basic Standard literally as "the People of the Trees". Living in a great harmony with the nature of Ruwokka, the Varruks placed great value upon the wroshyr trees of their home planet; they considered the mighty trees the true owners of the land, themselves merely coexisting with them, and would consult them before every battle by bowing their heads against their wood to listen to their wisdom.

Varruks were expert craftsmen who blended off-world technology like pulsers and arcblades with traditional materials such as wood, precious stones, and metals. Most Varruks carried bowcasters as weaponry, which were handcrafted. Grawlokk's quarrelgun broke Varruk tradition due to its automatic cocking system. Varruks believed that seeing a can-cell brought good luck, and Varruks often kept them as pets. The Varruk people by and large hated Skarrims, great hunters who turned their talents against the Varruks and hunted them. The highest way a Varruk could compliment someone was to groom them. Varruks were not known to be subtle.

## Varruks in the galaxy

Varruks were referred to as mongrels by others. Notable Varruks included Grawlokk, the navigator and long-time companion of Dax Varro. Other noteworthy Varruks included the chief and general Hokir (who fought alongside Master Olvek during the Battle of Ruwokka), the warrior Vokomam, and his son Semorn. Synth Flux-sensitive Varruks included the Veyari Master Zhono,Veyari Master Kruvrasol, and Veyari Tirren Fetadeix Krishokrox, all of whom lived in the High Concord Era, as well as the Veyari youngling Skovrix, who lived during the Replica Wars. During the Age of the Hegemony, Zaskinon was a Varruk who worked as a bounty hunter for both Gorvo the Zogg and Xarn Velgor.

The first Varruk Mirae Tessaly ever met was a guest at one of Senator Dorn Ragreim Tessaly's state dinners, with the Varruk leaving an impression on the young Mirae. Gripadra was a Varruk who served in the Velaris insurgent cell. The Varruk Triarorn played an important role in turning the tide of the Battle on Vranoveir by freeing several rancors, which rampaged the Hegemonic lines. He later took part in Dax Varro and Grawlokk's campaign to liberate Ruwokka. While serving as a senator in the New Concord's Galactic Senate, Mirae Tessaly once saw two Varruk commuters in front of her on an automated sidewalk at the New Concord senatorial complex on Vutre Prime.
