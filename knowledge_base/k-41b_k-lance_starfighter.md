# K-41B K-lance starfighter

The K-41B K-lance starfighter, also known as the K-41B space superiority fighter, or K-41B K-lance multi-role starfighter, was a single-seat craft manufactured by Guton Corporation and used most famously by the Pact to Restore the Concord against the Galactic Hegemony. Renowned for its speed and maneuverability during the Galactic Civil War, it became the backbone of the Insurgent Pact Starfighter Corps, being both harder hitting and tougher under fire than its main adversary, the mass-produced VEX/ln space superiority starfighter of the Hegemonic Navy.

Years following the destruction of the Galactic Hegemony and the establishment of the New Concord, the K-41B was initially retained by the New Concord Defense Fleet before eventually being replaced by newer K-lance models, such as the K-52 K-lance starfighter, and the K-63 K-lance.

### Overview

The K-41B K-lance starfighter, referred to as the K-41B space superiority fighter, or K-41B K-lance multi-role starfighter, was manufactured by Guton Corporation. It was designed to excel in all aspects of starfighter combat, being highly maneuverable and heavily shielded, with a small profile that made it harder to hit. Unlike other starfighters the K-lance could excel in atmospheric flight. This included outmaneuvering the VEX fighter, which was otherwise faster and more maneuverable than the K-lance in space. The K-lance was classified as a starfighter, specifically a snub-fighter due to its size. The starship weighed 18,000 kg.

It had two pairs of wing-like strike foils, or S-foils, mounted at the rear of the craft on opposite sides. Normally, the foils on each side locked in place flush against each other; during combat, however, the foils were folded out. This gave the craft its distinctive "X"-like appearance when viewed from the front or rear. The S-foils were controlled by an S-foil servo actuator located in the back of the starfighter. With the S-foils open in attack position, the craft had a greater spread of fire as opposed to whilst shut.

### Weapons and defense

The craft was armed with four Heizu & Bomox KX9 laser cannons, placed at the tip of each wing. The laser cannons could be fired singly, in alternating pairs or all at once. By default the convergence or "zero" point where the lasers would intersect was set 500 meters in front of the K-lance. Two Jidrarn MG7 proton torpedo launchers were located in grooves near the middle of the underside of the fuselage on either side of the K-41B.

A deflector shield generator located in the rear of the K-lance created a protective field which could be adjusted around the craft with the help of a Hozom "Defender" deflector shield projector. This included shield projectors along the leading edges of the S-foils which, when in attack position, helped to expand the protective field. A Tranegox "Screamer" sensor jammer provided additional defense by scrambling the sensors of enemy starfighters and homing warheads. The K-lance could also use its laser cannons to shoot down enemy missiles.

### Systems

Four 4L4 fusial thrust engines were attached to the wings of the K-41B, next to the fuselage. Some K-lances were fitted with 4j.4 fusial thrust engines, which were virtually identical to the 4L4 models. These provided the K-lance with incredible maneuverability thanks to three factors: the use of differential thrust between the four engines to adjust trajectory; a high-mass electromagnetic gryoscope fitted to each engine for swinging through tight curves; and the ability to fire retro-thrust forward through the turbine nozzles. Infrared suppressors were used to conceal the hot exhaust.

The K-lance itself was powered by a Keimukei 04-Z cryogenic power generator located in the center of the ship. Cryogenic capacitors located in the S-foils stored additional power for the engines. Power couplings allowed for energy to be distributed and balanced between the various systems.

### Origin

The K-41B K-lance starfighter was envisioned by its designers as a fighter with both the speed and power to take on Hegemonic Nova Dreadnoughts, ultimately delivering on both promises. It managed not only to become the backbone of the Insurgent Pact Starfighter Corps, but incorporated lessons learned in assessing the performance of Replica Wars–era winged starfighters, such as the popular Z-95 Motheper and the powerful ARC-170 starfighter.

Ironically, the Guton Corporation had first designed the K-lance for use by the Hegemonic Navy. The Galactic Hegemony had asked for a new rugged, single-pilot starfighter to help fill out its ever-expanding fleet, to which Guton was obliged to fulfill. Taking inspiration from the Z-95 and ARC-170, Guton engineers created a prototype that combined aspects of the two earlier designs. After receiving Hegemonic approval, production began on the new K-lance line, only to be halted. Ostensibly the Hegemony had found new concerns with the starfighter which caused the stoppage. In truth Guton had been sidelined in favor of Maskum Fleet Systems and their (much cheaper) VEX series starfighter. Faced with a glut of expensive starfighters going unsold, Guton was more than pleased when agents of the Insurgent Pact eventually contacted them about making use of the K-lance through methods that remained confidential to the greater Pact.

### Galactic Civil War

Several T-65Bs were kept at the Pact's hidden fortress on Orvax 4. K-lances were used alongside J-lances in the Battle of Quubem, aiding the insurgent fleet above the planet to help Stray One steal the Void Core plans. During the Battle of Orvax, Corin Vantreil was assigned to an K-lance for the battle, the goal of which was to destroy the Hegemony's approaching Void Core. Although many of the K-lance pilots were killed, Vantreil was able to launch his proton torpedoes into the battlestation's exhaust port, causing a chain reaction and destroying it. Tobin Thoneskias was the only other K-lance pilot who survived.

Aside from the Insurgent Pact, the Partisans, a loosely-affiliated group led by Dorrak Dreihix, utilized the K-lances as well, although they were given extensive paint jobs matching their organization, with several of them being used by the Cavern Angels, as well as Kianosk.

### Legacy

In the years following the destruction of the Galactic Hegemony, newer K-lance models, such as the K-52 K-lance starfighter would eventually fall into the hands of the Defiance, and the K-63 K-lance would see use in the New Concord Defense Fleet. Despite the wide-spread retiring of T-65s in favor of newer models, Corin Vantreil, now a Veyari Master, continued to use Red Five as his main starship.

While training a new generation of Veyari, Vantreil lost his students when the umbral tide adept Vrask drew Vantreil's nephew, Tov Varro to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. Becoming "Zerek Vash," Varro and the Knights of Vash killed all of Vantreil's students and burned his Veyari temple. In search of the First Veyari Temple, Vantreil went into exile and traveled aboard his K-lance alone. After traveling to Krelath-To, he purposely ditched his K-lance in a shallow inlet, leaving himself stranded on the island, and cut himself from the Synth Flux, until Kessa came to be his final apprentice before dying and becoming one with the Synth Flux.
