# Corin Vantreil

Corin Vantreil, a Synth Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Veyari Master who fought in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Galactic Hegemony. Along with his companions, Princess Mirae Tessaly and General Dax Varro, Vantreil served as a revolutionary on the side of the Pact to Restore the Concord—an organization committed to the downfall of the Galactic Hegemony and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Vantreil became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Veyari in galactic history.

The son of Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil and Senator Naira Corvelle, Corin Vantreil was born along with his twin sister, Mirae, in 19 BVC. As a result of Corvelle's death and Edrik's fall to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, the Vantreil children were separated and sent into hiding, with Mirae adopted by the royal family of Belisar while Corin was raised by his relatives on Sarrakesh. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Vantreil joined the Insurgency and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane, whose first apprentice was Corin's own father. During the Battle of Orvax, Vantreil saved the Pact from annihilation by destroying the Hegemony's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Veyari Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Olvek. After his master's death, Vantreil participated in the Battle of Thalwen in 4 AVC, during which he confronted the Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Edrik Vantreil. With Corin's help, Edrik returned to the radiant tide of the Synth Flux by killing the Hegemon and Dark Lord of the Morkai Xarn Nyxarion at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Chosen One. Following the Battle of Thalwen, Vantreil trained his sister as a first Veyari apprentice on Devrosk Hoshor, but Mirae ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Morkai's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Hegemony, Vantreil became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Veyari Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Thalwen, Vantreil was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared Grand Vizor Nathurn Rarukrian in the Rokroth sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Leinesk. Around 6 AVC, Vantreil committed himself to the liberation of the Skulox and their homeworld Hokim from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AVC, Vantreil rescued Sena, a Kordathian Synth Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Olvek, from Vizor Fabroth's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Pavei. During the training, Vantreil was visited by Edrik's former tirren, Ilyra Seshu, where they discussed Sena and how Vantreil sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Veyari path. After Sena's caregiver, Kol Lodia, visited the planet bearing a gift for Sena, Vantreil offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Kordathian, or take Olvek's arcblade and continue with his Veyari training. Sena chose to return to Lodia, while Vantreil bid Seshu farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 AVC, Vantreil had taken on Tov Varro, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Viakrebu with the explorer Trubo San Papeisk to study lost Veyari lore, Varro began to be groomed by Vrask, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Vash. In 21 AVC, Vantreil started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Vozix. Vantreil journeyed to Tanisk, where he saw a vision of Vozix and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Edrik Vantreil appeared next to him. Following this, Vantreil was able to defeat Sizhurn and the spirit of Gaskosk Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Vozix after failing to track down an assassin called Dide.

In 28 AVC, sensing the extent of Vrask's corruption in Varro and glimpsing a dark future, Vantreil momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his arcblade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Varro's fall to the umbral tide. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Vantreil that the time had come for the Veyari to end. Vantreil therefore sought exile on the distant world of Krelath-To, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Prime Mandate. His solitude was interrupted in 34 AVC, shortly after the fall of the New Concord, resulting in a chain of events that led Vantreil to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Prime Mandate. Before his death, Vantreil declared that the Veyari Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Leinesk scavenger known as Kessa.

A year later, Vantreil's spirit communed with Kessa during the final days of the war. The Veyari apprentice had exiled herself to Krelath-To, following in Vantreil's example. However, he urged Kessa to confront her grandfather, the resurrected Xarn Nyxarion. Believing he had been wrong, Vantreil told Kessa that a Veyari's destiny was to confront fear. Following the Hegemon's destruction, the spirits of Vantreil and his sister watched over Kessa as she buried their arcblades in Vantreil's childhood home on Sarrakesh and adopted the Vantreil name as her own, honoring their legacy and recognizing herself as a member of her chosen family.

### Pre-birth

During the Replica Wars, Veyari Master Olvek set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Leziavreisk, the ancient Morkai homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who guided his journey told him that there was another Vantreil, and Olvek heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events alluded to the birth and destiny of Corin Vantreil, as well as his twin sister Mirae.

### Early life

After his father, the former Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, had fallen to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux and injured his wife, the senator Naira Corvelle, she was taken to the asteroid field of Ostra Dreya by Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane to seek medical attention. In the surgical suite of the medical facility on Ostra Dreya Base, Corvelle was dying despite being otherwise healthy. Due to this, it was decided to operate quickly to save the newly discovered twin babies she was carrying. Corin Vantreil was born on 19 BVC, two days after the proclamation of the Galactic Hegemony. He came out of the womb crying, and in her last dying breaths, Corvelle gave him the name Corin. She stroked Vantreil's arm before giving birth seconds later to his sister, who Corvelle named Mirae. To protect the children from the Hegemony and their father, the now-Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor, it was decided that the twins should be split up. Oru-Kei Talvane volunteered to take the infant Corin to be raised by Edrik's stepbrother, Halder Odrin and his wife, Dramiax on the planet Sarrakesh, while Senator Dorn Tessaly agreed to adopt Mirae together with his wife, the queen of Belisar. Talvane boarded the late General Kharzul's starfighter, the Soulless One, once more flew to the planet Nar Bamos and sold it there to transport Corin Vantreil to Sarrakesh. There, Talvane bought an eopie and rode it the Odrin homestead, giving the swaddled baby to Dramiax Odrin. She brings the baby to her husband Halder, who fauns over the child before looking out to the twin sunset of Sarrakesh.

Corin Vantreil spent his youth on the Odrin' moisture farm, where his uncle unsuccessfully tried to discourage his adventurous tendencies, telling him that his father had been "a navigator on a spice freighter" during the Replica Wars. His aunt, Dramiax, would read him storybooks. One day, when his uncle had business in Kar Deli, she took Vantreil to Grekatu to use the Nitiarn connections there and show him images of forests, rivers, and oceans.

### Joining the Insurgency

In 1 BVC, Vantreil was working on the family farm by repairing moisture vaporators with one of their WED Zegibro repair mechanoid when he spotted a battle over Sarrakesh; unknown to the young man, it was the Insurgent starship Zhavian IV being chased by the Hegemonic I-class Nova Dreadnought Devastator. Heading to Zhusun Station, Vantreil swapped stories with his friends, among them a visiting and recently-graduated Sethevim, who was reluctant to believe Vantreil's claims about a battle upon looking up with Vantreil's macrobinoculars and merely seeing the two craft sitting still in space. Nonetheless, Vantreil was then eager to tell his friend of his recent exploits aboard his skyhopper in Beggar's Canyon, which Sethevim was amused by. After warning his friend not to crash the ship, he leaned close to Vantreil to tell his old friend a great secret: he intended to defect from the Hegemony to join the Insurgent Pact Starfighter Corps.

Promising to keep an eye out for him, Sethevim and Vantreil then parted ways once again with the hope that they could reunite one day. A short time later, the purchase of two mechanoids—K7-M4 and T-8LX—by Vantreil's step-uncle changed the young farm boy's life forever. Obtaining the mechanoids from a band of Skrits to help with various tasks around the homestead, neither of them were aware that the mechanoids had narrowly escaped from the battle Vantreil had glimpsed, and that K7-M4 contained a set of plans for the Hegemony's dreaded Void Core superweapon. While cleaning the two new mechanoids back at the homestead, Vantreil learned that they had been involved with the Insurgent Pact, and witnessed part of a desperate message from Princess Mirae Tessaly requesting help from someone named "Oru-Kei Talvane," whom K7-M4 claimed that he once belonged to.

### Hero of Orvax

Despite his assistance in the fight, Varro largely divested himself from the Pact after the Battle of Orvax. When Vantreil heard that Varro had camped out on Skotro, he and some members of the Insurgency briefly went there to meet him. Vantreil approached Varro and Grawlokk on the Meridian Kestrel, the latter roughing him up when he insulted the ship. Despite Varro's prodding and boasting of getting his reward money, Vantreil insisted that he was not running away from Insurgent life and had just come to see him. He asked Varro for a favor to deliver a few crates to the Insurgency's surveillance outpost on Hujetoth. Varro insisted that he was not a part of the Insurgency and did not want to do it, saying he had already done a mission for Tessaly and saved Vantreil's life at Orvax. Vantreil insisted that it was serious and that lives were at stake as Insurgent pilots started to bring the crates aboard the ship. Vantreil pointed out that Varro was the best smugger they had. While Varro tried to insist that they did not have him because he was not a part of the Insurgency, Vantreil thanked the pilots for bringing the crates on board as he left. Varro ended up successfully completing the mission.

Soon after the Battle of Orvax, in 0 AVC, Vantreil, Varro, and Grawlokk were sent on a mission by Princess Tessaly to negotiate supplies from the planet Rion. Tessaly put Vantreil in charge, which surprised and peeved Varro. Once arrived on the planet, Varro noticed that a big price could have been won by winning a swoop race. Vantreil, however, thought that it was better to fulfill the mission without being distracted and risking to loose money.

### Insurgent Commander

Entering the battle, Vantreil called out to Rogue Eight to warn them that someone was on their tail, but their K-lance was destroyed. Varro advised the squadron to stay close to the destroyers, and as they did, Vantreil noticed the decrepit state of the Kestrel and asked what happened to it, Varro merely replying that Xarn Velgor got to it and warning Vantreil that he was still there somewhere. After Varro's plan paid off, Vantreil made a joke to him by calling him an Admiral, which Varro told him never to do again. From the base, Tessaly hoped out loud that Vantreil would spread the word after learning that he was out there.

Knowing that they needed to spread the word to the other cruisers that they could launch their fighters but not wanting to split up to land on them, Vantreil told Rogue Squadron to form up on him as he made a run past Sihen's ship with his display trail on, ignoring Thoneskias' warnings against it. As Sihen watched, his attention grabbed by the display trail, Vantreil flew toward the bay doors on one of the cruisers, which opened. Seeing this, Sihen moved to communicate this so that all of the cruisers could deploy their fighters. Varro then announced to Vantreil that the Kestrel was too damaged and so he was flying a K-lance.

### Traveling to Murgath

Vantreil then traveled to Murgath, which turned out to be a remote swamp planet, where he encountered Olvek, the former Grand Master of the fallen Veyari Order. In their initial meeting, Vantreil was frustrated and impatient and was rather short-tempered with the diminutive Olvek, who did not initially identify himself and purposefully tested Vantreil's patience, telling him a story about three brothers, and taking him back to his hut to partake in a meal of rootleaf stew, which he did not find very appetizing.

However, Vantreil gained a new appreciation for the small creature upon learning his identity, and Olvek agreed to train Vantreil only at the insistence of Talvane's spirit. Vantreil's training proved to be difficult, as his adventurous past had shaped his mind toward action and emotional instinct.

### Velgor's revelation

Upon his arrival at Sky Haven's East Platform, Vantreil felt Velgor's presence, and went into the nearest building, where he was shot at by Drayk, who had come to claim a bounty put on Varro by Gorvo the Zogg, and tried to pursue a group of shocktroopers and Hegemonic officers who held Tessaly, Grawlokk, and T-8LX captive. The princess attempted to warn Vantreil that he was walking into a trap, but his path was diverted to the carbon-freezing chamber where Xarn Velgor awaited him, with the intention to freeze him in ferrolite to present him to the Hegemon. Vantreil engaged the Dark Lord in a fierce arcblade duel.

During the duel, Velgor praised Vantreil's training in the Synth Flux thus far and attempted to goad him into giving into his anger and hatred, claiming that it was the only way to defeat him. Velgor managed to knock Vantreil into the carbon-freezing chamber and appeared to have frozen him like he had Varro, but Vantreil leapt out of the chamber before the process was finished, which left Velgor impressed.

### An uncertain path

Upon their return, Maldonne found Vantreil training to fire a pulser with his prosthetic hand. Observing the would-be Veyari's struggles, Maldonne suggested that they return to Velmora so as to retrieve Vantreil's arcblade. As they talked, Vantreil was suddenly confronted with a vision of a female hooded figure catching his arcblade and telling him to follow his destiny.

Vantreil, accompanied by Maldonne, Tessaly, and K7-M4, returned to Velmora, Maldonne assuring Vantreil he knew where his arcblade ended up. However, they were fired upon the Velmora Wing Guard upon approach, though Maldonne urged Vantreil not to fire on them and instead take them in as close as possible. Vantreil was sure that they were gonna get killed, insisting destroying the Void Core was different when Maldonne suggested he could do it. Fortunately, getting close enough meant Maldonne was able to remotely disable the city's turrets, but the team were soon ambushed by shocktroopers upon landing. Needing to carry out their missions as quickly as possible to avoid capture, the insurgents split up, with Vantreil and K7-M4 heading to the smelting chamber where Maldonne claimed his arcblade should be located, walking past the spot where Velgor cut off his hand. When they got there, Maldonne commed him asking for backup against shocktroopers, Vantreil agreeing to help once he found his arcblade.

### Veyari of the Insurgency

While Vantreil was away, Bohir referenced her meeting with him to Ulla, as she believed Ulla to the most arrogant insurgent she had ever met next to Vantreil. After he returned to the Pact, Vantreil took part in an attack on the Hegemonic Unity Day Parade on Bar'leth, one of the Insurgents' first large scale operations since the Battle of Kryost. Tessaly and the rest of the Pact hoped it would announce to people that they still existed and could still strike back against the Hegemony, hoping to do so without harming civilians as well. Vantreil flew in his X-Wing as part of Red Squadron during the attack. After destroying the Hegemony's vast artillery on display in the parade, Vantreil told the others over comm that K7-M4 had detected a wave of VEX fighters launching from a nearby Hegemonic airbase outside of the city and suggested they leave. Tobin Thoneskias, flying as Red Leader, acknowledged Vantreil and told everyone to follow him, leading all of the Pact fighters to a successful escape.

Afterward, Vantreil participated in a toast to the Insurgent Pact lead by Thoneskias before having a drink and sitting down. He spoke with Tessaly, telling her that they had earned the celebration because the attack had been their first solid win in some time and was done without casualties. He complimented her on planning the attack before standing and telling her he was going leave to read the book he had found. He explained that it was full of incredible things and that he could not stop thinking about it, adding that even holding it made him feel more connected to the Synth Flux. He was then approached by Visko Veinotrith, who asked him to show off some of his "magic" because it made them feel like they could not lose and had the light on their side. He was reluctant to do so because it made him feel like a show-off, but he agreed, igniting his yellow arcblade. Veinotrith called it magic while many looked on in awe.

### The last of the Veyari

In 4 AVC, Vantreil set in motion the plan to rescue Varro from Gorvo the Zogg due to their failure to do so during the Attack on the Executor. Once again, Vantreil returned to Sarrakesh, and to Talvane's hut, wherein he found the missing parts he needed for the construction of his own arcblade, the pieces left to him by Talvane. While his friends bustled about preparing for the rescue, Vantreil retreated into the solitude of a desert cave they had chosen as their rendezvous point. Using the Synth Flux to assemble the weapon, he brought the vethra crystal inside to life, making the weapon his own, almost an extension of himself.
