# Ilveren

Ilveren was a bountiful planet in the Shiatrotre sector of the Mid Verge, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Far Verge Territories. It was home to the indigenous Heihis species and to a population of humans known as the Ilveren. Ilveren was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Morkai Deito Malverin, who served as its representative in the Senate of the Galactic Concord.

Under the guise of his Morkai title Xarn Nyxarion, Malverin secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Merchant Combine, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Federation's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Senate for both Malverin and the newly-elected Queen of Ilveren, Naira Corvelle. Corvelle led a successful resistance movement and removed the Federation from her world during the Battle of Ilveren. Malverin subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, with Corvelle eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Ilveren would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-galactic Replica Wars, which saw several military engagements against the League of Independent Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Malverin had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Galactic Hegemony. Malverin's reign would eventually be ended by the Pact to Restore the Concord, an insurgent movement led by Corvelle's children, Mirae Tessaly and Corin Vantreil.

Despite his origins on the planet, Malverin did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Ilveren was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder, a protocol that the Morkai Lord had put in place to destroy Hegemonic worlds should they fail to protect their Hegemon. Through the intervention of Tessaly and Queen Gronein Mojol, however, Ilveren was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Pact, the New Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Prime Mandate during its war with Tessaly's Defiance.

## Description

Ilveren was a small pastoral world in the Mid Verge, located near the border of the Far Verge Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Core Worlds. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Heihiss, who built their homes in the waters of Ilveren, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Kreskiriar. Despite this, certain Heihis navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Ilveren's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Ilveren a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Ilveren's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Ilveren was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

## History

Originally, Ilveren was home to the amphibian Heihiss. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Heihiss. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

Approximately around 4000 BVC, Gelatrei people had discovered the planet, which they had named Drosusk, after one of their principal deities. Ilveren quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Mid Verge, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Heihiss retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

### Twilight of the Concord

The fourteen year old Naira Corvelle was elected to be the new Monarch of Ilveren after Queen Nadraso decided to only have a single term. While Nadraso favored isolationism, Corvelle wanted to promote friendship with other planets in the Shiatrotre sector, leading to her hosting the Shiatrotre sector summit. Additionally, after being urged to by Captain Nozus Queibask, Corvelle took on a group of Handmaidens, whom she quickly befriended, planning her days with them instead of Queibask.

Right as the Ilveren summit ended, about ten years before the Replica Wars, Ilveren was blockaded and invaded by the Merchant Combine. Corvelle guided her home planet through the invasion and sealed a new friendship with the Heihiss. In an upsurge of sympathy and solidarity toward the invaded planet, the queen's emissary in the Galactic Senate, the seemingly unassuming Senator Deito Malverin, was made Supreme Chancellor of the Concord.

### The dark times

Following the rise of the Galactic Hegemony and Malverin's self-proclamation as Hegemon, Ilveren hosted one of the new regime's military garrisons. Shortly after the end of the Replica Wars, thousands of citizens gathered in the streets of Quillan in order to assist to Corvelle's funeral procession. At that time, Mabos Tharn had finally decided to retire from governorship over Ilveren, setting the stage for an election that Jugen was seemingly going to win. However, Ilveren's internal politics were also interfered with by the new regime; while the people were still permitted to elect their monarchs, those who gained the office had little real power or influence. The position of governor was also abolished. Instead, all major decisions regarding Ilveren were placed firmly in the hands of the sector's Vizor, a position held by Malverin's close ally and former Security Forces officer Nozus Queibask, who was appointed before the election for governor could even happen.

Some time around 18 BVC, Lovi Zhunal and his adoptive daughter Krofeth Jotreir bombed Hegemonic vehicles on an Ilverenian bridge as an early act of their insurgency.

### A new era

When news of Hegemon Malverin's death during the Battle of Thalwen—which resulted in the apparent defeat of the Hegemony by the Pact to Restore the Concord—spread across the galaxy after the battle, an outpouring of joy occurred on Ilveren, the inhabitants of which considered themselves free at last. While it quickly became apparent that the war was not over as the Hegemony survived, even without its Hegemon, Ilveren was liberated from Hegemonic control. Nonetheless, all equipment stored within Malverin's small countryside retreat was stripped bare by the time it was explored by the New Concord, preventing the government from discovering whatever secrets Malverin had kept within.

Some twenty days after the Battle of Thalwen, the Royal House of Ilveren received a visit by Princess Mirae Tessaly of Belisar, who had been sent by Sel Aldren, Chancellor of the Pact, which was in the process of reorganizing into the New Concord. In the royal palace's throne room, Tessaly met the then-Queen of Ilveren, Gronein Mojol, and asked for Ilveren's support in the establishment of the New Concord and the restoration of the Senate. The queen heartily accepted Tessaly's offer, claiming that the Ilveren felt great shame over Malverin's actions.

### Rise of the Prime Mandate

During the Cold War nearly thirty years later between the Prime Mandate and the Defiance, Senator Broveis Baquer represented Ilveren in the reconstituted New Concord Senate, and was killed when the Prime Mandate destroyed Vutre Prime, the home of the Senate at the time.

By 35 AVC, Ilveren's skies were being patrolled by Nova Dreadnoughts from the Prime Mandate as part of its expansive efforts to establish galactic dominance. At some point later, Ilveren engaged in open insurgency against the Prime Mandate, simultaneous with other planets such as Velmora and Velaris leading to the successful overthrow of the entire regime.

## Society and culture

Ilveren was inhabited by two societies: an indigenous species of intelligent amphibians called the Heihiss and a group of peaceful humans who were referred to as the Ilveren. In the distant past, a cultural misunderstanding resulted in a serious quarrel between the Heihis and the human colonists. Both sides refused to acknowledge their responsibilities in the rift, and the two groups ostracized each other for the longest time. That situation lasted until the Invasion of Ilveren by the Merchant Combine forced the humans and Heihiss to join forces.

Ilveren culture fostered the artist in every citizen, encouraging children to become musicians, performers, painters, and similar professions. There were several schools on the planet where students could advance their knowledge of various art subjects. The planet also featured a shipbuilding industry, using ore imported from the nearby planet Gorereirn.
