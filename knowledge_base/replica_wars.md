# Replica Wars

The Replica Wars (22–19 BVC) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Galactic Concord and the League of Independent Systems, which had seceded from the Concord. Also referred to singularly as the Replica War, they were named after the replica troopers used by the Grand Army of the Concord against the Secessionist Mechanoid Army. Due to the high volume of battle mechanoids deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Mechanoid War. Furthermore, the existence of the League granted the conflict other names, such as the Secessionist War, Secessionist insurgency, and Secessionist uprising. With both replicas and mechanoids being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Xarn Nyxarion, under his alter ego Deito Malverin, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Merchant Combine's invasion of Ilveren to become the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Morkai apprentice Count Varnoth, he sparked the Secessionist Crisis, causing thousands of Concord star systems to secede and form a new government as the League, with Varnoth as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Kharrab, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major slipspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Concord victory when Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil and his Tirren, Ilyra Seshu, secured an alliance between the Concord and the Zogg Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Nejes, Tadern, Jiagreibam, and Thessil. During this time, the Secessionists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concord launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Kharrab, which obliterated Kharrab' battle mechanoid foundries, and a failed Veyari-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Mechanoid Army General Kharzul at Trivrukis. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Kordath and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems—struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Concord Senator Naira Corvelle and Secessionist Senator Mina Quiameim collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Varnoth and Kharzul' bombing of the Concord capital on Velaris. Later, a peace conference on Kordath also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Veyari worked with local militaries, including Sel Dradan's military and Ilveren's Heihis Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent insurgent cell to retake Bretheis from Secessionist occupation. Similarly, the League relied upon factions such as the Juneirn Militia and the Bokrazhann Slave Hegemony. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Traquan and her son, the now rogue Morkai Lord Skorn, strove to exact their revenge on both the Morkai and the Veyari.

During the war's third and final year, the Concord drove the Secessionists into redoubts within the Far Verge Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Far Verge Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Kharzul launched a massive strike on Velaris through the Deep Core. Much of the League fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Count Varnoth, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Nyxarion as part of his plot to lure Vantreil to the Umbral Tide. The League quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Kharzul subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Netho, and the Secessionist Council being massacred on Pyrrhax. This, coupled with the issuing of Directive 91 and the deactivation of the mechanoid army, led to the total capitulation of the League and, with it, an end to the war.

Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved—combatants and bureaucrats alike—the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Xarn Nyxarion, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Morkai Hegemony and to transform the Concord into the Galactic Hegemony with himself as Hegemon by completely eradicating the Veyari Order. Nyxarion was deceiving both sides. Nyxarion's deception was eventually exposed by Nyxarion himself who revealed himself as the Morkai Lord and cause of the Replica Wars. Evading arrest by Joran Ashkar and a cadre of Veyari, Nyxarion would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil, who Nyxarion manipulated to betray the Veyari and join the umbral tide of the Synth Flux and molded into his new apprentice Xarn Velgor. Nyxarion publicly and deceitfully branded the Veyari as traitors and triggered Directive 91, which forced the Concord's replica troopers to betray and execute their Veyari Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Veyari Purge. Malverin then declared an end to the Replica Wars in an extraordinary session of the Senate and proclaimed the transformation of the Concord into the First Galactic Hegemony, and the Galactic Senate into the Hegemonic Senate.

### A millennium-old vendetta

The origins of the Replica Wars dated back to the time of the Old Concord, which reigned over a millennium prior to the war. That era marked a dark age in galactic history, as the Morkai battled their ancient enemies, the Veyari, for control of the galaxy. The Morkai suffered from infighting, which ultimately led to their defeat at the hands of the Veyari in 1032 BVC, but although the Veyari believed they had vanquished their foes, the Morkai had merely retreated into hiding. Xarn Skar, the last surviving Dark Lord of the Morkai, founded a philosophy known as the Rule of Two, which dictated that the entire Morkai Order would be comprised solely of a master and an apprentice. Operating in the shadows, the Morkai accumulated power and influence in preparation for the endgame of their grand plan: galactic domination and revenge against the Veyari.

From the remnants of the Old Concord, a modern Galactic Concord was formed. For the first time, a Galactic Senate afforded political representation to hundreds of worlds and star systems. The Old Concord's armed forces were disbanded, leading the Veyari Order to assume a peacekeeping role within the Concord, serving at the behest of the Senate and its Supreme Chancellor. Over time, the Senate incurred notoriety for its stagnant bureaucracy and corruption, and rifts emerged as the central Core Worlds—including the Concord's capital, Velaris—became reputed for their willingness to exploit the resources of the outlying Far Verge Territories. Adding to the morass of bureaucracy and inaction was the fact that corporate blocs such as the Merchant Combine, the Gonojei Banking Clan, and the Thobrein Union were granted Senate representation alongside the Concord's member worlds.

### The creation of the replica army

With Skorn gone Nyxarion, needed an apprentice. Edrik Vantreil became his desired apprentice in the long-term due to his extraordinary power in the Synth Flux because he was the prophesized Chosen One. However, because Edrik was too young, Nyxarion decided to have an apprentice in the interim until he came of age. As such, he made former Veyari Master Count Varnoth, who had already been an agent of his for some time, his apprentice in the interim, although Varnoth was unaware he was just a placeholder.

Nyxarion and Varnoth, the latter of whom adopted the name Xarn Relel, plotted to manipulate and deceive the Concord into a sham war with an opposing state. The Veyari would be forced to serve as generals for a Concord army of soldiers, who would eventually serve as their executioners. Furthermore, the conflict would destabilize the Concord and render it vulnerable to the Morkai takeover.

### Secessionist Crisis

Eight years after the Invasion of Ilveren, a pan-galactic League of Independent Systems began to take shape when Count Varnoth commandeered a Nitiarn relay station in the Drudax system and berated the Concord with condemning rhetoric. Over the next two years, a Secessionist Crisis arose when thousands of star systems seceded from the Concord and flocked to the League. The Secessionist Crisis sent the Concord into a frenzy, particularly as Varnoth repeatedly spurned Malverin's public requests for negotiation. The Concord Senate soon became divided over the passage of a Military Creation Act. Advocates of the bill argued that the Concord required a military force to defend itself from the Secessionists, whereas others such as now-Senator Naira Corvelle believed that the measure would provoke full-scale civil war between the Concord and the League. Such a war became increasingly inevitable, with the Morkai Lords poised to lead both sides of the approaching conflict that would tear the galaxy asunder.

Fearful that such meetings would grant the HKX state legitimacy, the Concord Senate also declared it illegal under the Concord Senate non-communication law for Concord representatives to meet with Secessionists in formal talks. By 22 BVC, the Morkai had nearly completed preparations for open conflict between the Concord and the League. Nominally the League functioned as a democracy, and delegates from its myriad member worlds formed a Secessionist Senate, though in truth it was ruled by the Morkai. At the same time, the galaxy's major corporations pledged their support and resources to the League, and their leaders thereby earned seats on Varnoth's Secessionist Council. Its members included Chairman San Hill of the Gonojei Banking Clan, Foreman Wat Tambor of the Thobrein Union, Magistrate Skajei Suni of the Corporate Pact, Presidente Pajeth Vrofix of the Commerce Guild, and Archduke Shapan the Lesser of the Shoquosu hives, as well as Senators Po Thiashath of Leisho and Fala of Sel Dradan.

### Year one (22 BVC–21 BVC)

The Battle of Kharrab commenced when Ashkar and his Veyari strike team infiltrated the Quizeha arena to save Corvelle, Talvane, and Vantreil from gladiatorial combat with three beasts: an acklay, a nexu, and a reek. While the Shoquosu spectators fled the arena, Varnoth unleashed a massive infantry of B1 battle mechanoids, B2 super battle mechanoids, and droidekas against the Veyari. During the ensuing ground battle, Council member Grezia Quajeim came within seconds of ending the conflict when he nearly managed to kill Varnoth, but Rogan Drayk gunned him down before he could do so. Ashkar thereafter beheaded Drayk, an act that would earn him the ire of his son, Teko. Within a short span, only Corvelle, Talvane, Vantreil, Ashkar, and a fraction of their Veyari allies were still standing. With his battle mechanoids encircling the ring of survivors, Varnoth demanded their unconditional surrender, but Ashkar refused to allow himself and his comrades to become hostages.

Just as Ashkar and the other survivors were about to be executed, Olvek swept in with reinforcements, the Grand Army of the Concord: 192,000 replica troopers aboard Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ships, with MNFT/i gunships ferrying them into the Quizeha arena to defend and extract the surviving Veyari. Corvelle, Talvane, Vantreil, Ashkar, and the other surviving Veyari withdrew from the arena aboard Olvek's gunships and rushed to Kharrab's red sand plains, where a ground campaign was taking shape. Veyari such as Shalax Treimer, Kit Didrian, and many others immediately accepted command positions and led the replica troopers into battle against the Secessionist Mechanoid Army, working to decimate the enemy infantry and prevent the Thobrein Union's Skologa transports from escaping. While Olvek proceeded to the Concord's forward command center, Ashkar oversaw an assembly area, where Replica Commander Ponds handed him command of five special commando units. Colonel Lohex Gebres and other top strategists from Concord High Command advised the Grand Army on its battlefield tactics.

### Year two (21 BVC–20 BVC)

In the Mid Verge, Ilveren became the site of the League's foray into biological warfare. Operating out of a secret Merchant Combine laboratory beneath Ilveren's eastern swamps, Doctor Sefix Jaquu succeeded in weaponizing the Blue Shadow Virus, a lethal waterborne plague with no known cure, as an airborne strain. He also manufactured viral bombs to release the plague to key Concord star systems. When the Royal Ilveren Security Forces discovered a patrol of Jaquu's battle mechanoids, Naira Corvelle and Nib Nib Tolk returned to help Queen Loshujin and Captain Lerial Vrivran investigate the potential incursion. Corvelle and Tolk traced the battle mechanoids' point of origin to the eastern swamps, where Jaquu's forces captured them.

In response, Talvane, Vantreil, and Seshu led a raid on Jaquu's laboratory to rescue Corvelle and Tolk, as well as prevent Jaquu from escaping with his cache of bombs. Captain Tarn and the replica units Calm Tree, Snow Wolves, and Wildfire provided tactical support for the Veyari's operation. After saving Corvelle and Tolk, Vantreil aided Talvane in apprehending Jaquu. In addition, the Concord task force's cadre of replica ordnance specialists managed to disarm Jaquu's entire supply of viral bombs before he could release the plague to Ilveren. Talvane and Vantreil thereafter escorted Jaquu to Ilveren's capital, Quillan, to stand trial for his crimes.

### Year three (20 BVC–19 BVC)

As the war's second year progressed, a cycle of betrayals and vengeance consumed the Morkai hierarchy, which in turn affected a number of campaigns between the League and the Concord. At the Battle of Brekra, Mebril Hokrijoth spearheaded the Secessionist navy's strike on a Concord fleet. Flying her personal Defask-class fanblade starfighter, Hokrijoth led the mechanoid tri-fighters of Trident groups one and two in overwhelming the Resolute. Greiniar Reskar narrowly escaped from his flagship before it went down. Building on her success, Hokrijoth gave chase to Oru-Kei Talvane and Edrik Vantreil in their Delta-7B interceptors. However, both Talvane and Hokrijoth crashed in the hangar of the Secessionist command ship, with Vantreil following them there.

From afar, Xarn Nyxarion sensed Hokrijoth's growing power in the Synth Flux, and he recognized her as a threat. Suspecting that Varnoth was training Hokrijoth as a means of supplanting him, Nyxarion demanded that Varnoth prove his allegiance to him by eliminating Hokrijoth. Though reluctant to do so, Varnoth obeyed his Master's wishes. Varnoth recalled League reinforcements that were inbound to the Brekra system, then betrayed Hokrijoth by informing her that she was no longer his apprentice—and that she would die. Shell-shocked from Varnoth's betrayal, Hokrijoth dueled Talvane and Vantreil until Varnoth ordered DF-912, the tactical mechanoid in charge of the Secessionist armada over Brekra, to sacrifice their flagship and obliterate it.

### Last days of the Concord

Thanks to the Emergency Powers Act from the beginning of the Secessionist Crisis, Malverin remained in power for the entirety of the Replica Wars, although his prolonged time in office did begin to draw suspicion from the Veyari as the war reached its end and relations between the Veyari and the Chancellor continued to break down. As per his plans to transform the Concord into an autocracy with himself at its top, Malverin used the Replica Wars to further cement his political power and undermine the democracy he claimed to represent. By the end of the war, Skorn had come to believe the Concord was long since dead and there was nothing the Veyari could do to save it. Indeed, while senators like Sel Aldren, who would oppose Malverin's power grabs, were distracted by the conflict with the Secessionists, Malverin, Traga, and his other supporters waged a war of their own in the field of public relations, securing the love and mandate of the people.

Malverin managed to amend the Galactic Constitution again and again, always using the war as his excuse to secure more power for himself. Although the Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill was defeated in the Senate, a number of other emergency war powers were passed, with Malverin becoming the most powerful chancellor since the Loquarn Reformations. In particular, four amendments to the constitution were cemented in law: one of the four was the Reflex Amendment, which granted Malverin expansive powers by ceding near-total power over the Concord Military to Malverin, who could thus bypass the Senate on military decisions. The Sector Governance Decree was an amendment passed near the end of the war on the claim that it would bring unity and sure up the supply line; under the decree, Malverin was granted the ability to name individuals regional governors, who would then unify neighboring star systems and focus their resources on the production of war materiel.

### The new era

The cataclysmic Replica Wars had raged for three, almost four, years by the end and accomplished exactly what Malverin had set out to achieve; the Veyari Order was slandered and destroyed, the Concord was transformed into the first Galactic Hegemony, and the Galactic Senate into the Hegemonic Senate, with Malverin as its Hegemon. Nevertheless, during the following early months of the Hegemonic Era, the Replica Wars were not entirely over, as minor pockets of resistance, including a force of insurgents on Juneir, continued to fight the Concord's successor state. Accordingly, the newly formed Hegemonic Military was dispatched to crush these remaining obstacles to bring about the Hegemony's view of peace. The galaxy at large, however, welcomed the Hegemony as a way to move forward from the destruction and devastation of war. Mobon Trade League member Jet Tribrorn was not surprised by the rise of the Hegemony, but reflected solemnly in a journal that they did not expect the people of the galaxy to wake up to the price they were paying in return for the conflict's end.

In the aftermath of the conflict and with the dawn of the Hegemony, Senator Tessaly reflected that he was unsure if there was a "right side" to the Replica Wars. In regards to the HKX, he still opposed what he deemed the cruel, destructive tactics of the HKX military and the greed of its corporate sponsors, but he did recognize there were well-meaning people amongst the Secessionist state. Nonetheless, he still believed that non-violent routes had still been open for the Secessionists instead of deciding to go to war. As for the Concord, meanwhile, he had come to ponder if the war had been a misdirection of sorts by those in the government who already wanted to transform the government into the Hegemony. He recognized that Malverin and his followers had pushed for the fighting—something Malverin had tried to cover up with public apprehension about going to war—and had his own thoughts on Varnoth, although he believed the particulars of Varnoth's situation were not as important. Overall, he was forced to admit that, by taking part in the war, himself and everyone else involved had played into Malverin's plan to establish the Hegemony.

### The unofficial conclusion

In 2 BVC, a last unofficial skirmish occurred when the Spectres insurgent cell encountered a Secessionist holdout of still functioning battle mechanoids, led by the super tactical mechanoid Puparn, while attempting to scavenge the wreckage of a Secessionist supply ship. Puparn took the Spectres prisoner and, upon confirming that the group contained Replica Captain Tarn, who had joined with the insurgency instead of supporting the Hegemony, Veyari Knight Jorem Rufem, and his tirren Skagruth Hollin, forced them to run through a tactical scenario, intending to prove that the Secessionists had a higher chance of victory and end the Replica Wars with a victory for the League.
