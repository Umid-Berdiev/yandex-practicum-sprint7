# Battle of Orvax

The Battle of Orvax, also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Orvax, was a major and decisive battle of the Galactic Civil War, in which the Insurgent Pact was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Galactic Hegemony.

## Prelude

During the Battle of Quubem, Skuquem Jogros and Teodric Kriasi managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Hegemonic battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the Pact Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Mirae Tessaly. Hegemonic forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Morkai, Xarn Velgor, soon captured Princess Mirae and the crew of the Zhavian IV.

Princess Mirae was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Velgor to Grand Vizor Hotrern Helvar. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Dax Varro, former Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane, a young farmboy from Sarrakesh named Corin Vantreil, and Varro's First Mate, the Varruk named Grawlokk. The group, which also included Insurgent Pact mechanoids K7-M4 and T-8LX, in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Insurgent base on Orvax 4—closely pursued by Helvar and the battle station.

### The approach

With the Void Core approaching, J-lances from Gold Squadron and K-lances from Red Squadron and Green Squadron were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 J-lances from Gold Squadron as well as 22 K-lances, 12 from Red Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron. The starfighters weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Red Six (Breiquim Hiagron Dudrom) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Hegemonic VEX fighters, including one piloted by Lieutenant Sarra Jebrum, devastated the Insurgent attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Red Four (Jibra D. Zhianin) was the first to be shot down by the incoming VEXes.

During the battle, Insurgents in the temple, including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Driaram Kreske. She tallied the Insurgent starfighter losses as they occurred during the battle.

### Trench run

The first attack run was performed by a trio of J-lances from Gold Squadron. The three J-lances, led by Gold Leader (Jon Safa) with Gold Two (Heihe Kisein) and Gold Five (Griazhax Hozhex) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Velgor. A second trench run was performed by a trio of K-lances from Red Squadron flown by Red Leader (Garven Dekriask), Red Ten (Gudrian Driafis), and Red Twelve (Puck Dibrask). Xarn Velgor picked off Driafis and Dibrask, but Dekriask was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord, Dekriask ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of Red Leader, Red Five (Corin Vantreil) led the final attempt with Red Two (Tobin Thoneskias) and Red Three, Corin's old friend, (Biggs Sethevim), as wingmen. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Velgor and his fighters. One of Velgor's wingmen damaged Thoneskias' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Velgor personally destroyed Sethevim's K-lance, forcing Vantreil to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Insurgent leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his targeting system for the attempt, but Vantreil assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Talvane to rely on the Synth Flux instead for his attack.

### Destruction of the Void Core

Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman Grobror collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The VEX fighter pilot flew into the side of the trench and knocked Velgor away from the Void Core, and this allowed Vantreil to fire his shot. With the aid of the Synth Flux, the torpedoes shot along the trench and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving K-lances, the lone surviving J-lance and the aging freighter made for Orvax 4. The surviving insurgent pilots included Vantreil, Thoneskias, and Gold Three (Visko Veinotrith).

On the Overbridge, unaware of his impending doom, Governor Helvar continued to examine the battle as the technicians counted down the moments until firing commenced. Mere seconds before the nova lance could obliterate the moon, the torpedoes struck the reactor and the station exploded, killing all aboard and saving Orvax 4. The explosion of the Void Core could be seen from the ground of Orvax 8, lighting up the entire sky and leaving debris within Orvax 8's atmosphere.

### Insurgent Pact

Vantreil, Varro, and the surviving Insurgent fighters returned to Orvax, where they held a celebration ceremony and awarded the pair with medals for their achievement in saving the Insurgency. Aldren, having heard of the victory, permanently discarded her contingency plan to surrender to Malverin. Thoneskias' move with which he saved Vantreil's life during the battle would be remembered as the Thoneskias Maneuver.

Shortly after the battle, the Insurgents were forced to leave the site of their victory, fleeing the Hegemony once again. According to the Wild Space cartographer Fediask Didrer, in the days after the battle, the insurgents' evacuation was briefly interrupted by a Hegemonic Nova Dreadnought which threatened to destroy the base before the evacuation could be completed. The insurgents managed to disable the crew of the Nova Dreadnought, however, they soon discovered that its weapons were primed to fire at the base within minutes. Thanks to Veinotrith's efforts, the ship misfired at its intended target, with the evacuation being resumed. Some time later, the Hegemonics regained control of the Destroyer.

### Galactic Hegemony

The Nova Dreadnought Fortitude was caught in the ensuing blast of the Void Core's destruction. Despite surviving the blast, the ship incurred significant damage and loss of life. In spite of the damage, the ship was never brought to a shipyard for repairs, with the damaged sections and the bodies of the fallen Hegemonics being retained as a sort of memorial while the ship continued its service to the Hegemony under the new name Helvar's Will. Lord Velgor, one of the few notable Hegemonics who survived the battle, was recovered in his damaged VEX Advanced x1 by Hegemonic pilots Kruzes Ree and Mijein Sai. Sarra Jebrum, who was outside of the Void Core's explosion, managed to pilot her damaged VEX Fighter to Orvax 4, where she ejected before it crashed into the jungle. There, she patched her wounds and stole a transport from the Insurgent Base during the festivities.

Nearly all blame on the defeat was placed on Helvar and his overconfidence. He had assumed that the station would not need an escort, and had launched no VEXes. The only group had been the single squadron Velgor had launched on his own authority. Regardless, the Grand Vizor was no longer alive to defend himself, and the Hegemony would never engage the insurgents without exploiting their larger numbers compared to their opponent again. The destruction of the Void Core was a major blow to the Hegemony and represented the threat that was the Pact to Restore the Concord. Queigol Vranu was given the rank of Grand General, as Hegemon Malverin had taken note of his argument against using the Void Core as the sole weapon of the Hegemony, and his awareness of the degree of the insurgent threat. Velgor was chastised and personally reminded by Malverin about how he alone was alive to bear the blame of the destruction and failure of the Void Core. He was then ordered to act upon the will of Vranu.

### Further influence

In 1 AVC, Velgor sprung a major trap against the insurgency in the Battle of Mako-Ta, which he described as an attack on the Pact's hope itself to Admiral Rufiarn Vigriarn. The battle would end with major insurgent leaders dead, over half of the cruisers in the Pact Navy destroyed, and over ninety percent of the Insurgent Pact Starfighter Corps lost. In his report to Malverin, Velgor argued his "master stroke," as Malverin called it, was a success despite some insurgent forces managing to escape: whereas the Battle of Orvax had shown weakness in the Hegemony, the Battle of Mako-Ta did the same for the insurgency, with Velgor declaring the galaxy would see it as proof the Pact would never succeed in its crusade. Ultimately, however, galactic sentiment continued to turn against the Hegemony as the war continued.

Even years after the Battle of Orvax, Hegemonic supply lines remained thinly stretched after to the insurgency's victory at Orvax and as the Galactic Civil War continued. Due to those thinly stretched supply lines, the elite shocktroopers of Task Synth Flux 99 found themselves left to their own devices while performing their duties. The squad's leader, Sergeant Trelex, truly believed in the Hegemony and thought its victory in the Galactic Civil War was inevitable even despite the loss of the Void Core; as he reasoned, the Hegemony would be able to build twenty more battle stations to make up for the loss of one, whereas the insurgents lacked such firepower and could not match Hegemonic numbers. However, upon landing on the once-Hegemonic loyal world of Tranu at some point during or after 2 AVC and seeing how its populous had turned on the regime, Trelex came to believe the war was lost: in seeing people flee from his squad in fear and watching children play as the Heroes of Orvax, he believed the hearts and minds of the galactic populous were what mattered, instead of weapons like the Void Core, and realized shocktroopers like himself had hurt the Hegemony's reputation. Consumed by fear that they would be remembered as villains once the histories of the war were written, the squadron turned its weapons on the population.

### New Concord Era

Six years before the destruction of the Vutre system, an unofficial political faction in the New Concord's Galactic Senate known to admire the Galactic Hegemony's rule, the Centrists, questioned the official New Concord historical accounts of the Battle of Orvax and the Galactic Civil War. They believed that the Void Core was destroyed by a Hegemonic traitor and not by Corin Vantreil and the Insurgent Pact. Another Centrist, Senator Kozir Navabron, would later remind Mirae Tessaly how the destruction of the Void Core had cost thousand of fairly-innocent workers and employees their lives while listing the flaws of the Insurgency.

Some time during the New Concord Era, the Brodriabox cartographer Feifir Bravol created a collection of maps that used a calendar system dating all events in reference to the Battle of Orvax.
