# Dax Varro

Dax Varro, known only as Dax until being given the surname Varro, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Pact to Restore the Concord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Meridian Kestrel, a leader in the Insurgent Pact and an instrumental figure in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Galactic Hegemony. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Dax came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Veyari Corin Vantreil and Princess Mirae Tessaly in the fight for the Insurgency. Following the war, Dax and Mirae welcomed a son, Tov Varro, who became the dark knight Zerek Vash after being corrupted by the umbral tide of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Mirae hoped that Dax, as Tov's father, could find a way to break the umbral tide's hold on their son. Dax Varro became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Insurgency.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Fadrelern, Dax yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Triagreith'ra, Dax lived as a street thief with Fadrelern's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Kriagam went bad, causing Dax to flee Fadrelern. He was given the full name "Dax Varro" by an Hegemonic recruitment officer while entering the Hegemonic Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Hegemonic cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Hegemonic Armored Division. On Skushiarn, Dax met the thief Kriaze Shiler and wound up deserting the Hegemony and joining his crew to steal unrefined quorite from Queimeisk in order to pay a debt to Kebria Drel, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Triagreith'ra, Dax was forced to kill his mentor Shiler after a brutal double-cross. Dax also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Berrin Maldonne and the Varruk Grawlokk, the latter who became his best friend. Maldonne owned the Meridian Kestrel, a fast ship with which Dax made the Queimeisk Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Berrin in a game of vantik.

With only Grawlokk remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Feilanian smuggler, Dax Varro embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Gorvo the Zogg, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Sarrakesh. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Hegemony, Varro owed a great deal of money to Gorvo, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dax and Grawlokk were hired to charter Corin Vantreil, Oru-Kei Talvane, and the mechanoids K7-M4 and T-8LX to Belisar, avoiding Hegemonic shocktroopers who were searching for Corin's mechanoids. However, as they find Belisar destroyed by the Hegemony's new superweapon, the Void Core, Varro became caught up in the insurgency against the Hegemony, rescuing Princess Mirae Tessaly, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Insurgent base on Orvax 4, and saved Corin from the Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Orvax, allowing Vantreil to destroy the Void Core. Varro fought with the Insurgency for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Hegemony.

After the Battle of Kryost, Varro sought refuge with Tessaly—with whom he had fallen in love—Grawlokk, and T-8LX on Sky Haven, which was operated by his old friend Berrin Maldonne. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Vantreil to the city. Varro was frozen in ferrolite and taken to Gorvo's Palace on Sarrakesh by the bounty hunter Teko Drayk, where he remained encased in ferrolite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Pact Fleet, Varro was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Thalwen. Varro led the Pathfinders to Thalwen, where he, Tessaly, Grawlokk, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Tumlis, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Pact Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Thalwen, which saw the deaths of Hegemon Deito Malverin and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Insurgency to form the New Concord. In the early days after Thalwen, Varro continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Hegemony. He married Tessaly, and the two had a son, Tov Varro. The boy struggled with the umbral tide of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Corin Vantreil, Tov was Synth Flux-sensitive—so Tessaly sent him to be trained as a Veyari by Vantreil. Tov was seduced to the umbral tide by Vrask, Supreme Leader of the Prime Mandate, resulting in the destruction of Vantreil's attempt to rebuild the Veyari Order.

Having lost their son, Varro left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Meridian Kestrel to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Kessa and a defected Prime Mandate shocktrooper named Brannoc, who carried with them a mechanoid, DD-5, that had part of a map to Vantreil's location. Varro, who retrieved the Kestrel from Kessa and Brannoc, returned to Tessaly, who led the Defiance against the Prime Mandate, and fought in the battle to destroy the Prime Mandate superweapon known as Gehasox Base. During the battle, Varro saw his son, who had taken the name Zerek Vash, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Vash stabbed his father with his arcblade. Mortally wounded, Varro fell to his death in the bowels of the Gehasox weapon. However, Varro's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Synth Flux vision a year later to convince Vash to abandon the umbral tide and defeat the resurrected Xarn Nyxarion, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Kessa and Vash, being enough for Tov Varro to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Kessa end Nyxarion and the Morkai for good.

### Early life

Dax was born in a rough-and-tumble favela on the planet Fadrelern during the waning years of the Galactic Concord in 32 BVC. Sometime after he became famous, it would be rumored by a Shalever bounty hunter that Dax was actually a descendant of the Prince-Admiral Pavath Varro, a member of the ancient Feilanian royalty. During his formative years, his father Ovan worked in the Feilanian Engineering Corporation shipyards, building vessels such as NV-2700 light freighters until he was laid off. Varro's father wished he could leave Fadrelern on such a ship, but was never able to afford to. According to Dax, he was never close with his father. At one point his father brought him to the freighter factory where he worked and pointed out to the young Dax that the ships being built there were his contributions to the galaxy, but that they ultimately didn't matter. He advised him that he wasn't meant to build ships, but rather to fly them. Eventually, his father abandoned him. At some point he met the Brodriabox Niquom.

Around 24 BVC, Dax joined the White Worms criminal gang as a scrumrat. Here, Dax met fellow scrumrat Triagreith'ra. Despite being in service to the gang, Dax showed considerable defiance towards them and their methods. One day, he intervened as a smaller child was attacked by a group of bullies, which led to Dax getting beaten as well. Afterwards, Triagreith'ra asked Dax why he helped the child despite not getting anything in return. Dax answered that while that might be how the galaxy works, it did not always have to be that way.

### Serving under the Galactic Hegemony

Dax and Triagreith'ra managed to reach the Coronet spaceport on foot. There was a strong Hegemonic military presence at the spaceport, where there were long queues offworld. They witnessed several people, including a family, being arrested by shocktroopers. Dax and Triagreith'ra joined a queue. Triagreith'ra was worried about being trafficked by the Crimson Dawn, but Dax vowed to protect her. Since they didn't have identification, Dax and Triagreith'ra used the quorite vial to bribe the Hegemonic Emigration Officer Jiatahe Siadren into letting them through the customs booth. After depositing the quorite, Dax managed to cross through.

Before Triagreith'ra could cross into the other side of the terminal, she was apprehended by the White Worms enforcers Rebolt and Thola. Triagreith'ra told Dax to flee, and he vowed to return for her. Dax managed to evade Hegemonic forces and the White Worms by donning a stolen hat and hiding behind several crates. He witnessed more people being arrested by shocktroopers. Lacking sufficient funds to travel offworld, Dax found an Hegemonic Military recruiting post. Dax spoke to the Hegemonic recruitment officer Drawd Krobrei and volunteered to join the Hegemonic Navy as a pilot. Since Dax lacked a surname and family, he dubbed him "Dax Varro" in the absence of a surname. Approving Dax's application, he told Dax to proceed to Transport ID-83 to the Hegemonic Flight Academy on Dreibras, promising Dax that he would be flying in no time.

### Life as a scoundrel

After Dax and Grawlokk's escape from Skushiarn, Shiler took the transport hauler to the snowy, mountainous world of Mugal. While watching over the snowy mountains from the hauler, Dax thanked Grawlokk for saving him, acknowledging that he only survived because of the Varruk. He shared his plan with Grawlokk, explaining that they would do one job with Shiler's gang in exchange for their freedom. Grawlokk accepted the deal. Dax asked Grawlokk for his name and settled on "Grawl," finding it easier to say than his full name in Pahodisk. Upon landing, Shiler enlisted the two of them for a planned heist to steal a shipment of the hyperfuel refined quorite from a 20-T Keitradil conveyex transport on Mugal. While surveying the train tracks from a distance, Shiler outlined his plan, which involved targeting the conveyex between the tower and the bridge. Rio dropped them onto the conveyex, allowing them to separate the payload container and lift it up onto the AT hauler. Rio jammed their communications signals while Vakom blew up the bridge, providing them an escape route. On the night before the heist, Shiler warned the gang about the threat posed by Queiske Nest and the Cloud-Riders.

While resting by the campfire, Vakom criticized Shiler for involving Dax and Grawlokk, considering them amateurs, questioning why they couldn't have simply hired professionals such as the Lupel sisters or Hiram. Shiler defended his decision, explaining that it was a way to settle their debts. During the campfire conversation, Shiler and his associates questioned Dax about his motives. Dax revealed his plans to become a pilot and return to find Triagreith'ra. Rio expressed his preference for being tied to nobody, while Vakom discussed her relationship with Shiler. Varro then asked Grawlokk what he would do with his share of the profits, with Grawlokk responding that he would use it to help the Varruks on Ruwokka. Shiler promised Dax and Grawlokk that if they followed his instructions, they would have more than enough to buy their own ship, before disassembling one of his pulser pistols into a HM-44 pistol and tossing it over to Dax, who gazed down upon his new weapon.

### Working for Gorvo the Zogg

Eventually, the pair came into the employment of the Far Verge crime lord Gorvo the Zogg, who was based on the desert planet Sarrakesh and was the leader of the powerful Zogg Clan. Varro and Grawlokk smuggled for Gorvo for many years, and Varro became Gorvo's top smuggler. The smuggling duo also spent much time in Sejesk's Spaceport Cantina looking for work.

Varro and Grawlokk were smuggling for Gorvo when they were faced with an Hegemonic blockade. Faced with the choice of either surrendering to the Hegemony or dropping Gorvo's cargo, they dropped the cargo, and escaped to the planet below.

### Galactic Civil War

After dropping a cargo of Queimeisk spice they were smuggling for Gorvo, Varro and Grawlokk had a 50,000-credit bounty placed on their heads. In trying to pay off Gorvo, they traveled to Sejesk's Cantina on Sarrakesh, where they looked for work. Around that time, Varro acquired the mechanoid Zegibro from Skrits and assigned the unit to help maintain the Kestrel. While Grawlokk left the table, Varro took the opportunity to flirt with a B-girl named Jenny. She sat on Varro's lap and the two witnessed a bar brawl between the exiled Veyari Master Oru-Kei Talvane and the criminals Sakram Baba and Regradresk Potron, who had physically assaulted Corin Vantreil, leading Talvane to cut off Baba's arm with his arcblade. As Grawlokk joined them, Jenny realized that Varro didn't even remember her name, leading her to throw the remainder of Varro's drink in his face before storming off.

Afterwards, Talvane and Vantreil made their way to Varro's booth, Talvane having talked with Grawl before the brawl. They arrived and began negotiating. Varro assured Talvane that the Meridian Kestrel was the fastest ship around, because it had made the Queimeisk Run in less than twelve parsecs. Talvane informed Varro that the cargo was only himself, Vantreil, two mechanoids, and no questions asked beyond an admission that they wanted to avoid Hegemonics entirely. Therefore, Varro set the price at 10,000 credits—higher than he normally would have. However, Talvane raised Varro's bid to 17,000 credits: 2,000 in advance and 15,000 when they reached Belisar. His interest piqued at such a sum offered when it was so needed, Varro quickly accepted the deal. The smuggler then told his temporary employer to meet him in Docking Bay 94 for departure as soon as he was ready, before warning Talvane that some Hegemonic sandtroopers had entered the cantina and were looking for Potron and Baba's attacker. Talvane and Vantreil hastily fled, leaving Varro to express his elation to Grawlokk at having garnered such a high-paying job to pay off Gorvo. Grawlokk left as well right after.

### Rescue and marriage

Through her tracker, Triagreith'ra learned that Varro had been brought to Gorvo's Palace and discovered he had been placed on the Zogg's wall as a decoration. Traveling to the Meridian Kestrel, where everyone aboard believed Varro had died in the fall to Jequal, Triagreith'ra revealed Varro's survival and imprisonment in Gorvo's Palace. Tessaly agreed to wait for Triagreith'ra to confirm the time was right to launch a rescue operation, knowing she was needed with the Pact and could not spend time on a rescue mission at the moment. Triagreith'ra promised to reach out, even arguing that Varro would be safe in Gorvo's Palace while the insurgents waged their war with the Hegemony, but Crimson Dawn would be destroyed in the Battle at Bivre Station.

Later, the Insurgent Pact began preparations for the mission. Mirae, Grawlokk, and K7-M4 sought the help of Greipu Kriale, but were forced into a brief skirmish with the Shede bounty hunter Travres. After quickly overpowering him, Mirae took his clothes, helmet, and thermal detonator as a disguise to sneak into Gorvo's palace.

### New Concord

Before the formal establishment of the New Concord, Varro, T-8LX, and the Pathfinders participated in a raid on an DNR black site at the Wretch of Dumor. During the assault, the Pathfinders managed to down an HK-VO walker, which caused a breach in the facility's hall. This allowed Varro and his men to enter the base and secure the computers before the base commander could initiate a purge of the computers. With the aid of T-8LX, the Pathfinders were able to hack into the Hegemonic data files and discovered that the Hegemony had begun to execute Operation: Cinder, a posthumous order issued by the Hegemon to devastate several worlds including Ilveren as retribution for his demise. The Pathfinders' actions enabled the Insurgent Pact to deploy forces to halt Operation: Cinder, saving many lives and worlds.

Several months after the Battle of Thalwen, Dax arrived at Greipu Kriale's castle on Skeitririar to rendezvous with an Hegemonic defector named Triaskiarn Quiateir, who had stolen intelligence crucial to the liberation of Ruwokka. The Hegemony had quickly discovered his treachery and followed him. Recovering the data cubes and fighting their way back to the Kestrel, Dax and Triaskiarn were saved by the arrival of Inferno Squad, buying them time to escape the planet.

### Cold War

At some point after the public revelations about Mirae and Tov's relationship to Xarn Velgor, Dax's son, Tov Varro, was seduced by Supreme Leader Vrask and eventually succumbed to the umbral tide, killing the Veyari Corin was training. Taking the blame for Tov's fall, Corin exiled himself. Unable to cope with the loss of their son, Dax and Mirae separated.

Dax and Grawlokk went back to being smugglers, which Dax claimed was "the only thing he was ever good at." Meanwhile, Mirae became the leader of the Defiance, a paramilitary organization aimed at fighting the Prime Mandate, which had begun to pose a threat to the New Concord. Dax then got himself into a lot of debt with various creditors including the notorious Taki Death Gang and Kabitho.

### Legacy

Shortly after the battle, Mirae asked Kessa to tell her about how Varro died, as she only had sensed it. Kessa told her that while she didn't hear what Varro told Tov, he didn't fight nor call out, to which Mirae assured her that he always knew when to run and when to make a stand. Sometime afterwards, a funeral was held for Varro. During the evacuation of the D'Grarorn base, one of the proton bombs carried by Dreivreil Shofosk's bomber, Cobalt Hammer, succeeded in destroying Captain Moden Theivris's Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought Jogadix. The ordnance crew had a message inscribed on the bomb in Roskeihos, stating "Dax says hi."

After Varro's death, Kessa, Grawlokk, and K7-M4 flew in the Meridian Kestrel to the planet Krelath-To to recruit Veyari Master Corin Vantreil to the Defiance's cause while Zerek Vash recovered from the injuries he sustained during the Battle of Gehasox Base. As Vantreil cut himself off from the Synth Flux since he went into exile, he was unaware of what was happening in the galaxy, so he didn't feel Varro's death, leading him to ask Kessa and Grawl what had happened to his brother-in-law. Upon becoming aware of Varro's fate, Vantreil regretted not having sensed it, leading Grawlokk to hug him before Kessa asked him to train her.

### The smuggler turned general

Dax Varro was a human male who stood at a height of 5 feet and 9 inches as a young adult, though eventually grew to a peak height of 6 feet and 1 inch, before shrinking down to 5 feet and 11 inches tall during his later years. He had light skin, brown hair, and weighed 80 kilograms. Dax was initially a cynical, disagreeable individual whose primary concern was gaining enough money to preserve his own life. He and Corin immediately disliked each other: Varro found Corin irritating and overly eager, and his open disdain for Talvane's beliefs and actions did little to change Corin's initial view of Varro as an extortionist. During his younger, more selfish, days, he was willing to sacrifice a loved one when the situation called for it, as in the case of shooting Kriaze Shiler to save himself despite considering him a mentor. However, he came to change as he met Corin Vantreil and the Insurgency. After Dax demonstrated his bravery on the Void Core and proved his loyalty, they became genuine friends, and soon after, Dax became loyal to the Insurgency. Aside from his seemingly self-centered ways, Dax was actually very loyal to his friends, and a good man. Having endured a difficult childhood as an impoverished orphan with no identity, Dax instantly related to the Leinesk scavenger Kessa, who shared some of his skills.
