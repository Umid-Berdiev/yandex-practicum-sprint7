# Xarn Nyxarion

Xarn Nyxarion, born Deito Malverin and also simply known as the Hegemon, was a male human Dark Lord of the Morkai and Hegemon of the Galactic Hegemony, with his reign lasting from 19 BVC to 4 AVC. Rising to power in the Galactic Senate as the senator of Ilveren, the secretive Morkai Lord cultivated two identities, Nyxarion and Malverin, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Galactic Concord and the Veyari Order through the Replica Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Thalwen. However, the umbral tide of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Hegemon was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Vozix by Kessa, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast replica son Grabroth and his daughter-in-law Bradax.

Born on the Mid Verge planet Ilveren in 84 BVC as the High Concord Era ended, Deito Malverin possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Morkai by Xarn Kribregrein with Malverin taking Xarn Nyxarion as his Morkai persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Morkai Master, in order to train his own Morkai apprentice, Xarn Skorn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Morkai Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Ilveren, a crisis orchestrated by Nyxarion, led to the Senate to elect Malverin as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Skorn, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Ilveren. The Secessionist Crisis and the Replica Wars were masterminded by Nyxarion and his second apprentice, Xarn Relel, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Veyari Purge commenced with Nyxarion enacting Directive 91.

With the Veyari all but extinct, Nyxarion installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Galactic Hegemon and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Relel, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Veyari Knight Edrik Vantreil to the umbral tide and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Pact to Restore the Concord. During the Galactic Civil War, the Hegemon sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Corin Vantreil. However, Nyxarion' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the umbral tide and killed Nyxarion to protect Corin. Thus, with the Morkai's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Edrik's destiny as the prophesied Chosen One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Nyxarion transferred his essence to an imperfect replica vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Galactic Hegemony and replace it with a new Morkai Hegemony, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concord and the emergence of the Prime Mandate, the creation of Supreme Leader Vrask, who turned Tov Varro—heir to the Vantreil bloodline—to the umbral tide, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Hegemon announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AVC, the Defiance invaded the planet Vozix, Nyxarion' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Hegemon discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Varro and Kessa, a Tirren sired from the Malverin bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Kessa called upon the strength of past Veyari to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Nyxarion and his Morkai Eternal cultists.

### Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Xarn Nyxarion were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Verge planet Ilveren as Deito Malverin, eighty-four years before the Battle of Orvax. The dynastic, noble Malverin family belonged to the affluent class in Ilveren society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Malverin did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Malverin was raised in the cultured city of Quillan, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Malverin had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Nyxarion came to study under the Morkai were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Morkai apprentice to Dark Lord of the Morkai Xarn Kribregrein and took on the name Xarn Nyxarion by 52 BVC. As a Morkai Lord, Nyxarion learned all his Morkai Master knew about the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Xarn Skar. While apprenticed to Kribregrein, Nyxarion constructed his own Morkai arcblades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

### Political career and double life

In his public persona as Deito Malverin, Nyxarion took a great interest in politics and the public life of Ilveren. In 65 BVC he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Quillan power generator, with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of senator for his homeworld in the Galactic Concord Senate as part of the Ilveren delegation, being elected in 52 BVC. His role also made him the senator for the rest of the Shiatrotre sector, meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planets. Once elected, Malverin quickly joined incumbent Supreme Chancellor Jebo Drosku's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Ilveren. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired the Concord. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Hotrern Helvar. The duo met for the first time when Helvar was still a cadet of the Brekra Sector Pisubil Academy, and while Malverin was still apprenticed to Kribregrein.

Taking an interest in the younger man, Senator Malverin resolved to help him to join the Judicial Department, according to Helvar's desire but advised him to also consider a political career. In the following years, their relationship became more and more confidential. Malverin often lauded Helvar for his talents and skills and remarked they were very similar: they both came from planets at the edge of the galaxy but were destined to have great repercussions on the history of the galaxy itself. Helvar eventually became one of the few people who called Malverin by his first name.

### War on Ilveren

Nyxarion, as Malverin, moved to seize power from Supreme Chancellor Finis Hogon, who had virtually no political power and counted Malverin as a close friend and ally, allowing for Nyxarion to easily manipulate him. In the Senate, Nyxarion secretly ensured that the Senate repeatedly battled over bills concerning taxation, bringing about voting sessions that ended in both Federation defeats and victories, while Nipath falsely believed Nyxarion was working towards Federation victories all the time. Following a discussion in his office about the latest version of the bill, Hogon requested Malverin to stay behind and confided in him that he was considering having a Veyari step in to serve as an independent moderator, which Malverin appeared to be surprised by before requesting he hold off on that option for the time being. Moving up his plans upon being told of the Shiatrotre sector summit that threatened to strengthen the sector, Nyxarion used holographic communication to convince Nipath to blockade the planet Ilveren, making it seem as though it was in protest of the Senate's taxation of trade routes to outlying star systems. Eventually, driven to desperation by the inactivity of the Senate, Hogon secretly dispatched two Veyari Knights—Master Sael-Rhal Ormis and his Tirren Oru-Kei Talvane—as ambassadors to negotiate with the Merchant Combine's Senereixn leaders.

With Nyxarion pleasantly surprised at the ingenuity the Shoquosus had shown in creating the Federation's battle mechanoid army, the Merchant Combine initiated the Invasion of Ilveren on the orders of Nyxarion, who also instructed Nipath to kill Chancellor Hogon's Veyari ambassadors. When Nipath questioned the legality of the invasion, Nyxarion simply stated that he would make it legal. The Senereixns acted accordingly, but the Veyari survived the assassination attempt and escaped the Federation ship. After Nyxarion terminated his communication with Nipath, Queen Naira Corvelle contacted him concerning the Federation's denial that the Chancellor had sent ambassadors to speak with them as promised. Nyxarion, as Malverin, feigned concern at this supposed revelation, but was cut off mid-sentence by the Merchant Combine's communication blackout, notifying the Ilveren to their invasion.

### Further preparations

Over the next ten years, Supreme Chancellor Malverin amassed a favorable reputation as a mild-mannered servant of the common good, and the public remained oblivious to his true, insidious persona. He kept one of his arcblades hidden in a statue of Nopurn Zeskern, which was coated in neuranium and thus protected from scans, while the other was kept in an unknown hiding place. As Edrik Vantreil grew older, his relationship with Malverin deepened; on many occasions, Malverin lauded Vantreil as the most gifted Veyari he had ever encountered, one who would surpass even Olvek, the Grand Master of the Veyari Council. Vantreil, who grew increasingly frustrated with the strict lifestyle of the Veyari under his master Talvane's guidance, appreciated Malverin's praise and greatly took to his counsel. In truth, this was all part of Nyxarion' plan to gain Vantreil's trust and groom him into his eventual Morkai apprentice.

However, Nyxarion was well aware that his plan to turn Vantreil to the umbral tide could not bear fruit for many years to come, and with Skorn presumed dead, Nyxarion needed an apprentice in the interim. As such, he turned to Varnoth to fill that role, making the former Veyari his interim apprentice under the name Xarn Relel, although the latter was unaware that he was just a temporary apprentice. Nyxarion and Relel gained control of a behavioral modification biochip that Skenem-Vriadrian wanted instilled in each replica as a safeguard against rogue Veyari, and the two then reprogrammed the chip for Nyxarion' purpose. Subsequently, bounty hunter Rogan Drayk was hired as the genetic template for the replica army on Thessil, and the replicas were fitted with the Morkai's reprogrammed biochip, the latter containing a protocol that would ensure their total obedience to Nyxarion when the time came for it.

### The Replica Wars

Under Nyxarion' direction, Relel rallied several thousand solar systems into declaring independence from the Galactic Concord, instead joining the League of Independent Systems with himself as its leader. The League was established in 24 BVC with the Drudax Address, in which Varnoth blasted the Concord for its corruption and Core Worlds favoritism. Within two years, as more and more systems left the Concord to join Varnoth's new state, civil war seemed to be imminent and the Veyari's numbers were too few to maintain galactic peace. Around 23 BVC, Varnoth's sister Rezei, who had become aware of his dealings with Nyxarion but only knew him as "the hooded man," urged her brother to abandon his plans, warning him that Nyxarion was corrupting him. Varnoth instead ordered his assassin and apprentice Mebril Hokrijoth, who at that time was unaware of Nyxarion's existence, to kill his sister, which she reluctantly followed through with.

In response to the beginning of the Secessionist Crisis, the Senate passed the Emergency Powers Act, which allowed Malverin to remain in office past the end of his term and remain for as long as the crisis lasted. Afterwards, Malverin established the Loyalist Committee with the claim that they would act as his personal advisors, bringing in senators such as former Queen Naira Corvelle, who was by that time the Senator of Ilveren, Dorn Tessaly, Ask Zathol, and Orn Free Taa. As the crisis continued and the Senate grew fearful, many in the Senate pushed for a vote to create a Grand Army of the Concord, proposing the idea in 24 BVC. Keeping his true motives a secret, Malverin resisted all of the Senate's attempts to create an army and the push to go to war with the League. Debate over the Military Creation Act continued for a further two years.

### Subverting the Concord

As per his plans to use the Replica Wars as an excuse to consolidate and centralize his political power to make way for his reign as Hegemon, Malverin had become the most powerful Supreme Chancellor since the days of the Loquarn Reformations by the end of the conflict. Using the war effort as his excuse, Malverin had the Galactic Constitution amended multiple times during the conflict. One constitutional amendment known as the Reflex Amendment granted him near-absolute power over military affairs, thereby allowing him to bypass the Senate in such decisions. Towards the end of the war, Malverin passed a constitutional amendment known as the Sector Governance Decree.

Under the Sector Governance Decree, Malverin created a new structure of regional governance by appointing certain individuals as governors. In theory, the decree was designed to shore up the war effort by uniting star systems into singular blocs and transforming their production centers into war-focused installations. In reality, however, regional governors could become leaders of the systems they oversaw, allowing Malverin to circumvent and one day disband the Senate by creating a new bureaucracy composed of individuals he sent out. The decree, however, proved to be too much for senators who formed the Delegation of 2,000, which quietly assembled the Petition of 2,000 in the name of protesting Malverin's conduct and growing powers. Led by Corvelle, Aldren, and Tessaly, the Delegation hoped to make Malverin give up his emergency powers and intended to present their petition to Malverin once it was signed by two thousand of their fellow legislators.

### Age of the Hegemony

After the Veyari Temple fell, Nyxarion arrived within and congratulated his new apprentice after affirming he had done as commanded; Velgor had killed many Veyari during the siege, including Tirrens and younglings, much to Nyxarion' delight. After assuring Velgor he had done well, Nyxarion ordered his apprentice to bring peace to their newly rising empire, dispatching him to Pyrrhax to wipe out the Secessionist leaders. Their conversation was captured by the Temple's security holograms. Nyxarion then contacted the Secessionist Council to inform them that Velgor would arrive and take care of them, although none of them were aware that Nyxarion had actually betrayed them and had sent Velgor to kill them.

Assembling the Senate in special session, Nyxarion, as Malverin, addressed the Concord and deceitfully accused the Veyari of attempting to assassinate him and overthrow the government, using his newly scarred face as evidence. Affirming that the Veyari insurgency, as he called it, had been shut down, by the loyal Replica Army, Malverin assured the Senate any remaining Veyari would be found and wiped away. Taking advantage of the fear and death he had spread across the galaxy and presenting himself as the man who had ended the war and the supposed Veyari insurgency it had been building to, he announced the Concord's reorganization into the First Galactic Hegemony, promising a destructive conflict would never again be unleashed under its watch. In the process, he declared himself Hegemon of the new regime, with little if any resistance from the majority of the senators present.

### Growing insurgency

In 14 BVC, the secret Void Core project was still being constructed above Kharrab and was supplied by a series of marshaling stations. The Hegemony faced a new threat in the form of Ruriask Teller, a former Concord Intelligence agent who opposed the Hegemony. He led an insurgent cell consisting of survivors and witnesses of the Skadreil Atrocity, a mass crackdown on Skadreil 4 following the Replica Wars that had seen the killing of innocent Jiles and Lafagri loyalists. Teller had a special animus for Helvar, the architect of the Skadreil Atrocity. He was secretly aided and equipped by Vice Admiral Dodd Lokrisk, the Director of the Naval Intelligence Agency and a rival of Helvar.

Following Teller's attack on Sentinel Base, the Hegemon convened a meeting of his Ruling Council to discuss the discovery of a cache of communications jammers on the planet Brelidrel. The Hegemonic Security Bureau had purportedly discovered the cache and feared that dissidents were planning to disrupt the Hegemonic Nitiarn; echoing the Secessionist Petrekos broadcasts during the Replica Wars. The Hegemon dispatched both Vizor Helvar and Lord Velgor to investigate the matter. However, this played into Teller's hands and allowed the insurgents to steal Helvar's corvette Carrion Spike, which they intended to turn into a symbol of resistance against the Hegemony.

### Galactic Civil War

Later that year, Axis agent Biarosk Lodosk, in his role as a DNR director, gained access to a cache of secretly scavenged files maintained by his colleague Tado Krafi; she had collected these in an effort to get the information she needed to root out Axis. Lodosk discovered evidence that the Hegemon's energy program was a cover for the development of a super weapon being secretly constructed by Neven Kafe and was killed after passing on the information to Axis. However, Lodosk's viewing of Krafi's files was uncovered and his body found. Kafe arrived on Velaris to investigate Krafi, meeting with Major Gragum Hotretha who defended her, pointing out how miraculous it was that the secret had been kept as long as it had. Kafe commented that they were only a few tests away from successful completion but Hotretha retorted that he should tell that to Malverin. The information about the Hegemon's project was ultimately passed on to Insurgent Intelligence agent Captain Teodric Kriasi and through him the leadership of the Insurgent Pact.

Around the same time, having deliberately placed a trap inside the Void Core, scientist Aldric Gorex Jogros dispatched pilot Bodhi Rook to Theper to meet with Dorrak Dreihix's Partisans and inform the Insurgent Pact of the existence of the planet-killing superweapon. Fearing that the Hegemonic Senate would learn of the Void Core's existence, Grand Vizor Hotrern Helvar decided to test the Void Core's nova lance on Theper City, killing the traitorous pilot and proving that the station worked. Helvar would relay his decision to the Hegemon, who was waiting for the Governor's report. Director Kafe, however, expressed disappointment that neither the Hegemon nor his favored emissary, Xarn Velgor, would be present for the occasion. This prompted Helvar to explain that their absence served as a protection for Kafe should another failure occur during the testing of the weapon. Despite the animosity between the commanders, the Void Core fired on Theper City, destroying it within moments. Soon after, Kafe asked Helvar if he would report to the Hegemon that his efforts had paid off, only for Helvar to reveal that he would instead inform the Hegemon that he would be taking executive control of the Void Core.
