# Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk

Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk, more commonly referred to as Gorvo the Zogg or simply Gorvo, and formally styled as His Excellency Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk of Skushu Trenim, Eminence of Sarrakesh, was a Zogg male gangster and crime lord, as well as a leading member of the Grand Zogg Council and influential leader of the Zogg Clan, who operated and led a criminal empire from his palace on the Far Verge world of Sarrakesh. Gorvo was a major figure on Sarrakesh, where he controlled the bulk of the piracy, slavery, and trafficking in illegal goods that generated most of the planet's wealth. He was also highly influential in the entire Far Verge as one of its most powerful crime lords.

During the Replica Wars, Gorvo's influence and power over the Far Verge, specifically its sliplanes, was sought by both the Galactic Concord and the League of Independent Systems, who both courted Gorvo's approval. When Geiskar, Gorvo's son, was kidnapped, the crime lord promised his support to whoever returned his son. When the Secessionists were revealed to have orchestrated Geiskar's kidnapping, Gorvo pledged his support to the Concord.

Gorvo continued to operate in the Far Verge after the end of the Replica Wars and the rise of the Galactic Hegemony, preserving his power base, despite the interference of Xarn Nyxarion, the Galactic Hegemon. After the destruction of the Void Core by the Insurgent Pact, the Hegemon sent Xarn Velgor to negotiate a deal with Gorvo to secure raw materials for Hegemonic military production. Gorvo's alliance with the Hegemony allowed him and the Zoggs to resist the Hegemonic crackdown against criminal elements in the Far Verge, as well as rid the Zogg of his competitors.

After the smuggler Dax Varro failed to repay him for lost cargo, Gorvo placed a high price on his head. Varro was eventually delivered to him by one of his bounty hunters, Teko Drayk, as a gift from Xarn Velgor. However, this capture brought him to the attention of Veyari Knight Corin Vantreil, who sought to rescue his friend from Gorvo's imprisonment. As he attempted to execute the Veyari and his allies in the Great Pit of Morir, Gorvo was choked to death by Mirae Tessaly. With the Zoggs unable to decide who would inherit Gorvo's criminal ventures, many of his slaves, including the Viquus, were free, and his palace was occupied by his former Hutetei Ose Sajeim, who took his place as Dodreim of Sarrakesh until Drayk killed and usurped him. Drayk sought to rebuild Gorvo's criminal empire in his own image, intending to rule with respect rather than the fear that the Zogg instilled in his followers.

### Sarrakesh Crime Lord

Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk was a Zogg who hailed from Skushu Trenim. He was born in 600 BVC. He was the grandson of Kiho and Papa the Zoggs, and the nephew of Rokra and Bratel, as they were the siblings of his father. His mother left enough of a mark on him that, years later, he would force the Nehaskarn dancer Gefial d'al' Kekum to wear special makeup to make her look more like his genitrix. He also had a cousin named Zhikron and a nephew named Miaqual.

Several centuries before the Galactic Civil War, he became the reigning crime lord in the Far Verge, owning several criminal enterprises which included smuggling, glitterstim spice dealing, slave trading, assassination and piracy. At some point, Gorvo became affiliated with the Babedros Syndicate. As a result of his collaboration with the Babedros, he gained a position on the Grand Zogg Council. As a crime lord, Gorvo based his illicit operation out of his palace in the Northern Dune Sea of the desert world Sarrakesh, from where he spread his operations and influence across the Far Verge by employing a network of smugglers who moved his goods across the galaxy.

### The High Concord

Years before the Great Disaster, Gorvo, along with other Zoggs, attended a banquet on Kruhemask as part of a treaty negotiation between the Kruhemaskn royal family and the Grand Zogg Council. The Zoggs planted Kefes Punch in the drink menu as a means of inciting a diplomatic incident. When Veyari Master Greinial Gazul-Tashosk, Stellan Gios, and his tirren Maskeskeir Vragial, noticed the Kefes Punch and attempted to save the Zoggs, the Zoggs opened fire on the banquet, claiming that the incident was an insult that justified violent action against Kruhemask.

After the Veyari settled the situation, Gorvo and his fellow Zoggs attempted to escape, only to be stopped by the Veyari themselves. Queen Mohith, Ruler of Kruhemask, stepped in to officially capture Gorvo and his fellow Zogg conspirators for attempting to disrupt the treaty and to assassinate attendees of the diplomatic banquet, putting him for ransom to the Zogg Council.

### Later years of the Concord

Shortly after the start of the Invasion of Ilveren in 32 BVC, Gorvo presided over the Keikol Eve Classic skimrace, where his entrance in the stands was greeted with cheers from the spectators who came to watch the race from all corners of the Far Verge Territories. Gorvo signaled the start of the race, and the skimracers began their laps, at the end of which Edrik Vantreil, a young slave from Kar Zhezes, emerged victorious. Gorvo had to be awoken after falling asleep during the race, as skimracing did not entertain him—aside from the gambling involved.

Ten years after the Ilveren Crisis, the galaxy was plunged into the chaos of the Replica Wars, a massive galactic conflict between the Grand Army of the Concord and the mechanoid armies of the League of Independent Systems. Shortly after the outbreak of the war, Gorvo's infant son, Geiskar, was kidnapped. Though Gorvo did not know who the kidnapper was, it was carried out by his uncle, Rokra, and the Secessionist Pact, both of whom had their own schemes at play. Gorvo requested the assistance of anyone who could help, and the Concord and the Veyari Order answered the call. It was the hope of Supreme Chancellor Malverin that the Concord would be able to negotiate an alliance with Gorvo for use of Zogg slipspace lanes in the Far Verge, as the Secessionists controlled the major lanes.

### Age of the Hegemony

At the end of the Replica Wars, the Concord was transformed into the Galactic Hegemony, and the chancellor declared himself Hegemon. Gorvo continued his illicit operations during the Age of the Hegemony, continuing to hold influence as a crime lord. Around one year into the Hegemony's reign, former Tirren Ilyra Seshu decided against going to Gorvo for help while avoiding the Hegemonic Military, deciding the Zogg would not help her despite her role in saving his son during the Replica Wars.

When the human Safarn Trulei ran a smuggling ring on Nar Bamos, he prospered for years, delivering a portion of the profits as tribute to Seivrix the Zogg, a very powerful figure on the moon. However, this brought a change for ND-5, a FH-series commando mechanoid under his control, when Gorvo appeared, as they used his hyperlines. To repayment Gorvo, who was impressed when ND-5 blasted two of his guards when the Zogg threatened Trulei, Safarn sold ND-5's services to him. ND-5 worked for Gorvo until Safarn began planning his score on Nudreix Bapol. However, the debt that now belonged to the mechanoid was still unpaid.

### Legacy

After Gorvo's demise, the Zoggs were left to determine who would fill Gorvo's seat and take up the reins of his criminal empire. Several months after Varro's rescue, a decision had yet to be made. Immediately following Gorvo's demise, in order to determine the exact nature of the Zogg's death and settle the debate over the matter of his will, the Zogg Clan had the wreckage of the Skidrilus examined. What they found was only a holorecording of Tessaly choking Gorvo in his last moments. Most of the circulated copies of this footage and their vendors were hunted down by the Clan to prevent proof of their own vulnerability from circulating.

Many of Gorvo's slaves were freed after the battle at Morir, leading the prominently enslaved Viquus to admire Tessaly for choking the Zogg, earning her the nickname "Tefabreis." Coupled with the Hegemony's collapse, Gorvo's death left enormous power vacuums in the Queimeisk spice trade, leaving trade routes once guarded by Zogg-hired mercenaries vulnerable. Over the years following Gorvo's death, a Zoggese song known as "Gorvo Flow," composed by the Shag Kava band, was named after Gorvo as was a non-alcoholic beverage known as "Gorvo Juice."

## Personality and traits

Leader of one of the most famous cartels in the galaxy, Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk technically controlled Sarrakesh's underworld. He was legendary for his vicious temper, endless greed, gruesome appetite and fondness for violent entertainment. Because of this, it was common for people to avoid irritating him in any way. On one occasion, when they tried to play Queimeisk Vantik at Gorvo's Palace, the game was canceled when Gorvo appeared, as they preferred not to risk winning against him by betting on the game. Gorvo was an animal collector, interested in acquiring specimens of rare species. The Zogg's sadistic side also extended to mechanoids, with torture being employed. The Zogg was also known to collect various trophies that signified his power. One of the most prominent examples was when he hung Dax Varro's ferrolite-frozen body on the wall of his throne room as a decoration. Unlike his uncle Bratel, Gorvo had remarkably poor table manners. Gorvo had a tattoo of the Kruskoske kajidic symbol on his right arm.

Gorvo had an affinity for young female humanoids, often enslaving them as dancers or companions. He would chain his favorite slave girls to his throne and force them to sit with him when they were not dancing, as was the case with Nisorn and Mirae Tessaly. According to Jess, Gorvo's favorite slave was better protected from harassment from the palace guards and guests, though that protection came at the cost of dealing with Gorvo's advances. Gorvo's penchant for female humanoids would eventually become his downfall when Tessaly used her proximity to the Zogg to kill him.
