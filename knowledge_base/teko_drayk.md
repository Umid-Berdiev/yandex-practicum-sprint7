# Teko Drayk

Teko Drayk was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Galactic Concord to the end of the rule of the Galactic Hegemony. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered replica of the famed Kordathian bounty hunter, Rogan Drayk, who raised the boy as his son. Teko emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Kordathian armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Danegro-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Rogan. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Drayk was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Hegemon Malverin. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Hegemony and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dhural Raiders of Sarrakesh for a time, he took over as Dodreim of Kar Zhezes during the era of the New Concord.

Teko was created on the Wild Space planet of Thessil as a result of an arrangement between the Morkai Lord Xarn Relel and Rogan Drayk, a Kordathian foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the replica troopers grown from Rogan's DNA, Rogan regarded Teko as his son. In accordance with certain Kordathian traditions, Teko was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Rogan, whose chain code documented his participation in the Kordathian Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Teko. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Teko and Rogan retreated to Kharrab where the latter was killed in battle by Veyari Master Joran Ashkar. Orphaned by Rogan's death, Teko sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Ashkar, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Velaris, Drayk took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Hiram, Hadeis, and Mebril Hokrijoth, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Zev Skar.

In the aftermath of the Replica Wars, Drayk continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Gorvo Kruskoske Skefusk, as well as the Morkai Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Galactic Civil War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dax Varro to Velmora on a contract for the Galactic Hegemony, hoping to deliver him to Gorvo's Palace on Sarrakesh. During the journey, Drayk stopped on the moon of Nar Bamos in order to make sure Varro would survive his time in ferrolite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Drayk regained Varro and delivered him to the impatient Gorvo.

Drayk tried to prevent Varro's rescue by the Pact to Restore the Concord, only to fall into the Great Pit of Morir when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Drayk survived his ordeal with the man-eating gorrath, his armor was sold to Kroliarn Brozhask by Skrit scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Dhural tribe, Drayk eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Kordathian warrior Kol Lodia on Tanisk, who had taken Drayk's armor from Brozhask. After helping Lodia to rescue the foundling Sena from the clutches of Vizor Fabroth, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Sarrakesh where he killed Ose Sajeim and claimed the late Gorvo's throne as Dodreim, with Fennec Morrow by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Drayk intended to rule the territories of Kar Zhezes by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Brebiam Thobrusk such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Vosk Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Drayk overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Zev Skar - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Kar Zhezes's denizens.

### Early life

Around 33 BVC, an extensive replica army was secretly commissioned on Thessil by Veyari Master Skenem-Vriadrian, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Galactic Concord. The army, however, was later taken over by the Morkai in 32 BVC shortly after the Battle of Ilveren and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Veyari Order. Prior to the creation process, Kordathian bounty hunter Rogan Drayk was recruited by the Morkai Lord Xarn Relel. Rogan was to be the genetic template for the replica army. Rogan agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a replica who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Thessilan scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a replica, Alpha regarded Rogan as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Teko, he was the very first of many replicas modeled on the Rogan Drayk template, but was only one of two known replicas that were pure genetic copies of the original Drayk template, the other being Omega.

### Replica Wars

Sometime later, Teko joined with a team of bounty hunters, including Sati Lyth, Hiram, and Jenas to carry out his revenge against Ashkar for Rogan's death. Together, Teko and the bounty hunters crafted a plan to assassinate the Veyari Master aboard the Concord vessel Endurance. Teko posed as a young replica cadet, who was nicknamed Lucky, using the fact that he was genetically identical to the other young replicas to his advantage; no one onboard Endurance was aware of his true identity. Once aboard the Endurance, Teko and the other replicas were greeted by Ashkar and Edrik Vantreil, before being put through tactical training. Each replica was able to engage in target practice, and they all missed their targets—except for Teko who was able to use the skills his father taught him to his advantage.

Later, while on a tour of the ship, Teko slipped away from the other replicas and found his way to Ashkar's quarters, where he quietly installed a bomb that would be triggered by a laser tripwire in the doorway. Ashkar did not enter his quarters, however, but a replica trooper did, killing the soldier in Ashkar's place and forcing the entire ship into alert status. Lyth instructed Teko via comlink to sabotage the Endurance's reactor core, thereby killing everyone on the ship, but Teko was reluctant to kill everyone. His only goal was to kill Ashkar. Nonetheless, Teko did as instructed, sneaking away from his fellow replicas once again, before being caught by a replica trooper who called for security. The two engaged in a brief scuffle near the core before Teko was able to stun the replica with the trooper's own pulser. He also shot at the core command console.

### The ensuing years

At some point during his childhood years, like how he had with Lyth, Drayk learned about the bounty hunting trade from Zev Skar and Fuvron. At one time, Drayk worked for the Skarrim Mesiax Mofial, but the two eventually parted ways. Drayk was also present for a shootout at the Far Verge desert planet Gotrope, during which Drayk took notice of the bounty hunter Sugi and her effective usage of the XS-3 carbine rifle. Inspired, Drayk began to favor the weapon, which he used throughout his bounty hunting career.

As the years passed on, Teko gained a reputation as one of the deadliest bounty hunters in the galaxy. One of his known tactics was disintegrating his adversaries. Teko's armor was also adorned with numerous braids hanging from the shoulder, which he displayed as trophies of his fallen prey. While the bounty hunter only took on certain assignments, he would devote himself to these few jobs with fanatical skill. Teko assumed the mantle Rogan had left as a notorious bounty hunter, as Teko became known as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy.

### Galactic Civil War

At some point during the Galactic Civil War, Drayk killed a insurgent pilot on Havriarn, a planet in the Far Verge. While transporting his bounty aboard a mechanoid mount on the desert world's surface, he came across a campsite inhabited by the bounty hunter Mebrir Quobos, his associate Drabruniath, and another human whom Quobos was about to execute. The group saw and recognized Drayk from a distance, leading the human to flee while Quobos and Drabruniath prepared to fight. Drayk passed straight through the camp, not wanting to get involved in a skirmish without getting paid according to Quobos. Drayk arrived at a town and brought the pilot's corpse into a cantina, where his skills were commended by various patrons, including a Senereixn and a Skarrim. Drayk met with a Metiarn'lek Guild Agent who compensated Drayk 50,000 credits for the job, and suggested taking a deal on the Zazhox Spa to relax. The hunter began looking for new bounties instead, with the agent suggesting that Drayk hunt down Quobos, an accused Insurgent collaborator who had also killed Guild hunters, leading to an 100,000 credit price on his head.

Another bounty hunter overheard the reward and jumped at the chance to take it, but backed down after an intimidating stare from Drayk. The hunter offered to buy Drayk a drink and breathed a sigh of relief when he left the cantina instead. Drayk traveled into the desert and found Drabruniath, who had warned Quobos of his coming and had been mortally wounded so that Quobos could steal his water and survive the march across the Death Plains. Drayk shared water from a canteen at Drabruniath's request, and Drabruniath requested that Drayk kill Quobos for him. He pointed Drayk towards Quobos's path and Drayk spotted him with his binoculars, firing a non-lethal shot towards him, which caused Drabruniath to laugh as he succumbed to his wounds.

### Interregnum

When Drayk fell into the gorrath, he was assumed dead by the galaxy at large. He was remembered amongst his fellow bounty hunters, such as Hadeis and fellow bounty hunter Mercurial Swift who entered into a fight in Coronet City on the Core World of Fadrelern. During their scuffle, Hadeis pulled out his particle array gun and aimed it at Swift. Thinking of a way to survive, Swift pretended to see Drayk on a nearby rooftop and said his name in false confusion, causing Hadeis to turn his gun towards the roof. Taking advantage of this, the younger bounty hunter defeated him. However, he let Hadeis live, as there was no bounty on his head.

### Survival

Upon falling into the gorrath, Drayk found himself being digested with a Skrit and Shiago and quipped if either had a deck of Vantik cards with which to pass the time, which the Skrit happily held up. However, Drayk was knocked unconscious and became trapped in one of its stomachs, beginning the process of being digested for a thousand years. Fortunately for the bounty hunter, he eventually awoke suddenly, gasping for air, and activated his helmet light to discover a shocktrooper who had also suffered the same fate. He yanked out the trooper's air tube and attached it to his own helmet, and took a deep breath. Thinking quickly, Drayk activated his flamethrower to burn through the Gorrath's hide and dug himself out of the pit. Exhausted and suffering wounds from the encounter with the beast, Drayk collapsed near the wreckage of the Skidrilus.

Some time later, the bounty hunter was then found by Skrits who separated him from his armor, which Drayk somewhat helplessly witnessed. When he attempted to resist their attempts at theft, Drayk was knocked out by the Skrits and left for dead. His armor was later acquired by the Sarrakeshian sheriff Kroliarn Brozhask. Still injured and lying in the middle of a sandstorm, Drayk was found by a tribe of Dhural Raiders who took him as a prisoner. Drayk was forced to travel a very long distance by foot, dragged along in chains by a dunebeast ridden by members of the tribe. Some distance into the journey, Drayk gave in to his dire pain, exhaustion, and dehydration and collapsed in the desert once more, dragged against the sand.

### Indebted to Lodia

However, Vizor Fabroth arrived in force, intent on capturing Sena. Two V-82 dropships were sent down from his cruiser and deployed shocktroopers in a skirmish against Lodia, Drayk, and Morrow. Drayk took on several shocktroopers with his cycler rifle and gaderffii, and seeing the Razor Crest open and unguarded, took the opportunity to reclaim his armor. Once backed into a corner, Drayk used a thermal detonator to defeat multiple troopers, now donning his ironveil armor, which he had retrieved from the Razor Crest. Utilizing its full arsenal, including wrist pulsers, knee rockets, and jetpack-mounted missile, Drayk quickly eradicated the remaining shocktroopers and their transports.

Suddenly, a turbopulser from a ship in orbit struck the Razor Crest, obliterating the ship. Four dark troopers then left the assailing vessel, abducting an exhausted Sena before Lodia and Morrow could reach him. Drayk pursued the troopers in Slave I, but Lodia told Morrow to use her comlink to tell him not to attack them so as to not risk Sena's safety. He still followed the troopers, recognizing the vessel harboring them as Hegemonic design before returning to Tanisk's surface.

### Dodreim of Kar Zhezes

Drayk and Morrow returned to Gorvo's Palace on Sarrakesh, now under the control of Sajeim, who expressed surprise at Drayk's survival after he fell into the gorrath pit. Without responding to him, Drayk shot Sajeim dead and threw his corpse off the palace's throne, taking his place on the throne as crime lord with Morrow by his side. Declaring himself the Dodreim of Kar Zhezes, Drayk then modified his armor once again and turned several palace rooms into his personal quarters.

In the following weeks, Drayk experienced dreams of his past, though they temporarily stopped. News of the former bounty hunter's survival spread swiftly across the galaxy. On one morning, Drayk healed himself in a mendgel tank he had installed in his quarters to heal from the gorrath's stomach acid. During the healing process, he relived painful memories of his homeworld Thessil, the aftermath of his father's death, his escape from the Gorrath, and his introduction to the Dhural Raider tribe he became so attached to. Eventually, Morrow woke Drayk up. As he rose from the mendgel tank, Drayk told Morrow that the dreams were back. Morrow told him to prepare for the guests about to arrive. Drayk, with the help of his mechanoids, suited up and prepared for his first public moment as Dodreim.

### Legacy

Drayk was remembered as one of the best bounty hunters in the galaxy, with a reputation and legacy that eclipsed even his father's.

Following the Battle of Jozeis in 34 AVC, Barrik Fuvron mentioned Drayk in his book titled the Galactic Explorer's Guide, including him in the "Rogue's Gallery" section for Velmora. He also included a poster of Drayk and information on Slave I in the planet's section as well, mentioning that the ship had been owned by Rogan, Lyth, himself, and then Teko.

### The young bounty hunter

As a child, Teko developed a strong bond with his father, learning much from Rogan's years as a bounty hunter. Teko was suspicious of Oru-Kei Talvane upon the Veyari Knight's arrival on Thessil, recognizing him as a potential threat to both himself and Rogan. He took pleasure in fighting Talvane both on Thessil and in orbit of Kharrab, expressing joy at the thought that the Veyari had been killed. His personality hardened after Rogan's death, as a traumatized Teko swore vengeance on Joran Ashkar, yet he did not take any pleasure in the collateral damage wrought by his mission. His better nature eventually won over in honor of his father's memory, as Teko turned against his bounty hunting allies and allowed himself to be taken into the custody of the Galactic Concord. Despite realizing the errors of his ways, he swore he would never forgive Ashkar for the murder of his father.

During the Replica Wars, Teko expressed aversion to being compared to replica troopers. He did not want to see himself as being the same as other young replicas or a replica in general, as he saw himself as Rogan Drayk's son. He nonetheless did not care to harm the replicas, and briefly considered leaving his bounty hunter companions to stay with the other young replicas who escaped the Endurance.

### Adult life

As the years went by and the Replica Wars gave way to the reign of the Galactic Hegemony, Drayk became known for his lethality and extreme methods, even displaying trophies of his prey on his armor. During the Hegemonic Era, the confident bounty hunter had a penchant for disintegrations and proved to be ruthless, willing to kill those who had wronged him. He was still able to find enjoyment in life, particularly enjoying the surprise less experienced hunters showed at his mere presence. While working on Havriarn, Drayk received a reputation as a hunter of criminals after saving a village, but he insisted that he was only interested in pursuing bounties regardless of the ideals of his victims. His father's death at the hands of Veyari Master Joran Ashkar caused Drayk to develop an aversion to the Veyari. When Drayk encountered Veyari Knight Wes Jimi on Bragrar, more than a decade after Rogan's death, he told Jimi that he had no pity for the fate of the large number of Veyari who were killed during the Great Veyari Purge. While acknowledging that the two could meet again, Drayk told Jimi that he hoped that would never happen.

Beyond becoming one of Gorvo the Zogg's favorite bounty hunters, Drayk also realized the Zogg may have considered him the closest person in his life to a friend. In truth, Gorvo had his only friend in Seivam Sel. Gorvo also once referred to Drayk as his "pup." Still, on some nights, if everyone else in the palace was asleep, Gorvo shared a bottle of gardulla with Drayk and, after waking up Grenern Jejian and ordering him to play a sad song, tell private, personal stories to Drayk. Even though he made sure to never take off his pulser, the bounty hunter agreed to his boss's request, taking off his helmet and listening. Though Drayk could never understand what Gorvo was telling him, he found the Zogg's company tolerable, considering how much the crime lord paid him for work. Drayk was also none-too-keen on Gorvo's showboating, such as how the Zogg tried to enslave Mirae Tessaly and make her watch the attempted execution of her friends. He was also made uncomfortable by the way Gorvo treated his prior dancer Nisorn, whose name he made himself remember after her death.

## Skills and abilities

Teko's talents as a bounty hunter were infamous across the galaxy, and he became well-known to galactic fugitives who had bounties on their heads. From a young age, Teko was trained by his father in combat skills, both in hand-to-hand combat and weapons, able to handle a pulser at a young age while being deadly in battle scenarios. Teko helped Rogan fight against Oru-Kei Talvane on Thessil, displaying his ability to utilize the heavy weapons on Slave I. His skills grew throughout the years, and despite his body condition, was still capable of using strategy, tactics and brute force to make short work of a shocktrooper contingent with a gaderffii stick. He displayed an affinity for leadership as he formed his own syndicate of bounty hunters. He also commanded the loyalty to those close to him, such as Hiram, and could communicate in Drayk Code with his father, Rogan.
