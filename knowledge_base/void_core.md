# Void Core

A Void Core was a gargantuan space station armed with a planet-destroying nova lance powered by vethra crystals.

### VC-1 Battle Station

The VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, also known as the VC-1 Orbital Battle Station, was a superweapon that was originally designed by the Shoquosus during the waning years of the Galactic Concord. The project was later taken over by the Galactic Hegemony; however, the plans for the battle station were stolen from Quubem by the Insurgent Pact, which led to the weapon's destruction at the Battle of Orvax, but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the Hegemonic security complex on Quubem, Theper City on Theper, and annihilated the entire planet of Belisar.

The name "Void Core" was coined by the scientists who found themselves working on the project, such as Aldric Gorex Jogros. The name was adopted by the Hegemony at large, with Major Gragum Hotretha of the Hegemonic Security Bureau considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."

### VC-2 Battle Station

After the destruction of the first Void Core, the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Void Core constructed by the Galactic Hegemony and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the Battle of Thalwen, the Void Core II's energy shield was destroyed and the Insurgents used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.
