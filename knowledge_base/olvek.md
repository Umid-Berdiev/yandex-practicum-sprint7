# Olvek

Olvek was a legendary Veyari Grand Master who led the Veyari Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Morkai, and during the transformation of the Galactic Concord into the Galactic Hegemony. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Olvek trained generations of Veyari, ultimately serving as the Veyari Order's Grand Master. He played an integral roles in defending the Concord during the Replica Wars, survived Directive 91, lived to pass on the Veyari tradition to Corin Vantreil, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BVC, Olvek served the Veyari Order for centuries, becoming Veyari Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BVC, Olvek helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Veyari and the anti-Veyari Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Veyari Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Veyari High Council to train Tirrens aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Veyari in fighting the Gatis pirate organization in 232 BVC. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Olvek reappeared in 230 BVC on Fadrelern, where he helped his former Tirren Suken Vatreth and other Veyari battle the Gatis, accompanied by a former Veyari who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Nezhiax Lazhosk. As the Gatis had also weaponized the Nameless, Olvek hoped that Lazhosk's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Veyari find a way to fight them.

Olvek was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Veyari High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Joran Ashkar and Briagrax-Gredreil-Skabrom. During the Invasion of Ilveren, in 32 BVC, the maverick Sael-Rhal Ormis introduced Edrik Vantreil to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One. The Veyari elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Vantreil too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Veyari Code. After Ormis's death during the liberation of Ilveren, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Olvek's continued opposition to Vantreil's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Veyari discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Morkai, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Olvek was convinced that at least one more Morkai Lord remained active following Oru-Kei Talvane's victory over the Morkai apprentice Xarn Skorn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Varnoth, Olvek's one-time Tirren—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BVC. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Veyari took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Olvek himself leading an army of replica troopers against the Secessionist Mechanoid Army of the League of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Replica Wars, rescuing the Veyari and Senator from execution. For three years Olvek led the Concord military's war effort as a Veyari General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Sael-Rhal Ormis, who helped Olvek learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Olvek's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Xarn Nyxarion, the Dark Lord of the Morkai who conspired to destroy the Veyari and restore the Morkai to power. After the Great Veyari Purge commenced in 19 BVC, killing thousands of Veyari both on Velaris and across the galaxy, Olvek confronted the self-declared Galactic Hegemon but failed to cut short Nyxarion' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Morkai Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Edrik Vantreil-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Olvek's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Murgath. After the death of Oru-Kei Talvane, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Olvek to train Edrik's son Corin Vantreil. Olvek initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Talvane's request. After being convinced by Talvane to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Corin Vantreil, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Veyari Knight. He trained Vantreil for a time, until despite Olvek's warnings Vantreil hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AVC, with his health rapidly declining, Olvek died of old age when Vantreil returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Corin redeemed his father Edrik, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Nyxarion.

Years later, Corin Vantreil's attempt to reform the Veyari Order would be foiled when his nephew Tov Varro turned to the umbral tide and destroyed his Veyari Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Veyari, Vantreil went into exile, just as Olvek had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Kessa, also abandoned her training, Olvek appeared as a ghost to Vantreil. Olvek taught Vantreil to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

### Early life

Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Thalwen, Olvek belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Olvek's early life; he never revealed his homeworld. As he was keenly attuned to the Synth Flux, he joined the Veyari Order as a youngling.As a Veyari Initiate, Olvek made use of a training arcblade. After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering, he received his own personal vethra crystal, and Professor Grebas helped him construct his first arcblade aboard the Crucible. The circumstances surrounding Olvek and the construction of his first arcblade were exciting, according to Grebas. At some point during his time as a youngling, Olvek skipped across the lily pads in the Veyari Temple's meditation pond.

Although Olvek spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Skavrorux N'Skashox Pavrax Hushir. Olvek was paired up with a personal mentor at a younger age than usual. During their time together, Olvek's Veyari Master told him the tale of K'ungfu and Quipurn, two great Veyari of the past. As a Tirren, Olvek visited multiple planets and during his first years he could have named them all, but in the following centuries, he couldn't remember how many he'd seen and later estimated the number to be in the tens of thousands. Around the age of one hundred, in 796 BVC, Olvek was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Master, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Veyari. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty thousand Veyari.

### High Concord Era

By the High Concord Era, Olvek was considered a great Veyari Master. He had also gained a seat on the Veyari High Council and the rank of Grand Master. Two other Veyari—Fomix Thareim and Gravre-Paskuth Veter—shared the Grand Master title with Olvek. Eventually the human Veyari Thisosk Briagrax-Skareith also attained the position. As the Veyari who taught younglings, Olvek one day met the human youngling Kraske Pede, who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Pede in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Olvek and Pede watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Olvek used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her fear and always get back up to try again.

In 382 BVC, after learning of the Battle of Theper and the failure of a peace treaty between the warring planets Desern and E'ronoh there, Olvek went to the Concord Executive Building on the galactic capital Velaris to meet with with Supreme Chancellor Triazhath Brezovral while they waited for more information from Theper. They spoke to Veyari Master Lekisiax Sun and Veyari Knight Vianox Forte, who were on the ground on Theper and confirmed that the E'roni ambassador was dead and the Deserni had deployed a mechanoid army. They requested to perform an investigation on the Far Verge Territories planet Mafen of the Path of the Open Hand cult, considering their involvement in the failure of the peace talks, the fact that Veyari Master Namibreth Sho felt a disturbance that made the Veyari ill at the Path's almshouse, and that Veyari Knight Kraske Pede believed the Chancellor's criminal son Dushos Brezovral was working with the Path. Olvek admitted that he had sensed a disturbance beyond Theper, and emphasized that they need to be careful, reminding them that there was always those who thrived in chaos. After much deliberation, Olvek and Chancellor Brezovral eventually agreed to a fact-finding mission accompanied by two of her guards and to send Veyari Master Char-Buziax-Vafiarn and Tirren Broleix Keen to Desern and E'ronoh to try and keep the peace and discover what was causing communications buoys to be down, Olvek explaining to them that Lemam Sheka, the Path's Herald, was responsible for the riots on Theper.

### Master of Varnoth

Several decades later, Olvek took on the human Varnoth as his last known Tirren in the era of the Galactic Concord. In 90 BVC, Olvek undertook a visit to Draquiam with other Veyari Masters and younglings Skenem Vriadrian, and Varnoth. Olvek later took Varnoth on as an apprentice in 86 BVC, and while Varnoth initially wanted to become the Tirren of Lene Diahux, his disappointment subsided when he learned he would be Olvek's apprentice. Once on a visit to Draquiam, Master and apprentice together, Varnoth's father shouted at the Grand Master for bringing his son to the planet, holding a dislike for the Veyari Order and Synth Flux-sensitives. During one mission to the Varruk homeworld of Ruwokka, Varnoth witnessed his master face off with a giant, dreadful terentatek. Decades after his apprentice graduated to Knighthood, in 42 BVC, Olvek was informed by Varnoth that he was departing from the Veyari Order, much to his disappointment, and when his former student offered to turn over his arcblade, the Shobeikrex allowed him to retain ownership of his weapon.

### Olvek's secret war

Around 40 BVC, Olvek rescued a Synth Flux-sensitive child named Lo from the Flesh Mongers on the planet Seshex, intending to begin his Veyari training. After dealing with the pirates, the Veyari Master was joined by Master Sael-Rhal Ormis and his Tirren, Oru-Kei Talvane, who had been tasked with escorting the child to the Veyari Temple on Velaris. While on Seshex, Olvek sensed a disturbance in the Synth Flux and chose to follow its call which led him to Druvriavra Prime. Upon landing on the planet's surface, the Veyari Master was suddenly surrounded by child warriors who attacked him. After defending himself with the Synth Flux, Olvek told the children he meant no harm. The Veyari came in peace as he was drawn in by the Synth Flux. The group realized he had heard their Giladar, and believed his Pakruskiar to be strong. They then led Olvek to their home, Sehata. There, he learned that the Mithejar used shards of the Sacred rock during their prayer, the same call that Olvek felt. The rock was unlike anything he had ever seen before. It was a piece of blue stone, a type of rock imbued with the Living Synth Flux which Olvek could sense. The Mud dwellers told him about their war against the Jumeishun, a rival tribe. He listened to the history of their homeworld, and how the conflict began. Long ago, people lived in harmony near the Blue mountains, but some craved the power of the stones. This greed divided them into warring factions and ultimately destroyed most of the mountains. Only one remained, known as the Great Mountain, which was claimed by the Jumeishun who then banished the Mud dwellers to the Mud Fields. They carried as much stone as they could for their children, now the only survivors of the tribe. The children then led Olvek to a boy from the rival tribe they had taken prisoner. The Veyari sensed his deep fear of the Great Mountain, the sacred home of his tribe. As the children argued over his fate, Olvek urged them not to be consumed by fear. He asked them to release the child so that he could guide the Veyari to his people.

After walking to the Mountain, Olvek and the freed boy reached the Jumeishun who attacked him with the stones. Refusing to fight back, the Veyari Master was captured. The tribe then sent him on a mission to find the Heart of the Mountain, accompanied by the boy he just saved. Olvek began to climb the path leading to the Heart, but his companion was overcome by fear, convinced that only death awaited them, just like all those who had climbed before. Despite Olvek's efforts to reassure him, the boy fell to his death. At the end of the path, the Veyari entered a cave where he encountered the lost parents, known as the Old Ones, who had once been sent by their children to find the Heart of the Mountain. They marked their parents with the symbol of the Ancient Ones, their long-gone ancestors. The Old Ones told Olvek they chose not to leave this place, preferring the peace they found within the mountain to the war raging outside. Olvek replied that they abandoned their children to the very war they had provoked. He continued exploring the cave and came across a child named Dadrein, abandoned by the Jumeishun, who believed he was too weak. After saving him, Olvek asked the boy to become his teacher and show him the ways of the Pakruskiar. During their training, the Veyari Master discovered the Heart of the Mountain and realized that the stone was not only alive in the Synth Flux but was in fact a living being. After nine days of communion, Olvek heard its voice. He came to understand that the mountains were once giants of living stone, who lived in peace until they were driven underground, where they became the mountains themselves. Olvek and Dadrein eventually emerged from the cave. But the Veyari chose not to reveal what he had learned to the Jumeishun. Enraged, the tribe threatened him. However, Dadrein revealed the secret of the mountains to his people, brought Olvek’s teaching to an end, and returned to his faction.

### Pre-Invasion of Ilveren

Olvek visited Ruwokka on several other occasions prior to the Battle of Ruwokka, serving as Veyari negotiator in many incidents involving the Varruks and their Skarrim neighbors. This earned him the ancient title of "Defender of the Home Tree," as well as becoming a member of many Varruk leaders' and all the inhabitants of Jiazesheith's honor families.

Olvek also taught outside the Master/Tirren relationship, and trained all the Veyari younglings prior to their teaming up with Veyari Masters, including Master Oru-Kei Talvane. Since his days as a youngling, Joran Ashkar, a human who would eventually rise to become part of the Veyari Council, never responded well to the Grand Master's humor, which became one of the reasons Olvek continued to use his humor. Jorem Rufem, as Zheivras Sheneisk, often heard Olvek counsel, "Do, or do not. There is no try." He also taught groups of younglings in the basics of Veyari training, including arcblade instruction. He oversaw younglings undergoing the Gathering to find their own vethra crystals; on one such occasion, he told youngling Ilyra Seshu how she would know her crystal when she saw it. With these words in mind, Seshu successfully found her crystal. At another point, he taught Seshu how one could find things they were not purposely looking for.

### Invasion of Ilveren

By the invasion of the planet Ilveren by the Merchant Combine, Olvek still served on the Veyari High Council, yet he was the sole Grand Master of the Veyari Order. By the invasion, he had been training Veyari students for about 800 years. In the time before the invasion, Olvek took part in a council meeting. Several hours after that meeting, he took to mediating in a small garden in the temple, only to be interrupted by fellow council member Joran Ashkar, who, after Olvek waited for him to explain why he wanted a private discussion, expressed his belief that Olvek had sensed something he was keeping private. Olvek repeatedly responded that he sensed nothing: the issue was not that there was a problem to be sensed, but that something was being hidden. Not understanding but trusting the Grand Master's judgement, Ashkar excused himself while Olvek, incorrectly believing the umbral tide was far away, tried to find what it was that he could not sense. Olvek was unable to uncover the fact that the sensation was caused by the secret plots of the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion.

Concurrent to the Federation's occupation on Ilveren, Olvek was present when Veyari Master Sael-Rhal Ormis presented Edrik Vantreil, a young boy from the planet Sarrakesh, to the Council in the hopes of training him. Although Ormis believed Vantreil to be the Chosen One, prophesied to bring balance to the Synth Flux, Olvek and the other Council members did not share his opinion, sensing that the boy was harboring a high amount of fear and his future was uncertain. They therefore refused to allow for his training.

### Secessionist Crisis

Ten years after the Ilveren crisis, several thousand systems that were disillusioned with the Galactic Concord seceded from the government and formed the League of Independent Systems under the leadership of Count Varnoth, the former student of Olvek who left the Order due to widespread corruption in the Concord. As the Galactic Senate, the chief governing body of the Concord, debated the creation of a standing army, an unsuccessful attempt was made on the life of Senator Corvelle of Ilveren. After Veyari Knight Oru-Kei Talvane and his Tirren Edrik Vantreil cornered the assassin, the killer was in turn killed by a mysterious person who escaped using his jetpack. The events prompted the Council, which included Olvek, to appoint Talvane to track him down.

Talvane's investigation led him to attempt to find the planet Thessil, whose location had been missing from the Veyari Archives. This discovery alarmed Olvek, who surmised that only a Veyari could have erased the files. Interrupting a training session with the Bear Clan, Olvek helped Talvane pinpoint the world's exact location through meditation, allowing the latter to travel there. Following Talvane's departure, Olvek experienced a vision while meditating, in which he heard the voice of the spirit of Sael-Rhal Ormis, and sensed that Edrik Vantreil was in terrible pain. He discussed the vision with fellow Master Joran Ashkar afterwards.

### The Replica Wars

News that Vantreil and Talvane had been captured prompted the Council to dispatch a Veyari strike team to Kharrab, while Olvek headed to Thessil to take command of the replica forces. As a replica strike team interrupted the execution of Talvane and his associates, the replicas suffered casualties at the hands of the numerically superior League battle mechanoid forces. Olvek led the replicas into battle, thus beginning a galaxywide conflict that would become known as the Replica Wars.

As the replicas engaged the League's forces in a massive battle, Olvek headed to rescue Talvane and Vantreil from Varnoth, who had incapacitated the duo in a duel. The diminutive Veyari Master showed his mastery and power over the Synth Flux surpassed that of Varnoth by casually repelling the latter's telekinetic assault and Synth Flux lightning. He then engaged Varnoth in a brief but intense duel, having the upper hand. Varnoth, realizing he was outmatched by Olvek, used the Synth Flux to bring a large metal pillar down on the unconscious Vantreil and Talvane, forcing Olvek to break off the duel and use his own powers to stop it from crushing them. Varnoth used the distraction to flee aboard his personal craft. Although the battle on Kharrab ended in the Concord's favor, Olvek was concerned about the growing influence of the umbral tide of the Synth Flux and the outcome of the Replica Wars.
