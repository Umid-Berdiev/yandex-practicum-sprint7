# Velaris

Velaris (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds, or Hegemonic Center during the Hegemonic Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Velaris system of the Velaris subsector in the Core Worlds's Temosk sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Galactic Concord and the Galactic Hegemony that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Hegemonic City, Velaris was the ancient homeworld of the Kribrol and Quashoth species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Velaris was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Velaris's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Velaris's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Veyari Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Galactic Concord, Velaris was at the center of many historic events, such as the Veyari Order's conflict with the Morkai. Although the planet fell under Morkai control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Velaris with the aid of the Veyari Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Morkai became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Galactic Concord. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Velaris where the Galactic Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Veyari peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Velaris into a dangerous environment by the time of the Replica Wars with the League of Independent Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Deito Malverin, who was, in fact, the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion, declared the Veyari enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Veyari Order. Without the Veyari to oppose him, Malverin dissolved the Concord and declared himself Galactic Hegemon to the applause of the now Hegemonic Senate.

Velaris continued to serve as galactic capital under the Hegemony, having become the Hegemon's throneworld, although Malverin himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Hegemonic Ruling Council. The Hegemony maintained a firm hold on Velaris throughout the Hegemon's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Thalwen in 4 AVC by the Pact to Restore the Concord, citizens of Velaris celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the insurgents restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Mas Traga's rule was limited to Velaris while Fleet Admiral Jeshesk Venn, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Hegemony, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Hegemonic government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Traga ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Venn's death at the Battle of Leinesk in 5 AVC, Traga signed the Galactic Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Hegemony's capitulation and the end of the Galactic Civil War. Traga himself was permitted to return to Velaris as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Velaris as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concord Era, Velaris had become the capital of the New Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concord Senate, Velaris enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Velaris had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Jozeis.

During the Prime Mandate-Defiance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Prime Mandate to Velaris in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Galactic Hegemony once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Vozix, various worlds sought to drive the Prime Mandate military from their soil. Velaris was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Prime Mandate.

### Overview

Velaris, also known as Hegemonic Center during the Hegemonic Era or as the Jewel of the Core Worlds, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Velaris system in the Velaris subsector of the Temosk sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Worlds and The Interior. The homeworld of the Quashoth and Kribrol species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Velaris, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important slipspace routes including the Feilanian Run, the Vriasho Trunk Line, the Lethekron Trade Route, the Drogrim Corridor, the Kavaveim Trade Route, the Quazar Alley, and the secret Nexus Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Galactic Concord, then for it's successor state, the Galactic Hegemony, and even briefly for the New Concord in 9 AVC, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Lokriathe's importance in galactic history, even later governments such as the Prime Mandate would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Velaris had four moons—Mashus-1, Mashus-2, Mashus-3, and Hesperidium. Velaris orbited Velaris Prime, the only star of the Velaris system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days, with each day lasting 24 Standard hours. The Velaris week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Velaris is some way— Dothitrax, named after Velaris's sun, Breisagru, named after Velaris's primary moon, Kridratriax, named after the native Kribrol species, Negonath, named after the native Quashoth species, and Brafasia, named after the Kreifei Skothe monks from Velaris. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Velaris's orbital period and rotational period respectively.

### Hegemonic City

Velaris's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called Galactic City until it was officially renamed as Hegemonic City during the Hegemonic Era. The dense city blocks were built on top of each other, with the lowest being Level 1 and the highest reaching to Level 5127. The lowest known habitable level was Level 5. At its highest level, Galactic City's skyscrapers were built with many reaching 6,000 meters into the atmosphere, with sleek, transparisteel edifices standing next to older duracrete structures. As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Velaris provided the ideal conditions for a thief to prosper, especially targeting tourists from quieter planets.

Galactic City was divided into sectors named by designated coordinates. Some sectors also had unofficial names; for example, sector H-46 was the nicknamed of "Sah'c Town" due to the Sah'c family's ownership of much of the sector. Sectors were further divided into zones, with zones determined by purpose, such as financial, senatorial, or industrial zones.

### Early history

In ancient times, Velaris was populated by two sentient species, the Kribrol and the Quashoth. Sometime in its distant past, Velaris was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations. Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Velaris was widely accepted as the human homeworld. Nevertheless, the human Dushos Brezovral claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Velaris did not have grand creation myths. In time, humans expanded throughout the known galaxy, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Velaris becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life. In 100,000 BVC, the lowest levels of Velaris, such as Level 1, were once called home by the people of Velaris. As the planet expanded up, however, the buildings turned to ancient ruins that were buried. It was believed those low levels were left uninhabitable.

Many different political systems held control over Velaris during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and dictatorships to democracies. Velaris was one of the Galactic Concord's original Core World founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered slipspace routes. As these early slipspace lanes became well-established trade routes, increasing numbers of alien species migrated towards Velaris; this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed. Velaris's effective status as the center of the Concord led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts. As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000 years before the Galactic Civil War, it almost covered the entire surface of the planet.

### The Galactic Concord

Over the millennia representatives gathered in the Galactic Senate for diplomacy and dispute resolutions, creating and enforcing laws, and maintaining peace. As such, Velaris became known as the galactic capital for thousands of years. In 382 BVC, a Sheidequox became inebriated and was arrested for disturbing the peace on Level 1392, resulting in their imprisonment on Level 2623. Around the same time, the son of Supreme Chancellor Triazhath Brezovral, Dushos, arrived on Level 2623 to visit the Tefox's Parlor casino. However, after starting a fight in the gambling den, he was arrested by members of the Velaris Guard, who revealed they had been sent by his mother. When cult guide Drilen Bratrex reformed the already-radicalized Path of the Open Hand into the militant Path of the Closed Fist to war against the Veyari and other Synth Flux-sensitives, she had dreams of spreading their insurgency as far as Velaris itself, urging the Path to follow her across the stars. While the Path quickly fell under Bratrex's sway, the ensuing Battle of Mafen marked the destruction of the cult, although Bratrex herself escaped and began a new plan that culminated in the creation of the Gatis marauders.

It was towards the end of the Concord Era that the Morkai Lord Xarn Nyxarion executed his plans for galactic domination, beginning with the blockade of Ilveren, his homeworld that he represented as Senator Deito Malverin. The Morkai Order, having survived its near destruction through Xarn Skar's Rule of Two, conspired for generations to reclaim their place of dominance in the galaxy. By Nyxarion' time, the Morkai secretly resided on Velaris in spite of the Veyari Order's presence on the capital. The Veyari at the time were under the belief that their ancient rivals had been extinct for over a millennium.

### The Galactic Hegemony

As part of his endgame, Nyxarion issued Directive 91 to the entire Grand Army of the Concord, using hidden inhibitor chips to force the replica troopers into killing their Veyari Generals. On Velaris, the Great Veyari Purge commenced with Velgor and the 501st Legion's siege of the Veyari Temple. With the replica army under his control, Nyxarion claimed the title of Galactic Hegemon after issuing the proclamation of the New Order in a special session of Congress, declaring a new era of order and security under the first Galactic Hegemony. The Secessionist attack on Velaris had exposed the capital's vulnerability, which, combined with Nyxarion' allegation of a "Veyari insurgency," compelled the Senate to support the Hegemon and his New Order.

Olvek and Talvane returned to Velaris in the aftermath of Directive 91. After learning of Vantreil's turn to the umbral tide of the Synth Flux, the two Veyari Masters resolved to destroy the Morkai. Talvane left Velaris to find his fallen apprentice, whereas Olvek remained on the capital to confront the Hegemon. However, Olvek failed to defeat Nyxarion; as a consequence, he exiled himself to Murgath. Meanwhile, Velgor was grievously injured on Pyrrhax by Talvane, resulting in his reconstruction on Velaris as a cyborg.

### New Concord Era

With the Galactic Civil War concluded, the New Concord sought to reshape galactic politics in an effort to both prevent the abuses of the Old Concord and bring in disaffected worlds that had seceded from the Concord prior to the Replica Wars. To accomplish this, the restored Galactic Senate decided that all worlds would have an equal say in their government, and as such all worlds had an equal chance of hosting the legislative branch on a rotating basis, rather than simply remaining on Velaris alone. The New Concord would eventually move some government operations back to Velaris as the planet was already designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure, transforming Velaris into a key administrative hub for the young government.

In 9 AVC, after Breidenoth had taken on the capital title for a time, Velaris was named the New Concord's capital. All the while, Velaris remained a populous world, home to a trillion permanent residents, and was still referred to as "the center of the galaxy" during the New Concord Era. The New Concord Defense Synth Flux maintained a presence on Velaris, providing security and structure, while overseeing the decommission of warships from both the Hegemonic Navy and the Pact Fleet. Inoperable Hegemonic-class Nova Dreadnoughts were disposed in salvage yards such as the Shipyard Depot on Velaris. Some Velarisi, however, displayed a gleeful disregard for whatever politics shaped the galaxy and happily supported whatever government was in charge, displaying a lack of care for what it entailed beyond Velaris.

### Rise of the Prime Mandate

In the middle of the Prime Mandate-Defiance War, Velaris, along with the the Inner systems, mounted a valiant and successful effort to repel the Prime Mandate's forces twice. As of 35 AVC, at least two sizeable battlegroups had made a push for Velaris in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from Velaris due to it once being the seat of power for the Hegemony, but these efforts were defeated. There was, however, a good deal of confusion regarding recent events at the end of the war, preventing much public understanding surrounding the push for Velaris and the subsequent defense of Velaris. Defiance files made reference to a planned "Operation on Velaris."

Velaris took part in open insurgency against the Prime Mandate, which saw the Prime Mandate ultimately defeated.

## Inhabitants

Although Velaris was believed to be the homeworld of the human species, the Velarisi were as varied as the galaxy itself, especially as one journeyed below the surface of the skyscrapers and into the underworld. Trillions of beings resided on the planet, and humans made up 68% percent of the population. According to Sael-Rhal Ormis, there were people from a thousand worlds living on Velaris. Th'er, the Priestess of Wood from Skadel'n, disdained Velaris's planet-wide urban sprawl as an "abomination." Nevertheless, Ormis encouraged Th'er to take advantage of its diverse population, noting that there were many individuals on Velaris whose worlds faced adversities similar to that of Skadel'n.

As the center of galactic power, senators and delegates of every represented planet had a residence on Velaris. From there they would spend their days either working in the Senate or hosting events such as parties and political meetings. Additionally, the planet served as the home of large communities of various species, some who rarely returned to their original homeworld. Many of those communities of species that were not human resided in the lower levels of the city. With the formation of the Hegemony, even the humans whose home planet had fallen out of favor with the Hegemonic government, such as Belisar, were forced into enclaves in the underworld.

## Government

Velaris, as the center of the galaxy, thrived on the rule of government. It served as the capital of the Concord which kept the peace and prevented a major war in the galaxy for over a thousand years. Under the Concord, the Chancellor and the Senate had a direct influence on the planet's governance. Major events that affected the Concord equally affected Velaris. In its last years, the Concord's Senate became corrupt, bloated, and ultimately ineffectual, though some continued to fight in the belief of its ability to govern.

With the formation of the Hegemony, the Senate transitioned into a limited role, providing council and legislation but ultimately at the power of the Hegemon. As Velaris's government services were strained, the Hegemonic Senate moved to have each senator open citizen assistance offices that would supplement the work of the local government. While the goal was to primarily assist the members of each senator's homeworld living on Velaris, the assistance offices were open to help all Velarisi.

### The upper city

CoCo Town was a dilapidated industrial area of Velaris. Home to mostly hard-working laborers, CoCo Town was located on a surface area of the city. The area, however, was not as run down as other heavy industrial surface districts and contained relatively well-maintained streets, speeder lanes, and a shuttle bus route. On one of the main streets built on top of the urban blocks was an eatery called Heihe's Diner. Owned and operated by Dexter Brelejax, a Treifeifon with a colorful background, it served mostly hard workers from the area. The diner offered home-style cooked meals and freshly brewed ardees. Brelejax worked as the cook and employed two waitresses, Fofemax Sihon and her mechanoid partner, WA-7. Shortly before the Replica Wars, Veyari Knight Oru-Kei Talvane came to Heihe's Diner to see if his old friend Brelejax could identify a poisoned dart. Brelejax identified it as originating from the planet Thessil, which he knew of from his days as a prospector.

During the civil war that erupted after the Hegemon's death, CoCo Town became an epicenter of the fight between insurgent and Hegemonic forces. The fighting saw the formation of the so-called Shagiazur Brigade, a group of youths familiar with the underground maintenance systems, who would use them to sneak behind Hegemonic lines and disrupt their abilities to fight.
