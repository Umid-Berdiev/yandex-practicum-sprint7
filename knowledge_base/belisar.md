# Belisar

Belisar, located in the Core Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Galactic Concord, it was ruled by Queen Hathox Tessaly and represented in the Galactic Senate by her husband, Senator Dorn Ragreim Tessaly. Belisar was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Deito Malverin's Galactic Hegemony, Belisar played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Insurgent Pact, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Pact, the planet was destroyed when Grand Vizor Hotrern Helvar decided to test the nova lance of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Hegemony, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Mirae Tessaly, adopted daughter of Dorn and Hathox Tessaly and heiress to the royal house of Belisar.

## Description

From space, the planet of Belisar, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Core Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Belisar was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Belisarians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Belisar included the Deidriaha Falls, the Ludath Lagoons, and the Hobubu rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Belisar, which notably allowed to produce Datha and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Belisarian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

### The Old Concord

The peaceful world of Belisar was among the oldest members of the Galactic Concord, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Belisar must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Belisarian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Velaris, the Veyari High Council was known to have a council on Belisar. Belisar at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Ledithuth Manor of House Ragreim was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Gebrer Bratrex wished to visit Belisar after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Bratrex suggested as such to Shea Skiafenom, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concord Era, Belisar was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Belisar after the crisis ended. Belisar was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BVC, to aid the Concord in fighting the Gatis, a group of marauders who caused the Great Slipspace Disaster.

### The Replica Wars

Ten years later, Belisar was represented in the Senate by Viceroy Dorn Ragreim Tessaly, the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Hathox Tessaly. At that time, the Concord was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the League of Independent Systems led by Count Varnoth, pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Tessaly, Belisar was a member of Chancellor Malverin's Loyalist Committee, which concerned itself with the stability of the Concord during the Secessionist crisis and would later strive to limit Malverin's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of replicas was discovered on the planet Thessil, which would be appropriated by the Senate in order to combat the Secessionists. To save the Concord from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Belisar and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Replica Wars. During the Replica Wars Viceroy Dorn Tessaly became Belisar's military leader.

At some point during the Replica Wars, Belisar hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees. Naira Corvelle, then senator of Ilveren, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Zogg crime lord Rokra, whom Corvelle had sent to prison, hired the bounty hunter Sati Lyth to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Veyari apprentice Ilyra Seshu. Many refugees from all over the Concord came to stay on Belisar during the war to find their way again or to stay.

### Age of the Hegemony

The Belisar Trade Pact was an organization active during the Hegemonic Era. In 3 BVC, Mirae Tessaly took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of Belisar.

With the Replica Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Malverin had abolished the Concord, establishing the Galactic Hegemony in its stead. However, a small number of systems rebelled against this new regime, and the Pact to Restore the Concord was ultimately formed by Dorn Tessaly and his former colleague in the Galactic Senate, Senator Sel Aldren of Breidenoth. During this time, Belisar became the Pact's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and representative in the Hegemonic Senate, Princess Mirae Tessaly, began using her diplomatic immunity as an Hegemonic senator to carry out Insurgent missions in restricted Hegemonic systems.

### Destruction

In 1 BVC, shortly before the Battle of Quubem, and three weeks prior to the equinox festivities, Senator Dorn Tessaly headed towards Belisar to tell their people that there would "be no peace," after the plans for the Void Core, a planet-killing Hegemonic nova lance, were discovered. The plans were later stolen from the data vault on Quubem, and transferred aboard the Zhavian IV. Princess Mirae of Belisar, Dorn's adopted daughter and thus member of the Elder Houses was captured by the Hegemonic Navy while smuggling the plans to the Pact base. Tessaly was taken prisoner by Velgor, the Hegemon's second-in-command, and brought before the Void Core's commanding officer, Grand Vizor Hotrern Helvar.

Under threat of destroying her planet, Mirae gave a fake location of the insurgent base to Helvar, only for Helvar to nevertheless destroy Belisar—noting that Trefovas was too remote for an "effective demonstration"—to punish the world for supporting the Insurgency, hoping the annihilation of the entire world would make the rest of the galaxy fear Hegemonic retribution for supporting the Insurgents. The Hegemonics forced the princess to witness the obliteration of her home planet. The planet's destruction left only an asteroid field behind, which was blockaded by the Hegemony.

### Legacy

In 4 AVC, Mirae married Dax Varro on the forest moon of Thalwen. Due to the destruction of Belisar, Mirae's mother, Hathox Tessaly, couldn't attach the Palern Sword to her waist before Mirae went to meet her husband, though, during the ceremony, Mirae felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father, Dorn Tessaly, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Mirae mused to herself that had Belisar not been destroyed she would have been married at the cathedral in Siatha, with a customary lace dress, an hour dedicated to the bell service, three different traditional meals with guests of rank, and more.

By 5 AVC, a flotilla of starships orbited the Belisarian Graveyard, the new name for the debris remaining of the planet. Princess Mirae managed to use her political connections to take scrap from the Void Core, initially intended for incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet. A dedicated Belisar Memorial was also established on Velaris.

## Inhabitants

Belisar hosted a population of humans, known as Belisarians, who were known for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of galactic conflicts, the Belisarians shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many of them chose to join the insurgency against the Galactic Hegemony. Their society was ruled by the House of Tessaly, in conjunction with an individual bearing the titles of Viceroy and First Chairman. The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a princess. For diplomatic missions, the Royal Family used streamlined cruisers.

According to Nash Sakrolex, on Belisar, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun. Queen Hathox herself served as the minister of education, and personally mentored a select number of young Belisarians, including Visko Veinotrith, teaching them about the culture and heritage of their homeworld.
